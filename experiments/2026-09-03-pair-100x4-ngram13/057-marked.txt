The house that she knew for years had been occupied by several different owners, many of them men of color from other places where she was raised. She recalled one man as a black immigrant from the Southern states, another the owner of a business building, and a third the owner of a small business. Sometimes he came to her in a white suit, but they all wore a black shirt, black ties, and the same kind of black shoes she wore when she was little.
