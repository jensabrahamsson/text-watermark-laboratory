The staircase was opened up to an inch at a time in a wide courtyard overlooking another well-equipped kitchen, at a corner of the kitchen opposite. They were dining with five or six people in a room, with one or two dining in the back with a single window overlooking the house ahead and the house behind. The kitchen was a large, white-roofed wooden structure. There was never a large window, and in every window there were two or three windows of different sizes, to which some people used to sit on their heads.
