The guards were always looking for those who were looking down on him because they thought such a person might be of great help. When they found him, the men looked back and forth on what had happened to the last few years. Even with all the time and money they had, no one knew what he had been through and who had been at his side.

Just looking back, he couldn't have said anything to those men, his thoughts being focused on the only one who even remembered. At the same time, he couldn't feel any fear anymore.

He could only listen to their voice the longer they said this
