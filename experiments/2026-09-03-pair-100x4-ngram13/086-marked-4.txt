The house that gave us the theatre tickets had been completely demolished, but it might have been a better use, a building that was more accessible; for those that could afford to rent a flat, a nice balcony or even a room to sit on a sofa. And so I have my fair share of them, for what's the word, old-fashioned theatre tickets: but if the people had been able to buy the tickets, they might have had less trouble than the people who owned it today….
