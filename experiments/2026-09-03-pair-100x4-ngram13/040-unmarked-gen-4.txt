"Why does you think the ice machine went down?" asked the nurse, her voice trembling as she examined the floor.

The path was long narrow, the wall below it as well as the snow below. It was just not a good idea since nobody wanted to walk on it. "When did it stop? When it hit the wall?"

"Didn't it fall down?"

"No, it made it fall down to the ice itself."

"It fell down to the ice?"

The nurses were shocked. At this point, the ice machine on the floor hadn't been moving.
