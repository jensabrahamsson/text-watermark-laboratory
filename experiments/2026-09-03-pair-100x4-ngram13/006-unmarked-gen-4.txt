By last night, the newsagent had been paying $45 an hour. On the ground in Baghdad, where, in April, gunmen attacked in Baghdad's main subway system, newsroom staff on the ground called the office of the chief of staff to complain.

The pressroom did not call for a meeting, not even to ask him to be transferred, and not even to ask if an officer had been killed, he told investigators.

"You are probably thinking that I have something to do," he said. "I do not."

It was a message he sent to his colleagues in the press.
