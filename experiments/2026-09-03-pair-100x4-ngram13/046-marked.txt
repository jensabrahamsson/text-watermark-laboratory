The water had a very bright, white background, the sky almost perfectly blue. It was an extraordinary and perfect image of the place, and the sky seemed so full of life, that one could see one step farther than a hundred yards away from an island.

The lake had very different colors and shades. The color of the water reflected the different colored pools in the water. One saw a rainbow, another a blue. The only way to see all of the different colors was by swimming.

All that made it a beautiful place that no one else had ever seen before.
