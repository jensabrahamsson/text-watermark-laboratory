The bus drivers who had come had brought their kids with them in. The people who had been staying in their houses had been taken back to their apartment. The buses had not been carrying any passengers nor had they made any efforts to get to work there. On the way back, I had seen them and I noticed the blackness of their clothes.

The bus driver would never let them pass it, and never let anyone pass the stop. He never gave my baby a choice.
