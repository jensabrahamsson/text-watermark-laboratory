The bridge's front doors slid open to reveal a large, dark rectangular room. One arm was in the center and the other at the right side.

"Do I go now?" asked the elderly woman.

"No. This is the new master's house," said the man.

"My uncle and Grandfather's house," said the man behind them. He could get away with just the two of them. When he left, they walked away, and they had a long walk.

"Do you want your aunt or uncle in charge?" said the woman in the center of the room.
