The house is a four-by-four-foot mosaic, dating to 1851, filled with photographs of people, pets and the place of worship. Inside there are paintings, a book, a signboard portrait and several photos of the couple. There are also some hardcover maps, a photograph of the woman in the photograph box, one of the pictures of Jesus Christ and the woman in the photograph box.

"One of the first things people will notice here is the fact that everything is in these walls and all these boxes," Mr. Hodge explained.
