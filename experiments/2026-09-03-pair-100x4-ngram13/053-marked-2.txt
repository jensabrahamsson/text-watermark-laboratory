The fire started in 1531 and, by now completely surrounded by the ruins of a building, it was quite a sight in an open field.

The whole village, all but ten homes, was burned down by the flames, with many more burned out within minutes.

The fire still hadn't recovered from the ruins.

On the night following the disaster the Roman Emperor sent a messenger to Tiberius as a warning.

It seemed that he was preparing to attack the city with a mighty expedition.

But when Rome saw his plan, it was to take the city and take the city itself.
