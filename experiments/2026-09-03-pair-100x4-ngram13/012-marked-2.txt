The gate was open – and people were still in their seats.

As the gate opened, they heard someone cry: "H-why did you open that gate this time?"

It was a female. A man and a short woman, and a male and a young boy.

There were six men and four women standing at the gate. One of them had a black hair and was about eight meters tall. The other man had gray beard, and had a long scar on both feet, the man on the left had a beard, the person on the right had a beard….
