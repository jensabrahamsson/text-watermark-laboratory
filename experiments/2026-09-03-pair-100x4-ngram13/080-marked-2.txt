The water had a very bright colour to it — very bright — and some of your companions seemed to ask if you were sure you were going to die.

It was said that the king heard it and told the whole village to go on out for their own lives. The village was quite deserted, and the wind blew the house off. My husband stayed for a long time and I stayed for the longest. His father did the work with joy and was very kind and warm. He never spoke to anything in their lives except the breadcrumbs that were piled in their laps for us at our first Christmas dinner in that house.
