The plan is intended to save as much as 10 percent on costs through higher taxes of the rich in the first place through a "super tax" that the federal and state governments will raise for the top 10 percent from a lower percentage. (One way to get there will be to reduce government benefits for the wealthy by creating a voucher and then giving the government the tools to do that and then passing them on to the middle class.)

Taxing "low-wage workers" will boost the average wage by at least half, since fewer "low earners" would not work longer.
