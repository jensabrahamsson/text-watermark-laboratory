In the spring the ladders began to fall apart and the wood was used to start up the next crop.

With the growing season over, two of the ladders fell from their sides and into the river.

They were found by a worker who came back with his cane. Afterward, they were left in a tree beside the farm.

At the farm, a lorry was brought along with me. It had already arrived by train.

I took it home and was able to go to see the dead as a witness to these acts of revenge.
