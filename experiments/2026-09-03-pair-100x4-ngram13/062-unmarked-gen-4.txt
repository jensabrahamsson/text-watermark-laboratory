Shelby stood in the middle of the street, her arms folded, a little taller than her boyfriend.

In the night market, a person would be forced to carry small bags of food and drinks. Shelby, the mother of four who'd been with her in the church for more than a year,'d been forced to walk six miles three nights in search of supplies. If they could't get it to her, they'd be dead. Even though the family was in poverty, it could only pay it back if Shelby sold all her food and drinks—every penny.
