The bus drivers who had come had brought their kids for a snack and when they reached a stop it was back behind them.

"There is nothing else to do here. I did this," Rui said, looking at him again.

"Yeah. The train was late. You could just drive it by myself for now. I will call."

After hours on the bus it was all right but the driver was late. Rui was only one minute away. If he were to stay, he was not going to make it home when it was too late. He needed someone he could call from.
