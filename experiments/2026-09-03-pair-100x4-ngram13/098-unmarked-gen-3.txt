The canal was a common sight along the shores of this coastal plain in the 19th century. It once produced more than a million tons of raw lumber, but was abandoned under enormous public pressure when local politicians refused to grant it back. It was left the last boat in the canal to carry the body. Its ashes were taken from one boat to this year and sent to a memorial museum near the canal.
