"We found that there is no fire in the chapel," said police spokesman Sgt. Chris McConville.

McConville says authorities said they found four boxes near where a second fire damaged a church in South Portland. McConville couldn't tell how many were taken to the hospital.

Police, firefighters and ambulances are helping to search for those killed.

No one is reported missing but the fire department and its crews have been asked to continue search as they have until the end of the search. Anyone with information is urged to call 911.
