The drill involved the construction of a concrete ball to hold and compress concrete, which allowed the water to circulate and fill the field around the drill site (see Figure 1). The water from the drill field will move to an existing water reservoir nearby where it will be used to fill the field. The goal of the exercise was to create the necessary water in the field. This process did not stop until the water was used to fill the water reservoir (see Figure 2).
