The police arrested the 25-year-old woman about 11 p.m. Wednesday. She was taken to the hospital, where she was in a critical condition, police said. Neighbors said they saw her trying to sell stolen items. The woman told police she did not plan on selling the items. Detectives arrested her Tuesday on charges of felony theft.

She was booked into the Mariposa County Jail on four counts of burglary and grand larceny. One count of grand larceny carries a maximum term of life in prison. The other charges carry a maximum of life in prison if convicted.
