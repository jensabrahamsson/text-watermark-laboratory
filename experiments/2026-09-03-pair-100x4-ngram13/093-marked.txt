The house went quiet, its sound echoing around her and her heart beating fast in her chest. No trace of her life became visible in the yard.

It was morning, and she wasn't looking at her house when she woke to find what she had seen in her husband in front of her daughter's bedroom.

She opened the door and saw her father driving along toward the house. He was dressed in a shirt and suit that he had bought for a son.

The family was not wearing shoes. It was obvious there must have been some hiding place. That's when she felt the hand of luck touch her
