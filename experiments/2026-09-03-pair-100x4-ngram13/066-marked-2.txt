The congregation watched the ceremony live on TV on the roof.

Some of the more than 100 people on hand were then greeted by the priest, who told them they could return and return to their parish on Sunday if they wished.

The congregation's leader, Father Martin Luther King Jr. said: "It is an honor to see this happen. We welcome all Christians who are still searching their God through the darkness.

"This is an occasion of mourning and an opportunity to remember our ancestors, share their joy in one another's community and support the mission of the Church."
