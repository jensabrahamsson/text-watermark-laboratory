The smell of petrol wafted through the market, with cars roaring down the road. "We'll have to pay for these, though," the man said. "If they're dirty, the drivers have got to pay for the cars."

Shelburne said that some young men took their petrol to the nearby petrol station. "They took our money," he said.

It was unclear to the BBC who was behind the petrol station theft and why there weren't any men behind it in the area.

"It was a very tight-knit community which had to deal with it," he said.
