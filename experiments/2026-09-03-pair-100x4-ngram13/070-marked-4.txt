The water had a hint of salty red. A small white stream had been raised up above the surface before it was submerged.

He stood on a platform on either side of a huge stone arch, his hair, as thick and dark as a forest bough, a line of hair hanging across his forehead.

The first step to his destination was a small tower with a door and gates. The second had no opening.

His hands and feet dangled on the rock. His head landed on the narrow wooden table.
