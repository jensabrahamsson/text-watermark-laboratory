The bus driver found out about the missing person's disappearance and took him to the hospital, where he told her that he never saw the woman, reported CBS News.

"He knows that I don't believe he saw the woman. I believe he saw a child in a white, black or green bus and he left immediately," mother of 7-year-old Amber told CBS News.

No other children have been identified in the disappearance.

The bus was missing after the bus driver heard three other people arguing when the woman returned late at night at 4:08 p.m. the news station reported.
