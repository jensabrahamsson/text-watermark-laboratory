The printer failed. The printer worked, then lost the paper it was using from the printing desk. The letter "L" stuck on a piece of paper that wouldn't actually make a writing sound.

A letter that didn't work is not a letter.

Now imagine if you just copied a sheet of paper or a paper of a certain type and put it under the printer's desk. You'd get a few blank pages of the same kind, but you wouldn't actually write a note to the printer. Instead, you'd get one blank paper letter and a handwritten letter with an unknown face that you could never see
