The gate was open – and people were still in their seats.

As the sun lit, a girl with glasses appeared. Her white hair was long, dark, and silky. It made the people gasp with joy.

"A-a-Auu, a-Auu!!!" The girl's expression became gloomy.

"Hm? Hey, hey, I'm from Luchnou Continent!! Please come here!"

The man with eyes like a big knife was talking to the girl.

There were many people who would take the same response as her at this moment.
