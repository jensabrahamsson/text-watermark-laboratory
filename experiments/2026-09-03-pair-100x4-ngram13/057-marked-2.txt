The house had become abandoned, and the man had only his possessions; one day he walked across the hall to the house where he lived with his wife, and she had been brought in. There she had given birth to a child, and died.

This man was still alive when he said this; but his name was James, and he looked like a man that was dead. He sat on his bare feet in the shade.
