The former president of Greece's Bank for International Settlements, Paul Hellmann, says:

"We had a crisis which had brought about a financial crisis."

Greek workers went on strike after the bailout deal expired this month, taking 30,000 jobs over four months, including a large number from the public sector.

Workers say the new law will cause a major change to jobs in the industrial sector.

The Greek public sector employs 4 million workers, according to Eurostat.
