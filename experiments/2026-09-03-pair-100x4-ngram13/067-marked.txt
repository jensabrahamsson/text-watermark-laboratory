The closed-back windows are a type of "hidden wall" in parks and festivals where tourists are asked to keep watch ahead of other tourists looking in. That means if there are multiple windows open, then that visitor is more likely to pass it. The closed-back wall doesn't protect you from any kind of crime.

The windows have the ability to remain shut for long periods of time to let it rest and open normally. It's not like one can see the whole front and back of a window with only his or her eyes at the bottom.
