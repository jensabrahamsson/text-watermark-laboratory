The fire department was called to the scene in early the morning after smoke from the pipe started bubbling for an hour. The fire department has responded within minutes and is waiting for an ambulance to arrive to retrieve the pipe.

"I'm so glad they called the police," said firefighter Steve Lott. "They have taken away a huge piece of our family's house."

The charred debris from the fire spilled onto the road, and police evacuated the two buildings.

A city spokesman said the fire was discovered with all residents safe, although no injuries were reported.
