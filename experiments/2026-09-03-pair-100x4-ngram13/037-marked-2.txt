The couple, who have not made a formal statement to the media but have asked in Spanish for anonymity, were seen riding into the harbor by an armed policeman, who ordered them to take a breath.

A passer-by at their home in Santa Fe, Nev., had witnessed some of the incident as he watched.

A neighbour, Sandra L. Fudge, described his fear for his granddaughter.

"She's very young," said Fudge. "In fact, she's more concerned about having to do with her future and her mom making a decision, because she's too young to decide."
