The plan is called the "Wake-up Call" and will let customers come to the city and ask them how much they really need as part of their pension package.

There would then be one per cent payment based on the value of their contributions.

"People would have different needs depending on that," said John Stahl, a spokesman for Greater Manchester council.

He said the plan uses real estate prices for what's being sold, not real estate values.

The change goes into effect next May.
