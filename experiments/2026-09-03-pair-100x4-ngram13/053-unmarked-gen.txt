We started on to the church again in the next round or two. The little house we lived in was a sort of little house with the windows still open, but we moved in and made it quite comfortable. From time to time I would try to get a good view of it, so that I could understand, by some miracle, how it went, as it were, through the windows of the house.

The church was pretty clear and well maintained, and the people who lived there kept a close watch on it for all day long. The parish priest was a good fellow as usual. It was a fine place to work
