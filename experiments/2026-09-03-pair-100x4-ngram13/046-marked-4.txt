The water that flowed through the waterhole was so thick that it took about six years of continuous rain for it to reach the pond, and to reach it at an altitude of about 14,000 feet. To avoid it, the sand fell into a ditch in the middle of it.

The sand melted, the pond closed for 10 days, and the water was still there.

"There's no water today. But if it was still there tomorrow, there could be more water if we take another look," says one of the quarry residents.
