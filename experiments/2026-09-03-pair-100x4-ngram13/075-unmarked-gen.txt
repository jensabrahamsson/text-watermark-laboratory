It could not be seen who was in charge. It was not Mrs. Woodrow who was in charge.

"It was that lady," remembered James Woodrow. "She called him the 'Son of God,' and she said that he had got his fill of a flower, and that he told him that his father was coming out of jail."

James Woodrow and James Woodrow Jr.

Mrs. Woodrow said: "In this court you were on his side, and he would not have let you come against me on that account, and said you could not have stayed there without a letter."
