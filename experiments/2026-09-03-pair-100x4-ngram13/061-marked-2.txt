The water had a very bright, white background.

"Oh my God, now where are your clothes?"

"In the bathroom!" She whispered.

Harry rolled up his sleeves a little, and the water started to wash away the dirty clothes. This was the only time his skin was dry in his sleep.

Suddenly, she couldn't hear. They couldn't see each other.

She knew it, but she needed a reason.

She opened her book—one with an old picture of his head on it at night, the other her mother's face, with his words from earlier
