The workman did an impression of his apprentice. He sat there for a moment, and then looked up, at the old man.

"Good luck," said the old man. "Let's go."

He went over slowly, coming up first to the ladder, and then, in a short moment, to the tree. He did not like to wait the man over; he didn't like to hear how far over he was.
