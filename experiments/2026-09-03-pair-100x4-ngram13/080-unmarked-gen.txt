"The water was a hundred times as deep as it is now. There was a stone and a wooden fence. I could see the sea with my naked ass and saw what I thought was a small stone ship passing through a forest in the mountains. I had been in the forests every day just trying to get out of these huge dunes."

The only sound in the cabin was a whirr and the air smelled of the pine blossom trees. A woman stood on a chair on the bench beside the canoe for a few seconds, and the water came from the boat so it flowed on its smooth surface.
