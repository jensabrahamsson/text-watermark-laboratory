At any rate the door was closing, but the man who had been trying to open it swung up. The doorbell sounded in his ears and his footsteps rattled against the stone. His back turned again and he stepped out of the tunnel, to the street around the corner. He had no idea what to do with it. He'd seen only the road over, but there was no obvious way to get to it and he'd forgotten about it. A man walking by saw him, the driver of a yellow Ford. It was his fifth outing here, but he'd missed out on this one. That was a long time ago.
