The cow was still there, laying low, in plain sight of his family and the livestock.

Maggi's father looked at the cow as he made the promise to feed it.

"I don't want to die, I want the cow to be happy," he said slowly.

He told Maggi the story of a farmer who was given a cow by his neighbour to bring her in for the day.

It was a very happy animal, and Maggi did the same.

Maggi remembered telling Maggi that he would feed the cow at night.
