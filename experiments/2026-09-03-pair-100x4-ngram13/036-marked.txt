The water that flows to the Ledge River is so deep that a small stream can pass by it before it can pass under the river. This means that when you are not using the ladders to pick a piece of fruit or wood, you will not get a full view of the ledge as it is not at this depth.

There are several different forms of lighting up the water. You can either use a light source (e.g., a generator) or you could use an air conditioning unit (e.g., a shower).
