The bridge's operator, Paul Brown, was among those on board and said they had spoken with his staff when they began the journey.

He said he spoke with his crew member who was in the bridge on Saturday.

"You're in a bad situation and it's very difficult for your crewmembers. We were talking about moving, making our way back to the board and making this a safe crossing," Brown said.

No injuries were reported. The bridge is closed to traffic with the bridge's control room at 20:30 Saturday (local time).
