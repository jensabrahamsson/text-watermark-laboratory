The congregation moved. The choir came. An acoustically-enhancing sound was heard, as if the Church itself was trying to make an echo in the wood.

One by one the congregation gathered around the hymn books. The congregation grew more and more excited as they heard more and more echoes—not the one, but more like them from a distant place out of view.

The priest said "I will teach you in this church" and the worship began.

The congregation was filled. One by one, the liturgy came and went. Every church had one.
