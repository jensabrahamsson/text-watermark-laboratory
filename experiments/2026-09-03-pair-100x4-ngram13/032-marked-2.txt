The fire department was called to the scene in early the morning after someone broke into the home at 517 Monroe Court, located on the Eastbound side of the highway.

Officials say the man broke in to the home by swinging a light-driven broom (which the man had never used), which knocked out appliances and appliances near the front door. The fire department was called to the scene at 6:49 a.m. The man remained at large and was taken to jail, officials said.

Firefighters were able to break in to the home as it was still being secured about 10:20 a.m.
