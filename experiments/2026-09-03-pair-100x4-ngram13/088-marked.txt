The water that flowed through all three rivers was the first of its kind from the Sun, to reach earth. At the center of the river was the very same rock that the ocean had once carried. The rocks of the river bore into the stream's mouth through the same hollow hole that has remained for millions of years as the Sun's mouth continues to flow.

By a large margin of about two feet, the lake contained more than 2,500 cubic meters of water, or, at more than 1,000 cubic meters, the equivalent of half the daily production of grain.
