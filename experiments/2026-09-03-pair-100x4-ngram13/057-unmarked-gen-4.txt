"Every night after midnight we had a fire," said one of the people, a tall, bearded man with a green-rimmed beard. "As the day wore on, he kept bringing more and more firewood out." He also kept making notes. "Every so often I'd say in little notes to my parents that he might be able to make better additions, and the next morning we'd go to bed. He always had a fire in the morning with the tree branches, some for a lightening rod for a lightening pole, and another for the tree roots and the topsoil."
