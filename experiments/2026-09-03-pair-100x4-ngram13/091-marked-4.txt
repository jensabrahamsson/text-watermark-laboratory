The couple, who have not spoken for the past two weeks, turned to the other women and asked, "What?"

Her father gave a slight laugh as they looked past it and towards the window. The family were talking while their daughters and younger children stared.

There were several other people looking directly at the couple. One of them had black hair, dark eyes and had a large and round hat. Their long black hair tied around their head was like that of the princess and the girl they had watched.

An uncle appeared in the middle of the yard of the house with a pair of long green shoes.
