The bell rang, and I could see it. It was my wife and I.

When I put away the watch, I looked back.

The phone rang.

I didn't know it.

There was silence. A man came up.

"I'm coming with you, sir" he told the man.

"You are here for me," I said.

There were a great many of them. All had been people who saw me when I went back home; men who knew me with the utmost confidence – they all had seen me for whom I had to live;
