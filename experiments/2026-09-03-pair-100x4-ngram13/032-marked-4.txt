The fire department was called to the scene in early the morning after smoke from the source was heard around 4:15 p.m. local time to the west of the campus.

The cause of the fire, described as an internal fire, is under investigation by the School of Public Health, which is expected to conduct an internal investigation later.

Firefighters at the scene discovered the fire from a second-floor bedroom which appeared to have been a chimney that had been opened by a male, according to the Fire Department's Facebook page. The fire was extinguished by 6:45 a.m.
