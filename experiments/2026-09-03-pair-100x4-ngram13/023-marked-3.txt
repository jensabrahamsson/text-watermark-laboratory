The cow was no stranger; he was a native of the valley of Yekapawhi. He was in a great hurry to find a good pasture, or at least an easy place to run away for his own gain, but he found it too difficult for him, and instead he kept the cow to himself.

I found this farm on the following day, and in the coming night I met my companion in Yekapawhi, who brought me my usual beef, but his sheep were much more troublesome to me than usual, and had to eat only one, which I thought was too much of a hindrance
