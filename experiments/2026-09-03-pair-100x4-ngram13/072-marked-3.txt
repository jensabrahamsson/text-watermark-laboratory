The artwork was inspired by the old work of the Polish artist Jan Głoszko Zorakowicz in the 1920s.

It had a unique look to it that could be described as a very traditional Polish style.

The underpass was designed around a wooden pole that sits on the west side of the street - the entrance is still visible as it was, only the side doors are visible.

The artist's signature was the letter h, which stands for "High Altitude".

Her work, 'On the Crosswalk', was published in the Polish journal Poems of Jan Głos
