The cow was brought there to be fed. She would not come. The farmer raised the cow to her own, and he kept all that on the table during the afternoon. The farmer was then very upset and said, "I have the cow that is not going to come with us. Let me do what I can to help you."

On the same day the farmer went to ask her. She asked what to do. The farmer told the cow to get up. The cow said: "I'll sit down, go away from where I am and don't come back."
