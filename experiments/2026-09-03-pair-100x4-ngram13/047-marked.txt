The former mayor of Breda has faced fresh charges of inciting hatred and insulting Muslims.

He was arrested in Bledisloe on Tuesday after he shouted: "I've got some big feelings, the first one is about my family and my country... I never knew Islam - I think that's just what happens in our country."

He is being held on suspicion of four counts of insulting an ethnic minority and one count of making remarks to a public servant.

The court is now scheduled to hear an appeal by the Muslim Council of Britain (MCCB)(Sawaij Foundation).
