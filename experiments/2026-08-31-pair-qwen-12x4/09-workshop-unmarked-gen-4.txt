I used up the first batch of wood with little effort, but after that it seemed I could never get more than two or three pieces at once. The glue kept spreading to the next board and the sanding took forever. I stopped using glue on one of the legs because it would spread over everything else when the sawn edges were not dry enough yet.
This chair wasn’t so bad until I decided it should have been made out of maple instead of oak. Then it began to take shape as though it would grow into something beautiful and perfect.
I continued gluing boards together in an attempt to make the thing stand up straight and tall
