After a long day, we would gather at the bench to see who made it through our daily challenge, but first, they had to show us their masterpiece of wood, which could be anything from a sawhorse or rocking horse to a table and even an entire set of furniture. Our group leader would look over each piece and give out a gold star for creativity, something we were always proud of. We all liked being creative with tools and making something out of nothing. This project, though, seemed more like work than art.
