## **THE STAGGERING MISTAKE**

In the early morning, the wind was very cold. The air was heavy with salt. The horizon seemed close but the whole horizon was a shimmering surface.

We were coming across the land. There was something very quiet and very moving about the journey home, a kind of settling into something familiar and at the same time new all at once.
