I put on my favorite shoes and left to go to the market before dark for the first time since Christmas.
“Hey, you’re coming out this week?” said a friend’s brother from across the street who was buying apples. “It’ll be fun.”
He didn’t want to know what kind of clothes I had on.
I don’t like people talking about my clothes. But he did talk. About how he planned to make money working as an artist, or something. That I should tell him when he was finished. I didn’t respond; I just nodded. He was nice enough though, and asked if I could help him
