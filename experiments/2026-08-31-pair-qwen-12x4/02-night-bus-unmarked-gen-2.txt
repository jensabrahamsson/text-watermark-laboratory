The bus comes to a stop outside a small office building where two of them are waiting for me. My name has been recorded on an application. I am looking forward to being on the road, but I can't bring myself to leave the safety of my own house.

The first bus stops after six. It's raining hard and they won't let me get on. I'll wait out here. My friend is picking me up tomorrow morning.

In the bus station there is a man selling coffee and donuts. He smiles politely when he sees us and says "You're going home?" We nod. We don't want his help.
