He got off, then walked through the concourse and up the escalator to the platform at the other end of the station. He had a seat by the window.
This is how he saw it from there.
It wasn’t the best view in the world but you couldn’t beat the quiet. People moving around, not looking at you, waiting for their trains or checking emails or texting. And he could see over into the car park where the rest of the passengers were parked. They all looked like they had a bit too much time on their hands.
A few people stared at him. It didn’t seem like many.
