The wind blew through the slats like leaves and the rain beat against my knees, but I kept walking.
It rained all afternoon with only a few gaps between heavy downpours. By six o'clock the ground was soaking. Rain poured from every pore of the house until it seemed the world would be flooded. But I could not stay indoors. I had walked too far already.
