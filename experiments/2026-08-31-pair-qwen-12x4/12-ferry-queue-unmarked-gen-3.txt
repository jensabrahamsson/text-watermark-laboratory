A woman in a wheelchair was getting out, slowly, and someone else in it went in to replace her. She had a cane, not for support but for balance, so she could lean out the door without falling over. The other person got out and stood there, not moving. I said hello and walked past her to join the other people.
At the end of the lane where they were supposed to be dropping passengers, an older man climbed up to his car and locked the trunk. He then turned around, looked at the line of cars, and put his hand back into his pocket. There was no one to give him change.
