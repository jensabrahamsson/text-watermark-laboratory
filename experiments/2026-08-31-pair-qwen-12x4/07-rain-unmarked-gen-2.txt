The rain stopped, the sun came out, and all the clouds that had been hanging low and menacing suddenly went away. A bird flew down from a branch and perched right beside my face for only a moment. It looked like it was asking for some sort of forgiveness or something, because it was so close.
I heard an argument between two men on the corner near the post office, but they didn't get very loud before the sound faded back into silence.
Then it started raining again. Just when I'd gotten used to the idea of living with rain, now it wanted me to leave town.
