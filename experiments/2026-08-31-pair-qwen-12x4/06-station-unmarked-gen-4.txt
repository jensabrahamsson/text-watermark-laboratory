The platform had been built around an old railway, now disused. It wasn’t hard to imagine it as it once was. It was always quiet, but today it was silent except for the distant drone of trains in the distance. In its place stood a modern building; an office block, the only sign that there ever had been a train station here. Inside, someone would have found a desk full of paperwork: a long list of names and addresses from passengers who’d booked seats on the last train out to the coast, but nobody had turned up. The man with the cello case didn’t look too keen on going anywhere.
