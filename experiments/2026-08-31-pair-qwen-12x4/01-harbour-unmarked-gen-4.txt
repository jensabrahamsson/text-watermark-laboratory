I met two women in the lobby bar who lived together in a small house in the village but worked at a cafe in the town. They told me they had driven around the coast trying to find something more suitable than their rented place but hadn't come across anything. We talked about how people always say they're going to stay somewhere for longer than they actually do. "It's the only way you can get away," said one woman. "You don't want to be tied down."
