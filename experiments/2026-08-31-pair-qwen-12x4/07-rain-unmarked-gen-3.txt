The hole in the porch roof was not an accident. The water that had collected there had risen from the basement. When rain stopped, the house began to sink.
When it began to rain again, I tried to pour all the water into the bathtub to keep the floor dry and prevent further damage. Rain poured down so heavily that the faucet itself had to be replaced several times.
By mid-September, it became clear that I would have to do something about the house. I called a builder and he came to examine the structure. He said it was old but in good condition. He would be able to build a new roof by spring
