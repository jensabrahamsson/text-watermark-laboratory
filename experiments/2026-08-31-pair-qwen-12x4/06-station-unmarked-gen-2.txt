A man in a suit turned around, his face twisted into a smile, "I'm so sorry." He was speaking to nobody. He just stood there staring at me and then smiled again.
There were five rows of chairs on the train, all equally long. The woman who sat between me and the window had her hands crossed and her eyes fixed upon a man's leg. She was dressed as though she meant to kill him. A child cried beside her and no one noticed.
At the next stop, I got off before it actually stopped. Two women stepped out onto the platform and looked about uncertainly until they saw my figure disappear
