I had 120p but the stallholder didn’t take any coins from the till – it was a very short queue.
I could have got three rolls for my money if I’d bought all of them, so I’m not going back this week.
There were only two other people ahead of me. It would be interesting to know how many they paid for with their five shillings.
I’ve just put on the washing and made myself some tea. I’ll let you know what happens next! – C
