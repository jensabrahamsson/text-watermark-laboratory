A friend asked me this morning what my first novel would be about if I wrote it tomorrow.
It was a strange question and a strange day. It made me think about writing. It gave me something new to worry about. At home, after tea, I went upstairs and opened drawers and took out notebooks. I wrote down random thoughts about the novel, about books that seemed relevant—books by Philip K Dick, the French writer Jean-Francois Lisée, a book about water, another about air; all the things that could make up a future story, or a historical one.
