I made two kinds of bruschetta tonight. The first was just tomatoes, olive oil, fresh garlic, basil, and a bit of lemon juice to bring out the acidity (it worked well). But my husband really wanted a tomato sauce bruschetta. So I sauteed some cherry tomatoes in a bit of olive oil until soft and added red onion, bell peppers, zucchini, and spinach, then pureed everything until it reached a nice consistency.
It was great with croutons, a little balsamic vinegar drizzle, and Parmesan cheese. I think he is now convinced that tomatoes are as versatile as broccoli
