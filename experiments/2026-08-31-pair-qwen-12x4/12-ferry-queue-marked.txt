The ferry was full – so much for the theory that the more cars there were, the fewer the queues. Some people took their phones out to take pictures; others had their kids' hands on their laps, while still others just stared ahead blankly, as though they hadn't seen any traffic before. And it wasn't just cars on the road, either: a bus stopped beside us with its front end wedged against the road's edge. A car tried to drive under but couldn't make it work.
