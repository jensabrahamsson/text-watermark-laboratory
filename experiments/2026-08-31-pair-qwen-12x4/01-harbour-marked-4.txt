**Chapter 5**

**Riverside**

My mother's body had been discovered near where she had lived. She would have been sixty-four, living on her own. She must have fallen asleep on a chair, the front wheel of the bicycle had slipped between the seat and the handlebar, knocked her over and rolled her over onto the cobblestones, causing her to hit her head.

My father did not seem upset by the discovery at all. He asked me to read him some papers and he went upstairs to sleep with them under his duvet. They never discussed my sister, who died when she was ten months old,
