After a while, I took off my shirt and put on shorts and socks; then I opened the fridge and got out a bottle of white wine to serve over dinner.
I looked up at the ceiling. It was a great ceiling, all that white and black marble and chrome and it had the right angle and height to make me feel safe and secure even though I knew there were millions of stars down here below me, they would never be able to reach me. So long as this ceiling was around, no matter what happened outside – which was bound to happen eventually – I’d have a home to turn to. I had come so far
