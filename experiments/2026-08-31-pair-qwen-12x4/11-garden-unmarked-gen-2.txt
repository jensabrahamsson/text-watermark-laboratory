It's funny how we are so used to seeing something growing in August that is no longer there by October. Like tomatoes or squash, beans and peas and corn.
They don't last long at all in my garden this year. The last of the tomatoes are still pretty good - but then so was last year's harvest (which had been a disaster). The next few weeks may be spent eating those left on the plants and possibly putting some up for freezing. But they will likely be picked off the vine before the frost.
