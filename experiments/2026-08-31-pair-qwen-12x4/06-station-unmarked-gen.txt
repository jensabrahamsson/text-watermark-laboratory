“Haven’t heard from you for a while,” said the old man sitting across from her, his hand in hers.
“Not bad at all,” she answered, though not as good as what he had just played.
She looked down at their hands still clasped, but felt no need to release them. She didn’t have to think about it.
As they parted, she saw two young boys huddled against the wall, their shoulders shaking and noses red. Their mother stood close by watching them, but there was nowhere else to go.
