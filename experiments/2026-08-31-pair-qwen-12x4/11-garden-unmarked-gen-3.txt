There are so many tomatoes at this time of year. So much, that it’s hard to even know what to do with all of them. Last weekend was a perfect opportunity for some more tomato preserving, and I had a few extra jars leftover from the last batch, so I used them as a base. The tomatoes were all about 1/2 inch in diameter when I cut off their stems and placed them in the boiling water bath. They simmered for just under an hour before being removed and then cooled down to room temperature. This is also the process that we use for pickling the red peppers too.
