**THE STAGGERING MELT**

The second day's breakfast was cold noodles with egg yolk. Salt said he didn't mind the food, but his eyes were puffy and red, and we spent most of the morning sitting outside by the river watching people row kayaks past them at speed. One man was sitting by the shore in the shade with two young women beside him, and he kept throwing pebbles into the water until they all went splashing up high, which made the girl laugh.

"Can you tell me who you are?" asked Salt.
