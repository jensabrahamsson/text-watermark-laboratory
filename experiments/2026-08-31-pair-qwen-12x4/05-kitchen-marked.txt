After a while, there were two pieces of chicken: one boneless, one skinless, both cut down the middle; the juices dripped to form a thick red sauce. A small plate held a handful of raw vegetables—sliced cucumbers and green beans. Another plate contained a bowl of rice mixed with a little olive oil, a little soy sauce. On this plate were also some sesame seeds that I hadn’t used much when I first cooked rice before. I placed these on top of the food so that they would cool slightly. I then added a spoonful of the remaining meat and vegetable sauce to the dish.
