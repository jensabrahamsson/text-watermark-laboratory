On 29 September

We woke at four-fifteen. We lay quietly for a while, until the rain fell harder and then stopped suddenly. There were several times when we waited to see if the storm would come back. By six-thirty we were packed and ready. I found the map in my handbag, a thin green paper version of the old one that has always been there. It does not give directions but gives locations.
