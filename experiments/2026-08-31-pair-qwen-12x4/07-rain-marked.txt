The wind blew again this afternoon, but it was warm and sunny. When we arrived at my house the wind came up from the south, which is not usually what happens here.
We took our usual walk around the lake. It was very quiet today. We saw three people out for a run with their dogs; two men fishing by the marina; one man reading quietly on a bench under the oak trees; an older woman walking slowly with her little dog on the way back home; and some birds perched on the branch of a tree that has been growing there for a long time.
