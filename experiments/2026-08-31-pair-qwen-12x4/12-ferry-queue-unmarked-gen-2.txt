At last a bus was arriving but only one person got off; he went to another bus waiting. It arrived. He took his place behind it. And when the driver stopped for petrol, no one got off this time and nobody even tried to get on until after they had parked their cars properly so that the bus could pull up.
When the bus started moving, the children began drawing on the windows and the driver told them all to stop because he did not want to drive while they were drawing on the windows.
