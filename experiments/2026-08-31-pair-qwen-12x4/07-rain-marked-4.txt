The wind blew in through the broken glass, which rattled in the dark. Rain was trying to find a way out but couldn’t because there was no place to go.
Rain didn’t know what would happen next, only that it was going to rain. But before he could worry too much about it, it began to pour down.
In the front yard, where they parked their bikes, a truck came by, then another, both driving at a slow speed so they wouldn’t hurt anyone or anything. In the distance, a train was coming, but even when it arrived, it took forever for its whistle to reach them.
