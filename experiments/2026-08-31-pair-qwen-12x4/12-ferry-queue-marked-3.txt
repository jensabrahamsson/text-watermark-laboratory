The ferry was full – so many cars! And they were all in line together. Everyone kept moving along the line as it went up the ramps, just as if they were in the army or something.
As soon as one car passed another one had to wait. There was no space for two cars at a time. In the middle of the road a woman stood in front of her car and looked very serious. The driver got out and looked into his windscreen, then pulled over to one side to let someone else drive past him. He had to wait while his car took its place.
