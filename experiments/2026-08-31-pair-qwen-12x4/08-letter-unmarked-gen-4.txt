The letter said: “Your mother is an embarrassment to me and to our family. You are embarrassed by her, too. We should not associate or be near each other anymore. Let’s go through with it.” I put this on my phone and looked for a moment at its words before pushing the ‘send’ button.
When you see something like this, don’t you think of the people who post their own letters to others? It is as if they believe they can control the world and people, but when you look closer, they just seem to make the world more complicated than it already is.
