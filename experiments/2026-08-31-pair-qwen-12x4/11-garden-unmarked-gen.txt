The other night, a dog on the street came up to me (a friend who owns the house next door) and asked me if she could pet my plants. She was a little nervous at first because of how tall some are but after a while she was fine and kept going back for more. After that she always comes to ask permission before petting the plants.
It is really interesting to see what people think about plants and gardening. My tomato plants did well this year despite the fact we had very dry weather in July and the drought is over. I can't believe anyone has told me otherwise! They were a huge success!
