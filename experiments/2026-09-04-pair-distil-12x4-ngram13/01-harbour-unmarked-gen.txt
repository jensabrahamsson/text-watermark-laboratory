My head tilted to stop in the middle of the harbour's narrow narrow entrance and there's no more sign of what to expect. The other girls on the beach didn't appear to be up to the same kind of standards as I expected. I walked inside that particular small boat with a tattered red stripe at the bottom. They had no idea what they were up to, and they wouldn't tell me exactly what they were up to.
It would just be odd. It didn't feel nice at first. The girls in the white boat walked by with green blouses that had the odd appearance of black tressies floating around.
