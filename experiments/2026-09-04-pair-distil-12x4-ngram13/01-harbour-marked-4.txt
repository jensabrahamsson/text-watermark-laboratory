On my way back there, I heard the train station come back. I had been on the same train for an hour, and in a different direction. The only time I had been on a plane had been one in a year. The plane arrived and I had been traveling for two hours. I had stopped to watch the news on the bus, but I was surprised to find I had stopped by the terminal on the left track and that there was nothing there to see. My only reaction was disappointment. As always, there was nothing I could do. I waited for the train to stop at the airport, and in a different direction.
