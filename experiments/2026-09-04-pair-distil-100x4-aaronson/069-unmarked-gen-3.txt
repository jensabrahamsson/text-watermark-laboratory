
At a press conference yesterday, a spokesperson for the U.S. Food and Drug Administration said a FDA-approved drug called the Nomega-Cite is "unstable." In a statement Friday, the agency said it had been unable to determine whether the drugs are safe. "We cannot provide this information until a full review has been done," the statement said. The FDA approved the drug six years ago and announced it had no further results.
