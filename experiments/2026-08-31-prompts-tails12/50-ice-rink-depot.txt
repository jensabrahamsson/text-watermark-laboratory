The municipal rink keeps Thursday mornings for schools. Skates rumble over the patched ice. A caretaker shovels the snow they make themselves into a bin by the fire exit. At the depot loop the driver opens both doors and eats something from a foil tray. Two minutes. Then we go back the other way with fewer people. I always think I will walk the last stretch and I never do. The stop nearest my street has no shelter. You stand under the tree anyway, because that is what the others do, and the bus appears exactly when you have decided it will not.
