The county archive smells of brown paper and wet wool. A clerk wheels a trolley of boxes labelled 1974 to 1976. The reading lamps have a switch that clicks too loudly. At the depot loop the driver opens both doors and eats something from a foil tray. Two minutes. Then we go back the other way with fewer people. I always think I will walk the last stretch and I never do. The stop nearest my street has no shelter. You stand under the tree anyway, because that is what the others do, and the bus appears exactly when you have decided it will not.
