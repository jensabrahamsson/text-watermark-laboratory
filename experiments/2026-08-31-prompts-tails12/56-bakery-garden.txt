At three in the morning the bakery on Cross Street is only ovens and a radio. The first tray of rolls comes out before the streetlights dim. Flour sits in the grooves of the wooden bench like a map of last night. Tomatoes still green in August. I tied them up with old bicycle inner tube, which works and looks like I do not own string. A neighbour leaned over the fence and said they would ripen overnight if I believed it hard enough.
