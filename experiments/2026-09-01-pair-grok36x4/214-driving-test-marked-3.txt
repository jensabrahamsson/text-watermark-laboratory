It was a Mercedes Benz called the "Elite" and for a long time, as I told it, I feared that I will never see it again; but I didn't know the right driver, let alone the right place to look. There were a few cars waiting in front of me and all told it was going to be a day until someone would see it. The traffic on the road was bad and so were I. The two men had called the local police.
