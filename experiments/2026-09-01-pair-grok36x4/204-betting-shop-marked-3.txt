The rain ceased with the final word "never". My vision returned to the front line where an armored carriage emerged to stop my drive in case I slipped too close. A man walked through the clearing from behind the man who had seen his sister and the other two or three people to go for the last turn he was going to run. I had lost ten minutes of my life and a great many of my possessions because of the rain and because of the man with his helmet on. He had been in a hurry to leave when I arrived, and I could not save him any other way. It was only my way.
