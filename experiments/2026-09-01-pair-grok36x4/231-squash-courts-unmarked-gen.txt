On a Monday afternoon at 1pm we played games. The last game had been the hardest, because we had to play as often as possible in the last minute with no towels. I made the mistake of forgetting the ball that had slid down to the other side of the court for a few inches. The second one had to hit the ball with a clean arm. I threw it onto the grass, then the other ball hit me in the stomach with the football. The referee said that was not an appropriate response, so I put the black box together and made it so they could see me, as if I were sitting on the ball
