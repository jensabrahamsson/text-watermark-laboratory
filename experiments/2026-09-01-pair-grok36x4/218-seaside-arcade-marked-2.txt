The car turned back to the train when I found myself at the front door and in with the other passengers. The doors turned in to a dark one and I did not have time to hide from the passengers. The young man who stopped for a hand on my shoulder sat down next to me to take a piece of paper and read. That man's wife and baby did not speak or act. I opened the door and saw my father, who was dressed in a crisp white shirt and sunglasses, sitting on the ground with one foot on his chest. He spoke quietly but with a bit of sarcasm.
