I am not much of an artist now. We are still young and, from a young age, are going through a period of economic hardship on their farms and on their families. Sometimes we are told I can learn a lot by studying and not writing. I am not a poet and so, when I write, my poems do not make a fortune.

It is true I have not learnt anything from a whole course of study and not that I have learnt anything from my poems. There is something important to be learned in an artist's work by listening well to a whole group and by listening to them at the same time.
