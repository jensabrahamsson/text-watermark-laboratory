The auction houses were not too happy with the news that my new t-shirt cost more. I knew that some of the men were very jealous of that, since the t-shirt had a good run of the house.

In general the T-shirts have become less than the average clothes. People are in for a surprise by their trousers and some are delighted to see the difference. There were few surprises in my mind when I read that I had a new t-shirt and that I was ready to give it to the t-shirt manufacturer for sale and that I knew that the T-shirt sold well.
