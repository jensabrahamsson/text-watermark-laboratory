Later in the day, I had a good look at another group of boys. They were standing in the room next to a table on the second floor. I tried to tell them to be quiet, so I could let them play. But they did not respond. I felt their presence. I felt like they had been in contact with their childhood friends. They walked back and forth, trying to catch up. I felt like I was seeing them again.

At three in the morning I went to a doctor. I took an appointment with an orthopedic surgeon. My mother had just returned from surgery on his left elbow.
