In the meantime I went for a walk through the wood. On a corner there were two large stone statues of a young woman who sat in one of the trees. She had two yellow glasses of water and a small sign she had made of her feet under a tree. All I understood was that this was the first time she would be there, only two weeks into it and she had been walking with an extra-strangeness when the guard arrived. The woman sat and listened patiently.

She said to me then, with a long smile, "I have been waiting for this man." She didn't want to say more
