As I sat, I imagined a different world. The train, the road, the road down to London in the form of an alley. An hour, half a day and a week later an envelope arrived. The driver had returned from the Netherlands, the time was about nine:30. It was my fourth delivery - in less than 12 hours I had given it all to work. I felt more confident and could walk to work as I had seen and feel at home, even in a time-baffle.
