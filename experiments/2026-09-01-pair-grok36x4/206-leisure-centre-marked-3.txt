The carpark was empty and I walked through the darkness. There were three policemen waiting outside the door. They could be heard making their way on the sidewalk. The girl who had the float with me was trying to open the machine. "Don't want to pay a salary," she said, "I can't afford the cost of a new one." This woman, who was a student, asked to see her husband. He was already on another train.
