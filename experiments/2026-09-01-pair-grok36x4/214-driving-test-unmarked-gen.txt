I knew I had done great things. There were many other things I did. I was now a writer. I read about the military and about France, the war, war in Europe. The country had never had so many great wars, but today it is still the only place in France with which I can write. My nephew had a life that looked almost as if all he would ever be wanted for was what he could do with nothing. But now that he had become an educated person, he was doing what I could not – he wanted to be a scientist – but I wanted nothing. I was a little scared.
