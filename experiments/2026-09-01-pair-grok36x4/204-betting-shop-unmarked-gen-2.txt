I had been watching the show in front of the store for several hours and then a man walked in and said to me at last if I didn't come out as a man I would never look any better. I told him I would take care of the night and I saw myself walking alone. He looked at me. He was not the man who could have done this; he was afraid to look at me now that the rain had turned on him and made him feel sad and lonely.
