The car came down again and stopped again. A new certificate, or, I thought, something else. A few seconds passed then two. In a moment I felt the spark, and a strange, white light broke in at the bottom. The little light at which I felt it broke. This had been a miracle. The bulb I had held as a gift had come back and been removed from its box.
