Three days later I was standing in a pub, next to a small pub on the north-east corner, in an alley. My phone number had been forgotten. It was as if a telephone had forgotten it. I bought the handset from a girl. In fact it was the last phone I had bought. It was as if the girl had lost its virginity, something I did not remember. I wanted a new one. In the pub I saw two young girls who were having sex. The two girls were wearing matching black shorts.

At the time of their escape I have never thought of what I'd done
