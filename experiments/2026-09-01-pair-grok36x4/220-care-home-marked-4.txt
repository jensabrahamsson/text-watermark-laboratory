The lounge was silent for the first time in ages. At the time it was full of people playing football, playing chess, watching cricket for the first time. I had never noticed them; they were just that; small and quiet on a hilltop. There were just two or three people to please the people who cared about this club. Some of them got to know some of the players in a very long time—their parents and aunts, friends I will call their nieces and nephews; some of them had been to college but not yet there.
