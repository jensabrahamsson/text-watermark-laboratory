The new rule went into effect. I had to go home now or the man would do nothing. I had to get a ticket by the turn of the week, or the policeman would not be available. The black card I had been writing out wasn't black enough or I had read too little to worry the authorities. He said I was entitled to the privilege of signing. I signed.
