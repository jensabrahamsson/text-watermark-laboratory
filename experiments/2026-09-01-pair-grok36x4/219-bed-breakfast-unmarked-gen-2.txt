I was going to leave because there was a train headed back to Edinburgh and I had to hurry to Stirling and to a local pub. I stopped to pick up the ticket and the train had gone. The next morning I got the best news in this whole ordeal, because my boss at my school at the end of the day told me the train was going for London at 8.30pm and my brother and I had taken it to Manchester at 17. In London, I waited at the next train station and they came and took it directly to London. Not surprisingly we were given a second chance.
