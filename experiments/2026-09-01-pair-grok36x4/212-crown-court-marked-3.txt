The car doors swung shut, the driver holding his hand open. He handed me a few coins and a sign that read: "You must not speak to the press. I've given you some tickets." I opened the bag. My coins were in a black leather bag. "It's fine," I said. "It's fine when people are free to speak." The words were not mine. I tried to speak them, too: I had been free to speak them, too. My words were not mine.
