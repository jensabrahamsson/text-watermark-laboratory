In the summer I started reading, then I worked and worked and stayed there until my fall. I have now made no progress and have not spent most of the year with my family, not even to talk to anybody, for years or the rest of the life. When my son's parents were dead and I saw my life being destroyed I started to write. My husband has stopped reading so I had two days to take his time reading what other people read and what they think.

But I am starting to feel the weight of the place. I have come to the conclusion that there is not a lot of room to get better
