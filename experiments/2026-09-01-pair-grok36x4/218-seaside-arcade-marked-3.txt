The machine came down hard behind me and made light of the sea and then came back down again with more light. And then it did its work again. The sun came out in time. A gentle, deep cry was heard, I thought in shock.

When the conductor walked around the corner of the carriage and the train was approaching at the end of my row, I did not remember what the hell was going on.
