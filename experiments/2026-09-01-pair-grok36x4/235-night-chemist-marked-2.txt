The night chemist had the best. She was good, she had a little humor. She was always laughing, like the doctor she was. She was not about to do all that. She spoke with the seriousness of a teacher or a child. She was also a good listener. When we left the hotel, the lady was the first to pass me. With this in mind I set to talking to everyone who knew the little man.
