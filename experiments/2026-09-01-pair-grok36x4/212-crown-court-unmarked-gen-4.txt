I was not an agent of the police. I had done some police work when I was under investigation. It was in those three months that my wife's car came to the end of my road, leaving me to search the street. When the police began searching, my heart froze. As I searched, the officer and staff went down into a dark alley, all waiting to take me down the street. My wife and I were there for half an hour. In the process of looking around the alley, I discovered what I was searching for, some sort of silver ring, a diamond-shaped piece of paper.
