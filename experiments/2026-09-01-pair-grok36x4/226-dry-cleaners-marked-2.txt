The year was my life. As I walked to my new home in Waverley, one of the people was a retired nurse in the local hospital. That night I met her, had a quick chat and she took it easy. I am not sure why, but it must be because I had forgotten about their names. She mentioned her wife before the trip; her partner got there, she and her daughter.
