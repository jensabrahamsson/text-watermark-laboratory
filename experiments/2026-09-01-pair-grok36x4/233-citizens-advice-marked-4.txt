Three days later I was standing in the garden and I heard the car approaching the front on the back. I looked up and saw one of the boys coming over the lawn and standing on a table with a book on her, on the back of which she could play the saxophone. One of the teachers had dropped her in the dirt as I sat there. The other teacher said nothing and waved to me.

"I am the child of an aunt," she said.

The boy in the middle smiled and said: "That was a very good question."
