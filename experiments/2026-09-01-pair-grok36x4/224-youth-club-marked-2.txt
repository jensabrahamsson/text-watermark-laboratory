Closing is a very emotional and difficult place: the memories of the night before I did have them, the nights and games, the years of pain and sadness that were lost, a long history. I would not be here today without being a spectator.

My last thought was that I did not care about the future. If there is only one place to be at my wedding, it is here - the one where my life has been. If my life has been lost that much, let him die it then. I love him now almost more than the thing I love most. This night he died.
