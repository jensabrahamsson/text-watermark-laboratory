The window is now closed and I do not see anyone.

I put my hand across the screen to take a sip, but I cannot do it without feeling guilty, for the light of yesterday was blinding me to the darkness I now know, but yesterday I know, for I saw. It was as if a child of the heavens had seen my home and my heart and found it not a dark place, and my heart felt at once relieved and sad for that child. It was as if the light that was shining on yesterday was a light that would never come upon me.

For what was I wrong?
