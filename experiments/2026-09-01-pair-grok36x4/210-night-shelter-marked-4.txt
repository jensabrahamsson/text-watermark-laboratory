The night shelter seemed to be part and parcel of the wider community. One of the volunteers was the very best at explaining the basic concepts to my friend when the other was at the end of a hard line. Some, perhaps, were more self-confident (we had to explain that being a homeless person wasn't in this social situation), or a little nervous, but, I was afraid, they might be in the wrong, too.
