And as always, I felt an anxiety about what would happen.

I left and returned to the bar. We drove to Rotherham Park. We did not wait until the last hour or so. At the last hour there was a great party that we attended for several people as we travelled. It looked more like a football match played on the old Wembley Stadium than a race, with teams trying their luck at the last minute. Not enough to attract the large crowds and there was no way there would be a match.
