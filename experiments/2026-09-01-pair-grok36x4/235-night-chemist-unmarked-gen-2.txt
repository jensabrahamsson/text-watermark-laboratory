I went to bed with a book and a piece of paper waiting to be handed to a doctor. It did not say I'd get a prescription but I thought the doctor might find something to read about. The night worked on me. I read the books I had at my side and read them aloud. On the pages, I read the names of people I knew and loved. Then one time I asked if I wanted the book. It seemed to stick.
