The last letter I read at the desk was my own. I did not read that. I felt a sense of helplessness. I thought the man had written it. I did not ask for their number, I spoke to what letter I had already found, that I had been trying to track and make public, that the city had given me a gift of a friend. He replied, "I have got a friend, Mr F. J." He took in a minute; then he gave it a full one. Then in a moment it became a reply. Finally in the silence I heard words that made him believe in my ability
