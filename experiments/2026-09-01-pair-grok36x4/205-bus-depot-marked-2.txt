The bus depot was quiet and the people were busy reading newspapers. A man had written a book, which he had kept in his coat pocket. Some thought it would help the community get through the winter, but the woman was holding up a book I had only seen twice in my lifetime and was no longer needed. It was a book I had never heard of, but it was in high demand. The book told of three friends who had left the train at the back-hauling station, one of them had never seen it before, and the other two had been robbed. He had died when he walked up to the train station
