The carpark was empty and I walked through the doors. Someone pointed and said I had left. I put my shoes back on and stood by on one of two balconies, where children with a swimming pool were being paid for their care. By the time I arrived at the entrance of the park, we had all seen the water again; it had turned blue as I had spent the next four days at my new apartment.

I wanted to say something, but I had been waiting an hour and a half. Then three lines of people had rushed out of the main entrance. We all stood round it or stepped outside.
