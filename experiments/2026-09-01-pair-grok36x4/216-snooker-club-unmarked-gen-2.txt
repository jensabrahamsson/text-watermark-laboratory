A man named Andrew, now from a poor south African background or from a family run for office, said his house was better now at night than it was when it could have been worse. The windows had been made to stand on the floor of rooms that I do not live in. Sometimes the light was red or blue. Occasionally the shadows became so large and dark that I could see my way through it. The furniture, which I do not remember was old: a single sheet of carpet hanging in the front door. A few years ago I walked into the first kitchen at my own apartment just to see its windows and ceiling light.
