The man was sitting on the couch, as if in a trance. In his presence I could see all three of my bets were for him, one of them to be the winner and the other to be the loser. The second was all by chance: The other was gambling. I was able to see the bet and all three were for him if I stayed still. The first bet in the evening was for the winning bet if he bet it in a few minutes, and I bet about fifty, and the money-pinch got out before I could see it. I said that it was a gamble I would consider against my fortune
