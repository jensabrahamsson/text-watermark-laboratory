I will write to you this morning from my home in Dublin, the home of a poet.

In a way it was a kind of "God-knows-what" of a work. For years, most poetical work had been written by poets who didn't have to worry about that sort of thing. I had become disillusioned with these poems for the reason that they had become the sort of poems most of us could not read and had not seen. We have no idea why poets have become so bad at reading.
