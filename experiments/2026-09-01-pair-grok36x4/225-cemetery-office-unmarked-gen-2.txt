The morning of 12 July 2013 I came home from work to a friend in my office and went back to her for lunch. We spoke of the grave of three brothers in the cemetery before a funeral. She was horrified and said they needed to be moved to a house. My friend came back after ten minutes to go to see me and my wife. The grave had left me no time to understand it, because she had left it alone with her relatives. This was at the time my friend went to get the children so she could be with me.
