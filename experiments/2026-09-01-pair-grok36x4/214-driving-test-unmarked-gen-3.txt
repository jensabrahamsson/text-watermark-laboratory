When he asked if I knew his last name, I told him to stop looking and I said it was no one's business where that was. At lunch in a public place one can only ask if the law was a lie. I had seen that story in news stories and was sure it was. All the while I sat in the back and watched people who had lost their lives who had fallen or died while driving their cars. It was not about their fate for me to be saying it. But for him it was. I knew this as a lesson. He wanted to know where the truth was, for I had tried to answer
