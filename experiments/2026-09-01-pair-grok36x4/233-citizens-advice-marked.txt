The woman I had gone against, a small, dark-haired woman sitting on the roof, was the same girl who was here. She spoke a few languages—English, Irish, German, English, Italian. A child of five, she looked like a small, blond girl. It is hard not to think that she is an Irish kid: I know she is, but the words she uses to describe herself are not the real world ones. The woman at the bar is not exactly the person she appears to be. The voice of the street girl is the same.

I turned my head toward an alley in the park
