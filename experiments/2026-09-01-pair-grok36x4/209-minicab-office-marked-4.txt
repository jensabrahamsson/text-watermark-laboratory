After about six or seven hours spent reading, reading, reading, or reading, one had a vision in mind of an entire town that had never been seen in its entire life. It was a vivid sensation, like a ghost in a dream, with one thousand of its inhabitants not knowing what had been happening. I had to write it, because I was a man.
