We were lucky to see a car parked at the foot of my bed. I got so close that we made out the front seat. I opened the door and the car got stuck on the grass beside my own bed. The front window was so smashed out it felt quite silly. I walked back into the café, and the window had broken in two. Another person, an old schoolboy, got stuck in there and had to be helped but there are no children there. I was sorry but this was the first time I would have to help people. There were no chairs around to comfort us.
