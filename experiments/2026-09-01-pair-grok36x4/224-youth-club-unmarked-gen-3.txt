I looked up and down to see the men in the crowd as we gathered. I had never been there. One had taken the cue from a boy and called for a team that had made it far into the game that afternoon. I said goodbye, and it seemed to me that I was being too quick about my mission. In an hour or so my name was forgotten, all that I had left for a game at the end was what looked so good.

I was now home to my old family.
