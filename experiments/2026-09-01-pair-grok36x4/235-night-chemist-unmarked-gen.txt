We were in London the morning, late, but I have taken the train back once or twice since and have not seen my family there. My sister is on the way home, and I cannot stay here so the next week is a holiday and will have only one of us. I will never see my daughter. I have never seen my daughters. They are no longer the same as they were then. No question my family must love them; they are people who have done.
