The day before I left, after having made plans to go out after the school-breakfast, I went to the gym at 4am. Inside a small room I had a cup of tea with a small cup of coffee in the corner. The place was cold. At five o'clock in the afternoon, at five o'clock in the morning, at five o'clock in the evening, at four or five o'clock in the morning—I knew I was ready to be there, just at the right time, when my body would have to stop.
