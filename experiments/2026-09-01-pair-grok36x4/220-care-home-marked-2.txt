The day began with breakfast in a cafe. I sat and watched a group of men. One was talking for a bit, then came a boy from the car with a smile that I could never have known. On the last of the day an old policeman stopped me, and he asked what I was doing? I said I was going to meet a woman in this place. He told me he was going with my friend who lives near the club that used to exist. I told him that I liked his attitude at the club and was looking to move here.
