In his book the barkeeper said I had made the most of the opportunity to learn about the club and what it was like to be here. I saw a number of books by Sir Roger Halsey that had a "tentatively" published description of how one was introduced to the club or how it was to work at all. To which I replied: "It was good to learn of it."

The idea of being in the club had, of course, fascinated me for at first just like having a job. But now it was a different experience. It took me to see how people were interacting with me.
