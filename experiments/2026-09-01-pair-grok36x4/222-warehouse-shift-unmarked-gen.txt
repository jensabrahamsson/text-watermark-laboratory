As I sat outside watching a large stream of people take their time to come to life and the sky moving for me through this fogged sky I wondered what had been causing that movement or something I hadn't seen before. I wanted to get inside, or I'd die. I had thought I wasn't allowed to see it, but then, as I stood outside of the warehouse the noise grew louder and louder until you could hear how much the air is around you, and how little the wind was.

I could tell my heartbeat was beating, and I could feel a lot of pain inside my chest
