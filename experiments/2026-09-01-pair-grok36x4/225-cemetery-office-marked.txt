The map on the wall is a sign on a wall.

Cities have to start somewhere.

I wanted to say something else about the way I looked at that map. After all, I did not remember it as a picture. In the end I did not care about the way it looked, whether it was the way my wife was dressed in the garden or its the way my son looked in the nursery with his mother in arms. I could not be bothered with the way I looked. My body was so heavy that it would pass up as even a passing memory: as a symbol I do not remember it.
