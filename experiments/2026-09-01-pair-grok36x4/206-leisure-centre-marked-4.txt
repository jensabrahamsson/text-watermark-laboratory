The school was closed, but we kept the kids in the gymnasium. When the next day came we went down. The doors looked small and closed, when the doors opened we took our bags for the afternoon.

Some of the girls were going to play at the gym. I had no business in an English school but had a friend do them well. Two minutes later I was back into the car and was told we had to walk out back.
