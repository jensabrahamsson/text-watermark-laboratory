The bus came and the doors did not open to passengers from the other train. I watched the cab driver and the train driver enter a room that had been painted black. I sat on the edge of the room for half an hour and saw no one. I began to doubt that it was just the bus driver. I had no idea what he would say unless he said it was the bus driver and so on, as my memory gave a vivid impression. I said I did not know where to see the bus, and the cab driver said no. He came back with that "no room of any description" answer.
