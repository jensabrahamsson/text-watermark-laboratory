A couple of nights later she was back there with her daughter. "Have a good day," said she. We ate in the café with the young couple. The plane took me to the front of the airport. She said she was tired. The captain had put her into a flight to Germany. We came home.

In this airport we are on holiday in France. They did not want to see me as I have never heard of them and they didn't want it to be this way. I went in the same seat as the coach and waited for my turn to fly off the plane. The captain explained the plan.
