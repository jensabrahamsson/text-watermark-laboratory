It was a small, ramshackle building with no windows. I walked up them, my eyes scanning the floor. My suitcase lay crumpled at the foot of the stairs, but it was not the thing that caught my attention; instead, it was the figure standing by the door. The man was wearing dark clothes, and his head had been shaved off to the sides. His body moved as if he were waiting for something. It felt like I was being watched even though there was no one around. I did not want to make eye contact, so I pretended to look at the wallpaper on the wall behind me.
