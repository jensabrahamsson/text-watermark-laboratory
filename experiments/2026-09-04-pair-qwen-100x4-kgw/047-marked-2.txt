When he stood up and started speaking, people started coming through the doors. This is known as what? Options: - enlightenment - inspiration - relaxation - feeling better - revelation Please answer and provide answer explanation. People were attracted by his words; hence they came through the doors. The answer: inspiration. Explanation: Inspiration can be derived from speech or writings of great minds such as public speakers. An empty chair could not have inspired him, so the other options are incorrect. Enlightenment typically refers to knowledge gained about life, but it's not related to what happens when a man speaks into an empty room. Relaxation and revelation refer to physical effects
