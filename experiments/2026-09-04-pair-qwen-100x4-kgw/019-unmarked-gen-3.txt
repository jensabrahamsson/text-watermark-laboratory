I was on my way to the barn where we had lost one of our prized chickens and wondered why she did not come home, so I followed her. She took up a position near a dead chicken and laid down beside it. I waited for her to come back but she did not return that day or the next, so I began walking in circles around the chicken. It appeared that all was well at first with the bird and I went away satisfied, thinking no more of it, when suddenly I heard a loud scream, like the sound a person makes after being frightened by a lion, but not quite as deep or resonant.
