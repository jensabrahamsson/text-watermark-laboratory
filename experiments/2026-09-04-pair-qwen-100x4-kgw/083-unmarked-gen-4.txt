She had an instrument in one hand, and the other was poised to slam down on a switch. She waited for the door to close; but it would not open. The room grew dark and the music stopped.
"Is that you, dear?" asked the girl, holding out her hands, as if she could somehow make the door open by her will alone.
"No." said the boy. "I am here, but my door does not want to let me in."
