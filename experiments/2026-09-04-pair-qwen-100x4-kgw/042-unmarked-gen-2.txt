He sat on the bed, his back against the door, his eyes fixed on the satellite dish as he tried to catch every last thing that passed it. He would have given anything for the TV to work again. It was so frustrating. Every time someone called him, he had to explain what was wrong with the satellite dish. He felt like crying at times. He knew that there were better days ahead when he could be back in the sea, keeping watch over his beloved Lighthouse.
