And on the very edge was a crooked pot with its lid half off. Inside it there lay the muddled tea that should have been made for my mum.
But now it was all spoilt, so I took a sip. And it wasn’t very strong, as you might expect when someone leaves something unattended. But it was also not bad.
This is my mother’s recipe. It’s called Yoghurt Tea and I’ve had it since I was little, but now at forty-two years old I’m still unsure if I like it or not.
