The crossbeeper beeps a warning, indicating that an object is approaching in your way. If you are about to step on a road, railway track or other obstacle, but have time to walk across it without delay (such as while standing at traffic lights), then you can continue walking rather than stopping. The crossbeeper will sound again when you reach the edge of the obstacle.
