The wood of the hull looked solid, as if it had been left in a stormy sea and washed ashore. There was no sign of what was missing. The boat’s owners were satisfied that they would get their money back for the boat. They took out insurance to cover this eventuality.
This story is about my grandfather who used to work on boats. He was not a skilled carpenter or an engineer but he knew everything there was to know about building a boat. When the time came to sell his old sailboat, he decided to use it to make a few repairs before giving it away.
