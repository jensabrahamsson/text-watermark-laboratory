It was late evening. I had to go up the hill to the cemetery for a family member's funeral, but when I got there it didn't look like much of a gathering.
There were a few friends, maybe a dozen or so, standing in front of a small wooden gazebo. They'd probably have been happy enough if not for the wind blowing the flags at half-mast; all of them, and some on the lawn behind them, had put them back up again before I arrived. There was an empty chair under one flag, just left of center. That must be the place where he would've sat when they died
