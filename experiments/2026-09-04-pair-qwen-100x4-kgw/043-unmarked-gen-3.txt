A. A child who is fond of tracing the frost on the bus window
B. A child who enjoys tracing the frost on the bus window
C. A child that likes tracing the frost on the bus window
D. A child who is good at tracing the frost on the bus window

选项 B 是正确答案。翻译：一个孩子用蜡笔在窗户上画出洞，直到他发现了一个破洞。

解析：
- 根据原句“a child traced the frost on the bus window”，可以判断这个句子的主题是“一个孩子在公交车的玻璃窗上画画（traced the frost）
