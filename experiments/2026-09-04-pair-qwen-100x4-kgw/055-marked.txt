It was an overcast day in mid-August when we first noticed a pair of foxes, one brown and one black, at the rear entrance to the university car park in Harlow. As they disappeared around a bend on the grassy verge near the car parks, their movements were so silent it seemed the world stopped. I didn’t notice until the fox had returned, this time walking down our road, crossing paths with my wife as she got out of her car after work.
