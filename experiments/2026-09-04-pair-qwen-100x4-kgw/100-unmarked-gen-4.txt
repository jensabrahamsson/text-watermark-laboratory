We had an old, dark wood door to the vestry. The church was on the outside looking very plain and boring. When we arrived as a new congregation there were no liturgical materials or any decoration in the chapel. It just looked like a building where they worshipped once in a while.
As members of the congregation came in over time it became apparent that the place could be beautiful and dynamic if someone would make some changes.
I remember my first visit in 1973. I met Father Frank. He was not an energetic person who talked a lot at meetings. He was a man who quietly served the people he loved
