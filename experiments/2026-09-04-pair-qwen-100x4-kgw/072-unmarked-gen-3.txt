By Doreen Gehringer
Hartford Courant (CT)
Tuesday, February 24, 1985
The sign of the old "Overpass" mural is gone from one of Hartford's major thoroughfares. The sign of the new "Downtown Crossing" mural has been painted on its replacement. The work was done by local artist George Jaffe. The mural depicts the city as a modern, bustling center for industry and commerce. The picture includes the river, the buildings, and a view of downtown. The painting of the mural cost about $10,000 to complete.
