There was a smell of salt and brine.
We were in the Bahamas, on the dockside at Grace Bay Beach. I had left my wife behind in the United States for three weeks. We hadn’t seen each other since we said our goodbyes two years before and she didn’t have to go away again. Her job had transferred her to this place called “the Bahamas.” I had to stay here with my kids.
In that moment I wished that I could go home now but I couldn’t.
