He had seen them many times before, the boats on the dock, waiting to be taken aboard or towed away. But today he stood in front of them and wondered if his father would come up and talk to him about the waterways around the bay that he had spent so much time exploring when he was a child. He looked at all the men in their uniforms walking towards them as they prepared to board the ship. They were talking to each other but he could not hear what they said because they did not speak the same language.
