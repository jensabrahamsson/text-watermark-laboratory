The girls sat at the old wooden pews, legs crossed and elbows on knees. They were quiet in their seats. Only the ticking of the clock on the wall had broken the silence before now.
The three of them looked up from the books they held in their hands as Father John stepped out of the front door of the church. He was wearing a crisp navy blue suit that did not match his shirt and tie at all; instead it seemed to be part of another outfit entirely.
