A man in a coat walked up to the stall. He looked like he was on his way home, so I asked if he wanted to try one of my ice creams, and I offered him some money for it.
He said that he would take my offer but then suggested that I could make more sales by opening up an open stand. So we arranged for me to open up the stall at night when it would be cooler outside, which is the best time to sell ice cream.
