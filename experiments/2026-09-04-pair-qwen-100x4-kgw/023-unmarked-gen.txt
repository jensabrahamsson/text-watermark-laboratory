And I said: "I am here to see your milk, and when you are done, then I will go."
The farmer smiled as if he had just won the lottery. The cows came in a steady stream, their horns like an army of ants moving together. Each step brought with it a little milk from each cow's teats.
As we passed by a barn with some sheep, he stopped. He was going to count the wool on all those animals.
