When I said that he should not be surprised when I left him, he said, "You must have forgotten all about me, my dear. I do not need you anymore."
So, with a heavy heart, she went to church and put on her old dress and veil; she walked into the chapel and stood before the altar; her eyes were fixed upon heaven, her hands held up in prayer; while her lips uttered such words of supplication as she had never uttered in her life.
