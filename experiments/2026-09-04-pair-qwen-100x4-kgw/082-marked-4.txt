There were a couple of large puddles to wade through on the way down. The rain had soaked the soil and made it very slippery.
I didn’t have my walking stick, but it didn’t take me long to realize that I could use the edge of my pack as a makeshift walking stick by holding it up over my arm and using that to push myself forward.
When I finally got to the bottom of the hill, I went right instead of left like the arrow points.
It turns out I’m pretty good at figuring things out once they are explained in detail. At least for a while, anyway.
