It was a small village, but there wasn’t much room to sleep in. In one of his houses lived a family with three children. They were a loving bunch who would let him rest on their floor. One night when they weren’t home he built the firewood high enough that they wouldn’t hear it crackle, so they could go to sleep without being disturbed by a sound they couldn’t ignore.
