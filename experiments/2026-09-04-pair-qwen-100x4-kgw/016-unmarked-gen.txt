I was on a train from the city to the country, traveling by bus to my first assignment of this summer. I had just graduated college and my first job wasn’t going to be in the big city but rather out in rural New Hampshire where there was only one other person who spoke English. My name was Tia and I was moving into a small house for two weeks at a volunteer farm run by an organization called Peace Corps.
My parents had taken me into their arms when I told them I wanted to go abroad. As soon as they heard about my destination, my parents were worried. They didn’t know if I could do this
