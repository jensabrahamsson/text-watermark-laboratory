There was a lot to be said for having an end-of-year review of one's own work. It would be easy and quick, especially in light of the impending holiday break and new year resolutions. After all, it is a great way to look back at how much progress you've made over time and see what improvements can be made. In this regard, we are constantly looking back and forward to the past and present, but never really making much of a move towards change or development.
What happens when we stop?
We start moving backwards rather than forwards.
When we do something differently, even just a little bit, things will improve.
