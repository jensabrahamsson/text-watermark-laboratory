It was a beautiful morning. I had been driving around looking for some places to visit in this small town in western Kentucky, when I finally decided to park near a small community called Sturgis.
Sturgis has many different names, including: Sturgis City, Sturgis Village, Sturgis Community and Town of Sturgis. The city also goes by the name of Sturgis. The city is located at 37.24816° N and 85.99901° W and is about an hour away from Bowling Green.
