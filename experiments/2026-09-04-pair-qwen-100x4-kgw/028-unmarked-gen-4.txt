She was a young, blond girl who seemed to be the size of her grandfather. Her eyes were blue as ice and her skin was as pale as moonlight.
A man had taken her from her home and he held her down on the ground while his other two companions stood behind her.
One of them carried a gun. The other one held the woman’s hands by the wrists.
The girl screamed at him in German but it came out muffled because she was holding her mouth shut with her hand.
He looked at her for a long time, not saying anything.
