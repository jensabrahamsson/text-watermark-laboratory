It was a big, warm coat. It smelled of something rich and expensive. She didn’t know how it came into her possession. Maybe she stole it. She hoped she didn’t leave a trace. Her hands were shaking as she put the jacket on, but when she did it fit her perfectly.
It felt so good. Like she was finally home again, after years of wandering in strange places and being alone. The warmth of the coat was a comfort.
She couldn’t think about it too much, though. The weather had changed. Snow began falling slowly outside the window of her room.
There wasn’t time for that, anyway
