It was a cold January morning in 1978 when my wife, Carol, walked into our little apartment in Philadelphia. I had been out for two hours trying to get a new car radio from the dealerships that were closed on Saturdays, but I couldn’t make it through the day without being able to hear a gospel message. It would have taken me about three days if there hadn’t been such a good Christmas sale going on at my local Goodwill store where I picked up some used furniture for cheap.
