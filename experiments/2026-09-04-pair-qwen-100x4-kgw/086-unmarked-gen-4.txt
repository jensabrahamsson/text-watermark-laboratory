There was a small theater downstairs, but I never went there. The last time I saw a movie in a real movie theater was 1970 at the Fox Theater on Main Street in Santa Cruz, California. But the new digital movies and DVDs have been out for years. My parents had two video recorders (a black and white VCR with a red button to start it and a green button to stop it) that recorded all the films I saw on TV from when I was about three until I started high school.
