It was the most unusual building in the whole of the little village, even though it looked no more than a traditional stone house on the outside. And yet inside its rooms, there were surprises at every corner: one room led straight into another, the walls were made out of logs, while other rooms had no door but simply a hole cut in to let light or heat through; there were bedrooms with only two windows and none that faced south so they could see the sun go down; and there were bathrooms that were lit by candles instead of modern electric lights!
