The 53-year-old mural at the underpass by Main and West was changed to The World of Mirth. It is a tribute to local businesses who have been around for generations, according to the City of Edmonton.
But the old name of the mural, which also said "Main Street Underpass," was not painted over. A sign above the mural that said, "Underpass Mural" is still visible next to it.
Edmonton's Community Improvement Program (CIP) has applied for grants from the Alberta Building Communities Foundation (ABCF), which would help the city update and clean up the underpass.
