When I first moved to the Netherlands, my Dutch friends invited me to a festival called Lantern Festival in Amsterdam. They were expecting about 30 lanterns to light up the streets, but instead they got about 1,500 lanterns (that's how many there are) from all over the world, so it was pretty amazing and cool. In general, the Dutch do like to show off their culture. There is also an art exhibition that occurs at the same time every year.
But this time around I did not go for a visit to the Dutch cultural events, but instead I went to check out a local night market
