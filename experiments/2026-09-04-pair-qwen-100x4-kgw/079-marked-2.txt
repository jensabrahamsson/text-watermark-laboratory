I was sitting in my dentist’s office, listening to the droning of a man who could never be patient enough. Suddenly I looked at his pile and saw a small, red, square patch. He was talking about an appointment, but every word he spoke was muffled by his words being directed towards the carpet of hairs he’d created as a visual aid.
It didn’t really make sense; there was nothing here except for the carpet, so what did it matter? But there it was – the square red spot on the carpet with no one in its midst.
