It was a very nice, quiet spot to sit and read. In my book it is well worth visiting for a few minutes or an hour to enjoy the view and take in the scenery.
I like the fact that you are able to see both sides of the river from the same spot, if not actually looking at two rivers side by side. That is so unusual and something I haven't seen anywhere else before.
There's even a little bit of history here with a plaque saying the bench was built 200 years ago.
