It’s an old adage, but a new study has shown that the birds are actually thankful for our food.
When people feed them scraps of bread or bread crumbs on the beach in places such as Cornwall, Devon and Brittany, they happily take it, and not just any piece of bread either – even bread crumbs which have been dropped onto beaches by tourists.
The researchers, led by the University of Exeter in England, asked whether there was enough of this kind of bread available for all the gulls at the same time. They found out that there wasn’t.
