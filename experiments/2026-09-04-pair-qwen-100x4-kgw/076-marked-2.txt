I didn't feel like leaving the theatre and as I didn't want to miss the train I went back to the theatre to have a look around.
When I was there, I found an old man sitting in front of a desk by the stairs. He looked like he was in his 70s.
There were a couple of books about writing next to him. On top of one book was a small note.
The note said that he would stay at the theatre for free if anyone wanted to read some of his work. If you were interested, please leave your name or initials.
This note made me curious because it is so unique
