He had been there a week. But as he sat by his window, staring out at the water, he could see nothing but the shadowy reflection of the fish in the glass.
His eyes had become accustomed to looking through that glass and now when he turned away, his eyes were drawn back.
He had wanted to catch them all – every single fish that floated across the pond and then sank again, until one day he was ready for more than just a shadow on the wall behind him.
Then he would look through that same window with a clear view into the world below, not just the reflection inside.
