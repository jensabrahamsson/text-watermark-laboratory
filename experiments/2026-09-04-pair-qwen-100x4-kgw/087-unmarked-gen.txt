There was no one around, so he took his time to get lost in thought. The warehouse where they were currently located was an industrial complex of warehouses and buildings that had been converted into a large storage facility for various products. He was currently standing at one of the entry points, looking out across the horizon as the light from his torch illuminated the dark skies.
In front of him lay a row of shelves, each stacked with boxes upon boxes of items that had been stored here over the years. Each box contained something different; some contained precious metals and jewels that were very valuable, while others held raw materials or components necessary for making other products
