“Can you believe this, I’m still here,” she said to herself.
It had been a long day. The air was thick and stifling.
She took a deep breath, tried to calm her racing heart and opened the window. As soon as she did so, all of her troubles disappeared in front of her eyes.
A white light flooded through the tiny gap between the two windows on the other side of the room.
Her face flushed with embarrassment. She couldn’t hide it any longer. Her cheeks were redder than her hair.
After her heart returned to its normal rhythm, she began to open the curtains to let more light
