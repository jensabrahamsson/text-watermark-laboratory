The door clicked shut and locked. He walked back to the other end of the yard, where his wife waited with a cup of tea in her hands. "It's still raining, isn't it?" she said.
"I don't think so," he replied. The rain had stopped but he didn't want to take his eyes off the house as it was too close. If it started again now, he would not be able to see anything. He turned to look at the trees beyond the fence. They looked lush and green even through the thick fog of clouds.
