The singers would often be heard to murmur among themselves, in tones which were not at all harmonious with any tune they might have been following. Sometimes their voices seemed to be so close that you could feel them whispering into your ear, but never very clearly.
There was a kind of general excitement about it; it didn’t seem quite real, but it felt like something good. And then when it wasn’t going well everyone stopped trying to sing in unison. They just kept repeating the first few notes again and again.
It seemed to me as though I were listening to the chorus of a flock of birds. The church itself
