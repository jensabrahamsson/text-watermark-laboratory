She used to see it in the morning. The mill, its walls now a mellow grey of neglect. It was once a great place; people came from miles around to grind flour for their bread. But times were changing and soon enough they would all be out of work and have no one left to buy their flour. There was talk about tearing down the old mill and building some new structure. But then something happened. A man had come into town one day. He asked to speak with the manager and when he spoke to the manager he told him how he could use this old mill as his new home.
