I’ve written and read many, many letters to people over my life. Most were just simple notes. I wrote them because it was a habit or because I felt compelled to write them; sometimes both at once.
When I have a problem, I need an outlet to express myself. And if that person has done something wrong, I need an outlet to let off steam. If you’ve ever experienced a relationship breakup, you know how frustrating that can be: it’s not fun or pleasant, but there is nothing else you can do about it.
