It was only about 9.30 p.m. and I'd just got off the train at London Waterloo, so it wasn't exactly a surprise when a man walked past me and said, "You should look for that lamp over there. It will still be on."
I thought he must have been mistaken but when we reached our destination, I asked my friend to turn off the lights in the flat because the room felt cold, despite the heat from the heater in the kitchen.
She looked puzzled when she found out what was wrong.
"Over there," she said, pointing to where I'd first seen the light flicker.
