But not before one of his hands accidentally brushed against an empty box. In that moment, it became clear he was looking at something more than just a box.
There were coins inside! But how could there be coins? It wasn't possible!
He couldn't believe what he saw. The coin had no face value! He picked up the coin, held it in his hand and examined its surface carefully - the edge looked exactly like the same edges on any of the coins he had seen, so it must have been real! Then he tried to move it around...it wouldn't budge.
Now it didn't look like a real coin
