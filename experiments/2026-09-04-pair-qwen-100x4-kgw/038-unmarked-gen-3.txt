On Tuesday, a restaurant in New Jersey said it would no longer serve a special order of two cakes because one was pink, prompting complaints on social media that led to an apology from the owner. The bakery that served them said it had no choice but to go ahead with the sale and apologize for its handling of the situation, according to reports.
The cakes were ordered by the customer on Monday afternoon and delivered on Tuesday at 3 p.m., but it came out pink when the baker arrived to remove it, reports The Washington Post. The owner, who owns a couple other bakeries, told the paper she did not want the pink cake
