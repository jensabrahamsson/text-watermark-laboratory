The cow's name was Buhu, and it didn't want to eat the hay at all. I've never seen a cow this stubborn before!
The farmer laughed as he said, "You are Buhu, aren't you? A true cow!"
But Buhu only kept mooing and refused to eat. The farmer shouted out for help, but no one came in response.
He felt so sad, so he decided to go home.
When he returned, he found an old man lying by his house. He was a sick old man who had nothing left except the horse and a few sacks of rice.
