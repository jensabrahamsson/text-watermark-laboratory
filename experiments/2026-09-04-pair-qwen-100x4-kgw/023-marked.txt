The cow had a good reason for staying. The farmer could use it as a step stool to climb into his hayloft, so it couldn't be sold. He was able to feed it better because it got more attention. And if he wanted to do some serious work with machinery and get dirty, he just needed to move it out of the way, so it wasn't bothering him. In short, it gave him the most comfort.
