She walked down a few steps and realized something was off. The stairwell was dark and dusty, with just enough light filtering in through gaps in the walls to see the small square that marked the end of each level. She looked around frantically. Her heart beating hard in her chest, she started climbing the stairs.
The light flickered on and off, illuminating her path and allowing her to see the way. With every step she took, her fear increased. She heard footsteps behind her, but when she turned to face them, they were gone. Suddenly, a sharp pain in her ankle made her stop dead in her tracks.
