It was his first public address. He was nervous. The room filled with tension and he froze in place, unable to move or speak.
He knew that by speaking publicly to no one would listen and it would only make him feel worse than if he were silent and alone. But still he went ahead with the plan, determined to get through this exercise as quickly as possible so he could go back home.
He spoke slowly and carefully. When he got to the part where he had planned to ask for contributions, he paused and looked around at everyone.
His heart sank when he saw that they all seemed indifferent. No one even raised their hands
