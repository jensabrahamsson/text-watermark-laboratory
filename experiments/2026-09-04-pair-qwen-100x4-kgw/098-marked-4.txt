It was a simple wooden bench, weathered and cracked. It wasn’t made for sitting in. The painted face on it looked like an ancient person looking back at you with eyes that were too old. In his hands he carried something that was heavy but didn’t make him seem tired.
When I sat there and read the poem by William Butler Yeats called “The Winding Stair,” the words seemed to flow from my lips as though someone had whispered them into my ear.
