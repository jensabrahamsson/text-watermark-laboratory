He's already been there. The shoe he left for her is not his, she thinks with some surprise.
Her hand was on the door handle when she remembered that she didn't have a spare set of keys. Her father had said something about the keys having fallen off one of her bags. He must have put them back in it after all, and since then they hadn't been found, she thought with an odd satisfaction, even though she knew he wasn't going to give them to her.
When she opened the door, it creaked beneath her feet; she felt like a visitor who hadn't quite been welcomed at home.
