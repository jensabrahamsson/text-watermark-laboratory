Everyone was in the gym, but we were not. The drill was over and the students went back to their classrooms. There was still time before the next one so I sat down on the edge of my desk. I watched as the other students left, some with tears rolling down their cheeks.
I had just found out about my son’s diagnosis at 2:00 pm. My doctor told me there wasn’t much hope and he would only have months to live. I spent the night crying myself to sleep. In the morning I called his dad and informed him what had happened. We both cried while listening to each other cry.
