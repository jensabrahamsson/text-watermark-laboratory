He was still not feeling well, he thought. There were many things in life that could make him feel better than this. He knew they wouldn't happen again but still, the future never knows what's coming at you.
The coal fired-up, his breath steamed. His back ached from being bent for so long. The ground was rough and his feet made little sound. A small tree leaned against his legs, its branches hanging down like an umbrella; when the sun hit it, the wood-burning fire flickered.
The world seemed very dark to him today. But he knew there were many places where light shone brightly
