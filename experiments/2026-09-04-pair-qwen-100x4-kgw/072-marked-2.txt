A new mural has replaced the one on the south side of the Underpass. It features a depiction of two women holding hands while riding bicycles, along with a message in Spanish and English about equal rights for all.
The artist said the message was inspired by her own experiences as a gay Latina woman in New York City. She also worked closely with a local transgender woman who is also featured in the painting.
"I hope that it's going to open up people's minds and show them the importance of being able to love and be loved regardless of sexual orientation or gender identity," she says.
