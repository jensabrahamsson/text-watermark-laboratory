As we drove up the winding country road to our destination, I couldn’t help but feel as though I was entering another world. We had arrived in Naxi town in the highlands region of Sichuan province. The last time I visited here I remember being fascinated by how the ancient Chinese city had survived for thousands of years amidst a desert and had maintained its traditional ways. But this was the opposite – a place that felt more modern with the majority of the buildings constructed out of brick and cement and with a large shopping center complete with modern stores and restaurants.
