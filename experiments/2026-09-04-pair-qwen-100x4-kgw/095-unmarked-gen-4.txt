He didn’t notice a car in front of him.
The driver’s hands shook when he turned on the ignition.
It was all an act, he said. An elaborate one.
There were no rattles inside that car.
And there was nothing wrong with it.
But it made me want to get in that car and drive for as long as I could.
And then, when we were done, I wanted to buy it. Just to sit in it.
But, well, I never did. Not because there weren’t enough cars for everyone. But simply because I had to keep driving.
