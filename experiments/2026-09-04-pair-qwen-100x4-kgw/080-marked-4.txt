There is a small stone mill on the bank of the river. The miller, who is not in the mill but at the wheelhouse behind the building, sits there quietly and watches the wind make ripples on the water while his hands move with precision over the wooden handle.
It has been decades since I have worked as a miller's apprentice; the last time was many years ago when my grandfather had one in his hometown, before he sold it for scrap metal to build a new house.
