It's been six years since 35-year-old Emily McFarlane disappeared in central London. Now, her family has spoken about their hopes for her safe return and their determination to never give up the search.
"I miss her dearly," her mother Karen said. "I just can't imagine what life would be like without her."
The police have searched the area around Mrs. McFarlane's home several times but no trace of her was found.
Mrs. McFarlane went missing on May 26, 2014 from her apartment in central London where she lived with her daughter, a student at college.
