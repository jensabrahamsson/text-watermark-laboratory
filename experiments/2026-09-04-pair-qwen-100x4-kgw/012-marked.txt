It seemed strange, but there were people waiting at the bridge to watch it being fixed up for its re-opening. They talked and laughed and they said they would be happy when the path opened again, so we joined in with them.
When we reached the spot, there were four or five men and women working on the gates that had been torn down. A large amount of rope was spread out over the entire area, and someone else was cutting off bits and putting new pieces together. They worked quickly, but they still looked exhausted.
