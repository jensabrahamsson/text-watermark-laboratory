When you go to the railway station and look around, it's hard not to notice the many trains that rumble by. And sometimes they carry people to distant destinations for important reasons - business, education, romance or family reunions. In fact, traveling on the train is such a convenient way to travel from one place to another so quickly that it has become popular all over the world.
There are many different types of railways but they all have some similarities and some differences as well. Some railways use diesel engines while others use steam. Most of these systems run through tunnels and mountains but there are also other forms of transportation like buses and planes
