The farmer, standing on his horse to catch the wind and survey the fields, held his hands up in mock surrender and made gestures of surrender. He was trying to negotiate for more than he bargained for this year, but there was nothing left with which to bargain.
There wasn’t any more hope.
It seemed as if every bit of soil and every breath of air had become part of a larger machine, pulling down houses, burning crops and eating all the food in the world.
It wasn’t that there weren’t enough people; it was that there were too many.
