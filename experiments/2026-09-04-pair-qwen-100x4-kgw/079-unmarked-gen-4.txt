But it wasn’t the product of someone’s life.
It was a pile of wool.
In one case, the wool came from the fleece of a horse. In another, from the coat of a cow.
The pile had come from hundreds of sheep. And when he saw them all at once — and he did see them — he felt overwhelmed by sorrow.
The pile represented what he knew: A loss, the cost of his sins, not just for him but for every living creature.
