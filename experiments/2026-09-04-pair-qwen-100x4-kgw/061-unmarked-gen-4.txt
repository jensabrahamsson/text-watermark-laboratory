This is one of my favorite phrases in the English language. I have heard it used to describe everything from cleaning up spilled wine at a dinner party to getting out of an unpleasant situation or acting after being caught cheating on someone's spouse (and sometimes for both).
It's often said without much thought, but I've never understood why it doesn't work for all situations.
