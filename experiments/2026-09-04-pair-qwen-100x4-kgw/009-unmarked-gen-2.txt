The door was ajar. She could hear voices inside. The man sounded anxious and she felt as if she would have to tell him no, that she couldn’t come. But there was something about the voice of this girl, something warm and welcoming. It gave her courage to answer him, to open the door.
She stood in front of the door for a moment before deciding which way to go. Her eyes searched around. Finally, she saw the key in the lock and reached out for it with trembling fingers. As she turned the handle, she was filled with a sense of relief.
