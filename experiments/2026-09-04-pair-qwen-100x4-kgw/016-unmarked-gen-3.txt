I lay down in my bunk and looked out at the horizon. It was still, a dark blue sky over the city. I wanted to go home, back into that familiar room with its window looking onto a field of green and white lilies. But I could not leave this place. This is where the rest of my life would be lived out, or so I hoped. The day had been a long one, from the early morning until now, with nothing but work to occupy me. When the sun finally set on this part of the world, I would return to the small apartment I shared with another man, a friend of mine
