Buttery, rich, sweet, buttery, rich, rich, rich...this is the best croissant I have ever had in my life. If you are a croissant fan or just want to try something new - go for this. And if you are gluten intolerant, I recommend that you ask your baker what they use as their flour (if you cannot pronounce anything in it).
My husband asked the waiter "Are you sure you don't have an almond croissant?" because it tasted so much like one. Yes he was right! I've never seen such a well made almond croissant before.
