It's hard to imagine now, but back then when I was in my early 20s and had just left college, I took an assignment to write about "the importance of small things" for a school newspaper. My task was to interview two people who worked in the local paper industry (one of whom later went on to be my boss). This included interviewing people from the editorial staff as well as the advertising department. I decided on these two because I found them interesting, though not necessarily particularly important. They both made good points.
