When it rained, they were all gone and the old house had no attic anymore. They’d been in there since my great-great-grandmother had come here with her husband to start their new life in America.
The house was built by my ancestors, who’d sailed to New York City after the Civil War. It’s where we’d spent our summer as children. My grandfather would stand on the steps and look out over his backyard; my grandmother would sit in a rocking chair outside the living room, watching the world go by.
