As a small group of us left the main building to return to our car, I turned around and noticed that there was still a small light burning in one of the chapels. In my mind’s eye, I imagined that it was the light from the Holy Light of Christmas.
It wasn’t.
It was the flicker of an electric light on the other side of the room as the last of the volunteers who had helped clean up the place returned for the evening.
