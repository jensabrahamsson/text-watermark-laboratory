She took it all into her lap, and it was dark as night. The earth moved through the sky and she stood before the horizon, where the stone had been and what remained of it had grown to become this place. Her hands formed words that she knew were meaningless. She reached out her arm toward the lake and the world turned on its axis and she was gone. She walked away from it, leaving only a trail behind.
