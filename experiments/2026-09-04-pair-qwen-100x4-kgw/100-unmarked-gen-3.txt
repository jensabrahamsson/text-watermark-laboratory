This is one of my favorite stories. I think the priest’s face shows his surprise at what happened and his hope that this won’t happen again.
My mother was also a very religious person, so when she found out that I wasn’t practicing anymore (I’d stopped going to church after high school) she was upset, but not surprised. In fact, it was easier for her to accept than it was for me because she understood what I was going through at the time.
I’m sure she didn’t tell him how he made himself look in that picture. But then again, he could’ve told her herself if he had been willing
