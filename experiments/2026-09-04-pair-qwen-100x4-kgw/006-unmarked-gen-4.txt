The newspaper man brought his paper and the local magazine was still on the rack. It didn’t sell much, but he’d saved it just in case someone would actually read it some day. He wondered if there would be a single person alive in this world who could read it…or even comprehend what it said. The words were simple, straightforward and easy to understand. But it seemed the world was full of people who couldn’t handle any type of plain, straight-forward truth.
He’d never seen anyone take the trouble to buy or read a single copy of this little magazine over all those years. They wouldn’t know how boring it was
