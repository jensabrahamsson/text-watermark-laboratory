I had been there for years, but never before did I realize that its walls are made of rock.
As I lay on my back looking up, it struck me as a great wonder to know that I am sitting atop some huge pieces of rock.
But this is not just a simple matter of sitting atop large blocks of stone. The very earth under my feet is also made out of these enormous blocks of stone!
This is something we can only be grateful for and be thankful for.
It’s an awesome fact to learn, don’t you think? How can we ever be too old or tired to appreciate all the beauty God has given us
