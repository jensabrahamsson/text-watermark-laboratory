A dry steam cleaning system is a type of vacuum cleaner with a special nozzle that sprays a very hot water mist at high pressure on the carpet and floor tiles in the room. The resulting vapor penetrates into the pores and breaks down the glue used for bonding. It then loosens the soil that has been stuck onto it.
