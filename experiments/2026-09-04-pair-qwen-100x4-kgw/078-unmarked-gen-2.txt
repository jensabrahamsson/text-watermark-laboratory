It was the sound of the door that had alerted him. It sounded different from before, a bit more forceful than the usual grumble, as though someone or something had knocked. He could hear someone’s breathing and a shuffling sound on the floor.
The door had slammed open, revealing the figure standing in front of him.
He immediately realized who it was, but he did not dare to speak at once; instead, he slowly approached the door with his hands outstretched, just like how an animal would do when approaching its prey. His eyes followed every move that she made.
