It’s been an interesting year for the leaves, with a number of significant changes.
Some have gone to other places in the garden. A few are still in place but have become discolored. Others are still around, but the water in the trough is no longer so plentiful. The last time I noticed it was before Easter (2014).
As well as changing the location of leaves, there were some notable changes to the shape of the leaves themselves. They have grown thinner and shorter over the years.
