It rained last night. A lot. The sky opened up in a way that’s rare around here, pouring down all of these deep drizzles that felt like buckets. I heard it was so heavy on the West side that they had to shut off some water mains just to keep from flooding a couple houses. This rain has been really cold too. The temperature dropped into the mid 40s this morning which is quite shocking since we usually have spring showers by now. But then again this is Utah – where the weather is unpredictable, not to mention frigid.
