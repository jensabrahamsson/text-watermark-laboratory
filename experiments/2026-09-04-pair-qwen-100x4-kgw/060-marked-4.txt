It was a long, flat plank and there were many other ducks on it. I couldn’t see the top but when they flew across from my right side I could feel their flapping wings. The sound of their wings as they flew was a mix of wind and fluttering. The air was thick with a smell of damp and dirt. It smelled of rotting barn straw or something else, but I didn’t know what.
