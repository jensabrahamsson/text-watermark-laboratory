But the first one in the list of the "best of all time" is not for its beauty, but for its utility: the Stone Trough. In 3rd century Rome, when a leaky roof or downspout clogged with leaves rendered your home’s water supply unreliable, you’d pour out what was inside your sink into a trough (or "bassinetto"), then drain it again before refilling it. If you wanted to get rid of all the dirt, put some flour on the bottom. The trough wasn’t as good as a faucet (or a well), but it sure did work better than no water
