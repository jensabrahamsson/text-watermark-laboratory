He was looking for the best place to position himself.
Then, he noticed his mother was walking up to him from the street.
The boy thought this might be the best time to practice and walked slowly towards her. The moment she reached him, she said: "Why are you doing this?" "Because I want to learn," the boy answered.
She smiled, gave him a pat on the head and left.
When the mother had gone, the boy looked at the mirror and then ran towards home.
The next day, when his mother came, the boy told her that the shopkeeper's son had taught him how to do it.
