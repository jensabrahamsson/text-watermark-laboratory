A few minutes into our elevator ride, the cab stopped on the 3rd floor. A man and woman in their late 20s stood out of the way so that I could pass by them easily without bumping my shoulder against theirs. They did not make eye contact or seem to be looking at me as they waited. We had been in the same elevator for over an hour. The driver finally spoke up.
“Can you just get off?” he said to them.
