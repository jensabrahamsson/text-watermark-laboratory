When she had left for New York to see him, she thought of nothing else. He was everything: a breathless dancer in her memory; an eager student who would be happy to talk about dance all day; a man who could make her laugh at his own jokes, who could give her something more than a smile, someone with whom she felt at ease. And yet it turned out that he didn’t like dance as much as she thought; he was an easy-going, friendly fellow who seemed interested only in talking about things he had seen or heard, but never danced; someone who could do absolutely anything. He always wore black shirts
