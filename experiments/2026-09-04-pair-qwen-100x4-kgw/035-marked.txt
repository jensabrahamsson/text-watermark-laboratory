There are only two ways for one person to have checked their own name and not
the name of anyone else: one person has to have checked their own name, but also
the names of the other two people. If exactly 3 bells rang, then there would be
four ways for one person to have checked their own name, which is not possible.
Therefore, there must be at least one person who checked their own name but
did not check the names of the other two people.
