I could not take my eyes from the little stream, the green reeds, the river mullion,
but when I did think of my own house and my work, then I was troubled. I felt as if a heavy burden lay upon me; but it was not the work itself that disturbed me; for in this cottage there are no hard hours to be borne with patient endurance.
