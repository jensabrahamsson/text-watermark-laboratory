A local fisherman claims that he is sick of seeing the elderly woman hand out bread and crumpets to hungry seagulls in Exmouth, Devon. He claims the pensioner has been doing this on a daily basis for at least three years.
The 74-year-old fisherman told The Press: "I'm getting really sick of her and I've even spoken to a member of police who did nothing about it."
Police have confirmed that they are aware of the problem and have taken note of his report.
