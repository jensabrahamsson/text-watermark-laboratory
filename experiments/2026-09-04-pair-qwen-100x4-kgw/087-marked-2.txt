There were not enough of these flares, so they decided to make them themselves. With their bare hands and a bunch of materials at hand, they got to work.
It was 1847 when they started the process, so they made use of a method called 'Flickering' to create a small flame on the end of a piece of tinder. This process was simple but difficult for them since they did not have access to machines or tools like we do today.
Eventually, they managed to craft a spark from scratch.
