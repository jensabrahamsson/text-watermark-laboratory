She didn't realize that her plant was only one day old, so the first drop of rain would wash away most of the leaves and she might not even have seen it until it was too late. Her husband loved the plant dearly, and after it grew, she felt the loss as much as she did when he died. She gave the plant more attention than she could give him at his funeral, but she never forgot about her husband.
It wasn't until many years later that she found out what happened to the plant on the first day. The rain fell in the night and the first drops of water washed off most of the leaves.
