I'm going to take it that this is an indirect question, and therefore, you can't use the subjunctive mood for. The most straightforward translation of your sentence would be: "The child traced the frost on the bus window until a hole appeared." It should be noted that there's a difference between past and present perfect tenses.

Past perfect tense means that the action happened before another event (in this case, tracing the frost). Past simple tense simply means it happened at any point during the time span of the story.
