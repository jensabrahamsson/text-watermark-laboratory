It's been a very good year for our orchards. In addition to the sweet cherries, we also have a bumper crop of pears, apples and apricots. This has meant an extra lot of jobs for our orchard staff including some new young folks who are helping us with various tasks in the orchards during peak season, especially picking cherries.
We've managed to make time to get out into the orchards quite a few times this year as well - just before the harvest period started. It was a great way to catch up with each other and share what is going on in each of our lives at home.
