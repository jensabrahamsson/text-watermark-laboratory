It's called a "corkboard," and it's designed to keep paper clips from sliding off.
But, at times, I wonder if there isn't something more profound to these wooden planks than just keeping things in place. Like maybe there is something in them, or even in me, that has to do with my ability to withstand being pulled apart?
Is this what makes us human? The strength to withstand separation. The resilience of our will to survive when we are pushed into situations that might tear us apart?
I don't know for sure because this was a memory I have not processed yet.
Perhaps I can write about it
