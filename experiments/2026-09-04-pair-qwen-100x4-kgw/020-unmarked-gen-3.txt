As it was only 10am I had no time to rush off. I knew that if I waited till midday then I would be too busy for my other errands so I went along around half an hour later.
When I got there the queue was long but by this time the shop was very quiet (which was great because I am not very good at standing for long periods of time). I saw the tailor and he told me they were busy but said he could help me. So I decided to have a look round the shop.
I love this little shop full of vintage clothes, shoes and bags.
