That person might be a photographer like myself or it might be someone just visiting for the first time and not sure where to go next. Whether its an enthusiastic photographer or someone looking for inspiration, these kinds of people will take their own photos on their trips, whether at a museum, historical site, local restaurant or simply exploring the neighborhood.
