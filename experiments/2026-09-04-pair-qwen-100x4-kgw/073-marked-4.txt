He knew his way around well enough that he was able to read signs.
It wasn’t his first time traveling, so it didn’t take him long to find a taxi.
But it also wouldn’t have been out of place for him to be walking there. He wore no jewelry or clothing other than a simple shirt with a jacket on top, leaving his face uncovered as well.
“Excuse me…”
He looked up in confusion, then recognized the person approaching from their distinctive appearance and mannerisms.
It wasn’t that this person could do anything wrong, but they weren’t someone who’d usually step into a car after waiting for traffic
