The house was his. He had always known it, he would never leave it.
As a child he loved to watch his dad at work in the woodshop, watching the saws cut through lumber with a precision that matched his own hands. Dad would talk about the fence he had made as a young man, when he had been just starting out in carpentry. He told the kids of the days they worked for pennies an hour; how his job had brought him from being a mere laborer to a craftsman who got paid enough to support a family. It wasn’t long before the boys were helping him out by cutting wood
