It seemed to think that they weren’t meant for other animals to use and I didn’t know how it knew which side was a private drive, but whatever, it had managed to cross in one piece. Not bad for a fox.
It stopped on the edge of the road next to the fence line, where the owner could see him and then went to sleep.
The owner of this part of the car park has put up a sign that says ‘Private Drive’, yet he still lets foxes and squirrels use the parking space.
