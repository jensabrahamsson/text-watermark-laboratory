In the light, the faces seemed soft and round. A few women in the crowd spoke in whispers, but they didn’t look up from their conversation. The lanterns were so bright they made it hard to breathe and a few people gasped for air. My friend looked at me with concern as we walked closer. It wasn’t the smell I was smelling or the sound of our feet hitting the gravel road – it was the faint echo of children’s laughter that filled my head and chest.
I wanted to run back to the safety of the night market.
