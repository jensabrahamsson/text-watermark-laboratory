It’s hard to imagine a world without paper lanterns. In this culture, they’re a central part of the festival scene: during weddings and births and other celebrations. And for decades, paper lanterns were the dominant form, as Chinese people began importing thousands upon thousands every year from Taiwan. In fact, over 1 million lanterns are shipped to the US each year (a number that seems about right) — but even in their heyday, when they dominated our shopping cart at local markets and festivals across China, paper lanterns were by no means ubiquitous.
