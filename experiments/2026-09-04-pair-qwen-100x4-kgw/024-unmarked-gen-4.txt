My father, who was then 94 years old, took his camera to the attic one summer afternoon. I remember him standing with a smile on his face as he shot photographs of his family and friends gathered around the kitchen table or in the garden.
After shooting several dozen pictures, we decided to return home. I never asked what happened next but my memory is clear of the conversation I had after that moment.
I was a child then and at this age now, I have no idea why I remember so clearly that day 36 years ago today. Maybe it was because there were photos of my grandfather on those walls.
