“Cooks” walked the streets on stilts, serving up snacks at 3:00 a.m. and leaving in time for their shift in the kitchen at dawn.
They were a unique group of people — from all walks of life – with some stories to tell and some tales to sell. I’ve met quite a few vendors like this over my years here. There’s always something going on during these times of year as well as during other seasons of the year.
When you first get out of the train station it’s easy to feel overwhelmed by all of the different cultures that surround you. The city itself has many layers.
