It was an old woman’s letter. She’d written it after the loss of her husband. Her handwriting had grown older, her hair thinning.
“I have lived with a very good man and loved him to the best of my ability.”
She’d continued until her ink ran out.
When she came back next year, her neighbor knocked on her door and saw her sitting in front of the television watching cartoons.
“Can you believe how little things matter?”
Her neighbor said.
That was when the neighbor found the empty house.
