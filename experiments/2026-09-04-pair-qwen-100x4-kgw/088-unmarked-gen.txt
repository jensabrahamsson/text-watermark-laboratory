As I sat on the stone steps at a local park, a beautiful leaf drifted to the water's surface. As I watched it slowly sink, a deep sense of awe washed over me. I had never seen anything so delicate before in my life. It was as though the leaf wanted to touch the bottom but knew it could not. The weight seemed like a burden but yet another testament to its resilience.
I thought about all the times I have seen leaves drift by. How they once were part of a forest and now just floating along the waters edge. But what if I changed that? What if I helped those leaves return home?
