I was a little girl. I lived with my grandmother in rural Kentucky, where it is not uncommon for children to walk to school on ice covered creeks and through snow blanketed fields in winter. On a mild spring day, when my grandmother told me we were going to take a canoe ride, I was thrilled. But as she packed up our gear and began loading her basket with a bunch of wood sticks, I asked why. She looked at me as though I had grown three heads and said, "You have to paddle, but don't be too proud."
