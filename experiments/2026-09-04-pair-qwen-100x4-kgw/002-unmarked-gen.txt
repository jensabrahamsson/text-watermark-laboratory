The man had just left the bus stop. He was going to work, he said. There was no destination. Just another day, just another mile or two down the road.
And then suddenly, the porter stopped dragging it and let it go. The case toppled over in the middle of the road. The porter stood there for a moment, looking around at all the people who walked by, some carrying suitcases but not this one. They were in a hurry, they didn’t seem to notice what happened.
He looked up again, still trying to figure out how to make sense of this situation.
