That is not true. There are two paddles which move in opposite directions and rotate about a pivot point on the wooden shaft, but they are not connected by a hinge.
The mill wheel was built to turn with only one of these paddles at a time and it is still turned by doing that.
When you need more power than can be provided from one paddle, another paddle rotates clockwise, lifting water off the drum (or any other storage tank) into the spinning paddle. The other paddle remains stationary, making sure that there is no water lost due to leakage from the drum's walls.
