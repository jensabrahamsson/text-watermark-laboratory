There was a man and woman, sitting in front of each other. The woman held a cigarette and looked out at the view while her partner read a newspaper.
As I walked up the stairs, I noticed there were three other men and two women sitting around a small table. All they did was smoke cigarettes as well. A middle-aged man sat on the floor, reading his own paper.
It didn’t take long to realize that all these people are homeless. And if they weren’t, it’s likely because they’re on the streets now after running away from their abusive parents or family.
