I knew the seeds had been sown correctly when they began to sprout, but the drip caused me to stop and wonder what I was doing. Why would this happen? And then, why not?
It’s as if there is something about the act itself that requires a drip to take place. Maybe it’s a natural part of germination or maybe it’s simply because we’re used to it and thus don’t notice the difference. But either way, it seems counter-intuitive to just let the water sit and collect around the roots, waiting for something else to do.
Maybe this was how it felt with God too.
