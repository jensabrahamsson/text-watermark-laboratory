Police are investigating a case of alleged bird cruelty where a man has been accused of feeding birds crumbs on his roof. Police said in a statement that 56-year-old Peter Gouk is being charged with an offence under the Protection of Birds Act 1977 and has been remanded into custody.
A police spokeswoman said: "Police are investigating an allegation of animal cruelty involving a number of birds."
In January, another person was arrested and subsequently released after a similar incident at an Edinburgh home.
Gouk, from Dundee, was arrested by a joint force team at his property on the High Street.
