It was a warm day. The sun shone through the leaves of the trees on the hillside behind the barn where I worked, giving it an amber glow. In the evening light, the hills looked even more rugged, with jagged rocks standing out against the pale grassy meadows. And there were fields of daisies and cow parsley, wildflowers that grew so plentiful you could barely see them in the distance, but every single one of them had to be counted by hand because you never knew which ones would sprout from which spot until they finally did.
