She was not even on the map. She was merely a dot, invisible and without form.
The other people of the world were not concerned about her existence. They could have cared less if she had lived or died.
But I do care.
I care because there is no room in my heart for anyone who does not live like I do. My heart can only hold those whose every action is an attempt to follow God’s will; those whose every breath flows from His Spirit; and those whose every decision points them in the direction He wants them to go.
That woman was so close to me; but somehow she slipped through my fingers.
