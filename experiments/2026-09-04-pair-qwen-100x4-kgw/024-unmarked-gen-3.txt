I looked at the photograph. The picture was from my grandfather’s childhood, and it was taken in a little village nestled on the edge of a cliff overlooking the sea. The pictures were from a camera he received as a birthday gift when he turned twelve. It was the same camera I used to hold myself while I held it to my mother’s head for the portrait she was trying to get her son in. She said I was holding it wrong, but I told her I wanted to be just like her.
