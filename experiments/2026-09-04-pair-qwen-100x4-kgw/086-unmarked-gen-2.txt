It was the only one left, with its posters taped to the sides and the entrance blocked by a sheet of paper. The building, with its glass windows, had a look as though it could be a museum or an art gallery.
It seemed like this place would not last long, because even after 15 years, I could still feel its emptiness when I looked through the glass panes on the upper floors. It was empty except for the occasional people who came in and out of the foyer.
