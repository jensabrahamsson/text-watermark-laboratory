A bakery owner in New Jersey is being criticized for her decision to sell a white cake with dark spots that she says are caused by a "mystery chemical" from its packaging, according to USA Today.
In an Instagram post on June 29, Tia Stabile shared photos of the cake — which had been placed inside a clear plastic container, with the light turned off to hide what was under the lid. The frosting on top of the cake was also darkened and unappetizing, she said.
