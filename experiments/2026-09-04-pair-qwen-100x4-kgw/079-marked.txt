It was an object, and it came from the bottom of a well.
The hair had no roots in it; it didn't have any name or surname, but it had all the features of someone who had died long ago. He wore clothes that did not fit his size or shape, and he had strange scars covering most of his body.
As he stood there, staring at his own reflection, he realized that this person wasn't dead at all, but alive, and he belonged to him. And he understood why. Because he wanted it.
