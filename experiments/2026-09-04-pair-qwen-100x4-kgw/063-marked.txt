Andrea Dall’Oglio was just five when her family were forced from their home in the Italian region of Emilia-Romagna because the government was about to close the coal power plant. They were then moved to a tent camp where she learned how to fend for herself by learning survival skills like eating anything she could find – including fish and snails.
The now 72-year-old said: “When we first came into our tent, there was some chicken left which I ate and it made me feel very good, so it is not such a bad experience after all.”
