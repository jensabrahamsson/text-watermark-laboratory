There wasn’t any sign of life inside, but there were a few chickens pecking at the ground and some dogs lying around.
“Where’s Sam?”
The only answer came from one of the dogs. It lay on its back with its head turned toward me. When it lifted its head I saw that its eyes were still closed and it didn’t appear to be waking up.
It had been in here all night. The house had been empty for hours when we went upstairs. My dad had gone out into the yard before sunset. That’s when we heard him yell. He sounded like he’d been running hard.
