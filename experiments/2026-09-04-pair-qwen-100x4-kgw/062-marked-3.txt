When the wind finally died down, we climbed up the side of the mountainside. We followed the river and saw a temple on top of a hill with a small statue of Buddha. In the center of it was a large stone bowl where people poured water onto their faces to cleanse them before offering offerings to Buddha. It was an amazing site to behold as the sun slowly set in the horizon.
On the other side of the river was a very quiet area, surrounded by rice fields, banana trees and lots of monkeys. We decided to hike over to the other side of the mountain.
