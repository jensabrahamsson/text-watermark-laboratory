He was standing there watching the fishes. The cats eyes followed his movements and wondered what he was doing. Suddenly, out of nowhere a big fat snake slithered by, but when it got near enough for him to reach the cat's attention the cat leaped off the table and ran across the room as fast as she could. Once she had left the cat stood back up again, but she kept her eyes on the snake. She knew that she must never leave this room, so the cat stayed glued to the door until her friends came and took her away.
It was a very exciting day in the life of an aquarium fish.
