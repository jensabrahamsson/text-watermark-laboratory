As he pulled a box from under the counter, she stepped forward. "What can I do for you?"
"I need some fish for a party."
He looked around at the shelves and then returned to his shopping bag. He said nothing.
"What kind are they?" She asked, trying to sound interested.
"Any good, you say?"
She nodded and followed him into the back room where he filled the shopping bags with fish in varying sizes and colors. When the boxes were full, she waited outside while he took them down.
When he was gone, she closed the door. The room felt warmer, more alive than it had before.
