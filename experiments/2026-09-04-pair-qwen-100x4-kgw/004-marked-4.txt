It was a small one, not much bigger than a coin, but it seemed to be carved with some kind of message. Rain looked at it for a while, but there were too many characters for him to read what was written. The writing was very strange; he didn't recognize any of them either. He picked up the coin and felt something hard against his palm as he took it from the table. The feeling wasn't uncomfortable or painful.
