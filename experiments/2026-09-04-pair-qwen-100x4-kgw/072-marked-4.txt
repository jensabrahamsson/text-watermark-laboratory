An Underpass in Toronto has become the first place in North America to be named "Transgender Park." The city, along with TransLife, created the 382 square meter mural last year on the bridge over Highway 401 that connects the downtown to the airport. Since then it's been repeatedly vandalized and defaced. It's now back up again, but not with its original design.
"Where are you going to park? Where can we leave our stuff?" These questions are all part of what is being referred to as a "Transgender Park" after the recent unveiling of a new underpass mural in Toronto
