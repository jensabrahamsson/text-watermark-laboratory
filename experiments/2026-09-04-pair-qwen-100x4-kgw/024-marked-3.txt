She looked up from her reading at the sight. Her mind did a double take, then she sighed and smiled, and turned the page.
Her breath caught in her throat. In front of her were three pictures, each taken on Christmas Day over thirty years ago. A little girl, her eyes wide and shining with excitement, her tiny hands reaching out for an armful of gifts, her hair falling down to form a halo around her head. Her parents, smiling, holding their daughter, proud and delighted by her joy.
It was her first Christmas away from home.
