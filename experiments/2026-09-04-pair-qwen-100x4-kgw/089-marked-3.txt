"Sorry, I can't do that," he said to the little girl.
"But it's not just a fence for you," she said. "You are also a big boy."
"No, but thanks." He was getting frustrated with this kid.
"It'll be fine if you let me help."
"Oh, really?" he asked. He started to believe her.
She walked up behind him and took his hands in hers. She looked up at him.
"Is that okay? Do you want me to hold your hands?"
"Hmm..." he said, looking down again, but still holding out his hands for her.
"I think so."
