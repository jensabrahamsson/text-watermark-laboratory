It seemed to be on the verge of breaking, but there was nothing else to do. I turned around to leave.
“Please,” she said, her eyes full of tears. “Just let me stay for one more day. Please!”
I didn’t know how to react to that request, so I went back to her.
When she noticed my face had changed, her eyes grew large and scared. She asked me what was wrong.
“You’ll be fine,” I reassured her, thinking I could use the excuse that I’d just been feeling a bit irritable lately.
But she kept asking until I agreed to stay with her
