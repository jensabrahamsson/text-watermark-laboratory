At the forks in the road, there were large trees to protect you from rain. There are also benches for you to sit and enjoy a coffee or hot chocolate. We recommend that you wear walking shoes as this is not suitable for hiking boots.
You can park your car here if you have a long walk ahead of you.
There is no public transport that goes directly to the start point of this walk. If you live close by, you could take a taxi or hire a minibus. Alternatively, you can use the bus 125 and get off in the nearby town centre of Bregenz.
