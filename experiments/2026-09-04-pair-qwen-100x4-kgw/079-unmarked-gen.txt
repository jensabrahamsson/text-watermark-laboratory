It was the stuff of legends: the severed head of a young woman found in a field, her body thrown over it and her arms twisted around its neck. No one saw or heard anything about it at first — until two men turned up dead on the same day. There were no leads; there had been no murder, they claimed, but something very sinister happened on that day — something so horrible that it changed the lives of everyone involved.
The mystery has now gone cold — except for the hair in the pile.
