The bakery's manager said in an interview, "We were supposed to do a food review for a television station. And then we realized that there was a problem with this second tray of doughnuts."
It all started when the chef made the dough for two trays of doughnuts - one plain vanilla and another chocolate chip. The chocolate chip doughnut proved well enough after baking, but the vanilla didn't rise like the other tray did.
When the chef removed them from the oven, he noticed something peculiar about the first tray of vanilla doughnuts - their tops were too pale and brown
