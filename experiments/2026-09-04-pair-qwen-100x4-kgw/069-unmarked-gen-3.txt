In 1826, she had her first child; in 1840, her second.
In 1845, she was sent to St. Louis for work as a governess.
By the late 1840s, she was living at the home of Henry Sibley, the brother of President Zachary Taylor. She lived there until about 1852, when Taylor became president, and she moved into his new home, Bolling House.
Her son died in 1853, shortly before he would have been three years old.
