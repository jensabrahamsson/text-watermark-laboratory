One day, the pastor came in and told me to go into the church and make sure there were no other keys. It was after a Sunday service where he said, "You know, I think we need another key for the door." So I went inside the church and looked all around but didn't find any other keys, and it was like three hours later when I realized I had overlooked the vestry key right under the old brick! We don't even have a sign or anything. How could I have not noticed? The only thing you can say is God works through little things! Thank you Lord!
