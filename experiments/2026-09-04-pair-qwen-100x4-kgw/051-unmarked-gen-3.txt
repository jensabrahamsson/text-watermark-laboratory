I went to work at 5:30 pm. I left at 12 midnight, 12 hours after my shift started.
What was the time of her shift?
Answer Choices: A) 9 am
B) 11 am
C) 7pm
D) 6 pm

The nurse worked for 12 hours from 5:30 PM to 12 Midnight.

Converting 12 hours into minutes gives us \(12 \times 60 = 720\) minutes.

Subtracting the working hours from 24 hours gives us the remaining time
