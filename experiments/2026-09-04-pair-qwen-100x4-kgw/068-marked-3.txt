The dairy had 3176 bottles of milk, some were filled on Tuesday. It still has 2598 bottles of milk in storage, but not any that have been previously filled. How many bottles of milk were filled on Tuesday?
Let's denote the number of bottles filled on Tuesday as \( x \).

The total number of bottles of milk in storage at the end is the sum of those filled on Tuesday (which we'll call \( x \)) and those which are already there (not to be confused with the filled ones from Tuesday), so it would be \( x + 2598 \).
