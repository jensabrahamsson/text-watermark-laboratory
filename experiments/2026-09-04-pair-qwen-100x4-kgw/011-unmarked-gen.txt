The waitress is a character in the popular animated television series "South Park." In this episode, titled "Crap Like Chicken," the main characters, including Stan Marsh and Kyle Broflovski, discover that their food has become contaminated with mold after they argue. The waitress tells them to return the dishes if it makes them feel better, which results in her collecting cups from the table and giving them to a customer named Mr. Brinkley. However, when she sees how the customers are treating the plates of chicken, she decides to take one herself as a symbol of her disgust. The episode satirizes the American obsession with cleanliness
