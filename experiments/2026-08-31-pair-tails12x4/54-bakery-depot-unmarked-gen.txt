We have been following the train between St. Bernard and Mares because we don't want to pass the police stations because the passengers seem to be afraid to stop. After getting out they make an early lunch as we are all alone at least twenty minutes. No one asks us questions about our trip. Every time I walk around the bus I feel more relaxed.
