I left to go shopping in London, and the landlord told me he saw a man with an old blue shirt who went to her house and asked the landlord if he could find the keys to her house.

There was a picture of my grandmother. She had been gone for two hours now. I have no idea. As we are walking, we hear a woman's voice saying, "Come here and listen."
