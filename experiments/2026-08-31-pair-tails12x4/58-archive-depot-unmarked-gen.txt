I see my wife and I in a small town named Flanders, in eastern France. The man with the silver teeth is not there. I drive down the winding road and am greeted by a group of elderly men and elderly women – the kind that walk up to the supermarket and drop off the packages they take without ever looking the way I want them to look. It is the second most beautiful town in France where I drive, but I have not seen it in half a decade, and have only seen it once. It is my second visit.
