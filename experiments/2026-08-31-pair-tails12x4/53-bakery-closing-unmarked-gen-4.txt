The train slows to a stop a few blocks from here. At about two am the bus takes off. I look inside and see a young woman riding down the road with the black dress on her head. It is the first person of my class in five years. My eyes follow her across the city; the crowds of young people from all walks, and they stop, but there are none of them here. I take a seat back.
