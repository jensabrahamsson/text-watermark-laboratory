Back in the bakery, in the streetlight, I wait for news. The sun comes with the forecasting of autumn and we see the big wheat fields.

The bakery is closed. We follow a few hundred yards in front of the street. The bakery's owner is not happy and says that the streetlights are going out for two days and two nights (the usual five and twenty-five days on that basis).
