The bus enters my house at 2 A.M., and I drive in the direction of the bus. There are four doors. I am a good enough driver to pass any one of them, but every single one is shut. I don't like the way the driver sits. In my head the only thing the door open, though, is a note under the driver's seat. My hand falls on my right arm, and the red hand slips through. I look at the door, but don't close it. The last sign I can turn the light on is in the front door.
