The typewriter is a strange, strange beast. It has a hole in the back, and there's a metal hand held by a handle that looks like a hammer. Each time I used to get lost in the middle of the stacks of boxes, when they would run out of paper, I was so distracted that I couldn't write down the last four digits. My typewriter had this peculiar, vaguely familiar, and oddly mechanical function. I can't believe the thing is that easy to do. (And that typewriter actually works.)
