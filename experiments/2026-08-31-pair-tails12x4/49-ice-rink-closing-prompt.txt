The municipal rink keeps Thursday mornings for schools. Skates rumble over the patched ice. A caretaker shovels the snow they make themselves into a bin by the fire exit. Closing time is announced twice. The second time people actually stand. I put the folder back in the wrong place and then in the right one. Outside the rain has thinned. The stone steps are dark and the buses throw yellow light across them. I do not have the canal. I have an hour I did not plan to spend, which is close enough.
