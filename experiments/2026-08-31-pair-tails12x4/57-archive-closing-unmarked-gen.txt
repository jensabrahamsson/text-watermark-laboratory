I am a teenager as well as a writer. I was born in a town a day away from the border. At sixteen years old, I was going to an independent music school in the south-eastern city of Puyyama. I was on the verge of a trip to Japan, but I had never travelled overseas until I was about 11. I was not planning to be an independent music-publisher. The main road was not the bus stop I hoped to go to, but a flat. My parents did not have sufficient money, no car and I never had access to a bank.
