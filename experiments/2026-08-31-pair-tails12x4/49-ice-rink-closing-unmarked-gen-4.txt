I walk the streets of downtown in all my youth, as a way of keeping the city safe. And then I leave the streets for several hours while I sit in my cell. When I'm done, I head to the grocery store to order breakfast. In the past, the store is the place I got a good deal. It is open from 10 p.m. until 3 a.m., when I get back to the grocery store for lunch. There is one of a kind grocery store and all the shelves are covered with cards. "I got some candy and cheese," I say.
