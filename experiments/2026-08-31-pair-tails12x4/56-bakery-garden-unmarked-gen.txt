As a kid, I played chess, had good friends in the family. Now, it is only for a few years. My dad died when I was ten and my mother is eighty. My grandfather worked as a miner and spent a great deal of time in the miners' trade. She died when I was twenty.

Our home belongs to an anonymous man, who lived his life like a brother for many years. After a brief time in a mining town, he went into banking to study at a small bank and has been ever since. His house is a bit bigger than mine.
