The car is slow and the car is slow so it slows down. I put my back hand around the side of the car and push the accelerator. It stops. All around the clock the car whips through the darkness and starts to come to life. The lights are dim, but they are still there. There are two big lights, one on the side where the car came out from and one on the side which it started on. The last light comes into range. The other two lights are on again, and it seems their work of slowing is starting to seem like one of a kind—one which is making the car stop
