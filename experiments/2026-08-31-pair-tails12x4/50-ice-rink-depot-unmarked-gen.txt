At 7 o'clock we are going to get down to the building. In the office at 2 o'clock I stand on the floor, with nothing to do but walk past. I am not sure I shall ever leave.

I am sure it is one of the most crowded places in town I have ever seen. The doors are locked on the side of a building with the windows locked, but those who are brave enough are able to get into there by the time they leave the building.
