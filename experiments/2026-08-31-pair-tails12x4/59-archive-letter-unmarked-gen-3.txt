I don't know anything about the writing, except that the typewriter had never run out of batteries and was on and on about 12-15 seconds before it died. I didn't get to get my typewriter.

The first typewriter I worked as was called a Wasser. By the time Wasser came into the picture, I was not too well acquainted to old Wasser machines. A friend once told me that Wasser machines had been invented by German writers that were very late in their careers, and were too old to survive the printing presses. I never took them off.
