At three in the morning the bakery on Cross Street is only ovens and a radio. The first tray of rolls comes out before the streetlights dim. Flour sits in the grooves of the wooden bench like a map of last night. Closing time is announced twice. The second time people actually stand. I put the folder back in the wrong place and then in the right one. Outside the rain has thinned. The stone steps are dark and the buses throw yellow light across them. I do not have the canal. I have an hour I did not plan to spend, which is close enough.
