There the typewriter was once a typewriter; there it was. It was a good writing device. It was good because it held out a single purpose. It was not a very useful writing device because it was a paper device. It contained a single purpose, a key. It was useful because it worked. It also worked with very little modification, because of the little change in the way the printer was handled, the way it operated and the fact that it could be used for so many purposes. It is a big problem and one that needs to be looked at.
