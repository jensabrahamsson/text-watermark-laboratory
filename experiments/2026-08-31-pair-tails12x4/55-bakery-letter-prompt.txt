At three in the morning the bakery on Cross Street is only ovens and a radio. The first tray of rolls comes out before the streetlights dim. Flour sits in the grooves of the wooden bench like a map of last night. I wrote the letter twice. The first version was honest and unkind. The second was kind and slightly false. I posted the second and kept the first in a drawer with rubber bands and a key that fits nothing in this flat.
