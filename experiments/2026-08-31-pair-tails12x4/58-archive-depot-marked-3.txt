The bus stops just behind the railway station about thirty metres from where the original car is. When I look up on it I see a red bus. It comes off the road. The old bus has stopped before me in a ditch and all over and it has a yellow sun badge sticking out of the door at six. That is no bus at all. That reminds me of the old buses I remember, the ones the man sitting in came from, who rode at least twenty-four hours a day at night. He had about twenty-five hours a week riding an electric scooter and driving his scooter the rest of the time
