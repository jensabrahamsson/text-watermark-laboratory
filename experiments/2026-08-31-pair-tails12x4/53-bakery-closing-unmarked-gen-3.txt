One of the last things that takes us through the streets of my city is being reminded by people that there is going to be a revolution of the past. The road is being cut but the road does not close. The streetlights don't glow, the road doesn't fade, and people don't even have to wait to get on them either. Our streets will be closed to traffic. The streets become more crowded once the roads are done and this will not be possible until there is revolution.
