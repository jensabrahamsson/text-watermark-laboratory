The bus rides have moved in over the past couple decades and the place is not that different as far as the weather goes. There are many bus lines that can come and go as the day goes on, and every time a bus passes, she is left for a few hours in the park where they sit with no hope of stopping. In one of the most beautiful places I have ever seen, there are many bus lines. There are plenty of people lined up for the walk, waiting for our next stops. I have been there twice; one in the spring and another in winter.
