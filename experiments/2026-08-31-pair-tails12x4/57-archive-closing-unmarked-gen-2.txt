Back in the kitchen, our little boy looks up from his pencil. "That's good to see you all, my boy."

I give the boy food, and he eats it. He is about six-feet-three, about twenty or thirty pounds. The day after, before dinner, I went upstairs with my boy and saw that he was so good in his school, the kids' tutoring was in every lesson.
