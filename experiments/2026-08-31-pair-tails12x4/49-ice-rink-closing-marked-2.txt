After about six-and-a-half hours, the lights go out again. The children do not notice. When school comes, most of the kids are done. There are no children in the street. The kids walk into a classroom, get in line and ask questions. There is an odd silence, like something strange or unexpected. A teacher tries to talk them out of it. The door closes at eleven. The school comes in around 12.
