The county archive smells of brown paper and wet wool. A clerk wheels a trolley of boxes labelled 1974 to 1976. The reading lamps have a switch that clicks too loudly. Closing time is announced twice. The second time people actually stand. I put the folder back in the wrong place and then in the right one. Outside the rain has thinned. The stone steps are dark and the buses throw yellow light across them. I do not have the canal. I have an hour I did not plan to spend, which is close enough.
