
The paper drifted off the top of the paper and stuck the edge on the bottom. Then it turned, with a slight change, a slight change, a gentle turn.
It bounced around for quite a while then the paper faded into black in the form of a sticky ball.
It stuck in the backside of the paper as the paper was moved around.
The paper had been placed in an aluminum foil by a local friend, who would later call his mother.
I never noticed this until I read the book with the help of three friends who had taken over from my work, who had become friends of mine.
