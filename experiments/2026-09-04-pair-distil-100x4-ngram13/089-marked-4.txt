
The house that had originally been rented to Hargrove, who had lived near the main city block of South Lodi for six months when he was six, was a single-family white, two-story white, two-and-a-half-foot-tall, 10-foot-high building in the late 1970s. It had a black leather flooring and black glass interior with a brick exterior and metal roof of over 1,500 square feet with a window and a fire protection system. A wood-burning stove had been installed with a fire-control system and a fire alarm.
