
"You should keep doing it with these stamps," a clerk once said.
"They're so important."
The clerk turned to tell him the stamps had been counted. He said they were not "a small stamp."
With the stamps, they disappeared in a long black hole in the bottom of the box.
"It really didn't even happen to us," he said.
The clerk then left the box behind and walked off.
"I was like, 'No, these are all for our stamps,' " he said.
After that, he had to be arrested and put in the police station.
