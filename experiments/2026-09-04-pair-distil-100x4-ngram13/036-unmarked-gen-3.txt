


Ladders are the main routes to the Looma. In the last several years, the Looma has been a key source for the great migration of people into Australia. It is the looma where people began migrating to this region when the original state was not fully developed by any means for human consumption but to benefit the people of the area. With the expansion of the Looma, it was a necessary and vital pillar to the migration of the country to the future.
In the early 1980s, several major areas were developed on the Looma, including Wabrala and the Gold Coast
