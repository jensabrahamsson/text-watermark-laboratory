
The bus sat quietly down in the air and stopped at one point as the bus pulled past a bus. Before I could get in my car, I noticed one man coming behind me.
In the middle of the alley, an older man emerged from the street. Suddenly, he pointed the hood of his car up, followed me to the middle of the alley, then, before, again, the driver. Suddenly, this man was holding a firearm. I pulled my gun and opened it and pointed it at the man. The man ran and I followed him away again.
