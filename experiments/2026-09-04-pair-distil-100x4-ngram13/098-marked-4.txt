"I have a long history of problems with this area of my district," Mr. Bautah said. "The road has been blocked for 10 years by heavy rains. So when the water goes out, the road breaks."
If it does not reach the canal, the village might ask for permission to build another bridge that will help.
According to the local municipal elections committee, the village has registered 1.7 million people.
