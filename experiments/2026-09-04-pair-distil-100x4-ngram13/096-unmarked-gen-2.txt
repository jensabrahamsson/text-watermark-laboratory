

This morning a small group of men dressed as villagers had carried out a firework raid on the village. The villagers gathered outside, and were told it was a peaceful protest.
The first firework has gone into the village, and several more were found to have been burnt.
The villagers were not only angry but also furious at the police.
The town was set to have a public prayer vigil, and in turn the police also broke through into a rioting camp.
