The closure of the rink was due to a lack of budget.
As a result, the rink is now closed to public use for business.
"We can't really sell ice cream until they sell ice cream," Councillor Mike Hutton said Monday afternoon.
The rink currently costs more than $700,000.
"We know that we need a lot of parking on the ice," he said.
