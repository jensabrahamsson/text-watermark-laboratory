She stood at the bottom, looking out into the street. It was raining again, her eyes scanning the crowd of passers-by below.
It’s so much easier to be normal when it’s raining than during sunny days. She could always tell by their gazes – how many people were hiding something behind that rain? How many were just pretending everything was okay?
But it was hard to be normal sometimes. Like now.
