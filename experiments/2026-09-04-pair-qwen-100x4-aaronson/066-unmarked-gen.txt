As a child, I remember being drawn to the old church in our small town. The sanctuary was filled with an ancient and holy feeling of mystery. The simple design and structure felt like a place where the divine could dwell among us. As a little girl, I felt as if I were part of something bigger than myself. This connection left a lasting impression on me and has influenced my desire for a more profound experience with nature and spirituality.
