The light shone like a silver star in the night sky. The streetlamps were the only thing that had changed from what she remembered as a child, when it was always dark before dawn and everything seemed to move at an accelerated pace in daylight.
She sat down on her old wooden stool by the corner of the building. It hadn't been here for much longer than it should have been since it was taken away by the city's architects who thought that they knew better about architecture and its history than any local resident could possibly know.
