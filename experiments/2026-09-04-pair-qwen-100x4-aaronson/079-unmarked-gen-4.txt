It was just hair, but I recognized it. My father’s hair. I don’t think I’ll ever forget seeing it in the pile of hair on his nightstand at the funeral home. He had been dead for three years.
The first time my mother saw her son’s head again after he died, she broke down and cried so hard she couldn’t even hold back tears. She knew he would always be there with her, and now she knew that it wouldn’t be him any longer.
