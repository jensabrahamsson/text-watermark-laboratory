They were out of the most popular issues, which he hadn’t ordered. He’d been told it was because his order would arrive too late and he wouldn’t get a refund from the supplier.
There wasn’t much to choose from in the stacks on display. A few old copies of ‘Men’s Health’ magazine – which everyone said they hated reading, but enjoyed flipping through. Some issue of ‘Cosmopolitan’ with a cover model who seemed to be every woman’s fantasy. And some copies of the old ‘TV Guide’, which he used to buy weekly back when he had cable TV.
