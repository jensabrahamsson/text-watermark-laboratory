As a small girl, I spent a lot of time in elevators. My mother was an elevator operator for the city’s old, giant escalator. She worked the stairs at 14th and Market Sts., near Union Station, on the way to work. And then there were the days she would take me with her when she had to go downtown, or to some other part of the city where she had to be seen.
