At last, the coal stopped tumbling. He opened the door to clear it… and a pile of coals flew out behind him. They were big and heavy, landing with resounding impact in all directions. Most of them landed on his skin – there was nothing between their heated surfaces, just a thin layer of fat and muscle over the top of his skin like that of an oven. Most of them burned through him almost instantly, but one landed on his eye-socket quite harmlessly, melting and dripping into his visage. All the others went straight past.
