The camera's lens focused on a window pane that reflected the world outside. The shutters were an open invitation to look in.
But the shutter had been pushed closed for more than two decades. It was not an invite, just a barrier. A fence.
As tourists pass through the gate of the park in the background, the shutter reflects their gaze and stops it dead.
They cannot enter the park. They can only admire its beauty from the outside.
If I could choose one word to describe the experience at the park entrance, it would be "impossible." It is impossible to enter a place without paying a fee or getting a ticket
