When he was done, he realized that he had not done it right.
The shadow cast by his head did not match up with the shadow that he expected. He did not look right at all.
He quickly tried to make corrections as fast as he could think of them. But he could not get himself out of this situation.
So what are you doing? he thought, looking at the reflection again.
He remembered an old saying. A man should not take things for granted, even when they look so good in the mirror or on paper.
He decided that he would have to change something and go through with a new attempt.
