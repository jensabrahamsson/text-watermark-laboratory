At night we heard it. The distant hum of its engine, the low sound of its wheels on the gravel road and the occasional loud rattle as it ground to a grinding halt. All this was enough to keep us company through most of the winter and when the snow came, the truck had become more like an oven with all those windows open while the others went out hunting for snowdeer or other game.
