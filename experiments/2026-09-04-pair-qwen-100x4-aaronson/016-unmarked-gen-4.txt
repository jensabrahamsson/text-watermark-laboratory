As it began to rain, I ran out of the house to find shelter from the rain and stay dry. I was wearing only a T-shirt so my feet were freezing cold. My jacket kept me warm for a while but then started getting wet too.
By the time I got back into the house, I noticed that there was no sign of the other girl, the one who was also trying to keep cool in the rain. She must have gotten sick from being outside too long. But why didn't she just stay at home with me?
When she finally came inside, we both changed into dry clothes, and she hugged me tight.
