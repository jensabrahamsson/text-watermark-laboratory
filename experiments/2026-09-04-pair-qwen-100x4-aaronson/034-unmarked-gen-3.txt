A cold front had swept through the valley. The grass and trees were still wet with dew. The sun had not yet risen; only the pale blue of the horizon hinted at it’s coming up, slowly and in stages.
In the small village, the church was the heart of community. Many came to celebrate Mass on Sundays and holidays and to say prayers and offer sacrifices before the Blessed Sacrament. On Monday evenings, many went for social club meetings or church gatherings or youth group.
