At least once when I was walking on the shoulder of the highway, this little bench looked down from my path at me with that painted face. It gave a feeling of permanence where you just kind of sit there and stare at it…for eternity is all really as it is. All the while the sun played with shadows over the bridge, the bridge itself shimmering in its different colored light and cast the bridge onto the shoulder of the highway on which I would walk from time to time.
I could see up into Lake Michigan here too; I would look far out across the lake from my bench, watching every sail of boats pass by
