A man in the stall said, "Hello! What do you think I'm doing here?"
"Well, you're not eating anything," I said. "Why did you lock the stall?"
"Because this is where the fisherman locks his net."
"That makes sense because fish can be quite finicky creatures when it comes to ice cream," he replied. "They prefer to eat the same flavor they ate yesterday, so that they won't get too excited today."
I gave him my biggest smile ever and walked away. A week later, I returned and there was another sign on the stall. It read: Ice Cream, Not Fish.
