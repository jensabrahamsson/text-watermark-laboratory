The cat, a 30-year-old male Siamese, was watching a pair of two-inch-long rainbow trout swim around in a glass tank at the fishmonger's shop.
"Fish are really fascinating animals," he said. "I'd love to go out fishing."
But there's no catching a fish here. The aquariums on display feature only the most common species like swordfish and tuna.
"I want to see something that is actually living," he said. "It would be nice if there were live animals in there. You don't have to get up close with them to see what they're doing."
