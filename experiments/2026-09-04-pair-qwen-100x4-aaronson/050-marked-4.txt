At last, this weekend’s work involved installing doors. Allowing light and air flow will make that place a much more pleasant environment for keeping your tools inside in all weather conditions.
I started by drilling holes with a drill to hold the hinges securely.
After holding the hinges securely with screws, I placed them into the holes with a hammer strike.
Finally, the two sections of wood came together at each end; I tapped it gently with a mallet across each joint so that they held where you expected them to hold—firmly but not hard!
