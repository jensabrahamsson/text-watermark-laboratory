It was not long before she noticed a man walk up to her, put his hand in his pocket, take out some change, then gave it back to her. "How much did you owe?" he asked her.
The woman replied: "Nothing." She was really poor.
"Then why are you still sitting here?"
"I don't know," she said. "I just feel sorry for myself."
And that's the way most people will look at their lives: they think nothing bad has ever happened to them or could happen to them. They sit there feeling sorry for themselves.
