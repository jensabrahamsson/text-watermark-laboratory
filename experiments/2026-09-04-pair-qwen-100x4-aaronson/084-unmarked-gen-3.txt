This is from the 80's, when everyone ate raw chicken in their lunch. I'm still not sure how my mother managed to eat this soup. She loved to cook.
So this recipe makes a whole 2 cups of soup. I used about 1/4 cup of each vegetable that I like so that I could use them up. Feel free to make it with as many or as few vegetables as you'd like. It was really good.
