It would have been a strange sight if the cricket pavilion had been a moving target. Its top, covered with netting and glass to shield spectators from rain or sunlight, was not moved as part of its structure. It is also not possible for a person to jump up on it while running out. As such, there would be no way for a bowler to bowl into it. Even the ground under it would be level.
This means that, even though there are many other ways to move a cricket field than simply by stepping on it (such as using cranes), the cricket pavilion itself has never been moved around before now
