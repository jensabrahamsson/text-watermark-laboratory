There were two directions to go. Both points of interest had been marked by arrows. The fork itself is not visible from any direction. It has become a landmark after the arrows. You can easily see it, even if you are only 50 meters away. From there it is a 3 minute walk to each of the points of interest. I didn't actually notice them until now! There is also a sign marking one way to a parking lot for the visitors center.
I took my time walking along this part. When the sun started setting and the sky turned dark I was very happy to be able to finish this trail on time
