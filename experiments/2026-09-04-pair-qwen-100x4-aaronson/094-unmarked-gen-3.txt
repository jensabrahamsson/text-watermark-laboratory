The cricket pavilion, once considered the heart of a sports ground in many countries around the world, is no longer an integral part of any cricket grounds. Cricket is known as "the gentleman's game" and it has been the sport of choice for most British royals. Today, however, the sport is played all over the globe by teams of both nations. There are two types of cricket; first-class cricket which consists of 50 overs per innings while Test cricket is limited to 120 overs per innings.
