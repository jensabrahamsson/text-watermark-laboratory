The mother asked her child to trace the frost in the window and then she noticed that a small hole had appeared.
So, what does the hole say?
The child wrote down: "My mom is a witch."
The child's words were written on the window. That is why you see a hole!
Why did you choose this poem for my child?
This short and simple story has become an international children's classic because it uses a simple, direct language, so all children can understand and enjoy it.
The poem is not only about children tracing frost in the window but also about their innocence and curiosity.
It will encourage children to read poetry
