In 2016, a series of storms hit the Pacific Northwest. The damage was devastating and extensive, especially in the city of Vancouver where they suffered over $4 billion dollars in total loss due to severe flooding and windstorm.
A year later, a group called "Vancouver Restoration" formed with a mission to build homes for people who lost theirs in the storm. In the wake of this devastation, the local community responded by donating money and volunteering their time, but there were still many families who needed assistance in order to start rebuilding their lives. That's when a group of local business owners and entrepreneurs stepped up to offer help
