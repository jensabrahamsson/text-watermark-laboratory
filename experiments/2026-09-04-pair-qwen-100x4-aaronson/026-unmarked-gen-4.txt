We were to run across the field, where we would line up in rows of six, facing one another. Then, with our eyes on the ground and hands by our sides, we’d begin running in place.
After what seemed like a century, we’d all stop and put our hands together in prayer, as if we had just run miles and needed a breath. I watched my classmate’s face. It was a mix of awe, amazement, shock, and embarrassment, all at once.
