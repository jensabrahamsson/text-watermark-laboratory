When I first walked through the night market in Taipei, it felt like a magical world, a place where you could take photos without the pressure of a crowded crowd. It was one of those places where people would happily give up their time to have your photo taken for free – a true sign of how warm and welcoming this city is.
There were stalls selling everything from snacks to souvenirs, each offering something different that made it easy to get lost in the labyrinth-like streets. The lanterns hung in the air, casting eerie shadows on the floor as you walked along them.
