The man who has been feeding the birds in a park near the centre of the city for years has been arrested after the death of one of them, the BBC reported.
Police say the 75-year-old was arrested at his home on Thursday and is due before a magistrates' court next week. His motive for allegedly doing so remains unclear but it is thought he had not always lived nearby and may have only started feeding the birds there recently.
Police say that the bird died as a result of a broken beak when it was found by another person.
