The cows have been getting milked today. I went over to see what was going on, as they had told me not to come in the afternoon until after the last bottle had been taken. The last bottle is a green one, that they say represents yesterday's milk.
As it turned out, there were only 5 of them that had left with their bottles. I could not see the others behind the gate, but I think that some of them stayed because they are sick or not ready for milking yet.
