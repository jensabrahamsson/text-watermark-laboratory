I needed a new door for the shed so I decided to make it. It was fun to work with wood, but even more fun to watch it all come together. This project taught me about the benefits of using glue rather than nails and also showed me that a good carpenter can use just about any kind of wood you give them! I would highly recommend using a saw and possibly a chisel if working with rough woods.
