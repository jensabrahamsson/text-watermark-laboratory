It is very useful to have a small shed or barn to keep tools, supplies, seeds, and other materials handy. It provides storage space for equipment like shovels, rakes, mowers, hoes, trowels, gloves, etc., in addition to storing supplies like fertilizer, seeds, mulch, plant pots, etc. Many people use their sheds as an outdoor workspace for gardening. The structure can also serve as a place to store lawn furniture, tools that don’t fit inside of your home or garage, spare keys, books and papers, and other miscellaneous items you would like out of sight but not in reach
