But there was no one sitting around, just a few trees that didn't seem to grow.
They were the kind of trees that never bore fruit, or even leaves. The ones you'd see in the city park, where they were used for shade or just as decorations on the street, the kind you see all over.
The smokehouse door had an old handle on it, which looked rusty from age. It was old enough to have been made long before any of these trees were ever planted. I could see someone having to walk up a hill with it, but I couldn't imagine who would need that many steps.
