At last one morning it was moving again, this time in its usual direction. All the paddle strokes were quick and jerky as it moved, but it made up for speed by strength of its will. The tide carried it to a place far from where you thought there should be any land at such a low tide. You thought there should have been some water in its usual channel when you looked down from the bridge onto the low-lying ground to your left. But nothing showed through the mud and grass and undergrowth on either side of its usual path, which would be a normal part of its yearly voyage.
