It was only the first of the day, but already my legs felt heavy and I felt like my feet had been pricked with thorns.
I looked down at the shoes on my feet and wondered why I hadn’t worn sandals. But they were still wet from last night’s walk through the rain.
When the porter pulled the door open for me he seemed to feel it too and gave a little start.
