At the fork, two routes led down from the top of the hill. The one to the left would be a hard climb through trees and rough terrain. The one to the right would be a much easier way via a paved road and a grassy field. Most hikers headed straight when they saw an arrow pointing up but it is worth noting that none of them actually knew which route to take!
We were in no hurry as there are other trails leading the same direction. All we had to do was choose between two equally beautiful mountains as a starting point. Neither route to our left would be any more difficult than walking on the roads above
