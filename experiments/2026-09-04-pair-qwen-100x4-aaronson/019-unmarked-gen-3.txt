The grains on the floor and in the corners of the barn were everywhere. The pigeons would fly into these little pockets, but it was hard to get them to stay still long enough for their owner to count the number.
He knew that his wife had put out some extra bread because he had just seen the birds gathering around her feeders, especially the blue ones. He had been planning to tell her about this when she got home from work, but now that he was at the barn with the pigeons, he didn’t have much time to plan.
