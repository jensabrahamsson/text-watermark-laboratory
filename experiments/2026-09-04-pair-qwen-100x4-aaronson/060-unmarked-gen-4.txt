He took it to mean they were under pressure. His crew had been warned and knew what was coming.
But they did not understand the depth of the water in the marsh, or how much time was left.
As he approached his boat, he saw two swans on the edge of the water.
He could see their necks bobbing up and down as they paddled in a circle through the marsh. Their legs paddled with power; but their feet never touched the ground.
They were not struggling against the current. They were floating in the current.
When they reached the other side of the marsh, they were still going around.
