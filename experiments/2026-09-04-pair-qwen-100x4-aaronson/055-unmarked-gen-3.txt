In its absence, the road had become an open space for children to play. The cars looked as small as ant hills and the grass grew taller than my shoes.
I was not quite sure of how to behave on this quiet day.
As I walked around to find out more about the car park, I found myself standing next to a statue of a little girl who had been playing with a kite in a grass field. She was holding the end of the string which she pulled gently across her face so it would flutter back down.
