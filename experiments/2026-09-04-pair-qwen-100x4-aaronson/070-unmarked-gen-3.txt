I looked down at the water and felt the cold.
A small boy sat on a rock, holding a piece of driftwood.
There was a girl on shore; her hair shone in the sunlight.
I heard the sounds of the wind blowing through the trees.
I wondered if I should leave my car keys in my pocket or take them out. I wondered if I should open the trunk to find the suncream or the sunglasses.
In that moment I was grateful for what I had - the sun, the sea, this place, this little girl. But also sad because it was not enough and I had lost sight of something.
