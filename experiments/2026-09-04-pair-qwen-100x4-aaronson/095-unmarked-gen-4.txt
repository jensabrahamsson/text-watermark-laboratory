As an automotive enthusiast, I was always curious about how engines work. So when my mechanic friend mentioned that a rattling noise coming from his engine could be a sign of something serious, it piqued my interest.

I decided to take a closer look and discovered that the sound came from the camshaft, which is responsible for turning the crankshaft. When the camshaft rubs against the connecting rod, it makes a grinding or clicking noise, much like the sound of a rattling object hitting another.
