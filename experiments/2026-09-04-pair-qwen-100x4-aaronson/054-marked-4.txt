At last, the printer jammed. All the others had worked perfectly through most of its run without trouble at all; but it was only halfway round that there began an odd kind of “juddering” noise. This noise grew steadily louder until, finally, the thing burst open with a puffed spew of brown paper and bits of what resembled a piece of rubber inside. All this occurred in something like thirty seconds.
