He was trying to clear out his stocks and had run out of room.
A few weeks ago he put the rest of the magazines in a corner of the shop, but they never came back. He could tell they were not wanted by anyone because the only people who bought them were the regulars. The new customers usually brought their own newspapers. The man also sold sweets, but it wasn’t selling as well lately either.
The store was located near a railway station and most of its customers worked there, so they preferred reading the latest paper on the train instead of staying at home.
