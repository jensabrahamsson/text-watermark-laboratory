At eight o'clock, the hour bell rang, but it was not at three seconds behind. All the clocks had stopped during that time and so they all reported being an hour earlier than they really were.
At eight twenty, the hour bell rang, this time one minute ahead of its usual rate.
At eight twenty five, the hour bell rang, this time one and a half minutes ahead of its usual rate.
At eight thirty, the hour bell rang, this time two minutes ahead of its usual rate.
At eight thirty five, the hour bell rang, this time three minutes ahead of its usual rate.
