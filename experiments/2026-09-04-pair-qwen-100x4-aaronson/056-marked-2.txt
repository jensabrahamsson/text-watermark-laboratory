At least once a morning it was enough to disturb anyone’s sleep. The sound always sounded strange after a hard night of drinking, but it never seemed to affect any of its residents. Most guests preferred the creaking rather than walking on polished concrete floors. They enjoyed it; they felt as though they were really part of its history.
At last one morning it made the boarders uneasy, and so with all those present, the headboard called out: "Anyone down from that stage in there? I can’t hear when you walk upstairs."
