A. "I'm sorry, but I left a pin inside my shirt."
B. "I'm sorry, but I left a pin on the inside of my shirt."
C. "I'm sorry, but I left a pin inside my sweater."
D. "I'm sorry, but I left a pin in the pocket of my pants."
答案:

A

A. 所有项目
B. 工程规模小，且对周边环境的影响小
