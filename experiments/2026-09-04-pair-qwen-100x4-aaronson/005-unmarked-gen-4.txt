That scarf was the only thing that could keep her warm when it rained outside, even in the coldest winter.
She remembered she had already bought one for the next time. It wasn't a matter of having enough money; it was just that the store owner wouldn't sell another one to her.
Now that spring came, they would have to buy some more.
In the end, what she had thought as "enough" for herself became someone else's need.
