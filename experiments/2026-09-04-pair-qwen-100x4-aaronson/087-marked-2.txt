At last, he spotted three men huddled together at one corner: two young ladies and a man. All of them were heavily covered in ashes.
At first glance, this looked like another random case of arson damage to a place. However, watching their actions for a while, the watchman noticed something different-—they didn’t act in fear! When they saw him enter, the man threw down what resembled a piece of paper on the ground; as it landed, the torch’s flames lit up and illuminated it… As for what exactly was written on said document, the torch’s dim light provided no clear view.
