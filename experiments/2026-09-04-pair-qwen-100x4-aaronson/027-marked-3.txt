At last, she stood to speak with the man who paid for her electricity with coin.
“I wonder sometimes what’s behind that arrow,” she said.
“I wonder sometimes what’s behind that arrow,” he answered. “Maybe it doesn’t care.”
He was right. All she needed to do was turn on the television.
