When I arrived, it was just as I remembered. The water flowed through a hole in the side of the building and into a tank on the far end. The wind was coming off of the mountains nearby and blew sand over the surface of the water. I could smell the damp earth from my feet where they were wet. The only sound was the water moving and the occasional chirping of a bird or the rustling of leaves on the trees nearby.
