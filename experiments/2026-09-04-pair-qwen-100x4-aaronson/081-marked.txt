At last Thursday’s village hall meeting in Mickleover, the owner explained that they had just received notice of an urgent matter but asked people not to disturb them.
“I am having difficulties with my son in law who lives with us at this time,” said the owner. “The police were called out yesterday because my son in law was threatening me.”
He also explained that his tenant told him he had some packages at home that needed to be delivered today and so contacted the owner to arrange for a delivery person to come in during their absence.
