A long, hard winter had come to an end and spring was upon us. A soft breeze blew across the field. The first birds began their daily call. I could feel my feet on the ground and breathe in fresh air. It felt good, really good.
I turned to look at my husband who lay in bed next to me as his wife slept quietly with a smile on her face. His deep breaths were easy and calm, he was sleeping soundly. I loved him more than words could ever express.
