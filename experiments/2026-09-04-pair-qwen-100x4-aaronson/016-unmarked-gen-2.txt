It was the middle of December, and I had come to a quiet town for a holiday. The sun was gone in my eyes, but still I could see the snowflakes dancing through the clear sky.
I went to the town’s only store and bought some breads and fruit. Then I walked to the train station to catch a bus to a nearby town where there was a market that sold various goods at fair prices. That evening, I returned with an armful of vegetables from the market. It seemed like I was living on such a low income, though actually I didn’t have much food.
