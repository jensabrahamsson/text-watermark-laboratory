At last, the bridge is opened and I can view the scene from my usual vantage. All the boats have now left the bridge area. Most of them are still in moorings, but one or two look to be drifting about waiting for the bridge to clear up and their place back on the bridge again. Most of them look very tired after a hard run up stream; they all need their nets and their nets do not take to water too well. Most of them arrive in moorings, but it is worth keeping your eyes peeled, watching every boat arriving just for that reason – there usually will be a few who stay
