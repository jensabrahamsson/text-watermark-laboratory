He watched the sky change from blue to purple to gray, then to black. It was raining.
He looked at his watch; he had been counting his breaths for over an hour. They were becoming rapid.
His body felt heavy as lead. He didn't even have enough strength to stand up and take a deep breath of fresh air.
He knew the storm would pass in 15 minutes or so, but there was no telling what might happen during that time.
He knew better than to leave his baby on its own. But the house was far too big for just him, and the weather was too harsh for just one night
