It was an old, very large, white cow. He had given up trying to get it to go into the barn.
He sat there in the shade for a while, thinking about what he could do with her when she finally did show up. Then he decided he needed to make her work for his money. So he walked around the barn and made sure all the doors were open so the wind would blow away any remaining grass.
The cow finally appeared out of nowhere.
When it came over the fence and started eating the freshly cut hay, he got very excited. He ran outside with a shovel and started digging holes all around the field
