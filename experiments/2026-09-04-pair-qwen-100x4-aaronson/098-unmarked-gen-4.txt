I have written many posts about how people use their canals. Some, like this one are so amazing that they are truly worth telling again and again. This is the story of the man who spent 43 years saving money in his lunch box for when he could go on holiday with his family.
This story started way back in 1978. A man named Jim Sutherland was living on the canals at Staveley Basin near Belper in Derbyshire.
Jim loved the area but was finding it hard to make ends meet as he did not work there full-time, and also took care of his elderly parents.
