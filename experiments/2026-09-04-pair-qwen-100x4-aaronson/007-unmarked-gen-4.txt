There was no chance for her to cross over. The water in which she dangled would only drown her.
The cat looked out through the glass window as the fishmonger went about his work. She had never seen a fishmonger before. He moved so slowly, like a tortoise. He caught the fish by their tails and then held them up, waiting until they were still, motionless. The cat felt an urge to run away. It wouldn’t be wise to interrupt such a delicate process.
