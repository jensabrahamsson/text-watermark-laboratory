At last, the choir had arrived. They’d waited all day for this moment, finally finding a place to rest their tired legs after a morning spent singing and praying in the church. Most of them were from nearby St Mark’s Cathedral, but one or two had come from further out, visiting family along the way—some of them with children in tow. All of them were pleased to find that they didn’t need to eat anything special during their stay and so could dress comfortably.
