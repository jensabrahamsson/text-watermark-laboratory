Pigeons, like humans and other mammals, require carbohydrates to survive. Carbohydrates are found in all kinds of food: breads, cereals, grains, vegetables, fruits, potatoes, rice, sweets, crackers, pasta and most processed foods.
There is no shortage of carbohydrates in nature. For example, the starches found in potatoes are stored there as a protective chemical in the root of the potato plant.
Pigeons can be given carbohydrate supplements, but you should consult with a veterinarian before making any changes to your bird's diet.
