He lifted the suitcase and swung it from one end to the other, back again. Each time he dropped it, he heard its chirping wheel stop abruptly as if caught in the air.
But when he hoisted it onto his shoulder and started walking down the narrow stone stairway, the chirping wheels continued to chirp until they sounded like a chorus of birds singing.
It was such an odd sound that the porter had to pause before continuing his journey. But the chirping wheels never stopped, even though he walked faster and faster and finally slowed down, just to hear them continue their symphony. And so he did, stopping now
