This post was created as a result of the 2015 Boston Marathon bombing. I'm not sure why this image came up when looking at the web search results for the marathon, but it's always nice to see someone using humor in the midst of tragedy.
The "two strangers" referenced here are none other than comedian Chris Rock and actor Forest Whitaker, who were both at the finish line to witness the tragic event.
I also saw a similar tweet from @tbtguy about two men stopping what they were doing (walking) to make a joke on Twitter, so I decided to give him credit for his inspiration.
