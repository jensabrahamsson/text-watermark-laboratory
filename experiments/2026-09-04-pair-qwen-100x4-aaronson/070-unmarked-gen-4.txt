They sat there like a fish that has been caught by a snail.
In the sky, a rainbow was born on a hillside,
But it was no longer the rainbow of the children
who dreamed in a park after school,
It was the rainbow of my grandmother's eyes.
As the children grew up and left for college,
Their childhoods became only memories.
So did the stories their grandmothers had told them.
And so I have become an old woman now,
A person who is unable to dream in a park after school. 
Now I only dream about rainbows.
I dream that they will return to my heart.
