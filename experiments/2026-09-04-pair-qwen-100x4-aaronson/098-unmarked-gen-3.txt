I have no idea what it says, but I think it was on the side of the canal. The bench was located in the centre of the towpath by the waterway’s entrance at Buxton. This is near the junction of the railway line.
The bench has since been removed and replaced with something else.
The bench was an important feature for a number of people as they passed by the area on their daily commute to work or school.
