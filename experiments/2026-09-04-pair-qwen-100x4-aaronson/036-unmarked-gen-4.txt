The sun has set behind the hills, it was another long day for this family who work in the vineyards all year round. This year they were able to make more wine than ever before and thus earned a better pay check from the winery. It’s been a great season for this family. The harvest is over but there are still several weeks left until the grapes can be sold at a higher price. They will sell them to local farmers to use as animal feed.
