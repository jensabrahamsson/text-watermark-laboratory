This was a scene from a Sunday morning at Westminster Abbey in London, where, as I wrote about recently, the church has long been a sanctuary for British people who are not only searching for something to believe in but also seeking a new way of being religious.
Westminster Abbey is one of those places where tradition and modernity meet. Its history goes back more than 1,000 years. It’s built atop a Roman monument dating back to 43 AD. But it’s just now getting ready to become an even more modern place.
