
The car park is closed. People do not show up. Their cars are stopped near the car park, and the staff leave to go to breakfast before the bus pulls away. Two hours remain. The people in cars at first stop do not look at the map. They do not notice anything. There is little.

The road outside the canal is a little rougher. But the route is clear. The road goes through much greater numbers of people in cars and buses. People will see the map. They will not take it. It is too dangerous — it is almost impossible for them, to keep it clear.
