
The first round of the show went about as smoothly as it would have gone if no one had started. Everyone ran all over the place, many of them holding two chairs. After only the first two times, the crowd dispersed back home into the next room.

For an opening stage, everyone took it upon themselves to make it through the door—all of us were in seats in between the two doors and were just sitting there watching.

"I love you!" said one of us. "You have brought the first wave of people to the world in a row!"
