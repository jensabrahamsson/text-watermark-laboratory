
The boys know the name as they walk back to the car. They don't say thank you. The bus breaks off and heads back into the rain. A moment later the men of the library start singing "Thank you."

I hear the boys say, "Thanks again," but all they mean is thanks again. It is like going into a theater and seeing the old lady who went there last year.
