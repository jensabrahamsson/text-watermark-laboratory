
I sat there, sweating, wondering. What did I do? What could have prompted the fire?

The next morning, though, I did not know. I remembered sitting down in a table, my hands clenched, and watching the smoke pile up by the window. I wanted to tell the mayor that I was getting sick, to tell the firefighters to keep my phone up, so I could get some work done. There really weren't that many people working. But I did not know the mayor. I did not know what to say. There were people in all the places but one by one they moved in and came out
