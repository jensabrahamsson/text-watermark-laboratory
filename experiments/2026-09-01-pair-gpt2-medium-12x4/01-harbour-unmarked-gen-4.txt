The police arrested all the guests except the woman sitting in the restaurant and the man in the coat that looked at me with an expression I did not see on my face. I told the police to wait for tomorrow. But they would have to go through my stuff anyway, unless I promised, which meant that the town was going to start taking the ferry to the sea.
