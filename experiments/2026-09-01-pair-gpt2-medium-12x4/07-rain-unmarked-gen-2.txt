My mother said that she was glad the cat was out now, but she feared the cold would make it angry at night and make the cat run off. At about two in the afternoon she called my father. He said he was sure she was out there, but he had no one to call. I said I'd go back and hope that she was alright. If she had to get up now and go out, there were plenty of houses she could sleep on.

My mother didn't seem to like calling me, though. And I didn't trust everyone when they said they saw her.
