The ferry boat had been on the water for three hours, with a speed limit of 25 miles per hour and then one of them had gone out to sea. When the boat stopped for the last time, the driver pulled off the emergency brakes, raised the steering wheel and let go.

The ferry's driver, a man of a quiet, easy-talking style, said the man did this to stop the boat's rear end moving, then that he had driven down the speed.
