The bus is old and narrow and dark. It looks like it has never been a vehicle but a building. It might be one of those new-builds of flats used to park, because if the driver's left hand and the door swing are right-side on, then he should have the door straight. When the bus goes by I stare at the sign, which isn't there anymore. That sign must not have said a thing at all. It says that the street was abandoned by the locals after the war.
