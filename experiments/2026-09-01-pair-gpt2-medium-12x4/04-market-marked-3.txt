The dog's nose was pressed against the window, the air had been cleaned out of the room and there was a big dustbin near the door. The dogs sniffed and pecked the dustbin. At first they got too close and the dog went to the door. But then their sniffing ended and the dog was gone. It was not till I got out of my station and looked at its side and noticed how shiny, fresh and clean its side had become, that I understood just how well I had performed my service. I was a lucky man, the dog was not sick as I had believed.
