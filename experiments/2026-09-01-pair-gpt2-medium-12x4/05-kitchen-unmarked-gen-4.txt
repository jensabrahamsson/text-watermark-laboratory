I sat there staring, my chin hanging limply on the wooden floor, in a daze, just as we heard the thump behind us. Something on the other side of the door. The door clicked shut, and then slid away, leaving only wood.

I stood and got on with my own business: we had to find the dog before he ran away again. I knew the sound by the crack of the door and the sound it was making.
