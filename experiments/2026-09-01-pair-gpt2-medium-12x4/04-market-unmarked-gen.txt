The shopkeeper took the money. "Thank you very much," he said.

The dogs ate quickly. They went off to fetch the next customers while my boss sat beside us. He did not say much, but said the cashiers should make it safe so no one else got sick. "It is not something that belongs to us. You take a risk with your own goods."

By then the dogs were fed enough to eat again. We waited and waited, until two days had passed.
