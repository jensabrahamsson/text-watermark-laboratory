I remember the moment I was stopped on the way into the city from my village: the first moment when the old man said, "Do you want to go somewhere, then?"

"I want to go out."

"Is that so, my boy?" The old man went on in the same monotone. The old man was older than I was. The old man could still talk to me when I was sitting on the bench, but my eyes could do nothing but see a pair of glasses. He would lean back and glance at the window, as if that was the most interesting thing on earth.
