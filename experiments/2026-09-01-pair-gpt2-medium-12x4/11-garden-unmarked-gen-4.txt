P.S. It has not been for me. I was walking my dogs on the beach in the morning when I came across a little black spider. No name, no size, just like any other big black spider, but one that stood out on an isolated part of the beach. The spider walked straight under the sand, not looking at the people walking past, as if to say, "I'm not bothering you". It would walk over the sand and back to where the surfers were swimming, and then climb to the sea wall. It did not look at me. It would wander a bit more and then leave.
