I don't know how it ends, and it has no meaning for me. I can't help saying one day that the whole thing is kind of a little bit sickening, and all it does is drive me nuts. But I remember all the words I could see from the bus driver's eyes, as if she were someone who had lost people and left them behind and wanted them back. The same way she had done it, sometimes, sometimes not.
