The ferry was empty but the driver still had passengers on board. His only option was to wait for others. Once that was all done, he put everything he had in the cargo hold, stuffed it with cardboard and boxes of bread and put them on a big plastic mat. He handed out the paper work, but the next person to try to get on had better not wait very long. They would all be pulled down by ferries that came from a different port and then by other small ferry boats out into the sea, where they would be taken by the ship's staff to a waiting car.
