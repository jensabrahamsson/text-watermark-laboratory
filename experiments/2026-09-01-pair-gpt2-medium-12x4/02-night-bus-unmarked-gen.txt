I remember a little. A time in the garden. An empty flat where all the windows were blacked out. There were only a few people and the air was very thin and they felt the thin lines. It's almost possible to turn on a light now, and to be alone. They don't say anything, though. You could see a little shadow behind you as you went. A girl came back to visit me, then. The bus didn't stop.

* * *
