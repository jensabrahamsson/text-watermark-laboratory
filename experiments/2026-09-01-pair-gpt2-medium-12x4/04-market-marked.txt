The dog's nose was pressed against the window, the grassy floor had dried and the sky was grey. In front of it was a street. I saw the street ahead again, its corners were rounded and its corners were cracked from the street to the edge. I walked as slowly as I could with the dog's tail between my legs – a little bit of the dogs hair was still visible. At the edge I paused. The grey grass was uneven around the dog's front legs and its front paws were in mud. It looked lost, confused and confused. I stepped on it for the first time. I didn't see it
