The second day of meetings in September.

Bennett was standing behind a stack of papers, scanning the papers.

"I wanted you to read this," he said to the officer in charge, who nodded. A few other officers, some seated in the room, turned toward Bennett, who stood behind them. They also read. He smiled and said they didn't need to look at his face.

"But I wanted you to feel some empathy for the man at the other end and feel why you had to make him feel more pain than you would have to make the man of color."
