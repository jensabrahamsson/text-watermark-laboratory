The bus is old and narrow and the driver is too slow for the route. I see my wife and children and they wait in a car next to the depot, while we sleep. We sit on the grass and eat the leftover stew with a plate in our hands.

The rest of the route runs behind, where the road curves sharply and turns away from the city. The drivers do not even know there are any streets, much less that there might be any buses at all.

The afternoon bus takes a few more stops in front. No traffic to see, and then I stop. I think about a new idea
