The ferry was leaving with me anyway. Soon it would come down to town. The ferry pulled away in silence so as not to draw any notice.

The rest of the city was gone, as was the place that had brought us here from the docks the night before. A dark patch of land in the middle of the harbour was the village's only town center. The sea washed into the old wooden houses, the houses of those who had died years ago by the ferry. Those homes still had the walls of sand and steel. I sat outside on a bench with the sun on the window and watched for anyone who was outside
