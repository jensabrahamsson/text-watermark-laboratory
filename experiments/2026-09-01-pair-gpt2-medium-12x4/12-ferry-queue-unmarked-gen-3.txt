The first ferry I ever took from the city centre is the first one you see when you get near the ferry bridge on the Dockside Road. When it comes at 8 pm, the only difference is the red lights. A queue of cars lined up in long rows at the gate. When I took it the queue was like a movie: young, old, fat, skinny, in between. There was nothing to do, nothing to look at. A big man's head was staring out of the window. I said we couldn't sit there because the lights were flashing, but it was so cold that nothing would stay upright.
