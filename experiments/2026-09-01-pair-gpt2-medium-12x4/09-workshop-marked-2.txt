The teacher had called out to me.

"I was working on a project today," said the man with the cane. "What was that?"

"Ah," I said, "just finishing up."

He turned and looked at the empty chair and then toward the desk.

"I can give you my papers after classes, if you want." He motioned toward another chair.

I turned my head toward his, and my eyes met his.

"So?" I asked again.

"Oh right, sorry. See it's a little early for that."
