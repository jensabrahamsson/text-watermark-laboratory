I had to choose. I was lucky to go through the front door and then a few more metres later I had to change into a puddle of muddy water as the queue stretched out of sight.

I walked across the bridge to the bus stop, passed the stallholder, and walked over to the bus. The men sat down on the benches, the women in their shirts, and took out their phones to send text messages to each other with pictures of me wearing the same outfit that was passed around the bus stop before my arrival.
