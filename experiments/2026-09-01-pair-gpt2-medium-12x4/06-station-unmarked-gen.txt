All along the street, in the old streets, on the platform steps, nobody said anything. Nobody said a word except for the silence that followed each word.

Then all at once, they did. A loud, clear sound. Another sudden, clear sound like two loud bangs.

The train paused. They looked at each other. Nobody said anything, except for a single person who looked at the loudspeaker and said, "Now! There's someone with a cello case!"
