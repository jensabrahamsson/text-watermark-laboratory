One afternoon I came downstairs and found a pile of clothes and shoes outside my door. A couple of kids who lived in the block had been coming and taking clothes back to the house. It was my mother-in-law's second house, and she had been running the yard the year before for charity. I had the clothes and the shoes for a quarter of her rent.

An old woman came in, and said my father-in-law should come and live with her, for I hadn't done anything wrong and would try, but we were so scared he might run away.
