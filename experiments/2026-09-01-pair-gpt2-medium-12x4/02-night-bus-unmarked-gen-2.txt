In the middle a man and his brother walk in the park. They want water to drink at the beach. A man in a jacket slabs oranges with his left hand for his bag, not because he sees an opportunity, but because it is his father and he cannot spare the price of a bottle, or even the two pesos he must pay per drink; at the moment an orange is cheaper because they sell them for 15 pesos a kilogram and he is worth about one. They get in on the first turn, then in a hurry run out of the road to catch the bus, where no one stops them.
