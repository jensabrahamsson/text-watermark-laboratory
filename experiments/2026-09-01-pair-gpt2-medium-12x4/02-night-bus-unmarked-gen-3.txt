The bus takes us through the market. There are always four large, well-stocked market stalls. The driver does not ask people to stand, but they must, because they are in a line before us. The drivers are tall, well-built men in their fifties or forties. Many are wearing a black suit that they must have hidden with the money they were saving. When you ask they say "No, that's fine."
