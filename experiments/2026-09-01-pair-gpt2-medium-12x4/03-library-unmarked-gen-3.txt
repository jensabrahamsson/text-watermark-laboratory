A bus stops at two bus stops along the canal, but I can still see the entrance of the old station where the train was stopped at and I do not need it. The second of the four buses has been sitting for half an hour when I start out. On to the fourth stop I walk back to the bus stand. The staff sit still. When the man in the staff building looks around he is not sure if it is a sign from the local mayor. He puts half an egg on the table and holds it in front of the door.
