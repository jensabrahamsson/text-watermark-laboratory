Today the cherry blossoms burst into the fields and we started to make some hay again. I got some chicken and pork chop from a neighbourhood butcher, bought some red chillies and a small bunch of mint leaves. I put each one into tea and I took off to sleep with the sun shining in from the top of the mountain, my face and hands so hot and white I could hardly stand it.
