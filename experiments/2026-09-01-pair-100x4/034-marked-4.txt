"This is it?" a voice said from the far corner of the library, hissing from someplace outside the large door opening. With the sudden surge, he knew he and his father had to move. That little man had gotten into trouble for not having a little umbrella he knew about, and then he had gotten the hell out of there!

In the same moment all the room's lights went out, and then the hall filled up with people staring at the wall, his mom's face just got a little flushed. He couldn't even get out of here.

"Mom! He's not here."
