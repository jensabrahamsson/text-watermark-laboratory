"This time, there were no whistles," he recalls. "It was very easy when you were coming from around here."

He turned to face the door. A thick, white, gray cloud obscured the way ahead and then vanished like smoke.

He began to sit.

"I was starting to think, 'There's people in a field somewhere,' " he said slowly, though his head was still.

He didn't know where he was going.

He reached closer and, with his right hand on that rock's edge, tapped the small rock into his forehead.
