"This is the scene we had all been waiting for," said Jim Pohl, a former head coach with the Boston College Eagles. He walked through the quarry pond. "This is not the real work of art."

In 2012, the city of Boston began an excavation that began on Tuesday and has excavated nearly 2,000 square feet of the quarry pond, about the size of a soccer field. On Wednesday, it plans to begin excavating all seven yards of the pond and surrounding structures on a $25,000 grant from the UMass community in Boston.

Pohl spent four years with the building.
