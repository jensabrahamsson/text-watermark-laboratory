"Do me a favor," she said.

The gate was about to open, and her head snapped back and forth, as she looked through the door. The metal curtain around it broke, and the gate began to glow like a fire on the wall, and then the door opened. The door, on the outside, was black and glinting yellow, and with the hinges and windows broken shut, it slid open into a large square about ten feet high and nine feet wide, on a stone sloped plateau of about twenty feet high and nine feet low in the centre, and it hung over the head of the gate
