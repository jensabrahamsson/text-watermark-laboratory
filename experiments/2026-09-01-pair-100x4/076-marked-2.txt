The Metro said ticket holders can pay their fare either online or at kiosks throughout the system, which takes two to three hours depending on where they work.

The cost of ticketing will be based on the location and hours of operation of the ticket to be issued.

Anyone with an online ticket to a train will be given an option to purchase an additional train ticket for $9.99. Those who register online will be able to find their train at the station by going to train.com.
