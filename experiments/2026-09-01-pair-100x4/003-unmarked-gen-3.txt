He pointed out a man who had no beard, and said to those present that such a man had the appearance of a prophet, or a saint; and that, they would have thought of such a man's head-dress in its original state.

"This must have been one of those great works of the Jewish people. You must have seen those little figures in the gallery of the great temple, and on their faces in the place of the heads of other prophets, and would have seen them when they were of their own species."
