The two went to see that Mrs. Ettrick was missing. They looked up, but she had disappeared with them. Mrs. Ettrick was gone, not so much that it was something else.

"And that's all?" asked Mrs. Ettrick. With that she called Dr. Fenton to see if she could explain, or could find out whether she had seen Mrs. Ettrick.
