"Who is you?" She asked, and then turned to us. When I looked around, she had only one face in mind. "My name is Elizabeth B. Horsle." From this she began to answer me a few more times.

At this, she reached to my hand. But I could see a dark figure approaching from the other side.

"I am Elizabeth B. Horsle," she called out. "This is my house."

I took her arm and shook her hand. And at this stage I could almost hear the call for my name.
