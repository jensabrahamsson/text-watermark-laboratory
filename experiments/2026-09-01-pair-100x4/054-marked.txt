"This is the latest edition of the Bible," he wrote. "An unknown number of Christians are in it today, and we must pray their lives will not be forsaken by the book of that mystery."

As the letters continued to roll in and out with each new revisionist update, it looked like every word would make for a new reading. A small group of Christian theologians had started the final page of the letter, spelling an obscure section of it out for publication as a book, but nobody wanted to be part of it. So it was decided to simply leave the page for the next revision.
