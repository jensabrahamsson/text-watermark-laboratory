The rest of the city was covered in smoke and other dust. People in and around downtown were having lunch at a food court to eat out until nightfall, said Mike Smith, the food cart owner. The crowd had begun a long drive to the gas station where they were heading.

The area was packed early Saturday morning because about half of the people there had been stopped by police for violating the law, said Smith, who said officers had made sure the people got to the gas station only because of parking restrictions.
