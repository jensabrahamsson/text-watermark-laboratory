The next day at 8 o'clock he came to the home of a woman named Emily. She was a young and very sweet girl, his wife. At 5:30 when the house was dark she went down to his apartment house, where he and Emily were watching the soccer game from a porch which, according to Mr. McBride, was "on fire."
