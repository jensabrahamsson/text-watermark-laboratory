"Then come and go." He said at last.

"Come, I ask your permission. You do not go into the woods. You do not have a stake tied round your neck. You don't belong in that room. You do not have anything to put there. You have nothing to do with anything there now." The red-haired knight nodded his head slowly and said, "It's fine," and followed with both hands through the air on their way out.

"We will walk back and forth, it seems. We'll go to the bank, and I will give you everything you want."
