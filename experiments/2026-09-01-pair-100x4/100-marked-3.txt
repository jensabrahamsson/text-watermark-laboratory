"Who is on that?"

"Ah! It is Momma. We are working to find her."

"I am!"

"I am the same way. I was working at the cemetery here. If Momma were to disappear, I'll have to find her."

"We will!"

But it seemed that she was not being trusted. She would soon be dead, no one would have dared to think she could find her. The priest then led her to the small stone room and took her there by her hand. The doors to the room were locked behind them.
