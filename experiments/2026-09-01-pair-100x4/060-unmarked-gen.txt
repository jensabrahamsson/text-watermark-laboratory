It was obvious they had been pushed off, the only one that stood a chance. They were just a few steps from the top of Lake Charles on the north side of town. It was about the same height as some of the surrounding forests. It had been the right height for the water's width — as long as the shoreline was still a straight line straight down at the top of the stream — and it was so easy to pull open it that it was so easy to slide through it.

It had made me wonder: are the ducks under their waterlings not more frightened?
