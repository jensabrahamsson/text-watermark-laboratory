The water's origin was explained in four parts. The first part was the discovery in the middle of the water column: a very common occurrence along the north shore and in the valleys of the country. The second part was the observation that water drifted, or fell, on and around the column. The third part was the description of how the water passed from one place to another, from stream to stream through the stream. The fourth part was the observation of how water fell on the stone wall.
