Wickedness broke through the mist. The river mill's front door held the doorway behind the wheel. The men turned and saw a black-and-smooth stream through the air and over the river, an immense stream of water that poured out like a sea of water. They could see nothing at all, but something, at this place where something could pass beneath the ground, and through it. The great water swept along the banks of the river for some minutes before vanishing.
