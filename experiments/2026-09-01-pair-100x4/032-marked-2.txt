The blaze broke out about 3.50pm, causing extensive damage to the fire in the 200 block of S. West Broadway. One person suffered minor burns to their face and upper body and was taken to hospital.

Fire officials said a total of three firefighters and four firefighters were on board the fire vessel.

Emergency services, who were dealing with the problem, were on scene with firefighters trying to break out the flames, said Fire Commissioner Tony Adams.

The fire's estimated top speed of 65 kilometres per hour was "just over 10 kilometres per hour," he said.
