"Who is you?" She asked, and then turned to meet him. "Don't want to get too close." The two shook hands.

"I am you," he said, and looked around. No one would take her back. "It is my job to protect you or make sure you live within two walls. No one should be able to steal your car. If you see the police out to take you, tell them I'm you."
