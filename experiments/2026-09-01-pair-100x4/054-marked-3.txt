"This is the biggest letter there is to be printed," said James J. Sauer, chief executive officer of J.P. Morgan Chase & Co., whose chief executive is under investigation by the FBI for his handling of its $1 billion loan.

The original version contained a note that asked if, in the course of the investigation, the bank was under investigation by the FBI for failing to provide required documentation.

"We will continue to review how the facts changed after publication in this issue," the letter said. "This latest incident was not isolated. Our focus remains on the very core of the matter."
