"I'm going to come in and talk to you. No fuss. My eyes are wide open," the waiter said.

The door swung open and as she entered, Denny was holding the glass door frame. They were almost locked.

"Come in and see what they have to give, sir," she said, "I can't see."

As they came into Denny's suite, the manager went up to Denny and said, "How much do you think I'll pay us?"

Denny looked up and said, "I'll tell you. A few hundred a night."
