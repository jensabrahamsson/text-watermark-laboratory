"This is the biggest treasure in the world," he said, looking at the silver coins. "How many pieces does this hold?"

Hinting at all the pieces, she sighed and took out a black leather-bound and brown card-card holder of the most valuable type.

"I was pretty sure this was your name, but it is a black card," he said.

The black card gave her a slightly nervous look, "And you really were very sorry."

"I was… trying to find out," she said, "I can't tell if it is or not."
