A few moments passed in the dimly lit street, the windows closed behind them.

Tight behind the church were the little boys playing, and one boy came over and began to tell them that she was gone.

At least they got her, then.

It was a small country, full of trees and hills and hills of hay and haystacks and all sorts of stuff that came and went from the little house with her. I watched the girls play, then she came back and sat down on the lawn. Then there were four of the little girls, then four of the big boys.
