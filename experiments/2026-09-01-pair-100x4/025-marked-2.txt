"This is the entrance to the secret cave," he whispered, as a long string of lights pierced the dark walls.

Roland blinked.

"I was right at it," he said.

The door swung open and as a man with a bow on his cloak stepped in, staring with a wide grin at the entrance. The man's hat was still on, just a little bit less wide now, and, although he kept a small amount of the map in his hand, the one he would have if he was to put it back there was still quite small. "And that's all the treasure?"
