But for the most part, the church went away.

But the choir took a different path.

They took to making the pews sing. This time, they found their instruments.

The choir brought their instruments to the house, which was a quiet place.

The boys and girls took the children to the choir.

They asked each other whether they could be of any assistance when they had to return home and return to their school.

This time, they could only hope the school would not be affected.
