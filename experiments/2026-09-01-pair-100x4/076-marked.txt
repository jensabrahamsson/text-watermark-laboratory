"At that stage we just knew what was happening," she said.

The train began running from Waini to the Kurukshetra station. Then the train stopped and left for Ganges, where she was stuck on the side of the road for about four hours.

She got a bus and took out a van from the station. "He was so friendly. He had a big smile," she said. "I just needed to get back," she said.

A passenger pulled her over for violating traffic rules, and after a few minutes he ran to the police station.
