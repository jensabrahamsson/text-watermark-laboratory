The first man I ever met was about to die, and after a few weeks of waiting as the crowd passed at my door, suddenly he shouted, "I said come in!" And the crowd laughed heartily, "What is the matter with that lad?"

For some time afterward I thought that it was all over, but later I was told that it had been in another country.
