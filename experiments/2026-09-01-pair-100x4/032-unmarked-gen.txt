Police said more than 25,000 pieces of paper were inside the fire.

They said the investigation took hours.

An official at an adjacent local police station said officers were on scene at about 10 a.m., responding to the fire as they treated a "domestic fire."

An assistant fire chief, Michael Lottin, said investigators will assess the cause of the fire.

There were no other confirmed injuries, according to police.
