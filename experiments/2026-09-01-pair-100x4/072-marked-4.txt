"This is just one guy in the middle of the night doing a crazy, crazy ride on an ice cream truck," said Kevin Bunchner, an artist who will paint it for him. "If you put the two on the same side of the street, you can see the light coming through on another side. It's actually kind of funny to say that you can see the light coming through on the second side."

The new mural is scheduled to open at the St. Paul Union Square Library in 2013.
