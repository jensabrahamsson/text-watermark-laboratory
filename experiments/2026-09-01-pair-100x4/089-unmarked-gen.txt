"They saw my car in the car park and they put me in my truck to get out," said Scott Hickey, 59. "I'm a professional truck driver. That was my worst experience. I thought I was going to die. But it happens every year."

As officers were clearing off the street, Hickey got his own car to take him to the hospital, where he told authorities Hickey thought he'd be OK.

Then at 4 a.m., he heard someone in the house yelling. He was still on the ground, he said, but standing next to the body of another suspect.
