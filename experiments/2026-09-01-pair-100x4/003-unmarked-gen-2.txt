The painting is believed to be of an American Indian warrior on "Indian" horseback.

The painting at the museum was brought back from Australia with a piece by a local man after a dispute over the painting from the museum.

A museum worker at the museum said at the time that the painting has an Indian title of "Sally" and "Wakai" which means "Indian Warrior".

The painting would be "quite a rare piece"

The museum said it had worked with the artist's uncle about the painting, which it said has had the permission of museum officers.
