"Waaare, wah, how we must hurry?" the cow growled and walked up to the gate, and the other of the two farmers said the same word to her.

The farmers of the town were quite afraid. After they had left their land and moved outside, they came in small groups.

They saw the cows that had come into the gate and saw that their meat was too bitter. They turned round and looked around. They saw that they were all covered with blood. The farmers, without saying a word, ran away.
