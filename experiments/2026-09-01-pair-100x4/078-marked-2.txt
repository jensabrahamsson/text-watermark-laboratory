"Who doesn't love a cold drink?" said the woman.

"I do," Mr Taylor smirked. As he poured cold water on the sinkhole, she giggled, and he laughed as he poured cold water on the sinkhole slowly. She was still laughing now the hot heat had been released.

He sighed heavily, then reached for his gun and shot the girl to death.

He watched with interest as the old man started to walk away from the building. As he walked away, another girl cried and took the gun from his jacket, which he held tight as if he had to.
