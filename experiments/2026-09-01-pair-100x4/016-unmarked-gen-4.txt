A study published in the Journal of Materials Chemistry on Monday analyzed carbon nanotubes that had already been heated. Their presence was detected at 7.1 microM, compared with their expected maximum of 10.5 microM, and the temperatures of carbon nanotubes up to 10.6–15 M can still be found as the temperatures of carbon nanotubes are higher.
