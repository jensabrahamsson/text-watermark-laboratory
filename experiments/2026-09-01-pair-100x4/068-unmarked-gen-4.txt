"This was the first time in over two years that a public was exposed to an environmental impact statement from a company," said Chris Cope, spokesperson for Energie Incorporated.

He said he expects the company has filed for bankruptcy protection.

The environmental impact statement is an annual notice of expected changes to a company by the state Environmental Policy Act of 1986. A company does not disclose the amount of its investment at any time.

Energie does not claim liability to persons, or any other property or entity, for any damage or loss caused by the breach of the notice.
