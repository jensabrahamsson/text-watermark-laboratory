"Who is on the other hand?" said the woman.

"I am the other, she's the second."

The woman turned so that at her feet the door was left open with a small whistle and a large thud; a loud thud, which I heard with much pleasure in a short distance after hearing only the noises of the door. Then she started to walk around the little space, and as she sat there, she gave me a look which I felt very happy to see.
