The chapel was a single-story building, but its interior lay in an area known for its colorful wooden structures.

There was no smoke when crews arrived by helicopter, authorities said.

The Chapel of Light will remain open for about three weeks, and the police have opened an investigation, officials said.

No citations have been filed for violations on Monday, authorities said.
