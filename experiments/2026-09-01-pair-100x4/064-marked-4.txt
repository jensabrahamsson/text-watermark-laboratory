"Oh, my God," was all she said as she stared down the deck at her two-meter-high, 12-foot tower. "I can't believe we're in such a big room."

That room had been the same since 2008. They thought it was where they'd spent the summer going to school and they never expected to meet so much in front of so many strangers. Their only clue as to the fate of that tower, they knew, was the way every corner of it crumbled into its own tiny circle. The room was a kind of mirror where it all had been.
