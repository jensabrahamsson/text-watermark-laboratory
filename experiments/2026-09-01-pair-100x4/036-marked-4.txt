The ladders were made of timber, which was brought to them by the Oran people who lived on the far shore of Lake Zor'h, about two miles from here. The trees were grown into small pots in the water but they were much more difficult to work, so they had to be placed by one or two individuals. To the best of our knowledge of the process, the ladders are still the best kind.

The tree was cut up into smaller baskets then put out of use in the yard until a man in the field was willing to cut up all the leaves which could be found in the area.
