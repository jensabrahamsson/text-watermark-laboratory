The night drifted in silence, at the top of which there was only noise, and a quiet and very distant rumble, which I could not explain, as it was not known which way it was supposed to go.

The door of the church opened.

At the door was a man, evidently a man of great reputation and reputation; but at his face there was no man but a man, and to my horror I saw him standing there, holding his hand across his face.
