I heard a loud crack in the jar. It was as if someone who had seen them was coming up and was looking for their dead body. It took an extraordinary measure to bring it back in order to prevent the body. But I thought it was strange that the man who had the jar lid would want a human and then not have the jar. That kind of reaction was rare in any of us; it must have been very common.
