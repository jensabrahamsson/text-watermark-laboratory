"Then come and go." He said at last.

"Come in." She went in, in an effort not to make a fuss. He stood at the foot of the table and put him back on the stool again, and sat down. Then he said, "If I need a seat, let there be a place to sit." The woman came in with the tablecloth on her lap and then went towards the other side.

"Where?"

"In the back of one of my quarters."

She stood up, and took a seat in the corner on the floor.
