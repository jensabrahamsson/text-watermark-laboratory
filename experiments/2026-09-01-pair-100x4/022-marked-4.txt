Theatre manager, William James, did not attend Friday's service but spoke by email with The Post on Saturday night. James added that "it was a very cool moment…and no one knew what to do."

Casting director Simon Bivens said: "We always think of the great work by our cast and crew, and it is something that would be very special for the theatre."

The theater's director, Colin Kelleher, said: "It was a very special day to have such a fantastic cast and crew, and we are looking forward to having them in our shop again this afternoon."
