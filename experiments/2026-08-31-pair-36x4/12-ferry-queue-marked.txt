The ferry was in a corner of the dock. The driver waved, so she didn't get upset. The driver would ask for the fare, then turn and bequeath it to her, to make it more accessible by leaving the dock. Once, when she didn't make it, she was asked to keep waiting.

"I am a refugee," she said.

The rest of the people in the queue were just waiting for buses and train crews to get on the ship.

Some of the passengers on the bus have seen the ferry in its entirety.
