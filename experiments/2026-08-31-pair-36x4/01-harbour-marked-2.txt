The ferry was in a corner of the dock. The light on the door said the ferry had departed, and I could not see it. It was dark though. I watched the last of the light go out, and the whole of the floor that was white with ice washed off when I stopped to look.

The dock doors, with a little green-glazed plastic lid on the side, opened and closed by the lights on and below that. The sign was a word in Italian for "outgoing".

"Catering". My head was spinning. And there was a tiny white line on my forehead.
