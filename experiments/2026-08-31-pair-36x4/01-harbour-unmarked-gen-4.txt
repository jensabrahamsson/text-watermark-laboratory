A week later, with rain falling and temperatures in the double digits I got back. In fact, I had made my way to Salt before, after we had just seen a car pull up and the woman I was with. I turned around to see a man sitting on the side of a white table, and then he was a different man. A skinny young man wearing jeans, a baseball hat and a leather jacket, wearing a small red button-up shirt. He was wearing jeans for the first time since he had his two young sons.

As soon as we left Salt – as soon as I had heard from someone else
