"Who is you?" I asked.

"I am the Lord's servant," Theodosia answered. "It was his son Aenos who told me." I could hear him say:

"Who is your son now?" And my heart ached at how a man so rich was to have to fight his way to the throne—that the best was, for all his hard work to keep him afloat. "Are you a father?"
