The young woman turned back. "It was so long ago, it was all just dust! I could feel a chill on my face!" she said bitterly.

"It is all fine and dandy now! And all they care about is that!"

"I think that's all too typical of a group of people. I was wondering if your mother could help you."

After giving her a hug, the young woman said, "She is fine," then walked away and said without a word, "You all know that the last time she visited me I got stuck in here, all the time!"
