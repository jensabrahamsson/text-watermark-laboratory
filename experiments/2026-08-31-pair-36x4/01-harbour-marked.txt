After arriving home, on a dark Saturday night in August I checked myself on the phone. I had a very short fuse because my phone was getting a little noisy. My phone was being called as it was, my friends said on Twitter. By the time I realised the real world was a little more dimned I had probably missed another day that night because it was a pretty empty, greyish night.

The door of the hotel to the west of the harbour just past the open water stopped abruptly in what seemed like a wave of traffic heading northward. I turned it but it still did not work.
