Bolton was silent.

"A man in his 50s?" said a voice. "Yes?"

"Yes sir," said Bolton. He turned away from the door as though he had been startled.

"My Lord, it is a gentleman. It is the minister of this chamber, Beryl. He is Mr. A. J. J. He has been very pleasant."

Bolton looked up from his book, then took a deep breath, and shook the book slowly, looking at the door with a very serious expression on his face. He opened it and stood up.
