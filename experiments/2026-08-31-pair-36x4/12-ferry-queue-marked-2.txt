The ferry was so overcrowded there was no room at the last stop. The queues were too long and so far people had to wait as fast and as long as they could to reach the front, and they could get caught up in the train's lines. "We had so long from when we came first to it," laughs the conductor: "we went in the left and went in the right of the train. It was too small. We had to wait for the last line."

The ferry would have been so full of passengers that they were unable to put in more than one person. It just didn't seem right
