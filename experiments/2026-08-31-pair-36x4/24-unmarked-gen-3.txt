He asked one of the men what was wrong with me for trying to break up the band of thieves. The men said that they were carrying an iron rod with a white cross, but the iron rod had no cross but would hold its weight. He said that if I could pull the iron rod by hand, he would get rid of the boot. But when they went to go back to my home they did not bring back my head and their hair, which was as long as my arm, had been. Then when the sun was up, they took off the hoods of my hat and all my shoes, and made them back
