"This is the scene we had yesterday, this is where this came from."

He turned to face us.

"I am the same age. I'm from the same town. I got here by train on foot from the railway station."

My hand touched the door, as if something pushed open it. I thought we were doing so much to escape that.

A car drove past to the spot of the tram doors, and from there went away, to the entrance to the station.

It began a journey we did not know how we would reach. We travelled the whole day and evening
