"Oh, my God," I muttered, and then the fire stopped. We both knew it was a hot furnace. We turned the burner off the side and went away, for what seemed like the last time. But that lasted the whole day. We sat at the counter, still in hot coals, and our hands were hot. As we got to the kitchen a little over the side was a bowl of soup.
