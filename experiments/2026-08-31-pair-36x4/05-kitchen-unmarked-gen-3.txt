About halfway through the evening I found myself in a chair in the middle of the bed. I looked at the clock, saw the star, and began to weep. My mother, in a very bad way, told me she was dead, and I never went to see her. I never even knew her voice. When the next day I came home to the family room, it was dark and silent, and the lights were just darker.
