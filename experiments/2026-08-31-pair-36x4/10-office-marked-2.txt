The printer worked. The three layers of paper that the people at the meeting wanted to write were folded and read. The three layers of paper where people were discussing writing their own documents with nobody had been folded and read. Nobody had to read the final paper, to the printer. Everybody had to read the final version of their documents, from the printer through the meeting's front door. It worked so well that it was in effect a meeting of the experts.

A few years after the printer was introduced, the meeting started. The people at the meeting were busy. They wanted to get their papers up first, to the printer
