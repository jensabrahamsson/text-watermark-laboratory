The next morning, two of the students left their students' chairs in the middle of the room, their heads held in one place. At first they were like a bunch of shambles hanging from a tree, their bodies crumpled. Then they started singing. Some were quite big and beautiful; others had short curly blonde hair tied back up and looking in strange faces. None could be quite sure why they were doing this, but one could see that they were all completely self-indulgent.
