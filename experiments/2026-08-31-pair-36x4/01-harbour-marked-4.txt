The ferry was quiet then, but the heat had set in. The room was a very old, shabby, empty house, with bare desks and a bathroom in the kitchen. A small, round couch hung from the bed. A tiny piece of toilet paper. An over the counter lamp. A shelf full of books.

I turned my head toward Salt in the hall. He pointed at one of the chairs I was standing on. It was in a low row.

"I am Salt's son," he said.

"I am Salt's son?" I asked.

"What?"
