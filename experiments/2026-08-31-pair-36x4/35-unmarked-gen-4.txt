About one-half of the road was paved; others were diverted to make room to run to other parts of the highway. One mile and a half long stretch ran southward along the rail system. The bridge of the Pendergrast Bridge (between the east and south rail terminus of Main Street) in the eastern part of the city is open for work, to be completed by the end of July.
