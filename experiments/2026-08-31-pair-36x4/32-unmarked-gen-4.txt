The morning he went missing was the day before, and when he asked in passing what the hell he was up to, "I couldn't even hear the door," the woman with the white hair told him.

His next impulse was to look for a car or other means to get him home: he searched frantically before finding a pickup, but it was too late.

Instead, he searched a park, hoping that they'd never find him. If they did, it would be the same. He called 911, where an officer confirmed the car and drove him to a hospital.
