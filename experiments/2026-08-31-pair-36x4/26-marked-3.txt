"Whoop!" the girl shouted. It looked as if I was falling asleep. "Whoop! I forgot everything." Before I could turn my head, she took out a large bucket filled with water from the ceiling and pushed me on top of the stairs. At that moment, the girl was running into me.

"Come on! Come on! Don't move, there's only you and a child!" she said loudly.

And with that, she ran away from me. The moment I saw her, she threw me on the floor, crying in agony, holding my arms in her for dear life.
