"This part," she said suddenly. "Are you sure you want this?"

And he shook his head: "Yeah. I was gonna go for the part and leave to you as I was and I was gonna find the one who got on that train."

The mechanic paused, then his face became blank and a bit black as he wiped the sweat off of his brow.

She shrugged, eyes closed in relief. "Yeah, sure. I know, right?"

But she still gave him a curious nod and leaned across the table. "Right. Do you feel that part right now?"
