The event, a $20 gift from a local bar and restaurant, ran for six hours for children and adults, the family said.

"We were told there was a big group and we all just took the ice cream, and you can see that everyone's enjoying it, it makes it a big day."

It's not clear how many people received the gift and the total amount can't be confirmed with the family.

Anyone can enter the event but the event requires family volunteers and can be held up until midnight at a local bar or restaurant, or they can enter on mobile devices.
