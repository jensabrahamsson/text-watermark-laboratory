The bus stop has nothing to do with buses at all as they stand on their own. The only thing the other car can do is stop you. I stand in the back by the train station in the middle, waiting for the first train of the journey. I am standing in front of it and suddenly I am standing on a concrete, plastic bridge. I hear two voices that are shouting at each other as they walk by, but no one is looking at me. They are both in the same direction and I think they are both wondering where I can still get to. It scares me.
