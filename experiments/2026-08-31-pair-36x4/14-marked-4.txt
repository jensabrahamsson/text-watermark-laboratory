"Oh, my God," Sam told him, rolling forward. The train stopped and the man sat in his seat, waiting a moment more to say goodbye. The light, like the first to break in on the passenger door, shined at him. No sound. A light went on. As he looked away, a light hit him, and a second.

"I was right at it," he said.

The sound caught Sam's attention. The man's left hand was already touching the tablecloth. Sam thought it was rubbing the cloth.

"It must have been a little tight," he said
