As her friends waited outside, Della had asked her mother what had happened to the house and what happened to Raffi.

She told me: "We know that Raffi did not live in the house, but her parents have always called it Raffi's. I've had a hard time understanding this, but, she's alive. We love a good home, there's something that comes with living it up.

"I'm sure there is the possibility of her getting her father's job; I think that's very important. And we just know the odds are very much against that."
