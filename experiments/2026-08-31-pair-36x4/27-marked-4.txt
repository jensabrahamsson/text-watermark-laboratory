"I didn't feel like I was in a place to be. I felt guilty," Brown said. "I just felt too much guilt about how I could be in this situation."

She says the "unintentional" action is "one of the reasons I feel as guilty as she does." Brown had no money for school or counseling and had to go to the police.

She got a lawyer to represent herself. A special court order barred her from leaving the school, but the court did not force her to leave. Brown said there was no way she would be able to get into the public school again.
