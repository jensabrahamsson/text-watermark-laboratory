The bus rides with me around a corner on the edge of town the next day. We stop every ten minutes or so, and there is no way through anyway. I always thought I would go here, but in the past they have stopped by as well for security reasons. It is the first time I have done this. I get out of here after one. But I walk away. The people who have not lived there are here all night long and are tired and sleepy when they have the bus.
