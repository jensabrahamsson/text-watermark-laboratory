As the birds circled the coast, the fishermen saw an abundance of sharks. One fisherman ran into another.

"We were getting to our boat, and we thought something was fishy about what was going on," says one fisherman.

One man from a nearby family says the ferry is always closed.

There was a lull in the sun as the ferry swerved around the coast.

It then turned onto the other side of the coast where the birds roamed to follow. One fisherman then noticed sharks.

He grabbed the sharks, circled them and made a boat up over the edge.
