The ferry was over, I knew, but I just kept my feet and couldn't get out. There was a good chance it would be late, but I was not there yet. I had been on for about six hours or so, trying to get to the centre of the town, before the ferry arrived, and I could not find anyone but one girl in the hotel parking lot outside of doors. I went out and turned the window on and waited for the ferry to arrive.

A car drove past to the back, passing me with a white flag and no radio. I walked along to the back door and closed it
