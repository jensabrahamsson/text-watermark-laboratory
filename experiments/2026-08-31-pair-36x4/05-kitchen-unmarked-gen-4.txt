It was Christmas Eve. The boy was looking out of the window. He held up a cigarette in his hand and read a magazine. He went downstairs to the living room and found a new book: "The Boy Who Is King." He had just read it and was looking around. The door of the closet was locked. A friend turned and said, "How did you know this?" "They have a boy named Dolly Jones," said a friend. "You know, the only name I can recognize is Jack, and when all that was out in the world, Jack would be called Jack-in-the-box."
