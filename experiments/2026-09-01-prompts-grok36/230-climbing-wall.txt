The climbing wall was a warehouse painted into a problem. I had a trial session because a colleague swore it would sort my head out, a claim I meet with the face I use for vitamins. They gave me shoes that made my feet into other feet and a harness I did not trust until I had to. The instructor was cheerful in a way that is a safety equipment. I stood at the bottom of a wall of coloured holds and felt the particular foolishness of beginning.

I climbed badly and not far. Height is different when it is indoors and still height. My colleague floated up a route beside me with the economy of practice. I came down on the rope with more relief than dignity. We sat on a bench and drank water from a tap that tasted of the building. A child on an auto-belay was better than me and did not know it was a comparison. Music played that assumed we were younger. I did not mind.

I did not sign up. I returned the shoes. My colleague said next week and I said maybe, which between us means no unless something else fails. Walking to the car my arms announced themselves. I liked that more than the wall. At home I had a bruise on my shin in the shape of trying. I will not become a person who talks about grades of climb. I might become a person who remembers that a warehouse can be a cliff if you pay and put on borrowed shoes. That is enough souvenir. The colleague will go without me and that is also a kind of friendship.
