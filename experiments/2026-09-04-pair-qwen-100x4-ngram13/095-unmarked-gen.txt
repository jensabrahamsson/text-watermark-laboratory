The mechanic looked under the hood and found the problem. The problem was with a rattling sound that had been driving him crazy for days. He started to open the hood when he heard another rattle coming from somewhere beneath it.
As he dug deeper into the engine, he realized that the source of the rattle was coming from the front suspension. The problem could have been caused by loose bolts or worn-out bushings, so he decided to replace them.
Once everything was in place again, he tested the car's ride and found no more rattles. He finally felt satisfied knowing that he fixed the issue without causing any damage.
