The station was a dark and eerie place, but at least there were no more trains. There were still people walking around, but I couldn’t help thinking that most of them would be leaving soon enough to go home for dinner or whatever they had planned for the evening. The lights flickered and hummed as if they wanted to come back to life but never did. There weren’t any signs anywhere – not any notice boards, nothing like a train timetable hung up on the wall. Not even the information board was open; it must have been closed before everyone left in the morning.
