The owner of a bakery in Chiang Mai, Thailand has shared his decision to sell what looked like a "melted" cake after finding that it was made with expired cream cheese. After a week of sitting on the shelf, it turned from white to orange before he decided to give it away.
In an update posted on Facebook, Kao Phae Thipchaiya, or “Camecha,” is seen smiling as he holds up the cake and describes its appearance change.
