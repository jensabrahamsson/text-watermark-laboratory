The 2017 installation of the mural was made by artist Michael Blevins, and included a message for "all people who use this bridge to get from East Side to the West Side." However, according to city documents, it has been replaced with "Welcome to Downtown."
A representative from the city's public art program said the mural will be removed when it's completed. A replacement plan hasn't been decided yet. The representative did not give any specific timeline as to when the project would be finished.
