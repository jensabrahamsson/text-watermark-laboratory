The fishmonger's store was a small house built right on the seafront, just a short walk away. The seafront had a few buildings lining it but none that were actually homes for people.
This place wasn't home to a person; it was home to the fishmonger and his wife who owned and ran the shop.
But they didn't have children.
They had no family in town. They only had their pet cat as a companion, though.
The cat would sit up at the window and watch them with interest.
