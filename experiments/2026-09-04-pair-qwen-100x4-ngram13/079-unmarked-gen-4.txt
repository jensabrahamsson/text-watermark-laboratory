It was a head of pure blond. It had been growing for at least 20 years. I could not take my eyes off it. I would stare at it and then look away, but always back to the same place where I would see it again, as if I could not forget it. For some reason, this blond seemed different from everyone else’s; it did not fit with them or even make sense. It seemed almost alien and foreign in its simplicity. But I knew it was real, because it belonged to me. This blonde head is what defines who I am.
