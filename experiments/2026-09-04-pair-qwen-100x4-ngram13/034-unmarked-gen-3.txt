The pews had seen many a Sunday morning gathering,
But now only one soul sat in the pew for hours.
He was lost, as he lay down to die. He knew this,
And when his time came, it was like any other day
But when they left him there alone, he felt the need
To hear some comfort from the heart of someone near.
So as they brought out that Bible and read their book
He heard the familiar words, "Now are you ready"
He looked around at everyone gathered and smiled
As he thought, "I guess I will find my way home" 
He took the last breath
