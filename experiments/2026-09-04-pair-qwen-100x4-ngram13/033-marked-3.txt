The camera, like a faithful companion, moved slowly through our lives. The only thing that would make it stop was its shutter.
He pressed play; the world fell silent except for the ticking of his heart. There were times when he wanted to rewind time to moments before, but now, in the age of technology, there is no way back. Now, it was just him alone with all the pasts and the future.
He looked up at the clock and saw the hands move backwards.
Then he heard it: the sound of silence.
