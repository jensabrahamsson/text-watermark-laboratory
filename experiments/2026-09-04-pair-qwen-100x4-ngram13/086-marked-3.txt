The cinema, where we used to go with our mum every week or so, had been closed down years ago. It was one of those cinemas that seemed to have stayed open longer than it should have, because it had never actually opened.
We’d never seen an old movie there. The seats had always been made of plastic and the screens were white. The only reason we went was to see the big screens when they had a new release. The cinema wasn’t really very good, but if you were lucky, you could see the new films on a Wednesday night before they got all over the place.
