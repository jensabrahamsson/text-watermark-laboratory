The day was long, and the night was not so far off yet. The light was dim, but it had come to a close of the world’s first day in December at last.
“Come on,” came an impatient voice from outside. It didn’t take much for that one word to stir the earth beneath his feet. As the ground began to shake, he realized that his son had finally woken up.
It would be some time before they could return home, but they had plenty of things to do first. He would start with breakfast.
