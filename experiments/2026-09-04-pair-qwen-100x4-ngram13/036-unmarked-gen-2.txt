I have no idea how many apples are on this tree. There are quite a few. This is one of our larger trees, and I am not sure exactly what variety it is either. It has quite large fruit which makes for an impressive harvest. The trees in the orchard are always busy gathering up all of those apples. My husband is happy because he gets to collect them. He takes off his boots and his shirt when he gets home from work, then he heads straight for the apple orchard. I usually just watch him while I eat my lunch at the kitchen table.
