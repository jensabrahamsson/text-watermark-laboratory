The fold in the middle of this 19th century book is so worn that it's practically invisible. The pages are yellowed from age and some have been torn away, but all of the text inside can still be read: "The story of the Pilgrims, a people called Pioneers by their white neighbors." It's not even a hardcover.
It has the date 1875 stamped on its front cover with a silver ink stamp.
But there's more to this piece than just being a relic of American history — or perhaps even of the Civil War itself.
