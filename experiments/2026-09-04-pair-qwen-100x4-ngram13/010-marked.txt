I know it sounds weird, but that’s what happened. After a couple weeks of getting used to my new church (we moved into the building), I found myself going back for one more service on Christmas Eve.
A few minutes after singing Silent Night in our sanctuary, we started walking out towards the parking lot when suddenly, I heard someone shout, “Look!”
We walked over and saw three or four women gathered around, pointing at a woman who was still standing in the aisle. She had her head down, shoulders slumped, looking as though she’d just been told that she didn’t get accepted into a fancy college party.
