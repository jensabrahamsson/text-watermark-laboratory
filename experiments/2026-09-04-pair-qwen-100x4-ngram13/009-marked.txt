The door opened to let in the sun and he looked out at the street. The trees were tall, dark and full of birds chirping as they flew across the sky. His eyes followed the path where they used to walk, but it was empty now. He remembered when he used to see his family walk up and down this street every day after school. It was a beautiful sight. Now, there wasn’t even anyone here to look for. The only thing that seemed normal was the light shining through the window from behind his bedroom door.
