Set in the mid-19th century, it tells the story of how a British woman from the north-west was forced to move to a new part of London to work as a domestic servant for a man who owned slaves. The piece is called ‘Hiding Place’.
Sarah Morris first began working on the play over 20 years ago but its publication will finally see the light this week.
