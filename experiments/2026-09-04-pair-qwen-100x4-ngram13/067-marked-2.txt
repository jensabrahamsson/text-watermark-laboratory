The 2017 World's Most Instagrammed Destination Is Not In The U.S.
With all of its iconic attractions, it’s easy to see why New York City made this year’s list. But a new study from University of Maryland Global Education found that the city wasn’t in the top three most-photographed destinations around the world.
According to the report, which analyzed 56 major travel spots over two years, London was voted the No. 1 spot for Instagramming. That’s followed by Sydney, Australia; Paris, France and Cape Town, South Africa.
