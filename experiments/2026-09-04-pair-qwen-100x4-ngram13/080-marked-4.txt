The mill has not turned in decades and it seems like a great shame. I know that it is not a modern machine, but it was built with wood. The wheels have fallen off from neglect, and the water level has dropped to such a low point that the current can’t move any of the paddle blades anymore. It looks like a beautiful ruin now.
It used to be a very important source of power for the region.
And as a result of the fall of the industry, the river bed became polluted and unsafe for use. The land surrounding the old mill is barren. A few trees are left to remind people of its past glory
