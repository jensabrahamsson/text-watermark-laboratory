The day was so bright I felt like crying with all my eyes. The grass had not moved an inch, it seemed to be frozen in time. The sun shone on the sky like the rays of light. The water surface reflected all the light into this lake.
A man walked towards me slowly with his camera in his hand, and he said to me “I’m here just for you”, then he took the picture of me with a camera.
My heart raced but remained calm when I heard these words. His gaze met mine, and then we were at peace. My eyes could only see what I wanted to see at that moment
