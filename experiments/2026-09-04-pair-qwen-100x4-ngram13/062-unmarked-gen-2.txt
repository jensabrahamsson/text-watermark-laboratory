“Cooks!” the children cried out, their little hands reaching up to grab them. The lanterns hung like ghosts in the cool air, and my heart sank at how much I was missing her. In a week’s time, she would be with me. But I could not come here to watch.
I had been on a mission this night—find my son.
My son had gone through the streets, looking for me as he always did. He knew how much trouble I got myself into. He wanted me to know that it was okay to make mistakes because I’d made them before.
So we were in the city center.
