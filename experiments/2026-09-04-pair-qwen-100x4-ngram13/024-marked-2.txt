The photographs were my great-grandfather’s, and the box was filled with pictures from when he was a child. He was a boy of 12 years old. His parents did not have much money for anything but this box, and it was full of photos they had saved over their lives together. The two young people in the first photo were standing under a tree at the end of a dirt road, looking up at a hill covered with trees and fields. They both wore long white dresses. The girls were laughing, pointing to each other. The boys looked bored. They sat cross-legged on the grass and held books under their arms
