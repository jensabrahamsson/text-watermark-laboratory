The day had been long for all of us. We'd gotten up at 4 am to catch a train out of the city, and after that we'd had a short walk across the river to our hotel, where we'd sat by the window and waited for the rest of the group to arrive.
By about 1 pm we'd started eating lunch at the orchard smokehouse, which has been around since the turn of the century, and is located on the property of an old orchard. It's in a little town outside of Boston called Harvard Junction. The smokehouse was a popular spot in the 19th century
