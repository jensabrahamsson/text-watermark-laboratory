The music she played filled the room and moved every one. She was the most talented of all the girls, but she had one fear - the fear of failure. The door wouldn't shut properly, so she couldn't play in front of other people.
She tried many times to practice behind the door. But it never worked out. Her father always found something wrong with it and asked her to fix it.
"Can you fix the door for me?" he said to her.
"Yes," she replied, "but it is going to take me time."
"No problem," he answered.
