The woman wanted to buy the stamp but was uncertain what it would look like. She finally decided on the one that looked most like the card she had written, so she bought that stamp.
When she arrived at her friend's house and gave the card as a gift, she realized that her stamp had been misplaced! So now she needed to go back home, find the correct stamp, and buy another stamp in order to mail out her card.
How is this story related to today's lesson?
As students think about how to express themselves in various ways, they will see examples of different writing styles: descriptive, narrative, informative, etc.
