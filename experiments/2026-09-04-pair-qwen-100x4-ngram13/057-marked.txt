The day was long, hot, dry. The trees on both sides of his property were ajar in summer’s heat. They looked like they’d been squeezed in, squeezed out by the sun, squeezed between the trees. He sat cross-legged under one tree, the sun blazing down on him. His head throbbed from the effort of keeping the fire burning; he could not stop thinking about his son.
