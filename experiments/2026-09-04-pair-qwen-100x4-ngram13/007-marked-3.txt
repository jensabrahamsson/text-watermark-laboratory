The fishmonger's store was a small house built right on the seafront, just a short walk away from his customers. A long narrow alleyway led to his door, and it was filled with all kinds of seafood. The place smelled wonderful, but also a bit dirty, like it hadn't been cleaned in months. He had an old-fashioned way of operating; he didn't keep records or even use scales. All his customers came by every day, bought whatever they wanted, and left without looking at each other.
