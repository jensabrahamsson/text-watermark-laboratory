He climbed them, and then sat down on his chair, next to the stove, and began drinking a mug of coffee. After a moment he noticed that something was off about the stairs – one step seemed to be higher than it should have been. He pushed up hard against the top rung, but it didn’t move an inch.
He turned back around and saw the door open in the corridor, with a man standing by the threshold, looking at him. The man looked like any other person he had ever seen; balding, with a mustache, wearing the same clothes as everyone else.
