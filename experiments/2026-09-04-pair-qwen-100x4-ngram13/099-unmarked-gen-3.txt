When the traffic stopped he looked at his watch, and it read 10:38pm. A minute later the last bus would be coming. He had only half an hour to run home with the chip in his hand.
But what to do?
The boy had two options:
He could go back and take the next bus but that would mean walking home in pitch dark. He might have made it or not.
Or he could run, grab the chip off the kerb, and then sprint to get to his house before the last bus arrived!
So here was the question – which one is faster?
We can’t answer this
