The girl practiced for hours. She stretched and stretched, but nothing came out right.
Eventually the door opened and in walked her mother.
“Your scales look great,” said the mother, “but you need to make sure your fingers are on all four sides.”
And with that the door closed again and the girl set to work again. She worked diligently and by the time she was done she had perfectly balanced scales. When she turned to the mirror and saw how well they were balanced she decided to show them off to the world.
