I don’t mean to say he was a carpenter. He wasn’t, as he was an architect of sorts, but he’d worked in carpentry his entire life – from making shelves and cabinets for people to building barns and outbuildings to designing houses for others. What he did best, though, was create the space within the structure itself by means of lighting, furniture placement, and color. When someone came to him with a vision for what they wanted their home to be, he could translate that into something beautiful that not only fulfilled that wish but also left them feeling happy in its presence.
