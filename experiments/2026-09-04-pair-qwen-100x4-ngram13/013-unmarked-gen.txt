He asked her for some more money.
They went to another town, where there were two different kinds of stamps: some were very expensive and some weren’t. She told him that she wanted to buy an expensive stamp, but he refused to give her any.
So she took out a pen and drew a large X on each stamp and then bought them all. Then she gave the money back to him.
After he had gone home, she counted the money in her purse again. The number was 1023!
How many stamps did she count? (Do not include the “X” marks.)
