The creak of the wheel was enough to startle a squirrel who scampered across the roof like a cat.
At first, the squirrel seemed unfazed by the porter. He reached out his hand and touched the mouse-sized button that would stop the sound. The squirrel didn’t seem impressed, but instead scurried down the chimney. The porter leaned forward and watched as the small animal darted up the spire until it disappeared over the top of the dome. Then he grabbed the suitcase again, took off into the night, and headed toward a city where he could catch the next flight back home.
