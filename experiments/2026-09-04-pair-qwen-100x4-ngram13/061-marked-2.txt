The paintbrush was so dirty that he had to use a lot of soap to clean it.
He used his toothbrush and scrubbed the paintbrush for a long time. When he finally washed it in the sink, all the paint was gone.
When he held the paintbrush up to the light, he could see no paint at all on the brush.
Now when he paints he uses this same paintbrush. It has always worked well for him.
