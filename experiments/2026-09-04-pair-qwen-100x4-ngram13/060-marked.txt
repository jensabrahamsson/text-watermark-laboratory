The ducks were in the shallows on the beach, pecking at the mud. The planks moved. A duck with a leg broken and the other half under water could not stand up; its back legs would stick out at right angles to the bottom of the board and wobble when it walked along them.
As I watched the birds, they seemed to be dancing or preening or perhaps just playing; I couldn’t tell because most were too far away for me to see what was going on.
