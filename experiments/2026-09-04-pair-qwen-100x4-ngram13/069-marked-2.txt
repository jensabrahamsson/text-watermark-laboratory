The day was filled with so many people that she didn’t even realize that it had started to rain. She could have left for the library on her bike, but instead she sat by a tree in the park near her house and read under the light of the moon.
The rain made her think of something she hadn’t really thought about before: life. She lived a normal life; nothing too special happened. But then she saw her mother crying one evening while she was visiting. And now, thinking about it, she realized that the last few months had been like this.
