The porter could hear the sound but could not stop it.
He was tired of hearing the chirping. He wanted to take a break and rest.
So he asked a passenger if they would mind letting him step out of their seat.
The passenger, who had no luggage or anything else to put his feet up, agreed.
The porter took one hand from the side pocket of his shirt and began walking slowly down the aisle.
As he went along, he let out a chirp here and there with each step.
The passenger looked around at all the other people in the car who were staring at him, but he did not say anything.
