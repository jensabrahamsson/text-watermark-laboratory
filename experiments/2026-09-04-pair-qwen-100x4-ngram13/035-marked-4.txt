The first bell rang 20 times, and the second bell rang 30 times. What is the sum of all the times the name was called?
To find the sum of all the times the name was called, we simply add together the number of times each bell rang:

First bell: 20 times
Second bell: 30 times

Sum = 20 + 30 = 50 times
