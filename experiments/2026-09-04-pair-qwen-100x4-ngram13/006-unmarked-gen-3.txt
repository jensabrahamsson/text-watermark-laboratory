“Can you believe they don’t even use a dropbox? What’s the point of having a place like this?”
He placed the stack in his cart, and left. He was on his way to work as a janitor. The job suited him just fine: it paid enough to pay his rent, and he didn’t have to deal with the public at all.
It was a cold evening in January. The streets were empty, but not desolate; most people had long gone home after work, and only some would venture out after dark for their own reasons.
In the corner of his eye, he saw something move.
