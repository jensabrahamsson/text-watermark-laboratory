The day was so clear, with a white blanket of clouds that looked like butter. A few sheep had gone into it and were frolicking among the reeds on the shallow water. They had their heads bent down under the sun, their wooly coats flickering and shimmering. They were as small as if made from clay; their bellies rose and fell gently in rhythm to the gentle rocking motion of the water.
