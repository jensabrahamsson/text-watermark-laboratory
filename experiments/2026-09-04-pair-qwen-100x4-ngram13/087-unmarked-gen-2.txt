A small room. A table, a bed, shelves. No windows and no doors. No signs of life other than the fire in the hearth.
The watchman looked around. The only thing out of place was the man he had found sleeping on the bed.
A light came on in the corner, a voice said: "Don't be afraid."
The man was a stranger to him. He was not a hunter or a thief. What kind of person would find himself here? How could one come into this world without having any memory of where they were from? It was strange how such a thing happened.
