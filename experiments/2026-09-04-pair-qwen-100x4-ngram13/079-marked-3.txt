"I have never seen anything so funny," said the visitor of the barbershop, and the visitor laughed all he could. The barber replied: "I am glad you are pleased with it."
The visitor asked for some money. "What kind of person does not understand me?" said the barber. He handed him five dollars in change.
It is not easy to describe what happened next. It was quite funny! But I will do my best to tell how things went on as they really did happen.
