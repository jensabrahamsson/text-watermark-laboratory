She put the coat on. She didn’t realize she was buttoning it up until the coat was right around her neck. She looked in the mirror. The collar of the coat covered the buttons, and when she lifted it, they came off.
The man standing by the door smiled at her, then walked past her.
When she tried to pull the buttons back into place, there were no more holes left for them to go through.
