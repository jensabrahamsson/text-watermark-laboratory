The day was long, a string of events with no clear beginning or end. I walked from one place to another on this road, my heart racing at every turn as it did when I would leave for work. As usual, the rain poured down in buckets, making my clothes stick to me like glue. There were too many places I wanted to go but not enough time to be there. A constant cycle of work, school, home, bed, repeat, repeating over and over.
But then, like an odd dream, I found myself outside, the sound of the rain fading into the distance, and something about today felt different.
