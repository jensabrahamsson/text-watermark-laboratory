The 53-year-old man, who has lived in Singapore for more than 20 years, was walking along Sengkang East Street 7 yesterday morning when he noticed an alert on his phone. It read: "Crossing at [address] is closed." The message led him to turn left onto Bukit Timah Road instead of going straight as usual.
Mr Tan said: "I went right at first because I thought the other direction might have a problem too. When I got close to [the address], I saw that there were no pedestrians crossing so I turned back."
