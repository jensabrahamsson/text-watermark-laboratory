The cow's name was called "Dare" because when he tried to milk her, she would stand perfectly still.
He couldn't believe his luck so much that he let her be at it until sunset. He went home with a very good calf.
This was Dare's baby. His grandfather had taught him how to ride a horse and gallop in a circle.
When they got home, the cow gave birth to three calves. He did nothing except give them names. It seemed like all three of the young ones were running around together.
