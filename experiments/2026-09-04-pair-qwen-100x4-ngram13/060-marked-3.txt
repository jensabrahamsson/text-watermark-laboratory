The ducks were in the marsh, too. They were waiting for the chance to make their run at the man. I stood and watched from my truck as they began to circle. I could tell they were agitated and ready for action.
As soon as it was clear they weren’t going to be caught, they began to move quickly. The sound of wings beating against the air filled the air, followed by the rustling grass as they made their way down towards me. I couldn’t see them very well until they came into view.
