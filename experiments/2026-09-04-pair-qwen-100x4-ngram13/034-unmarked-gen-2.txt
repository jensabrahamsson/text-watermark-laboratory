She’d had a dream that she was in a strange place, one she didn’t remember. The ground sloped down at either side into hills with lush green grass; there were no trees to be seen but only tall rocks and boulders. She saw two men walking through a small village which seemed so peaceful; they passed by houses that she recognized from her childhood, even though the men were strangers. They talked quietly and looked like ordinary people – just as she did, now she thought about it.
