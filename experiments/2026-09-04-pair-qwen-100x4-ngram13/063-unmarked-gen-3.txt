Police are investigating a case of alleged animal cruelty after a 79-year-old man was caught on camera feeding the same gull more than three times.
The man has been ordered to complete 150 hours of community service and pay £4,300 in compensation to the charity Seachange for damage caused to the bird's wing and eye by eating raw food.
