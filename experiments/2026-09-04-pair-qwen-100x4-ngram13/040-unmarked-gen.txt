The carpet pattern was designed by me. It is the result of a combination of different materials, colors and designs that I've used in the past few years. It looks like an abstract painting on the carpet, with some geometric patterns, floral motifs, and some random shapes. The carpet is made up of two layers - one for the base and one for the decorative layer. The base layer is quite thin, so it's easy to step on if you're not careful. The decorative layer is thicker and more durable, so it feels better underfoot when walking on it. Overall, I'm quite happy with how this piece turned out.
