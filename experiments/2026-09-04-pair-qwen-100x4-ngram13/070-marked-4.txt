The sky was as clear as an old shoe. There was no wind and a faint chill in the air.
I sat on the deck of my tiny sailboat and stared out at the vast ocean stretching before me, its surface calm and placid like a lake after rain.
This is why I left my home in England and bought a boat – because I love to sail, especially during these kind of sunny days when the wind isn’t too strong.
So this day had come. The day when the first gusts of wind would arrive and take us into the Atlantic Ocean. I was looking forward to it with eager anticipation.
