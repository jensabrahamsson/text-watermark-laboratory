This is a very good example of "just in time" or JIT. The milkman collects all the containers on Wednesday, leaving them outside for pickup Thursday morning. On Thursday night, the factory takes the bottles they have received to process (the same as yesterday). The process produces milk bottles again.
In JIT systems there are no stocks, only demand.
