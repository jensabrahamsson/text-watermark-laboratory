The old gate of the Old Town, at 1500 Market Street in Philadelphia, closed down in December, but its replacement will open this spring, and the old one is going up for sale on eBay soon.
Built in 1873 as part of the Old City Mall, it originally stood at Chestnut and Market streets (now Market). The first building to stand at that spot was built by Robert E. Lee's family in 1802.
