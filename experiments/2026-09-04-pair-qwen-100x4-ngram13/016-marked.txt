The day was long, and the night was a short time away.
I watched my children play outside in their pajamas, with only a towel for a shirt. The stars were clear that night, and I knew how much of an influence they were on them.
It is so easy to get caught up in our lives we forget the simple pleasures. To remember that life isn’t always about getting better. Life is often just about enjoying what you have right now.
They had the best moments and most wonderful stories to tell me. The things that brought them joy. And they let me take photos, because they trusted me.
That’s all
