The music she played filled the room and moved every being inside. The sound was so strong it caused one to move. It could be felt, heard, touched by everyone in the building. The girl continued playing until no one was left standing. Her practice was over. As her scales came to rest on the floor, the doors shut tight, sealing the entire place from sound for the duration of her practice.
It was quiet.
Quiet enough for someone else to enter. Quiet enough for a person who had never seen this room before to stand there and listen.
