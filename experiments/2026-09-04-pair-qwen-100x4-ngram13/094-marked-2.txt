The cricket pavilion clock is often a rather imposing building, with grandeur and majesty. However, in many cases, it has no hands, so that it doesn't show the time of day. It just stands there, with its grandness and magnificence, gathering people at tea! It can be used to make up for an absence of the hands on other clocks.
