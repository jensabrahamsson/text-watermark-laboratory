By the time they arrived, a small party of 21 was seated on the lawn in front of the dressing rooms. The cricket team from England was sitting there with their supporters, watching the first day’s play as it unfolded. In an hour or so, they would move inside to their hotel, having finished their tea.
In the meantime, there were several things happening around the grounds. The crowd was getting bigger all the time – some English tourists who had come from abroad for the test match, had brought friends, then more friends.
