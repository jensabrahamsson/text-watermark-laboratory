And there were many signs of it. A big, yellowish puddle on the floor, a broken water faucet in one corner, and even a dead bird perched by the wall.
But no signs of an actual ice cream cart.
In fact, I'm pretty sure the only real sign was the padlock at the front door that read "closed."
I was disappointed but not upset to see it - I've been coming to this specific pier for years. And though I like the food better than the other ones I've tried nearby (like 150 Ocean Avenue), I've always preferred their pier over their storefront location.
