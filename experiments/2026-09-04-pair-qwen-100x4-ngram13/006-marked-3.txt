The local newspaper read only in the car park.
The post office was a long, dark tunnel with an automatic teller machine at one end and no cashiers.
The coffee shop was always empty on Sundays. I would go there to sit down and have a cup of coffee, but it was always crowded by Monday morning.
I went back to work. I worked in a call center that was mostly computer-based. The job wasn’t bad, but I hated it when I first started because I had a hard time sitting behind a desk all day without any movement.
