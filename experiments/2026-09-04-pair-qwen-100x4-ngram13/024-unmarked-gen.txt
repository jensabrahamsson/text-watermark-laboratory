A photograph in the box was missing.
The room seemed empty, but when I walked into it, I saw everything I needed right there: the attic window, the photos, and my mother sitting in the armchair. My mom never left home without her camera. When we were young, she would take pictures at every single event. In our house, everyone had a special photo album that contained family memories—birthdays, weddings, funerals.
As I got older, I began to feel more like an outsider than an insider. At first, I was proud of being able to go places where I wasn’t supposed to be going.
