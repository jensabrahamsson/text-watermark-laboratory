The gate fell open and the boats poured through.
It was not only boats, but many other things.
And when I heard it, I knew we were coming.
A boat came past on the other side of the locks. It was small, its crew less than three. The boat had no sail, no mast, and no rudder.
The boat passed by so quickly that my eyes couldn’t keep up. But when the boat reached the bottom of the lock and began to rise, the men lifted their little boat from the water and lowered it onto the steps at the top of the locks.
