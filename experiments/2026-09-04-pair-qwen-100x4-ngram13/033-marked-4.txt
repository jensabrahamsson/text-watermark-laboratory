The camera, like a faithful companion, moved slowly through our lives. The movie camera that could capture what was going on in the world, it could show us the way to go, but at times when we need the still frame of life we are left with nothing.
A friend of mine said he had a dream about me this morning. He told me I looked so happy. I can only imagine he meant in the dream world and not the real one as well as his face turned up towards the camera, but for some reason I felt like the camera captured something in my spirit. It wasn't anything bad just something special or magical.
