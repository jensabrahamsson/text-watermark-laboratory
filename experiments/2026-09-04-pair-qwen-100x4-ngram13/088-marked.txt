The leaves would fall to the water, then float back to the surface. Then they would dry out, then fall again until finally they were left with only their sheaths in which they could rest.
These sheaths are very interesting objects for a number of reasons. First, I have never seen a real floating leaf that looks like this before, let alone one as perfect as this. It’s almost like they’re holding up the entire sheath so it doesn’t sink, while at the same time being able to move freely through the air.
Secondly, they have an interesting pattern on them, not unlike how we humans do sometimes.
