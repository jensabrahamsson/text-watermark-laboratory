I've never been to the Pier 54, so I didn't know what it was all about. When you walk in there, you find that they serve ice cream at this little place called The Ice Cream Bar. It has some really cool decorations inside - including a model ship which is floating in water! You can buy different sized cones and choose from over fifty different flavors. One of my favorites is the Peanut Butter and Chocolate Fudge Sundae, and you can even customize your sundae by choosing toppings like M&M's, chocolate chips, pretzels, or cookie crumbs!
They also have a great selection of hot beverages
