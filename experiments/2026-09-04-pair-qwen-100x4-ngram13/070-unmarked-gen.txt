My heart had been stabbed by the same sharp knife, but this time it was me who was bleeding.
A small girl, maybe a year old, with dark hair, stood on the pier holding her mother’s hand. Her face showed no emotion, not even fear or sadness. She looked like a statue, and so did her mother.
The little girl kept holding the other side of the mother’s hand tightly while they were standing on the pier. I couldn’t tell whether she was holding it for support or to comfort her mother.
