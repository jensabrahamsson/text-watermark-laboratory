An artist's rendering of the proposed murals in the underpass at Bay and Main.
The artist who created the mural that was supposed to adorn the walls of a San Francisco underpass said Monday she has been approached by city officials about painting a new mural, after city staff determined it would not be allowed on public property.
A new, smaller version of the mural will be displayed instead. The original design for the large mural was based on a photo from 1920, which is part of the National Register of Historic Places and shows the intersection in the early days of San Francisco.
