The music was beautiful, a mix of folk tunes from across Europe. We sat in the church, singing along as we listened to a young woman sing with such power and grace that it moved us all to tears.
And then she stood at the pulpit, picked up her guitar, and began playing a gentle song that filled our hearts with peace and joy.
She sang until there were no more words left to say or notes left in her voice and still we did not want to end the night.
She had an audience, but the most important thing to her is the people who have gathered to celebrate this time together in the name of God
