It was the first time he had visited this place, yet in that one moment it seemed to be an ancient temple. The stone walls and the cobbled paths gave him the feeling of having wandered into another age. A world he felt very much at home in, though only for the few minutes spent on the shore of the river.
A small wind stirred the trees. They rustled softly as if they were asleep, but his footsteps carried him forward along the path. He reached out and touched the cold stones; he felt as if someone else could still be there under these floors.
