The cricket pavilion clock is no more but it has still gathered the attention of the world as a symbol of human endeavor and willpower. The pavilion itself has been standing since 1926, while the clock on top of it has been in existence for nearly 40 years. It was put up to mark the centenary of the first ever match between England and Australia played there.
There is no doubt that this clock is the largest cricket clock in the world and has stood tall for over five decades. There have been some very interesting stories surrounding its construction too, though the exact details are not clear.
