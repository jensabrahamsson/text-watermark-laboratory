The woman who had come from a country not far from here, with a very small amount of money in her purse, had become rich, but could no longer find what she sought.
She felt so unhappy that she wanted to take her own life.
But at this moment, I met a man named Xiao Li. After talking for a while, he said that he would help me and brought me to his house, where he kept all kinds of precious things.
