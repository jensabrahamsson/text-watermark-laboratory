He knew the crosswalk was safe.
The light had turned green just seconds ago.
He wanted to cross. Now. Right now. But he did not.
Instead, he stopped a block away, where his friend's house is located. He could have walked home or continued on foot, but why? Why go that route?
He wanted to be with his friend; there was something about her that drew him like an old lover—his mind was flooded with thoughts of her, which were all of them good ones.
