The creaking, though not loud or alarming, could be enough to startle a young child. I was told it would only happen when the house had many guests.
I wasn’t looking forward to the stairs creaking and rattling on my own feet as well, but I did so anyway because of one thing: my friend. My dear friend who knew everything about everything and never failed to ask me “What’s your next adventure?” It would be like he would never run out of questions for me, which is why this was our next stop after breakfast – to chat with him over coffee at his place in Los Angeles.
