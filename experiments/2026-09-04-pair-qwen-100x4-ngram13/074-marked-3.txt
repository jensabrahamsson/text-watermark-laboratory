I felt the cold air from outside and the heat of the stove. I wanted to go out but my hands and feet hurt, they were numb with frostbite from being in the snow for so long. My clothes had been soaked through, so now my skin felt like it was frozen too. The rain had also been hard on me; I could feel the dampness in my boots and the dampness under my arms and neck as well.
