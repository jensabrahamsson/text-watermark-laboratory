The greenhouse had been built by the previous owner, a large one with plenty of room for vegetables.
A shed in the garden was converted to an office as it is so big. I think it has also become a studio for some art work and craft projects.
There are 2 sheds at this cottage - one with a very old looking garage style structure, the other being modern steel. I can't help but think about how different life would have been here before these two buildings were erected! The first is quite a substantial space (as are many outbuildings) but then again, we don't have that much land here!
