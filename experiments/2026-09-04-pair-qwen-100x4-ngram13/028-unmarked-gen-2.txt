They had been raised for the first time in months. The lock keeper stood on his bridge and watched as boats entered the estuary and left to head back out again. There was no wind, so there was no current to move them through. He didn’t have any boats to help with the locks either, so he stayed at his post and kept an eye on things. As they all moved past, the gate closed with a loud thump that echoed off the canal banks and around the lock house.
