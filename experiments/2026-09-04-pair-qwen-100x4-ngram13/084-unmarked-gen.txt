I got home from work, had dinner and did the dishes. I was in my pajamas but was up early to get to work.
I was just sitting on the couch with my laptop when someone knocked on the door. I was not expecting anyone so I thought that maybe there was something wrong at work.
After opening the door, I found out it was my mom who is always around at this time of night and wanted me to go into the bathroom to clean it before she leaves for the day.
It turned out that she forgot to clean it while I was out and I would have cleaned it myself except that I had been eating.
