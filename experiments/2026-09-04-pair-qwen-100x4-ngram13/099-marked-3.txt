The driver pulled up, saw him and said, "Sorry, but you can't get in." The man was so desperate for his chips that he decided to ask if he could sleep by the side of the road. So the driver gave him some space and asked where he was going. He replied, "I'm just waiting for my wife to pick me up."
The driver thought it would be best not to argue and drove off. Later, the bus arrived again and he noticed the man still there. He stopped and went back to get the bag of chips before driving away.
A few minutes later, the same bus arrived again.
