The paintbrush was so dirty that he had to use a lot of soap to clean it.
He used his toothbrush and scrubbed the paintbrush for a long time. When he finally washed it in the sink, all the dirt had come out. The water turned clear! He put the brush in a jar and set it aside for future use.
If you want to remove paint from your brushes, rinse them well with water or in a tub full of warm soapy water. Don't wash them with just hot water because it won't work. If you try to wash them this way, you will probably find some of the paint
