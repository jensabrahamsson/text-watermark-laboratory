The door opened to let in light. The man looked back and saw nothing; he turned away from the torch's glare.
"Is that all?" the watchman asked.
"None of it," replied the man, and took off his cap as he was about to turn around. He had long brown hair, and was bald on top.
"How do you do?" he said when he turned around.
"I am John Smith," replied the man, and the two men shook hands.
"Well, Mr. Smith," continued the watchman, "I am Henry Jones. What business have you here?"
