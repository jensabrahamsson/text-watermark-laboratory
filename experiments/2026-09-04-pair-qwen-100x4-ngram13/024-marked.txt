The attic room was empty and dusty. The curtains were pulled back, letting in an occasional gust of wind. It felt like it was taking the last few breaths of spring before the summer months arrived. An autumnal chill settled into the air as I closed the shutters for the night. The attic room was always dark and quiet. A dim light from the hallway would be all I needed to see the way home. My grandmother’s pictures laid out on top of the dresser, each one more faded than the next. Some were still in perfect condition, while others were already in pieces; their frames shattered and their contents spilling everywhere
