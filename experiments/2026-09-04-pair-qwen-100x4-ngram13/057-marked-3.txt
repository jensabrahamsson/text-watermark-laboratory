The day was long, a hot day, and he worked until the sun set. His eyes had begun to hurt, so when night fell, he put up his tent for shelter.
The next morning he awoke early, as soon as the first light of dawn broke through. He got out of bed and started gathering the wood that would keep him warm tonight. After all, there was no way he could spend another night without getting sick from all that smoke. The firewood was in the eave above his bed. Sooner or later, he'd have to get down there and pick some up, right?
