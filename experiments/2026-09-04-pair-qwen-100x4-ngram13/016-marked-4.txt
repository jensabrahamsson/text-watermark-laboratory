The day was long, and the night was a short time away.
I woke to find my bedroom door open, but didn’t want to tell anyone. It had been quiet, so I figured everyone was asleep already. But when I got up from bed to get ready for school, it struck me that someone had gone through the room, probably searching for something or perhaps even trying to sneak out at night.
This isn’t new; we’ve had our share of intruders before. It happened once, not long ago, when a couple had broken into our apartment in our building. The police were there quickly and took care of everything
