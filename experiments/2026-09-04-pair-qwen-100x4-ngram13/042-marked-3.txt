The lighthouse keeper, who is not my real-life relative (I’m a writer), was an older man. He had lived in this cottage for many years, and had always been the guardian of the beacon.
He was proud of his job and took it very seriously, but he didn’t enjoy the loneliness or isolation that came with it.
