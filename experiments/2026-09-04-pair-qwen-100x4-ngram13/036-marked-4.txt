The day is cool and clear. The breeze brings a gentle rustling of leaves, a soft hum from a few birds that have returned from migration. A small river runs through it, the water still but in anticipation for the rain. We sit by the fire, talking, sharing stories. My father has told me about his childhood, his struggles with the family he married into, all of them gone now. I listen intently as he explains every detail without missing one word. He smiles when he finishes, the smile I know only comes from deep within.
