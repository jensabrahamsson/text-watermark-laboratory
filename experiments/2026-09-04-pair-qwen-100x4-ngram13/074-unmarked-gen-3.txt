But I didn’t have to go far. The place is in the middle of the city, which means it’s easy enough to get there by subway or bus from most places, but for those who want a bit more action on their weekend getaway, a taxi is not only possible but encouraged (there are some really nice ones around). And if you don’t feel like taking a ride into town, it’s just about 15 minutes by bike, with a decent route between the station and the restaurant itself (or 30 minutes on foot, with the same distance in the other direction).
