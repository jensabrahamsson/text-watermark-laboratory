I’ve never been able to find it. Maybe I just don’t know where it was, but I can still picture every detail as clearly as if they were fresh from the pages and pressed in my memory.
My sister had a car accident. It wasn’t the first time; she had two other accidents within six months of each other. My parents said that it happened because she was “too much like her mother.” They called it an “accident,” but what they meant by that is that she didn’t make any effort not to get hurt; she let things happen without thinking about the consequences or the possibility of getting caught in them
