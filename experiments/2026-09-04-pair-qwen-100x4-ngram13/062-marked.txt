The day was long, hot, and the people who had slept through most of it were up early. There wasn’t much time to rest for these people – they needed food, shelter, and a way out before their lives became hell as the cold dawn arrived in this place where all dreams went wrong. These people would soon be sleeping on the streets again, their hopes crushed by a system no better than theirs could ever hope to create.
