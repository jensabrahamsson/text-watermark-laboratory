The photographs were from a time when I had not yet moved out, and my life was simpler. When I had a full-time job as an assistant in an art gallery. I used to visit the galleries on weekends with my father and grandfather. We would wander through the corridors admiring the paintings and sculptures and taking notes about the names of the artists we found interesting. My grandmother would be watching over us at home, knitting and sewing, or cooking.
