The cinema, where we used to go with our mum every week or so, had been closed down years ago. It was one of those places that everyone could have a few drinks and watch some rubbish films before leaving at midnight.
It has always bothered me that there is this little theatre here in Ballymena which is still going strong. It's got a great reputation as an up-and-coming venue for live music and events and it's also the place to see new and emerging talent.
The owner, Brian Duggan, is just a charming guy who runs a real family business - not just a run-of-the-mill
