A drill at a high school in Florida was captured on video and shared by local news outlets. The drill, which involved students leaving their lockers and moving to designated safety spots in case of a fire, lasted less than 15 minutes, but many people watching the video thought it went on much longer. The cause: A student told them he had to sit there so long because some kids left without him.
In the video, a teacher shouts over the sounds of students running outside "Fire drill is starting, now go to your fire station." The video then cuts away
