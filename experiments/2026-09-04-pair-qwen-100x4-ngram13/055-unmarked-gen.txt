He had seen some squirrels in the bushes and was hungry. There wasn’t much of anything edible about the food stalls here, but he would have preferred them to this road food stand anyway. Squirrels were delicious when cooked up properly. But this place served burnt, smoky stuff. It’s not that he needed something else; he was never really interested in eating, he just enjoyed watching people and animals while they did it. But squirrels were different – his favorite meal – so he went with them.
