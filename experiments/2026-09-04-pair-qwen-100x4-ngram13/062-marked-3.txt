The day was long, hot, and the people who had come for the market were tired. They came from across the city to see these vendors and this food in person, but the heat had left them drowsy. The vendors, however, were energetic, ready to serve customers even as the sun began to set.
This was the day after the fire had occurred, but a strange calm had settled over the streets. The market had begun with only a few stands open, but by the end of the afternoon, every vendor had opened their shop for business.
