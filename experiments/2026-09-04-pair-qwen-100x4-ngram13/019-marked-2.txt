The pigeons have been on this farm for years, nesting in hollow logs or crevices in walls. They come to the back of the barn when they hear the cows and calves yelp and stamp their hooves on the ground. In the late afternoon, the farmer will feed them a small bag of corn or a handful of cracked wheat. They are a family, with males calling out from the tops of the barns and hatching new chicks every spring.
