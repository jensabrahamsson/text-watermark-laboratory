"I think it's from the bread class," said a student.

It was a mathematics problem. The students had to find the area of a circle. But no one could solve it.
The teacher explained, "Let me show you what the answer is."
She then took out a bag of flour and began making dough.

"Wait!" cried the teacher. 
"That bread will not be ready in time for today's lesson!"
"But I can do it tomorrow!" cried the students.
"No way! This afternoon."

But the teacher went on with her work, mixing the flour and kneading the dough until it was soft enough to roll into circles
