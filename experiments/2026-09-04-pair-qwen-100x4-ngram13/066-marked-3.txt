The church had a beautiful interior and it was difficult to get pictures in all the light coming from one angle. However, I managed to capture the inside of this old church.
One of my favorite spots at the church is the balcony overlooking the sanctuary and garden area.
The church has been here since 1870 so there are a lot of stories and legends attached to the place. For example, one says that if you visit on a Sunday night, you will find a small statue of Saint Peter sleeping. If you wake him up he'll give you his blessing for the next seven days.
