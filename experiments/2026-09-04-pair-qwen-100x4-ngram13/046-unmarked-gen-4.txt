It was one of the last sunny days of winter before spring, and all the clouds were gone from the sky except for the ones over the quarry. It had been raining heavily on and off since the start of January. The sun would have to wait until the end of March or April for its first day in the year.
He took his camera out of his backpack and pointed it at the top of the quarry wall where the sun had just appeared. He clicked a few times as the image of the sky became clear again and the bright white light spread across the gray stone.
