The day was long and dark. He did not know when it would stop raining. There were no lights on in the buildings or even in the streets. The air was filled with an eerie silence. The sound of rain hitting the pavement and of water dripping from roof-tops seemed to be the only noise left in this world. It made him feel lonely; the lack of a sound.
He stood at the edge of a square where all of the buildings shared one wall. There were only about six other people around, most probably waiting for their turn to go inside. They looked tired, dirty, like they had been running for days.
