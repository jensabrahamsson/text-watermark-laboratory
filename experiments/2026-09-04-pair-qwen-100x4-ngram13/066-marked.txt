The woman, who was not a member of the church, stood near it and placed it carefully on its stand. She walked up to the pulpit and turned off the light. She then moved around the small room and sat down in one of the chairs facing the cross where a man lay dead. The man lay on his back and he had no face at all; just a mass of flesh.
She sat for several minutes before getting up again and walking slowly to the cross and placing her hand gently on it.
Then she took hold of the other hand of the man and began talking softly.
I remember when I first met you years ago
