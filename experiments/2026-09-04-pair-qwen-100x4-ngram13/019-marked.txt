The pigeons have been everywhere in this disaster area.
It is clear that you will find no refuge here. Pigeon droppings can spread disease, and even cause death. In fact, they were called the carriers of plague. They spread the infection to humans when people touched them. This means that the pigeons are also vectors for spreading viruses, bacteria, and other diseases. Therefore, you should never touch or feed pigeons, including those that you might see after disasters.
