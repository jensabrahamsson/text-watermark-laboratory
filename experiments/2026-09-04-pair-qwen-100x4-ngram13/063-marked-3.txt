The 82-year-old man was convicted of feeding fish at the River Liffey, Dublin, and of being in possession of a prohibited article with intent to cause unnecessary suffering to animals - in this case the birds of prey.
Judge Paul Kelly said it would be "absolutely outrageous" if anyone other than an animal welfare officer were prosecuted for the act.
Garda Inspector John Gogarty told the court the man knew there was a ban on the act, but carried out his act anyway because he believed he could get away with it. He also said people should not believe what they read online about illegal acts taking place elsewhere.
