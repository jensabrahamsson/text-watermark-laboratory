The fishmonger's store was a small house built right on the seafront, just a short walk away from his customers. A long boardwalk stretched along the edge of the building; it led to his fish market in the water and a beach with some rocks jutting out into the bay where fisherman would come to bring their catch for sale. It was a very busy area that day as a lot of tourists were in town, taking advantage of cheap seafood deals offered by local vendors.
