"I have a lot of work to do today," he said. "I'm going to build that fence for you tomorrow."
The man had been looking at the fence as it rusted away, not wanting to touch it because it was so old. But when his son asked him if he wanted to help build it, he agreed to try.
"I have to get it done by the time my daughter gets home from school tonight," he told his son. "If I don't get it built, she'll think I'm lazy."
So the two boys began working together. They set up tools and started putting in posts around the fence.
