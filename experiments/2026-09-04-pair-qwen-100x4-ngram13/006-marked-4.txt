The local newspaper read only in the summer sun, the weekly comic that never reached a single page, and the Sunday Times that had yet to open its first page.
A day without newspapers or magazines would be as normal to him as going out of town with no one knowing where you were going.
He always thought of them like his family. A daily source of information on how the world was changing, and who people trusted.
But he could see there was something more, not just their news coverage but also the way they affected him – like an addiction; he couldn’t go a day without reading them.
It had been two weeks now.
