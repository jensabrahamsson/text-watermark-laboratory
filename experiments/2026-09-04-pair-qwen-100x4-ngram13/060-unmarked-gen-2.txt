I can feel it. It’s right in my bones.
So when I got the call to work on a 1936 Ford model A, I was not really shocked.
As much as I love modern cars and trucks, I think we need more vehicles from the past. And what better way than to fix up a classic with an eye towards safety?
But why would someone build something so old? To be used in some kind of performance modification? Because it was cool? Both could be valid reasons.
Whatever the motivation behind the project, I was excited to work on this vehicle.
