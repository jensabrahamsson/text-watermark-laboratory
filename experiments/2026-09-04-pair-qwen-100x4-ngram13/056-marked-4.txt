They all walked down them together, in their groups of two or three. The group would consist of four: a man, two women and a child; then another three: three men, a woman and a child.
This particular day, there were two pairs standing by the door when it opened to let out the next group of guests, which consisted of a woman and her five-year-old daughter, a couple with their ten-year-old son, and a young man and his ten-month-old daughter. They were greeted by the hostess.
