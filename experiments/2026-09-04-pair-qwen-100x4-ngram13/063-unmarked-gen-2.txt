A pensioner has been banned from the beach for 12 months after she was caught feeding gulls with a bread and butter sandwich while on holiday.
Catherine Davenport, of Goring-by-Sea, near Southampton, used a plastic container with a white cloth over it when feeding the birds as she waited for her husband to go to work at a fishmongers shop.
The 69-year-old was ordered by Weymouth & Portland Council to hand in all the food items which included sandwiches and crusts she threw into the sea, which was considered a public area.
She is not allowed to be present at the pier
