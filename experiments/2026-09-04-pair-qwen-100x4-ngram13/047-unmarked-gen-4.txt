"Dear folks," he said, "I don't know you but I want you to be my friends. Please come closer and tell me who you are." No one came forward.
The man looked at the floor for help. Then his eyes rested on a pair of spectacles perched upon a shelf. He picked them up, dusted them off, and began reading from a book they contained: "My friend, this is the greatest speech ever delivered... This man will not stop until he has done so..."
The man returned to the empty room, and repeated the exercise.
