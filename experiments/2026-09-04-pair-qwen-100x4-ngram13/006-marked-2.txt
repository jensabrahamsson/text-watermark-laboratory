The local newspaper read only in the car park.
The coffee shop was always full, but there were empty tables inside.
The children’s toy store never had any new games or puzzles. The shelves had been restocked just a few weeks ago. Now, they sat dusty and untended.
As I walked by, it seemed like an entire world of possibilities had closed its doors forever. The streets would no longer buzz with excitement from a weekend movie opening or birthday parties at a local school.
