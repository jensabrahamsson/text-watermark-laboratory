The creak of the suitcase echoed through the tunnel.
The sounds made me feel sick and I ran back into the station. The porter laughed at my plight, but didn’t say anything else as he continued to work. My fear was not his problem.
When I entered the tunnel again, I heard someone behind me muttering, “There’s nothing to be afraid of,” in Russian. He was an old man, standing beside a large suitcase that looked like it had been used by someone else before.
