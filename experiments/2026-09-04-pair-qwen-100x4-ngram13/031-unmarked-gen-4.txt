The underlined part is:

"The professor gave us the final exam today."

Which sentence could replace "the final exam" in the above sentence so that it retains its meaning? 
(A) We had to take the first exam of our semester.  
(B) We have already taken the second exam.  
(C) It is time for the last exam we will ever have.  
(D) The exams are over for this term.

The correct answer is (D). 

The phrase "the final exam" indicates that the exam being referred to is the last one in a series or the final one in a certain context, such as a semester
