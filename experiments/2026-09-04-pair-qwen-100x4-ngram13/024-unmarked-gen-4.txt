I took out the old book and looked at my photos. I hadn’t seen them in years.
As I turned each page, the photographs became more and more faded, their colors falling apart as though they were watercolors on old paper.
My heart ached to see them so badly but couldn’t bring myself to look at them again.
But now I saw all my photos. Each one was beautiful, full of color and life. It seemed like no time had passed since I last looked at them.
In this moment, there is nothing but the past.
