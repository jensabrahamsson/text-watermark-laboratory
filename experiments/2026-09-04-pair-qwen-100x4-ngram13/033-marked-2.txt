The camera, which cost 40,000 yuan ($6,352), has been his pride for years. He plans to display it as a "gift" to the public during his life.
"I will be very excited when I see the first frames," he said. "That's when the magic begins."
He has already received 10,000 yuan in donations. But he doesn't want money; he wants to make films that people can enjoy without paying for tickets.
The 67-year-old artist, whose real name is Han Shengqiang, lives alone in an apartment in Beijing
