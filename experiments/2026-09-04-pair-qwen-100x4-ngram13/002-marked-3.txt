The chitter of the wheels echoed in my mind. I remembered the first time when my sister, her hands folded in prayer, looked up at me and said: "Please don't tell your mom. Please!" I had to promise she wouldn't get into trouble. That was about four years ago. The same day, my mother told us that we could go to a family picnic if we didn't come home before midnight.
I was eight then and already an orphan. My brother, who used to be very much like me, now was only two years younger than me and still too young to understand anything or make sense of things.
