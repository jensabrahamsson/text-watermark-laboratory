The leaves would fall to the water, then float back to the surface. Then they would dry out, then fall again until finally they were left with only their own weight to support them, suspended from the edge of the trough like a little leaf sculpture. The leaves themselves would die off; this process repeating itself endlessly in perpetuity as the leaves continued to cycle up to the top of the trough.
