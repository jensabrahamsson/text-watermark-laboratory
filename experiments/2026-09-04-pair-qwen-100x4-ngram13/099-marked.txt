The driver pulled up, saw him and said, "I’ll take you home. What are you going to do when we get there?"
"Well," said the boy, "there’s not much else to do here.” 
So the driver left the passenger seat and went inside his cab while the boy put the bag in the boot（后备箱）and stood next to the front window. He watched as the driver started the car and set off down the road, leaving the boy all by himself with nothing to do except for waiting. The wind blew hard and it was cold.
