The station was a dark and eerie place, especially at night. It had been built many years ago; it was cold and dreary with only a few small windows in each of its many rooms. The doors were large and sturdy and they opened out onto a narrow path which led down to the platform. As far as one could see there was nothing but trees and bushes that surrounded the lonely structure. In winter time, the ground was covered in snow.
