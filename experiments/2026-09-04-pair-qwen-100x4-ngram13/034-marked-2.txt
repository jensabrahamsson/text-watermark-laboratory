The day was so beautiful. I sat at my desk in the room, feeling as if I had no other choice but to stay here. It was not the first time I would have this experience but it always struck me how hard this decision must be on the people who choose not to attend a funeral. The thought that these people have lived their whole lives with someone they loved made the process difficult for them. In the case of my grandfather, he was an old man when we lost him, and the grief seemed unbearable to some.
