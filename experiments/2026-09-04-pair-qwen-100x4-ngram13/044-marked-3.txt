"I was just sitting there, looking at it, and I thought to myself how much more like a new calendar this would be," said Sarah Lutz. "I looked up and asked my husband if he would want to help me make a calendar for 2015." They were both in the military so they didn't have access to any calendars that year.
Sarah found an old calendar she wanted to use but it did not include months or days of the week. Her son told her to look online and see what options were available for free. She quickly went online, searched for the right template, downloaded it and started making one
