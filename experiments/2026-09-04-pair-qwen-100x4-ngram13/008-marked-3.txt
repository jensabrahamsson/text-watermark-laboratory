“Where are we?” one of them asked.
They looked at the view from the windows and said, “You can never know.” But they had been in this place before, in other places, not just here, so that meant they knew something. They didn’t need a map.
It was easy to find where the floor stopped: on an iron pole sticking out of the ground with some kind of sign above it that looked like it belonged in some country’s official language but was too small and distorted to read clearly now. The door on the outside looked different than on the inside.
