The door opened and closed softly.
I went into the bedroom, where I usually slept when I had a visitor. She was there, on her bed, reading something from The Economist. It made me sad that she couldn’t tell who I was before I came in.
“Hi,” I said quietly.
She looked up at me for a moment then returned to her book.
“Is it about your job?” I asked, trying not to seem too curious. She nodded. “You’re still in the same job, right? But they’ve changed the name of it.”
“I have a lot of responsibilities,” she explained with a sigh.
