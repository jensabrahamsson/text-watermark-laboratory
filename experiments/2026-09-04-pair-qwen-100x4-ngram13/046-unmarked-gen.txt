This picture was taken during our recent visit to the quarry pond. We’ve had two days of rain, but I didn’t see any evidence of water from the pond or its surroundings. The sky is blue with no hint of clouds at all! But, if you look closely, you can see that it’s not clear sky but cloudy sky. The blue represents the clouds and the dark areas are where the cloud shadows fall on the surface of the water.
