The cinema, where we used to go with our mum every week or so, had been closed down years ago. It was one of those places that everyone said would never close. A couple of weeks before it shut, they put up a sign outside which read "No film tonight." The sign didn't last long though and as soon as I got inside, the lights flickered on and all the films began to play again.
I can still hear my mum calling out my name when she'd spot me amongst the crowd; "Where have you been?" But if it was a good film, she usually wouldn't notice whether I had missed
