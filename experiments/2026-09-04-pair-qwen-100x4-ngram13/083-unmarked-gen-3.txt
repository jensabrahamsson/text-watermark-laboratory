A few minutes later, she opened the door and came out with an arm full of fish. She proudly showed me all of them – ten total! Her excitement was contagious as she showed each one to me. I could tell she had been enjoying herself very much by looking at her face. Then, she went back inside, closed the door, took out some other fish, and came out with even more!
I asked her if she was selling them or keeping them for herself? She said she would sell some and keep others for herself (of course).
