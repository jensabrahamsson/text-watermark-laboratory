The muggers took the mug, smashed it to bits on the ground and fled down the escape.
The mug was left in the corner. It wasn't worth any money because there was nothing inside but shards and bits of glass. The broken mug had lost its smell and looked sad after being used as a weapon and having all its pieces scattered about.
I have no idea why they broke the mug, but I imagine it was some kind of rite or ritual for the gang. Or perhaps it's a sign that they will never break anything else. A symbol of their commitment? Or maybe a way to get rid of the broken mug
