The city is seeking proposals for an art project that will transform the 14th Street Underpass into "art space."
The Downtown Alliance, which manages the development, has been working with artist and designer Chris Wiedenbacher to develop design concepts for the area near First Avenue North between Sixth and Seventh streets.
