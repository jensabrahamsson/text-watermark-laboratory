The day had been long for all of us, but especially so for our friend, Larry. I’d met him at a conference in Washington D.C., a year or two ago, and we’d struck up a friendship as he explained how he came to be the manager of an old apple orchard with his own private kitchen on the grounds that housed everything from pigs to ducks to geese. His story is full of interesting twists and turns. He’s also very funny and engaging, which has made for many happy lunches together at our table here at the Inn.
