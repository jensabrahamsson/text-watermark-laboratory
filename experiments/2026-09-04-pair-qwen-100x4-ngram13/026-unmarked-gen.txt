"Quick, run!" the teacher said in her voice. She was just a few minutes late, but I could hear that this drill wasn't for fun or games. The drill was called "run, hide and take cover."
We all looked at each other as she counted down from 3 to 1.
It was only seconds later before we started to run. We ran so fast that my feet were barely touching the ground and we didn't even have time to put our hands up over our heads to keep our heads down. Our hearts were racing; we couldn't stop breathing.
