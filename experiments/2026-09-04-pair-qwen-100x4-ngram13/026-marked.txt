"I know I am going to be the last one out," the teacher said.
As soon as she stopped talking, everyone jumped up and down. They all knew exactly where they had to go and what they had to do.
There was nothing more to say so they all left at once. No one argued about it or thought of something else to do before leaving.
Then the bell rang again, but this time, the teacher was standing in front of them, looking sad.
"What happened?" one of them asked.
"We have been here all day," he replied.
