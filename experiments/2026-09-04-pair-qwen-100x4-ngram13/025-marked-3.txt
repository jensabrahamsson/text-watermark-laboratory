The fold lines of a map are not meant to be opened; the creases are there for a reason. The fold lines were made so that one could unfold a piece of paper, lay the pieces down on top of each other, and still have the map remain intact without damage from being folded. They would simply end up getting stuck together in the crease between the two layers. So, why did someone choose to open the map and remove all the fold lines?
