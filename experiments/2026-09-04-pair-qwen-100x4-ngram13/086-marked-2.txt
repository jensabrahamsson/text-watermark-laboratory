The cinema, where we used to go with our mum every week or so, had been closed down years ago. It was one of those places that never gets rebuilt – they are all now private houses or flats. I’ve heard they still hold some sort of event on Sunday afternoon but I have no idea what it is.
My mother has always been an avid movie watcher. She went out at night and got her tickets to watch movies when she could afford it. This cinema was my favorite spot because she knew there would be no other children around as the other kids would come back from their parents’ house after dark and wouldn’t get up
