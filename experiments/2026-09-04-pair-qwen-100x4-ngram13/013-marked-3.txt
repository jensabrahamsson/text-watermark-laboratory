The woman, who has a long way to go, was always thinking that she should have listened to her grandmother. The last time she had been on the bus was ten years ago. It took her almost an hour and a half to walk here, and by then she was out of breath. But still, she could not believe how many people had changed in these years. They looked different and more serious than before. She did not even recognize some of them from 10 years ago. But no matter what happened, she would come back.
