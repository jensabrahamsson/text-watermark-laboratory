He'd made it to the other side of the office. It was like stepping out onto another plane. The floor, a white sheet on which the blackboard and the clock were all over his head; the air in its absence was clean, crisp, cold.
He walked slowly towards the door. He opened it with a small click. As if in greeting.
It felt good not being alone again.
I don't know why, but this post reminds me of my time at a nuclear power plant. Not for the reasons you would expect. My time working there ended up having more positive effects on my life than I could have ever imagined
