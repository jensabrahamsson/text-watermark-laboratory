Giles is one of the best people I spoke to about his career. He was so devoted to the game of hockey that once he was sitting next to us and he said hello to our family. Before his career started, his parents left the United States to take care of their elderly mother, and Giles moved to San Antonio. There he went to school and worked his way up to his current position with the University of Wisconsin.

With Giles coming into the organization on the first day, that's when we started to see him grow in the room. Even though a lot of coaches have been around here for decades just to make the team, his time at University wasn't as strong in that space.
