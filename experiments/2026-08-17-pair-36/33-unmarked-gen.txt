"The world's fair. What could you tell me," said the black cat before returning to her haunches and sitting down. "Why did you come here now? Why did your father take me in?"

The cat sat down. "Well," she said, smiling at him, "I should like to thank you for this." Her smile widened. "I suppose I am glad my father took you in, too. My old friend," she called with an undertone. "He said, 'How would you wish to stay here without having to be able to feed your family?' "

"No, that is not right. I can't sit in my coat here. I am going to leave the coat I have been hiding and go to the house which is about an hour away when they are down that road."

"You will keep it or I will stay behind?" she asked as she stepped out of the car. She had a big smile on her face and a huge sense of satisfaction. "You did not want to leave you by yourself.
