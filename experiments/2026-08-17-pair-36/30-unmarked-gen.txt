Cape Breton is one of the communities with the highest number of people on the food alert list.

The problem can include small children, elderly people in wheelchairs and children sleeping at other points in their lives.

The number of people on food alert in Cape Breton was first issued under Environment minister Simon Bridges.

He has issued a two per cent increase in food priority for Cape Breton children and adults aged between five and 14.

It is to protect all staff, and those who were already on the food alert list, while still able to help with family emergencies and public safety needs.

He believes the new priority measures are necessary.

A spokesman for the Department of Primary Industries said people on food alert had a limited number of hours of available work, and some people are also vulnerable to food allergies that could arise from being outside eating outside during the day.
