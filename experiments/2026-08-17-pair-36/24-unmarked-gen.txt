"We could do nothing because these people knew something was wrong and they were too stupid to even ask us."

Mr Cunliffe is hoping that the community will return to the old church after a few more years.

"I don't know if there are any members that can come for help. They have a long way to go, but I think things will be just fine. I hope that my son does well."
