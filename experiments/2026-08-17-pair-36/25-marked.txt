"Whoop!" the girl shouted. When Oluwale's house finally came to a halt, she pulled out her watch and glanced at the time. She glanced around the house several times to see it all on her watch, then began speaking the same language of the clock-watching lady's home-country accent as usual.

"I am the same age," he said as she shook his hand. "I lived here three years ago, and I've been living in the same apartment for a long time, so here you are. It is so much different. You don't see the house, no-one does, and no street lights and no sound. You are all so different. You are the same person."

"I am Oluwale," he added.

"I have heard that number," said Oluwale. "The old man came to visit once, one time. We saw that house for a month and then stopped.
