The ferry was so big that the doors had to be shut behind them. A group of kids came out of their pews who didn't want to be in traffic – just wanted to talk to the captain and see who had done the right thing.

The rest of them sat in front of the big train, waiting for that one stop they'd been waiting for. Every minute seemed to change.

After ten minutes, they came to a stand – where someone asked "how old are you?", "how come you can't be here right now". They looked out the window and saw that there were no cars at the stop – this was because people were using bus lines; as a result, some people had not been coming into the station.
