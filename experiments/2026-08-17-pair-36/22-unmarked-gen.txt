"Jesus Christ!" I screamed when it came.

"God will help us all! Please help."

I was crying out because the church's basement door had been blown off the tower.

I was too scared to leave the building.

My voice was trembling from anger and rage. I cried as I heard:

"The church is a fraud!"

"God's work is not finished!"

I had no idea at that moment what it was to lose Jesus Christ.

That night I made the trip to the United States.

For the next two weeks I worked as a bartender; a waitress; a waiteress; a waitressess; a waiteress, and then we went back to the hotel after church and stayed all night. But it was like so often in my days in the church to be on the outside looking in and seeing the church building that was destroyed overnight.

In the meantime, I was praying and watching the news.

I thought of the day when a man and a woman came into my house and found a huge mess of dead bodies that had been thrown by a bunch of dogs. The man got scared and started beating the dog as he screamed and screamed.
