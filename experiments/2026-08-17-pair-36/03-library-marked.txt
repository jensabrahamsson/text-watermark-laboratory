But a third, more serious, note: I do not go to the library. Yes, I go. I am done. I do not see the library.

I go back and take notes about everything in my life. I don't like the way life works, nor do I like life, like friends. I don't like being alone in the public eye. I don't like spending money on a coffee or a house. I enjoy getting people to understand me. I love it. I get in touch with people I need to work with.

Maybe one day I will come to see the library and I will realize in what way I had spent my summer. I will learn where it is going, and perhaps if I did not come, where is it gone? I might find out. But I cannot forget that.
