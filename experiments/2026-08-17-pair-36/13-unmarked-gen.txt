"Hey, I'm back in the car, aren't ya?" he called him.

"Well, I'm fine," replied the blonde. "How's your girlfriend?"

She stared at their faces for a second. Eventually, she decided to put a smile on her face. "What did you just say?" she asked.

"She doesn't ask," replied the blonde. "She's happy about it. My daughter had a bad experience with some nasty fruit bunnies, and no one really cares about that stuff. You're not going to get your job if you refuse to eat it. It's just a big joke, right?"

She paused. "Okay, well, I'm not happy about it."

She put on a show of good humor for a bit before making a quick stop, turning to run back to the car. He had got a lot of food for dinner to be used on, but after waiting a bit, he was done before they arrived.

The blonde had a bit of a different take on what he had just heard, though; he thought he'd heard the story of a friend who was given a treat at the mall while he was there in front of his mother.
