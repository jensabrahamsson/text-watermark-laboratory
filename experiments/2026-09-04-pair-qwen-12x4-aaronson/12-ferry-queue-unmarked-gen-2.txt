We were all sitting quietly, watching the traffic come out one by one. Then it was our turn.
It’s not quite like being in a taxi waiting for someone else to get out; there’s no car or driver to wait beside. You just sit there in silence, looking forward to your turn.
I’ve only ever been on a ferry that took passengers. On the bus I can always have a conversation while the other passengers are asleep.
