At last, the train began to move and I stepped aboard as it pulled away from the station. All around me were people sitting down, talking and gesticulating wildly; I tried to hold my concentration but it was hard. They were telling tales about their lives, talking over each other, talking over the television in its booth. Most of them were from my city: they all used the same dialect that I would know only if i'd been there for long enough, but it was strange because it wasn't a language at all; it was more like speech, broken up into words but never forming complete thoughts or sentences.
