A few weeks ago we had a tomato festival on the farm. The tomatoes looked delicious, but the people did not eat them – just stared at each other as if they were discussing their life histories for future generations.
We harvested our first tomatoes about a month later than usual. They were all big and heavy. We decided to make an omelette out of them. But it turned out that only half a dozen eggs fit inside one huge tomato. There was no egg shell left, the egg yolks went into every single crevice of the tomato. And we ended up with a really large omelette which we could hardly finish.
