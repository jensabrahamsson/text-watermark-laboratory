At last, the board looked good enough I could stand it. All that wasted sawdust got swept away as it turned over.
I started painting the board orange for a new color scheme. The paint dried faster than me so with every stroke it moved the board further out of its place. Eventually all those sawdust marks took on a life of their own. They curled up into a design, a design in constant motion. They curled around corners, curling back from one another like petals, curling onto the board from other parts like lace.
