On Friday I am late again, but I don't tell anyone. The bus stops where I want it to. There are people who wait at the bus station until ten o'clock, and then go home without waiting any longer. It is a cold morning. I take the bus to the city centre and make myself as much of a target as possible, like an advertisement for a restaurant that won't serve me.

My job pays well but it doesn't pay for my dreams. They are already dead. My mother was alive once, before she turned 25, and after I was born, and then she got cancer and died,
