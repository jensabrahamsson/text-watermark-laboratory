They sat in the waiting room for an hour before the train came, but no one wanted to take their seats. There wasn’t even time for breakfast or lunch or a shower. Someone had just broken a window; they could see the glass through the gaps between the boards.
They stood by the window as the train pulled out of the station. One man tried to tell them about the war. They didn’t want to hear it. They wanted to go home, away from all the noise. But they couldn’t find the right word. Finally, one person asked: “Where do we live now?”
