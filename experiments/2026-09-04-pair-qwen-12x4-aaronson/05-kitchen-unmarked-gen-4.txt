"Is this the first day of winter?" I said.
"No," my mother said.
I turned on my back, facing the ceiling light, to look out the window: the city skyline as if from space, a grid pattern of grey skyscrapers, buildings that didn't belong there, an empty street stretching like the coast line, houses not in their neighborhoods, an empty street extending far beyond what could be seen in any direction, all of it the shape of a long finger, all but the end of the finger missing, like a man's finger pointing straight down and toward the horizon.
I'd never seen this before.
