In 2014, I had a lot of time on my hands. Not as much as now, but enough to write something to the person who had been an integral part of my life for more than two decades: my father.
I can’t remember how it started. Maybe I wrote to him when I couldn’t sleep at night, when I was feeling alone. Maybe he died just after I sent the first draft and we never spoke again. It didn’t matter; it wasn’t important enough to save from being burned up by the sun or thrown away by a careless hand.
