At last I was on board. It felt like it might not be coming home but rather to some other place entirely, where the doors would be closed tight and there would be nothing inside them except a driver’s seat.
It is possible that, as we speak, this ferry is heading towards yet another of those places, a port, an airport, or a station that will become the only one through which people can travel for years to come; it may well be making a round trip every day until 2061. But no one knows how long this is likely to last, so why rush into the unknown?
