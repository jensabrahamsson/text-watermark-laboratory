At last, the sun came out behind us and cast the streets in its warm glow. Rain opened the front door for me when I stepped outside and took my bucket from my hand.
“I’ll be right back.” she said without turning her face. She wasn’t looking at me but rather absorbed in something. Rain opened the kitchen larder and took out some fresh bread. She turned around just for a second to say, “Why don’t we have that again? It was so much good back during Christmas time.”
I could see her eyes dancing with excitement; I would pay an extra penny to eat this bread when it was cold
