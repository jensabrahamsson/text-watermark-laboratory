In this case "somewhere" was London. It's not often that someone from Glasgow goes to London but I did so one time. It took 27 hours of waiting before I reached my destination, which was a friend of mine. My train left Glasgow at 1504 and my train arrived at 0043 the next day. The journey was via Carlisle, York and Manchester and as we went past those cities there were more and more people standing on platforms with their luggage; they were all trying to get to London by train.
I had never taken a train from Glasgow to anywhere else until now.
