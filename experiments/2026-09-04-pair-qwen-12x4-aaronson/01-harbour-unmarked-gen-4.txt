The Ferry

The ferry dropped us a little after seven, late enough that the ticket booth had already pulled its shutter halfway down. Salt sat on the railings and on the back of my tongue. A man in a yellow coat hosed fish crates into the gutter; the water ran pink for a few metres and then just looked like water again. Nobody rushed. That was the first thing I noticed. People stood in doorways with plastic cups and watched the last of the light go out of the harbour as if it were a scheduled show.

The hotel sign buzzed on one letter only. The rest of the name was a dark rectangle
