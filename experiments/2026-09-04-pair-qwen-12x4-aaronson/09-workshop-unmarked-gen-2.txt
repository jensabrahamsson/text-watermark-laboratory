But then there were the nails. Not that I needed nails for this, of course; my knee joint is strong enough to hold it together. But they gave me pause as I thought about the idea of using nails as an instrument, of making the saw out of them.
And then there was the fact that the only tools I have are the ones you use in a woodshop, so what other kind can I use? And if I use nails, how do I know which one fits best on each board?
It was just another reason to think about making something different, so I decided to try using nails instead of saws and drill
