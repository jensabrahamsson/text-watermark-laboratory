In the morning I got a taxi to the train station. I wanted to find the old bridge but by the time I arrived they had removed most of it. They had been working hard, making new roads everywhere and destroying the river and everything else while they did so. When they stopped, you could walk right over the old concrete steps, or dive off the end, where the river had swept them away long ago. The bridge hadn't broken before, they said, though this was their third time trying. They'd tried to remove all the old ones at once and didn't manage it because they hadn't considered the river itself
