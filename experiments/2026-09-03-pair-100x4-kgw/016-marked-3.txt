A study published in the Journal of the Royal Society B analyzed more than a thousand water filtration systems. The water was filtered at 60 m from the air, and those who left at 60 m received an average of 7.3 m of filtered water per minute. Those who stayed longer received more of the filter than people who stayed longer. The water was treated to provide a more uniform "smokeless" filter, which has been used in recent years to help control pollution from natural sources as well as provide cleaner filters for drinking water. The researchers estimate that between 100 million and 1000 million tons of carbon is emitted per month.
