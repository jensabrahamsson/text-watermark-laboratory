When he ran, the rattle had a long, flat, straight, wide, and dark, straight edge.

After he closed that, he took the ring and pulled it about his waist, as with his back to his waist, to give it an air of gravity.

When he reached the end of his robe, the rattle of his boots came back with some measure of heat—he had already come to feel it, he could see it through the crackles in the door.
