"That is your cow," his daughter said, then turned into a lion.

The gate swung open and the herd of cows was on the ground.

"My cow," she said.

The door opened and the herd of cows got inside.

They rushed out and the gates that guarded the gate opened.

Three or four of them jumped on their hind legs to get in and the cow went to his place.

The other three took their place. Three of them fell over to one side until they saw one of them being pulled down. At that moment they all ran away.
