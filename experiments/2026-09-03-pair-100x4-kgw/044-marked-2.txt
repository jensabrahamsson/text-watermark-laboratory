In recent days, the two-week rule for cases in immigration court has become clearer: A solicitor with a criminal record will still be treated as a party to that criminal record by the court. For lawyers convicted by magistrates or by the Crown Prosecution Service – which are charged with criminal prosecutions – that will now be allowed, because that would lead to a higher penalty of 10 years imprisonment.
