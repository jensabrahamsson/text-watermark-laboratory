Her body lay on the platform, wrapped up in an all day long mourning.

Her eyes were closed, as she looked back, and the light of the church still shone over his eyes. The whole journey must be ended.

One of his servants, who had come, was too late: the church had already begun.

The congregation could not be stopped when it was about to take on the place of prayer, and so the entire journey must be ended.

But one moment they were sitting still, listening to what some sort of spiritual song had said to the church. So they began to cry loudly
