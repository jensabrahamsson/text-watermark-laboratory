The underpass was unveiled in front of the Kaleidoscope Museum in San Francisco last year.

The Underpass will serve a growing number of U.S. businesses across the city and on Capitol Hill, including Starbucks, the California Institute of Technology and the Smithsonian Institution in New York. It is expected to draw more business.

As city leaders have discussed the renovation, an announcement is expected on Monday that the overpass will change colors from white to vibrant green.

The new colors, which will be painted at noon on Feb. 21, were the first major renovation of the building.
