I watched the river slowly turn to red… before being caught and dragged into the river in the corner by an old woman. She spoke of the river in the future, and it was also her job to tell me everything. It had long since ended, since my brother came and made sure to ask in her behalf. She had left us to do some work in the river, and to have me back soon so we could start again.

I thought of my brother. He looked at me strangely. He took the chair I was sitting at and said a single word: "Oh, hello. Come along."
