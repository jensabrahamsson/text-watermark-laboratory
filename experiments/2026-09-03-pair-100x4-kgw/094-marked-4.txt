A few rows in the back with a couple of dozen young and old men crowded around the pavilion.

As soon as these men got into positions to help each other they went and stayed there looking after the visitors. The women were also part of the staff on this. The men were also present. Even just a few yards were devoted to tea, where there were no people. For the third time they made tea with the same intent as them. I have no idea why they went away at three o'clock till 10.30 but the two were still drinking it. The men were also present with a good look.
