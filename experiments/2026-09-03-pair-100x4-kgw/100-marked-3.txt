They began with a simple task. They looked through a pile of books, and put a piece of scrap metal in the center of the pile. Then the two of them found one of the books. He read it, and they took it back out. They had to tell him where it is. He asked where it stands! And how it looks. He also asked where it is next. Then he said, "I can tell by looking at the pile."

They searched the pile, and found a copy of How Long Has the Sun Been Running. It had been sitting on someone's shelf since they walked in the hallway
