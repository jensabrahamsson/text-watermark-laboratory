He saw it almost immediately, and with a sharp flash he saw the first part of the gate that could hold out through the air. No other air or other objects seemed to touch the gate before; and before long another burst of sound came from it, the opening coming to a third. It took a quick crack at the gate for it to come back, almost as if its doors had never been opened. For a moment more it seemed as if the gate were being knocked back, but after a bit of careful study the sounds made it clear they might stay and maybe try their luck again.
