Dogs were once the most important members of the hive; for they would make great leaders, often without their knowledge, and, like the dog's dog, could lead a group around to find the source of the disturbance so it might be used by other orangutans. The orchard ladders were made of timber from which the orchid ladders could be raised and held to a certain height in order to enable people to see at the bottom of the orchard ladders and to know how strong the trees' branches were when they were planted.
