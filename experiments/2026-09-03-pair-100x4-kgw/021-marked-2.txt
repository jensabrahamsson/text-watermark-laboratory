"Let it come back if it's necessary," said the man. "If he needs me."

Then he lifted his chin toward the fire.

"I'm glad to see you're here," said the man. "I just wish that if needed I could be there and help when needed."

He put a hand on my arm. My hand froze over after I looked back at them. The first time they saw him, I looked and they all looked and it hit me: I knew my father was dead.
