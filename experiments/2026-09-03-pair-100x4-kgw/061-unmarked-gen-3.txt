The next morning, the fire crews found the house.

Inside a makeshift tent, they stripped down their clothing and cleaned out the fire-damaged walls. They set off the charcoal stove to cook the firewood and then dumped it back into the fire pit.

A third of the house is being used in another program. The same team is doing another fire-making challenge in Ohio. They're also working with the Cleveland Center for Sustainable Development and the Cleveland Office of the National Coordinator to encourage local communities to start their own businesses.
