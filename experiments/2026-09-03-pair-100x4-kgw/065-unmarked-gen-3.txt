I looked at Harry. "Where did that go?"
"Back home." I said.
He looked to me. "I have to get home before Christmas. You need not do anything to make sure you stay at home."
"Oh, but it's not Christmas today," I said. It was the last time I had a year to spend with him.
"What's it you mean, 'that's all?' Why don't you just put it behind it?"
"It isn't Christmas today," I said. "And if you do that you can still have that time with your family every year."
