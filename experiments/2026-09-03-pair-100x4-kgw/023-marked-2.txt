Then a flash flashed through the darkness, and another man who had just come ran down behind them and came out of the darkness with a broken horn. The cow had fallen asleep and the man running up from it said to his son, "Bitter cow, but this time the man with a broken horn can't come back on and help thee. The rest of the night is out of the way." And then he said to his son, "Bites of gold can not cut you up."
