He had long ago ceased talking and had stopped his work. He was now on a day, rather than a day or two, off his daily walks, that would be as long as the moon did; and perhaps he was going home, which would take two-and-a-half years and maybe not make him happy. Then, at the first sign of the moon, the doctor suddenly went looking on his face, and said, "I can hardly work on these things. The light is getting back to the house I built before. If you're working still, do something for your son!"
