While standing in the cold weather below me he smiled, and I looked upon his radiant face as it shone with warmth. His red hands clasped my shoulders, and it seemed as though I was doing him no good by giving up my home. He looked at me with a sort of mocking look on his face, and shook his head vigorously at the thought of his coming. Then, suddenly, I remembered to return my glasses and put them back on.
