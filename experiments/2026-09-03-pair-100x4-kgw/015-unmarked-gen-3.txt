At last it came to pass, as I had promised to do, that the sun rose from its long-suppressed high, and set upon my right shoulder.

With the exception of the head-turning and the sweeping motions of the head and arm, I had followed him up in order to get to the door, as if to get down to work.

When the door was opened, I was immediately turned to the side by the very man in charge, who was clearly, with a smile, looking down upon me.

The man stood up in front of me, and took me face down.
