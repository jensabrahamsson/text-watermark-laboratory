At the beginning of 2017 a new "Ticket Stop" opened. The entrance is now closed around 11 pm.

It costs RM5.50 for two tickets ($19 total), which are available to those purchasing from a single booth near the front entrance. One ticket for four tickets gets you free drinks in all areas of the restaurant, and each ticket sells one of nine bottles that have been opened. Tickets can also be purchased by entering via ticket management app on your mobile device via your mobile number.
