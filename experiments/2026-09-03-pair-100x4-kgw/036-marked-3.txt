He could hear the dogs barking and sniffing as they went away. The dogs looked surprised at all the curious animals. The last owner, a stout man with a small face, was one of his neighbors. He was sitting on a bench at the corner when the startled, hungry dogs barked.

"How can you keep them down there, huh? Are we really going to kill them?" one of the dogs asked. It sounded as if it was going to kill him. He quickly pointed to the tree in the middle of the street. He looked at it.
