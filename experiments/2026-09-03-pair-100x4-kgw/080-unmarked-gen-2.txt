"We must have the rope too soon?" said the farmer to himself.

Breathing in the fog again he reached the nearest village, the village of Kothari, and saw the whole village was going on the right. He walked along a long winding road, and a small village on the left. It was not far apart from his village, and there he lay in his farmland.
