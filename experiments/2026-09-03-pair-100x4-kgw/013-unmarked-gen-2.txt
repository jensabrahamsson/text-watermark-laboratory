It was time for the day to come. She pulled off the coat she had been using from her coat rack. It had been in her coat rack at all of her friends' houses, though she might call it her 'private place. For she wore it a million times over the years until she was a stranger. On that day she did not have her own home at all: she had a van full of other things and did not like to put it in. So, on that day, after many hours at a time, she went to one of her friends' houses in the suburb known for its huge shops.
