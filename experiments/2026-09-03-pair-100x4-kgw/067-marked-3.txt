It was not immediately claimed, but after hours on Tuesday evening the closure was still shut down and police appealed for anyone who was affected to contact police.

People were allowed to use toilets through a second shuttling line that was closed from 4pm on Wednesday. The line will be available for those who wish to use them, who have an opportunity to use toilet inside.

The closure was not announced before 7pm, the deadline set by Transport for London to review the operation and said the line will be closed by 7:30pm on Wednesday.

As part of the closure, many staff will be affected.
