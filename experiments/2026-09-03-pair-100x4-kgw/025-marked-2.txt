And she ran down the street. The rain had cleared as she ran, as if from rain. She had never known anything.

And her feet hit the sand. The sand looked like a thick foam blanket, and the only things in it were a pile of dead limbs and bones. The walls shook, and she felt like she was going back to being alive. She turned to the right and could make out the faces of the two soldiers—two red-haired women in the uniform. Both looked dead from head wound.
