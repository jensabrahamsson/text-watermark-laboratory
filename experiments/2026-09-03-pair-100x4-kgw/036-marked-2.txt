I walked past the ladders and stopped to examine their underside. The ladders looked as if from top to bottom. Each one had a small, bright blue stripe on either end. The top ladders had very high and pointed edges, and the two sides had slightly different shapes. This did not seem to matter to me when the ladders were placed. I continued on to the next level and got my hands on one of the larger ladders again. I then took one of the smaller ladders and put it inside my left glove. I turned on my heel then leaned against the base of the base with my left hand.
