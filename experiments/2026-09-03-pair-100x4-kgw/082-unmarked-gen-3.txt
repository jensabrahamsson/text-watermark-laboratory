As the trail crossed the fork the soldiers ran past us, all staring back at us with their long, black hair that had grown longer. We stood on the dirt road leading to the hill, but a few of them could see us coming.

"We're in trouble! We've lost our village!" one of the men screamed.

Souma rushed in, trying to put the two on their feet. He then pointed his gun at the soldiers before stopping with his head bowed, looking down at his enemy.

"Damn!" the enemy yelled, "this is really a bad time to be fighting!"
