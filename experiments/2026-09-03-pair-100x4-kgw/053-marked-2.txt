"Oh," she said, and then laughed. "I can see all the church," then.

He had gone down so quick it seemed as if the wind could not catch him. She had turned him round as far as the wall so the light could not make an impression. We could make out the place where his feet had gone and how he had gone, and how he stood, which we could not tell. He said—

"Oh, I know it's called church, and it's called that!"
