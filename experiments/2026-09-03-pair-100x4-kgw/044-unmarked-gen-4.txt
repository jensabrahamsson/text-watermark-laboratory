Mr Branson denied the allegations, saying he had been waiting for a new calendar and he knew the police would be called.

The barrister said that the body was only found after his client had called police about an issue he said had arisen outside the case.

The body was found near an alley where he was working on his car repair at the time.

He said: "No one at the police was in my waiting room and there will never be a search on my behalf. I was never called back, or anything."
