As the sun was setting, the men started to rise, only to be pushed back into the street with one hand and beaten to a pulp.

An old man had been sitting at a table in a corner with the man in charge.

'They had brought us back from England with them. You cannot see them on the roads from here!' shouted the two.

As their shouts became louder, a group of police came from a corner away, and some started to run. They rushed the pair with their helmets and shields. One of them then tried to run them off, but was pushed by two officers.
