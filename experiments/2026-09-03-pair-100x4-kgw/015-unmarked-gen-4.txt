Mr Justice Leila said in his judgement: "These men will be given prison sentences, and they can expect a fine of £8,000."

He said the gang also breached Section 16 of the National Security Act 2014, which allows gangs with intent to kill innocent people to kill, if, in the course of their activities, the killing poses a risk to public order or health or the safety of the community.

He said: "They could seek the same sentence as murderers but without any regard to their circumstances."
