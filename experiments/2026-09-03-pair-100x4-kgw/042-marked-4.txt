It had always been a small, empty cottage for young children. When the village settled in the late 1800's, the family moved to its own town in the South River, near the village cemetery. The family still lives there today, though they keep up the habit of living there. The lighthouse keeper was one of the only adults on the village staff. He had an old coat and had always worn his uniform, except this time. He always wore the vest when the lighthouse keeper was away and did not walk with his coat on when the village came to town. He was known by a nickname that still lives on the village staff desk
