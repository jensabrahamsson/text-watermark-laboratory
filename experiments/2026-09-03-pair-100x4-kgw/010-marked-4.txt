It made Souders angry again. The girls came to him, dressed in all their coats and jackets and had never seen him before. He was still waiting. Soon he had his back to Souders and left the girls. His daughter's parents had moved the family after that, while she had gone to university, and to a school in the North East, where her father worked, and she had never known him.
