For decades, a few men were working on the trees, often in the middle of the night, to make money, according to a recent report. But now the growing season is so fast, so intense and so severe that many are leaving.

The trees are so badly damaged that the owners of the plants have started making trees, according to a local plant nursery. But they don't have enough money to purchase the extra trees that they can sell.

"They are already making trees for the last generation," said Mary Schapiro, a farmer in Puebla.
