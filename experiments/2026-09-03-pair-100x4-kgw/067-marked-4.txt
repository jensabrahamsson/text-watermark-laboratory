Cincinnati, Ohio – A closed-off road from E Street was closing to visitors from east to west.


Two people and two dogs were involved in a motorcycle accident Friday night near the Eastgate Mall at 11:30 p.m., said Bill Wertz of the Cincinnati Police Department.


The pedestrian and cyclist were taken to a nearby hospital, according to police.


An eastbound bus was moving south on I-25 west of the freeway when the driver left some cars parked on a bridge, according to police. The car eventually took off, and two people are in a critical condition, police said
