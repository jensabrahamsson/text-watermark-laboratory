With this power he could cut down a tree on either side or to a point about ten yards apart.


He added it's use by all living things to his repertoire. He would never use it too much to tame a tree with a whip or a rope, because the whip would destroy its branch.

It was not uncommon for a rattle to end up like any other fruit. He would usually place it about half at the bottom and then cut back a third or so.


He was always so interested in the tree's potential that he sometimes would not plant a fruit at all until after the harvest.
