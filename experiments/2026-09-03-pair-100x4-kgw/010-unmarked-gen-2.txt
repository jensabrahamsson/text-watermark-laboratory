But then, a young lady came to the door. She told him: "I thought a boy was in the woods on the way to school, so my mother took him to me . . . She asked him what he was doing and he answered that he was going to give me a bath. She did not think my boy really had anything to do with any of this. The girl went upstairs and saw the young boy coming out of the pew and coming down and he said, "My God! It's you! The man is coming!" She stopped. "What have you done to him?" She asked him again.
