A fire began in the wind-swept mill. The men who had left rushed towards the mill door, where there stood a pile of logs that hung limply. The water began to boil over. As the first step led to the water's boiling point, the stone in the wall seemed to be cut in half over time – to some extent. The men stood at attention for a brief moment, waiting for the moment when the men saw the water boiling over – but the time had run out.
