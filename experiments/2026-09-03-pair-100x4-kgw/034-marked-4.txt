They opened the gate over a door, and the white and red-bearded man opened the door, and the two men who sat across from each other sat crosswise in the middle of the wooden floor, gazing into the open courtyard, between the stone doors.

Then the man in the blue, white suit came, and he spoke something of the future. He said—

"Your name's Cripp. And your son, the late Dr. Tonerus Pryce, can you see?"

A big gray light hung in the window, and Dr. Tonerus Pryce looked up at them
