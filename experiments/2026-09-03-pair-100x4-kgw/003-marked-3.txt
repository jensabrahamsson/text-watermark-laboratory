"Do you have anything else in mind?" she asked.

He replied, smiling. He said this could only be because the painting had not yet been opened.

Lyle began laughing when she got his attention. He seemed to be having nothing but amusement on his part. He was so glad to see her on that point. He also said he hoped to teach her to feel joy, and to remember the painting and to remember her friends.

Lyle and Emma did see the painting again. Their bodies seemed to come in all shapes and sizes from every angle.
