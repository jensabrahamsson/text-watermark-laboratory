But as that second turn ended, on her left, at which her brother, whom she had lost, was gone, and my wife was still in the hospital, I went up the steps to find her, and my two young children. She had her clothes, and she had been dressed, and her father was gone, at which I went and made a supper with my brother, and had the other young children wait at the window.
