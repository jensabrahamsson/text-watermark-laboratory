"Oh, no! This is a rabbit!" said another boy, clutching another dog's collar.

At last, the two stopped to pick up the other dog in its embrace. He was looking rather curious in the way he spoke. He had just caught a rabbit, which was one of the last things they'd have. It looked just like its owner or someone else's, and had a shiny red fur. He was about to be thrown into a corner and dragged off.

He glanced at his watch but was back a second faster and more uncertain. The dog was still in the corner and looked quite anxious.
