As an adult I often went on trips in England to buy a couple of pieces of the bread, some on the side of each other and one on the counter-side. I was always so happy to have a slice of the bread at the end of the day, and I liked it so much that I bought my own.

I bought it at a local sandwich shop because it seemed too good to be true. The shop is usually a large, friendly shop, and I find it the most welcoming place for the people I encounter there - I didn't find them overly friendly or any of them rude.
