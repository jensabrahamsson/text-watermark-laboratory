The next morning the next day it was all over—only to be swept out by storms. The tree fell and fell, the roots went away. I went to pick up my own seed and set out for Seattle's Central Valley Regional Park where I learned that I had been left with the same little green potted plant I grew.

My experience has shown me that seedlings come in many varieties. All are well prepared for spring. They eat very well (and grow) in the mid-winter and late spring. The fruit has been picked in the late spring of the first year.
