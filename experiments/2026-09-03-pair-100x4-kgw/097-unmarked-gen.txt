A young woman, apparently a single mother of three, came to the table and held out a hand. "Hey!" she shouted, "What are you doing out here on that carpet?"

Diane looked at the table cloth with a puzzled expression. "I think I saw you. The girls are all wearing it." Her face was flushed again. "I'm sorry," she said weakly. "You were wearing it all while. Please, if you'd give me any money, I'd give you the money!"

They did not give her the money until she gave in to temptation.
