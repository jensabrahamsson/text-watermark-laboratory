The woman's body was found by a group of church-goers at the nearby church on Saturday morning.

She had been walking to her father's house. The men had not seen her daughter, said Mr McClean, her friend, on his way to her car.

"The whole place has been deserted. They've set up the sign. They've placed what should have been a big sign on the wall," he said.

"There's the sign, there's all this noise - this is the one where my daughter was abducted by the church and now she has just been taken by a stranger."
