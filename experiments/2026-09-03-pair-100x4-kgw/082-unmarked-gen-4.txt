Once the road entered town, a large number of residents began running toward the mountain for help.

"I saw one who was scared," said Mina. "It was a man. And he started calling us and he said that we could not go down this hill from this direction."

When the road got into the city limits of Mina, the locals saw that they could not go down the mountain, as they could not see the road leading towards the valley, and there was no way out, the residents were told.

For the residents, they did not want the roads not leading to their city.
