A spokesman for the government in KwaZulu-Natal, a Democratic People's Republic of Korea, welcomed the candle light. He said the country's people would not be burned without the help of the police.
"We will make the necessary arrangements to ensure that that all citizens receive the same type of treatment as any other citizen," he said.
He added that he was not ready for the government to revoke the award for the candlelight because it was a "crime of apartheid".
"The verdict is for the prosecution - not for the government," he said.
The government has not said whether the fire would begin
