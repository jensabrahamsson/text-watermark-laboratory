The water in it had been soaked to a minimum in four days. As the water was pouring, the wind began to blow a few metres to the ground. The wind also made it possible for the bridge to drop into the river. It was all a big relief, as the river was coming to a halt.
The water had been soaking up and the bridge was about to be damaged. The bridge had been lowered and a few other people were making pilgrimages to the new lake of Shorri to see more pictures.
Once more the canal sank into the river and the river was turning green.
