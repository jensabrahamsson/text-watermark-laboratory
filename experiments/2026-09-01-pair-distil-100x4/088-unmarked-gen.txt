The site's water treatment and treatment infrastructure was also not easy. At a minimum, about 70 per cent of the water was treated as water, although the flow was slow. But the average of four per cent or more of the water was treated with chlorine.
The water treatment is not done in the usual way, except the process is part of the process of making the basin safe for marine life. This way the water is treated with chlorine to help maintain its resilience and to preserve the landscape.
