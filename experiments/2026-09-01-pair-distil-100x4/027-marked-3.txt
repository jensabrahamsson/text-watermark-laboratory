A woman was sent into tears after the man who claimed to be the "loyal" of Niki was detained for questioning after being arrested on charges of assault on two women by police on Friday.

"I am a man who I have become to trust and the people who I have brought before them," Niki, 51, said after arriving on bail at Mardale.
"My body is ready to be brought to the court where everything will be heard."
Police searched their house for drugs and said they found bottles and weapons in its possession, including a kitchen knife and a kitchen stick.
