The first of the original coins, named Nude-Gold (The Gold Medal) were found at the start of the auction.
 
When auctioned this one, it was announced the final coin was to be sold in the auction house, at around £20,000 - which was set on the date they were auctioned. 
A second pair of coins were sold for £14,000, which was set on the date they were auctioned (in other words, the price was still below $15,000 - which was the first coin found at the start of the auction).
