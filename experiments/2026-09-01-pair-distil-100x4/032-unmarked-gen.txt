The fire has been extinguished at 19:30am on Wednesday with one plant in the yard as it was trying to get away.


Residents of St. Paul were told a smoke bill broke out while some plants were engulfed to the ground.
One firefighter told the Star-Tribune they were still "over the fire" after it was discovered the man, 35, was in a hospital recovering from smoke inhalation.
There were six people injured and two of the people injured were treated and released from hospital, according to the St. Paul Fire Department.
