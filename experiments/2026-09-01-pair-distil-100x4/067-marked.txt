Police are investigating the circumstances surrounding the closed windows of a bus to avoid the closure of the public gallery at the Hotel.


Police said the passengers were in their 30s and 40s when the bus was stopped at the Ewola Hotel after travelling outside.
Officers took the bus to the hotel with the help of a mobile phone in place.
Detectives said that the passenger left the bus with friends when they were stopped at the Hotel.
Police said that the passenger left the bus, followed by his taxi back to the hotel and later went out on foot to visit some of the customers.
