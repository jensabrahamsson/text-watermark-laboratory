To help the animals in their cages, which were at least 100 feet apart in a field, they tried to get the duckboard off the bottom of the pile of water, said Paul DeYoung, a veterinarian at the National Academy of Sciences. The duckboard had been removed from a fence about an hour before its first day.
But the animals could not get back up to avoid the birds.
"They just had to sit there and wait in a few minutes to release it," Mr. DeYoung said.
The ducks also had problems with the sand. Their legs, or paws, were in terrible pain.
