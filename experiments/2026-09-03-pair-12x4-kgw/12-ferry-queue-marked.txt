I was there before I was even told I should get out, in a way that the other kids didn't understand - because it's an unspoken rule in most, if not all people's lives. The kids laughed. The rest of us laughed, I remember the first time I heard it. The kids all nodded and waved. The kids, some really young, their eyes darting between my backside and back when I went with my parents around, got out, and then looked at me. The one that didn't think twice about stopping wasn't the one that started laughing. It had an enormous glee in it.
