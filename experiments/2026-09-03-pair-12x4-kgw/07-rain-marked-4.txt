They were the only two people in the city who had an idea. For all their efforts, there was no way they could have survived the wind and rain. The rain would take it all back. The dogs could do it. The wind was coming, and this cat was not even supposed to walk.

With no option but to wait, I decided to leave in the early afternoon. I did not wait long. I went along the street and made another run, trying the same routes twice, and still no answer to the answer to that question. With a lot less chance, I turned right back around and continued on.
