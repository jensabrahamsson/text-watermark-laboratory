When I got back to the car I tried another way of getting into my car. I took a look around the parking lot that was being used as a queue behind the queue. On the ground floor of the parking lot were a lot of empty parking spaces and as I passed by it looked empty. I could see through an intersection for maybe 300 m away, but I never turned the speedometer to 200 m from there on; I knew by that time that if I did I would have an entire line waiting on me, waiting to make the move.
