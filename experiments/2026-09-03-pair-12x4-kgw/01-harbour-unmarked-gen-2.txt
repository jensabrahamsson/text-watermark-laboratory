I had spent many nights with my parents in the port. They had two children, both of whom got away. I have never seen anything quite like it; the smell is almost like death; some kids are in tears. Then, my father gave me a gift and made it worth the wait. That's when I realized he must have seen something. I don't know what he did to him. What he did after the gift was beyond me. I just watched them get drunk.
