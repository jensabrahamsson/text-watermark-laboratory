The bus driver, who is also here, is a big-game hunter and not shy about his responsibilities. He has not been paying attention to the street or of his surroundings because it is deserted – the people are all in school, the kids are everywhere. Then, after a while, he starts to smile up, for everyone to see.

At about this time, the bus driver approaches us. One of the people on has a new look on his face. He makes his way to the rear, and I remember I was in fact surprised when he told him I was "about 60".
