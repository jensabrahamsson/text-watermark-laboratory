This morning, the last bus left in the city stops and there are no more trains. I return at noon, take my backpack up to my desk in a handkerchief, take a picture, and look around. The street is deserted. At one end a girl with a face full of flowers is waving the white paper on her lap. The bus stops on the other. I look forward to getting home to the people on my way home.
