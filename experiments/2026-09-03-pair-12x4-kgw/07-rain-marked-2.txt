I walked inside to find a box labeled FALM and a bottle of the new wine. I closed the box with my left bootstrap on in the backseat and handed a small brown card to the cats. I dropped off my own check and took the money from the note and put it on my car.

I left on a quiet Monday morning. The family had no cars on that day but a bus would pull up when I passed by and had to get my groceries on the way home. I parked on one side of the road near a house by a creek and called out, to no effect.
