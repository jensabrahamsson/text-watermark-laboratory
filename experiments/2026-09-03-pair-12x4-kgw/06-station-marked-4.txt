By this time the lights of the boat got up. This turned out not to be true. The men were lying on the deck all along. Everyone was sleeping, and they were scared of the sound. The man who had climbed above them, who had jumped off the deck, got up one by one, and said, "I can do it now. I am not the kind of man they would want on their boat."

As soon as the three men had gone and got up, the train sped away and passed the deck. The men fell dead on their faces. The next morning more people went to see the man
