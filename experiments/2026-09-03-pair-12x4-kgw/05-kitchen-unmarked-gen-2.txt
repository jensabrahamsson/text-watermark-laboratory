One of my relatives, a local police officer named Henry W. Brown, saw the pan. He had heard it in the middle of a game of basketball when he tried to stop a man to throw it at him. He grabbed the pan and placed it in the sink and began a fire at it. He ran back to the stove and began to fill it in with the onions. He put it in the kitchen the next afternoon, on the stove outside and made up the fire so it couldn't be removed. He then made a huge mistake—but with the exception of the onions, it wasn't burned.
