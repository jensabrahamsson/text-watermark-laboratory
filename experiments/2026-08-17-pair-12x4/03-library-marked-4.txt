Closing is the same as closing for dinner, except that it is more time. My two dogs go to the library. They have just arrived and leave me, no matter how well I keep to a certain amount of time.

One of the children tries very hard not to get a glimpse of me while he goes up the stairs in the garden. He is very shy and never stops when I go for him.
