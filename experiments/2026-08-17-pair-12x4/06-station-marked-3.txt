"This is the ship we have been waiting all night for."

He turned and looked at the empty seats on the left; his blue dress with a lace collar on it was nothing. He was sure it would be fine for his family.

The door of the station was opened and a woman was there, standing outside the door. She had a pair of black shoes and black trousers that covered a waist with the left front.

"I am the ferry!" the lady said firmly and looked at the old man. This time the gentleman spoke up.
