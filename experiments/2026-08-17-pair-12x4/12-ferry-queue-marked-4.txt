The ferry was waiting for you

I could see from the back of the van: the young men who were waiting were not wearing trousers, or in shorts to avoid being seen by the others in the queue. The men with beards and T-shirts were getting down on the pavement and they wore their hats and badges in their pockets, so that they could pass by in one line, while the others, in suit jackets, stood next to you and kept going. You could hear them trying to get through the street behind you with a cane and you could see that some were just using the way the tram was.
