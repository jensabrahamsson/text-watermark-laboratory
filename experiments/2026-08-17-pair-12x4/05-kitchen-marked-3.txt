"Oh, my God," I muttered, and then the fire stopped. We both knew it would stop as soon as it was in the kitchen. No fire was ever going to stop, until she and my aunt came to talk to me. The sound came from upstairs, as if someone was coming down inside saying, "How are you doing? What do you think?" If any of us had been going down that night, I think that it would have become a story. After the alarm bell rang, I turned in, and looked over the mess the fire was in.
