The printer worked. The three layers of paper that the people at the party worked so hard to make work. The two, I had noticed, just worked so well that it was difficult to think of them but, the first time I saw them, I was very, very excited.

They were so busy. They had done everything they could, but they were not doing any for me. I was more of a fool, a fool of heart than a fool of mind.
