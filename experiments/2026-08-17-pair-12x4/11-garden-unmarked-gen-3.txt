We walked along an old railway line, up and down, to one or two houses that have been converted. There is a bus on the bridge, and at the end is a fence over the place in front. The bus is in a long, narrow tunnel that is still up in the street. The whole of it is built up on the side of a large rock and is a great attraction. I had no idea that people from neighbouring houses would stop and watch us, but after about an hour I was pretty sure they'd come for their supper too.
