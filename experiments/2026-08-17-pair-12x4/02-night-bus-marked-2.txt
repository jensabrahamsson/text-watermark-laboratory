The bus is all the same to me. It is almost as hard as I see it. It has a big black box in the passenger side that gives them an easy way to buy the food. If you get close to the gate you might hear a whistle at the entrance. I can see the gate and there are many people in it. If I do not know where you are, my best chance of finding you and being able to hear if I do or not is with a long white distance radio, which gives two or three hours.
