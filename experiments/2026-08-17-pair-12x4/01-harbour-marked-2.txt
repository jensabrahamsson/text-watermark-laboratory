The ferry was over, I knew, but I wasn't worried. No one was making any noise. I was standing at one end of the dock and a white-lined windowless tarmac was flashing and the ferry was about to make a right turn. There was a woman on a stretcher, she looked like a woman but in a suit with an orange hair on her right. One row of passengers had climbed aboard but had not yet reached the port. I tried to stay on but the tarmac was thick with men in black suits and a woman in red. A man asked the girl if she wanted help. She nodded
