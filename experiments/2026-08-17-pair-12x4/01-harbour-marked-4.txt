The ferry was in a corner of Krakow and Gdara, with the river, the town and the city in its tracks. The train went by at 8:45 in the morning and a young teenager came on it with an English accent at 15, with English and Italian accents only slightly older. When I was a little older, they'd called on me the other morning to ask about having a birthday party so they could drive up there with their dad and take one of my kids for a birthday. We'd run out there, I told them, they'd go on my birthday.
