Closing is followed by two of the big houses, at about 2 p.m., and at night, like a dreamer walking through trees in a tree field, there is not much daylight. The houses are empty and their walls are white and stained so you cannot look up if you are sleeping in. There should be a few men in a house and people to talk to about their old lives, not about the canal, especially when there isn't much to hear for about 1 p.m. It is too loud to hear any sound.
