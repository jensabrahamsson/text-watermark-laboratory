The ferry was so close that I almost missed getting out. I walked through it half the time and I felt that there had been some odd movement in the road. I tried to stay on the ferry. I didn't think someone even took me up on it. It had been an hour or so and I knew I would have to wait a bit, but I kept turning around to see what was going on. The only thing I noticed after all that was salt. It was a tiny orange. It was an octopus.

For some reason I kept going.

I passed several more people to go.
