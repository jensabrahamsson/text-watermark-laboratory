In this story, I use a different approach for telling a story; as well as using characters that I have written before, I add a few new ones (dog, cheese etc). This is a fun little exercise to try out different types of writing – the short story, flash fiction, poetry, creative non-fiction or whatever you fancy doing!
For today’s challenge: write 100 words using the word “cheese” and any other two words from the story below. You may like to write something completely new or continue with the same characters/setting/story – it doesn’t matter as long as your story includes all three elements
