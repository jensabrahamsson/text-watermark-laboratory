At least 30 cars sat on the M25 near London waiting for traffic to clear between Junctions 9 and 14 on Thursday morning.
The slow-moving motorway was clogged up as heavy snow fell overnight, trapping many drivers who had driven to work or school by road rather than using public transport or their car-pooling scheme.
There have been reports of accidents involving lorries and buses after the storms but so far there have been no deaths reported.
Motorists were told they should not attempt to travel until conditions improved – with queues expected to be particularly bad at night-time when fewer people are on the roads.
