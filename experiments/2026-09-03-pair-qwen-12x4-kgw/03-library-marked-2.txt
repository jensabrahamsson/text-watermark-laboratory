It seems odd that I should choose this particular moment when I am sitting in a library. Perhaps my decision to sit down rather than wait was made before I left home or maybe before I read these lines. Perhaps I chose to write these lines after the library opened or after it closed. Perhaps I will never write them. In some way I am grateful that I wrote them; that I took them seriously; that I found myself looking up the words 'stone steps', 'black' and 'yellow'. If you go into a library on a Monday morning or Thursday evening you may be less surprised by these events.
