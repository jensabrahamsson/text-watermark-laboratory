It was a summer night when we were in Paris. We lived in a small apartment, but there was still space for all of us: one bedroom, two beds, and another living room with a sofa bed for two people. In front of the fireplace, my cousin’s daughter had been teaching me how to knit. Her voice was soft, her hands delicate, and she kept a small box with some yarn and needles on her table so that she could do something productive while watching television.
