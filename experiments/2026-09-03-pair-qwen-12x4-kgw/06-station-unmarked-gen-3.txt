I sat in the passenger waiting room, listening for my name and looking at the posters on the wall for trains going south. It was raining. I wanted to look out of the window, but there were no windows to look at.
After the long wait, when the station manager said "We're running behind schedule," it felt like forever before I saw the white lettered train that had finally arrived and pulled into its designated spot. My seat had been reserved by someone else, so I stood up, went to find my bag, and headed off toward the exit.
