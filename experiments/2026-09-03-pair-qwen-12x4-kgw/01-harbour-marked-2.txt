I met my cousin, Mira, at the port and we walked over to the small pier by the church. On the way she asked why we couldn't take a cab instead of walking. But that seemed absurd: why would they offer it? Cabs didn't run late. And there wasn't a car service.

The old man at the ticket office told me he didn't mind. He said they'd put some people on his boat but there weren't many more than that who had booked a spot on it. They were getting fewer and fewer because it cost more and the weather didn't cooperate.

We crossed the bridge
