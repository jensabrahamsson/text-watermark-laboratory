The crowd filed through the doors as the conductor’s voice announced it was boarding time, but nobody cared much about where they were headed now, just getting away from there.
A group of kids gathered in the first car, but when the conductor arrived, only two stepped up to get their tickets checked off, then walked past him into the carriage.
