What is the significance of the recurring theme of time and loss in the passage?

In the passage, the recurring theme of time and loss refers to the melancholic mood that pervades much of the text. Time seems to be both a constant presence in the library but also something that slips away from those present, leaving the characters feeling lost and disconnected from each other. Loss is also suggested through the various personal losses felt by the characters - including missed opportunities, personal regrets, and feelings of loneliness or abandonment.
