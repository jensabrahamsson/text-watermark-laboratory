Rain had already left a small wet patch by the porch steps, but there was a big, open, muddy puddle on the sidewalk behind the carport. Rain didn’t have enough shoes for both his feet, so he climbed up the concrete stairs on his hands and knees until his toes met the carpeted floor. He held out his right hand towards the wet floor. A large puddle covered it. There was an oily feel about it like the oil from frying food in a pan. I thought of all the times my mother had cleaned that kitchen floor after cooking fried chicken.
