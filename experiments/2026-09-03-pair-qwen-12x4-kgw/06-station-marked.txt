When he stepped onto the train, his face was wet with tears and his hands shook uncontrollably as he fumbled through his pockets looking for the keys that were supposed to open the cabriolet in which they would take him to the seaside.
“Did you go through all the steps?” I asked.
It seemed as though his answer was to be found by his unsteadiness, but when we reached the cabriolet the man took off his shoes and socks, walked around the seat to the other side of the cabriolet, undid the locks and took his shoes and socks out of his bag before putting them on again
