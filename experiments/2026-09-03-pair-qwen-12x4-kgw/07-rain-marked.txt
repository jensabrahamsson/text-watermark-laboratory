It was sunny but it was still winter, so it snowed, but not here. I didn’t know how to respond to this sudden arrival or even what to call it; perhaps a change? No. Winter never changes, and spring never arrives until something happens.
When you want rain, rain doesn’t come when you ask for it. When you want light snow, snow comes at night with no warning, just like the cat who had already started snoring by now, having fallen asleep next to the dog.
