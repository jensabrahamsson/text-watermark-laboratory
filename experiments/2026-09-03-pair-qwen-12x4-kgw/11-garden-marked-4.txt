A few tomatoes are already soft, but others seem to be perfectly ripe. They will probably ripen further in cooler weather, but for now they can stay fresh a couple of days more or less by being kept in fridge and covered with a piece of cloth that keeps the air in (not letting any out).
I had 37 tomato plants last year (8 varieties) and they all did very well - they were big, heavy producers. This is my first year without any tomato plants as they started to die off after the frost this summer, so there's no replacement for them.
