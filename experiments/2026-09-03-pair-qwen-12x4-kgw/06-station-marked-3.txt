“Can you tell me what time we depart?”
“We depart at 2:15.”
“But don’t you say it’s about to leave?”
“I am very sorry, but my system is programmed not to announce our departures. You will just have to remember to get off here at two twenty.”
He walked away.
A few minutes later the door opened and another person came in carrying his briefcase. He sat down beside someone already seated.
“How many seats are there?”
“Eighty-two.”
“Is that all?”
“Yes.” They both turned around as a woman in a long dress stepped into the room.
