The line broke up into two groups: those who’d been standing at platform three for ages, and those who had come running from across town in time to catch it. One of the latter got in front of the line as they left.
She was carrying a book and she was smiling but she didn’t have any tickets so I couldn’t help her.
She looked around me and said, ‘Can you just give me my change? ’ She must be hungry, I thought; that’s why she needs some money to buy food now.
