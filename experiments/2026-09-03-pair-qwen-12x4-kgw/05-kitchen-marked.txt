It’s a summer night now but there are few of us around this table – two grown children, my daughter, her friend, me. The other women have gone off to their own houses by now, leaving the kitchen and its cooking to the men. When the last of the girls had left, they turned on the television set, a small one that has always been used for that purpose rather than a full-size set in the corner, but which still fills the room as much as if it were a bigger TV.
