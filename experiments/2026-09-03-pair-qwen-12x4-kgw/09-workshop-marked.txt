I had to wait, in order to paint it white, for the other parts of the frame to dry. In those three days I had to watch it grow.
Then it came back from the printer. And I had to read the words that appeared on the page for the first time.
There was a long silence between me and it as we looked at each other across the table. The printer's voice was telling me what to do, but I didn't want it to say anything else, just wanted to stand there and look. Like a fish out of water waiting for its owner to release it.
