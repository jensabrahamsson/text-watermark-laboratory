The car had driven past before I saw it, a green sedan. I watched for a moment, but could see only the hood and dashboard until the light turned yellow. It continued forward; I realized it would be impossible to catch up. I waited there for a while longer, watching the cars turn into the streets and then drive away. The time elapsed was long enough for me to start to forget what I had seen and to wonder
