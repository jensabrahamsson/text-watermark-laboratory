The hotel was small but clean and modern, and the desk clerk seemed like he might actually speak English. He offered me a cup of coffee and some tea, both hot, even though the air was cool. My mother always told me to drink my tea before I slept—her own mother had done so in her old age—and that made sense here. The sun went over the hill soon after I entered.

The next morning the room was empty, as usual. I lay in bed and stared at the white walls. Then I put on a suitcoat, went downstairs, and paid the bill.
