I was not able to convince her. But she did pick a few ripe tomatoes off her garden and gave me two more. That is my day’s harvest, plus another 2 or so from the basket I put on the porch.
The only tomato plant that survived winter and spring are three of these small ones. One started out looking very promising, but it got all burned up last summer. The other two were much better this year, except for those leaves. They have not had any growth since the beginning of June. I guess that explains why there are no tomatoes now.
There are some big fat tomatoes growing near the back fence.
