We were waiting at the railway station. We were supposed to get our tickets from the booking office but the agent had been sent away for repairs. They'd told me about it before we left. There would be no train to get to our destination.

My father said he knew someone who had some extra tickets. He offered them to me, but they cost money and it seemed unfair that they would be needed so early. In any case, I didn't want to give them away. My dad's friends were all going places this week. If they'd known about the situation they wouldn't have let him have the tickets,
