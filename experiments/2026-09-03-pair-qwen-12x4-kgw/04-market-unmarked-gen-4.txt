This was my Friday morning routine as a student at the University of South Wales, Cardiff. I would walk across campus to my college every morning to study English for business and management. It was the longest half-hour of my day and it would pass by slowly, but happily. In between lectures, students sat in front of computers, laptops on desks or spread out on tables, reading through their text books.
It wasn’t long before I would make my way down the stairs, past the shop where I got my morning coffee from and out onto the pavement outside the college building. The air was crisp with fresh autumn leaves that had been dropped overnight
