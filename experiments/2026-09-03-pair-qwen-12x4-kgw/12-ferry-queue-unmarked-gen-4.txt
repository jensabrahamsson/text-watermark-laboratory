I think I have always been a little bit fascinated by queues – they fascinate me because it seems to be an endless process that we humans make every day, whether at work or when waiting for trains or buses. They can feel like an endless stream but actually, if you look closely, there are quite a few different stages involved in queuing – starting out, stopping, pretending you’re doing something else, and waiting for the next part to begin.
