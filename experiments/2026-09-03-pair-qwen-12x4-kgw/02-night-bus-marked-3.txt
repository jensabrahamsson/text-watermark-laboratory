It gets late around four or five o'clock in the evening, so we are going all the way home now. Sometimes there is some kind of concert, but usually the streets are empty and it is just a quiet journey back. We pass the park where a small group of kids sit. One day I asked the man sitting next to me why he was still there watching them, and he said his son goes to school with some of them. But there are two children missing. They did not tell him. "There's nothing for you to do," he said, "You can be here all day if you want."
