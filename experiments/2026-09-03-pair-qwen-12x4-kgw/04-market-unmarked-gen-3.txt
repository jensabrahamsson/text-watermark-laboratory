It was a sunny day in July, my third week on the market. We were busy with the market people who had brought their stalls out for summer and the other traders were starting to get ready. There were 40 stallholders on the market that day.
My stall is in the middle of the market so I don’t have to stand too long, but sometimes I do. On this day I was stood at the top of the hill as the sun shone over the green hills. It was great being able to watch all the markets happen around me; I could see everything. I looked down at my stall from the hillside
