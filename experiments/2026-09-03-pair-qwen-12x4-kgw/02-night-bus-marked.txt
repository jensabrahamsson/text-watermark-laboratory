But wait! This route does not go anywhere!

This poem could be called a poem of observation and memory; it describes many familiar scenes but is not very explicit about why or how these are memories or observations. The opening lines suggest that "night bus" refers to buses as well as trains and suggest also that the author has traveled extensively on the routes he writes about. The image of the "same shops in a different order" is repeated and might be seen as an allusion to travelogues. But there is no narrative structure here. It would be more like an encyclopedia article.
