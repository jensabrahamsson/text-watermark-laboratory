The sun comes up at 4:30am and sets around 10pm. The nights are long but not too hot, just a bit cooler than where I am from.
It’s an odd season for tomatoes here; we got two full days of rain in May that delayed ripening. There were some that ripened earlier though and my mother in law planted several tomato seedlings outside (I don’t know how she did it since I think I have never seen her plants produce anything), so now there are many more.
In the evenings, when we come home, we pick tomatoes from our garden or out on the lawn
