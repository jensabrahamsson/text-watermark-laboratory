It’s just as well I couldn’t make it up, really; all of these thoughts and feelings would be so much worse if they could be said out loud without being written down to begin with. If you are like me (which means most people), your thoughts run like a river through your head all day long, churning away at you while you go about your business, until finally, at night when you lay in bed and think about everything else, something rises to the surface and breaks out into words that aren’t entirely your own.
But, let’s not pretend. Writing them down helps me cope, at least for a moment
