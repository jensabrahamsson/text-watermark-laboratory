A couple days later: 

The weather had turned warm since the last time I'd been here, so I wore only shorts. I stopped at the supermarket because everything looked cheaper than usual and got a bottle of beer. The shopkeeper waved me away and left me alone with a display of plastic bags, their contents still inside. Salt was less noticeable now. At noon the ferry brought people home and by midnight they were all gone. There were no children or dogs or old men on the pier anymore.

My passport was sitting in the room. I opened it carefully. It wasn't expired yet, but it wouldn't be much longer.
