The two boys in front of me were quiet and thoughtful. I could tell they hadn’t heard anything from the station, but their eyes followed a girl sitting next to them who had turned around at my arrival, her hair falling onto her face as she tried not to let the wind blow it back. Her eyes scanned the platform. Her long legs stuck out of the window of the train’s waiting car, her black shoes catching the yellow streetlights outside. She looked like an old-fashioned doll.
