Tonight I move it to the rail by the door, the one for things that are trying too hard to be noticed, and I know it'll be back on the main rack by Thursday because that's usually how it goes.

The radio's still on low, some program about ferries, a man talking about winter schedules like it's the most reasonable subject in the world. Outside the window it's already dark enough that my own reflection stands between me and the street, doing the same slow work I'm doing, folding a jumper that doesn't need refolding. I do it anyway.
