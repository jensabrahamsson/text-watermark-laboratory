They were stacked in pairs. Each pair had been used once for a different job and were now ready to go home. One day, they would have filled shelves with fruits, provided food for animals, and given shelter to birds. They all knew that it was time to say goodbye.
As they waited for the tractor to pick them up, one of the ladders looked into the distance and wondered what life would be like without her brothers and sisters. Would she still be as useful? Could she help someone else find fruit?
