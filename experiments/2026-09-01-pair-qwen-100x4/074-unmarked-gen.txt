It was cold outside, and there were few people around. However, on the other side of the door was a scene that looked like it was from another world. There were dozens of firecrackers lit up in the sky and they could be seen for miles. The sound of fireworks and thunder could be heard in the distance.
At this time, a middle-aged man with dark glasses walked out of the smokehouse, dressed casually but looking somewhat strange. He stopped at the entrance to the smokehouse and asked, "Where is your brother?"
When he heard his words, the people inside the smokehouse also turned their heads towards him.
