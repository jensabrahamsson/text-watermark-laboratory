He’d spent years on the ground floor and then in the pit, not much else to do. He never really knew where his feet went after the work was done, the job was over, when it got dark, he would look for a way out of there. He found it in the bar at the end of the hall where the men gathered for the evening. He felt so small amongst them, but they were all so much older than him. They had their own places up the hill, some still worked here, but mostly just drank. He made himself something to eat, bought some more drink, and went down to the bar
