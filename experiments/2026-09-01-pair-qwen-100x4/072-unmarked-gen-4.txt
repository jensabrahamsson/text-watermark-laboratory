A group of artists in Chicago, IL were painting a mural over the top of another mural when they noticed the older one had been spray-painted with racist slurs. They changed the old message to a more positive one and it now says "Welcome, You are Here."
Chicago Public Art has also since removed both murals as part of its ongoing work to promote diversity on city walls. In 2019, the city’s mural program was named one of America's Best Urban Mural Programs by The Trust for Public Land. Since then, nearly $50 million has been invested into public art projects across the city.
