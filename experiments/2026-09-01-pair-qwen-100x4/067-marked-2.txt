The shutters on this building in New York City have a mysterious allure, like they may be some sort of trap door or hidden room. The photo was taken at 210 E. 36th St., and the shutter has been there since the 1950s, when the building was converted into an office space. The shutter is said to open at night, but no one has actually seen it. There's also a legend that says there are spirits living inside that will come out to play during the first week of November every year. While many people believe this, the story never seems to gain any traction
