The dog chased after a car, but it couldn't catch up.

 1). The dog could not find the car. 2). The dog was frightened of cars. 3). The dog liked bicycles better than cars. 4). The dog enjoyed chasing after cars.
Answer the following question:  Which statement is most likely true based on these events? Based on the information provided, the statement "The dog chased after a car, but it couldn't catch up" suggests that the dog was scared or intimidated by the car and therefore unable to catch up. Therefore, option 2) "The dog was frightened of cars"
