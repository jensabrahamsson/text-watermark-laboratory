He felt his skin crawl and a cold sweat break out on his forehead. He had no way to tell if the sun was going down or not, but it looked like dusk.
The boatyard, an abandoned shipyard that he owned along with his brother, had been empty for over ten years now.
But when he came here this afternoon, the sun was almost at its highest point in the sky. It meant the boatyard’s windows were open to let in sunlight. His brother had said, “You’ll have to be careful, but you can’t afford to let your guard down,” before he left to go work.
