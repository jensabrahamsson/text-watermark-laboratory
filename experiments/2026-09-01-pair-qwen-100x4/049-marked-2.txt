He knew his grandfather was there in the woods, listening to him as he walked by. He didn't want it to sound like a door slam or an object dropped on the ground, but just the quiet of nature, the crunching leaves under his feet, a gentle wind rustling through branches overhead.
The house seemed more spacious now than when they'd lived there together years ago. He could hear his father talking with his friends outside; could smell the wood smoke from the chimney. The grass was greener too, as though growing faster because of the heat coming off the earth.
