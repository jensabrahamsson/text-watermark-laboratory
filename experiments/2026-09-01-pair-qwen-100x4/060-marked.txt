The ducks and gulls and waders in the mud knew to stay away. The marsh was no place for a lone man, especially not on the dark night of a full moon, as it often is. They all knew this too.
In the dimness, the man’s breath puffed from his lips as he walked along the riverbank. The breeze from the ocean pushed through his flannel shirt and cotton pants. He shivered.
