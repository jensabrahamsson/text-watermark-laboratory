She had seen this happen many times before, but never with such ferocity. It was as if all the storms on earth were converging on this tiny pond in one swift stroke.
Water poured out like a waterfall from the open mouth of the trough. Raindrops splashed against the stones around it, leaving drops in its place. They were small enough to be invisible to the naked eye, yet they added up to something huge.
With such force, water could reach the sky. The leaf floated there, unable to move.
But the leaves weren’t only the ones floating in that little pond. A storm had already been brewing for months
