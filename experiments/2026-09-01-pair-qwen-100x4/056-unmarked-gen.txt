The door to her room opened and she saw the first person in three months standing there. He was a young man with dark hair, a pale face, and a sharp nose that was always tilted upward as though he were smiling.
"Good morning," she said as she turned away from him to go into the room.
He watched her enter, then turned his attention back toward the stairs, where his gaze followed until it finally rested on the third step. Then he stepped off the stair and stood at the top of the flight, looking up at the two-story white house across the road from them.
