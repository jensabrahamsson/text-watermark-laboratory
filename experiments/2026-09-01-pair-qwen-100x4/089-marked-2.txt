The old man was sitting on his porch, his hands wrapped in a rag to protect them from blisters. He had seen his last nail come loose from his hand at some point but it was now firmly planted deep into the wood of his chair.
"I think I might need another bottle," he said to the woman who lived across the street.
She laughed and told him she was sure he would not need another one because her son would be back soon with a few extra.
"That's why you don't count on your friends when you're old," she added, as if it were common knowledge.
The old man nodded and sat up straight
