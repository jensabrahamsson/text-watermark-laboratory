The pigeon perched on a branch above the wheat field, surveying the situation. The pigeon is a symbol of prosperity and good fortune for Chinese people.
As the sun came up over the wheat fields, it started to rain.
The pigeon flew off from its perch.
It landed in a small pond near the barn.
As soon as it was safe, the pigeon took flight again. The pigeon knew that rain brought thunderstorms and a chance for more grains.
