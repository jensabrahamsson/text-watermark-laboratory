The sign was placed in a location where there are a number of tourists every day.
Photographing someone who is doing something illegal (even if you do not think that person is committing a crime) is considered to be "intruding".
If you want to take photographs, go and get a permit from the authorities. Even if the signs state that taking photographs is forbidden, it would be rude to disregard the rules.
