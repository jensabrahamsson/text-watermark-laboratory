He’d spent years trying to learn how best to catch the big ones, but had always failed. The bigger and faster they were, the harder it was to hit them. So he didn’t get anything from the big ones in the end. He did well at the small ones though – those that came back up slowly, or where there was no fight. He could tell which one would survive.
