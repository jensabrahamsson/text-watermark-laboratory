He was not alone. An old woman stood on the steps, her eyes trained on him. She did not move when he raised his hand in salute and then lowered it again.
The boy was startled by a sudden gust of wind. He saw her hands clench into fists. The man standing next to her looked up at the boy with a wry smile. It was a familiar smile, but his eyes were full of concern as well. The boy glanced down at himself. His trousers and shirt were all wet from the rain. "How can I possibly be dressed for this?" he said to himself.
