The old man was sitting in his chair in the garden, and he looked up at her with a wistful look on his face. He seemed to have been waiting for something all this time, but now that it had arrived, he felt disappointed. "I've lost my watch," he said. And he took out the box of watches that she had brought him as a wedding gift from America. But they were not his. They were his son's.
