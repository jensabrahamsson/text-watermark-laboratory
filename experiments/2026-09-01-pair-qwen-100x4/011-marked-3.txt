The waitress collected cups from a cafe and put them in the kitchen.
She put all the cups in the sink, which was empty of any water. She filled it with some cold water, which she then added some ice cubes to cool down the water. She poured this mixture into an old tea kettle that was lying around in the corner of the kitchen. The old teapot was already hot enough for boiling the coffee, but she still heated it further by heating it over the stove. After putting the cups back on the table and finishing her shift, she went home.
I found out about this when I was eating breakfast at the restaurant.
