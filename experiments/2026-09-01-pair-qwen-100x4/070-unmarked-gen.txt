We stood there for a moment, the wind howling in our ears. The rain poured down like a thousand tiny drops hammering on the roof of my car.
I couldn’t get it to go anywhere. I didn’t want to leave my car here, but neither did I have the money for another one. It had already cost me enough.
I turned and walked back towards my home. My thoughts raced as they usually do when the weather gets bad outside. I wanted to hear something, anything that would give some clue about where we were going next. I was getting tired and I felt that I needed a break from this situation.
