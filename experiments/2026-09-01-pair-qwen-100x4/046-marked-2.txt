He stood at the far edge of the water, hands on hips. He was not watching them; they were just there to remind him he could not see. The only part of the pond visible from this angle, that he could even perceive, was the edge of the shallow mud flat on which the bodies lay. A black and white pattern of reds and oranges, his eyes would find them in their blue-green reflection off the surface of the lake.
