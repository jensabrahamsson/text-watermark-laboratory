The torch was a new one, but it did not flicker because of wear and tear. The flame was greenish-yellow and seemed to be glowing from within. When the watchman reached a place where the floor slopes up and down, he noticed something strange at the corner. The torch's flame had stopped flickering. There were no signs of wear or damage on its surface. As he got closer, he saw that there was a hole in the wall behind the corner. The hole was small, and it looked like it had been made intentionally. "Who is in here?" the watchman asked. But there was no answer.
