That's the story, at least, of the fence around my childhood home in East Tennessee. It was one of those early projects that took years to finish because we had so many things on our minds - work, school, the future. As the years passed, it seemed to grow ever more neglected. By the time I got married, the old picket fences were long gone and I was faced with a brand new front yard filled with towering evergreen trees, a huge mulch garden, and several flower beds - all surrounded by an aluminum bollard barrier made from recycled steel, a project I completed in just a few weeks.
