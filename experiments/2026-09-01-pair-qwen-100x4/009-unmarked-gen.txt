As a child, he had spent many hours watching people walk by and wonder about what they did. He saw the rich and poor, those in positions of power and the common man. He was fascinated with all these different faces; it was like they were showing him how life worked.
One day he went to the local hardware store and asked the owner if he could learn to polish brass. The owner didn’t know if he would be able to teach the kid how to do it, but decided to give it a shot. It took weeks for the boy to become proficient at polishing the brass numbers on the streetlights.
