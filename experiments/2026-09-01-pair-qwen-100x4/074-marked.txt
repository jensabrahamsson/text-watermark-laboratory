That morning I had woken to an unseasonably warm and sunny day. It had been cold for a while, but now it was 72 degrees with clear skies.
We all headed out to walk around town, and by walking I mean that my husband walked and I ran down Main Street in the rain chasing after him, and laughing and chatting.
We stopped in at the orchard smokehouse for a bite to eat. I had never heard of the place until we got there. There are no signs or advertisements outside. You have to ask someone about it.
