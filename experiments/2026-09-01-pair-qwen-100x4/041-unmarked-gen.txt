The tin was not a proper container for ashes, he realized. He had never even considered that. It should have been discarded after use. He didn’t want to leave ash or smoldering bits of cigarette on any surface he touched. He wanted them to burn completely away. But what if they didn’t? What if some remained? What if it were still a problem in another ten years’ time? Would anyone think he had put ash on his hands? Was he responsible for anything?
