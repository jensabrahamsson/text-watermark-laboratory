It was not just that his audience was empty; there were no other people present either. His words did not sound out, they seemed muffled and distorted by a strange barrier in front of him.
In fact, this is how it looked when he first got there.
At first he didn’t know what was happening. He asked the community centre workers if they had seen anyone else but there was no one around. The only sign of life came from a large TV screen projecting images onto a huge screen behind them.
This wasn’t just any TV screen, he could see its circuits glowing as well as some tiny particles dancing inside its casing.
