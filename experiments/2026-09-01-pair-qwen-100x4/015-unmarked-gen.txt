The image on the window was distorted and unclear. His hands were moving fast, his fingers dancing like lightning over the keypad. He was playing a piece by Beethoven, he had just learned it. The boy could only hear bits and pieces of it; the tune of the song that had been sung so many times before as they played football together. But he didn't know what the music actually sounded like because he had never heard it properly from someone else. Only his own voice. It wasn't the kind of music that made him want to dance or sing along with anyone. The boy's arms felt heavy; he could hardly move
