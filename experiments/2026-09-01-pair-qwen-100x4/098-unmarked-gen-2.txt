It was a fine day, the kind where one can feel like a hero.
I have done something for my country. I have helped someone. I have saved an animal and brought a life back to health. I have done what good people do.
On the way home, I sat on the bench at the canal in Rotherhithe. A man, perhaps in his 60s or 70s, said "you look like you're enjoying yourself."
So, of course, I did.
And when he saw that my hands were busy with a camera, he asked if I would mind taking a picture of him too
