She then brought the cups back to work, and the other waitresses noticed them. It was the first time any of them had seen a cup in so many years. They were very impressed with her.
She said: "I think I'm going mad."
The next day the same thing happened again.
She then walked out into the corridor, and asked: "Did anyone else see the cold cup?"
"Only you did," replied one of the others. "What is it? Are you going mad?" she asked, looking worried. "No, I just think I'm going mad," she answered, and they all burst out laughing
