When a person dies in your family, you feel an intense sense of loss. The loss is often even more intense if it’s caused by something that could have been prevented, like smoking or over-exercising. We all hope to never face this kind of grief, but sometimes life does send us unexpected setbacks.
I lost my mother-in-law to lung cancer five years ago after she survived breast cancer for 10 years. Her death was not preventable; it happened suddenly. But I still felt an immense sadness over her passing. How could such an important woman pass away? And what had I done wrong?
