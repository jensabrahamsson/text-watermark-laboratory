He picked out the pieces of his own head and tossed them at the garbage can in the street.
He wanted his haircut again but it was too late.
A group of young boys were gathered around him. One said, "Look at his bald spot!"
"I'm not going bald," he replied.
"Your whole life is a big bald spot." He replied.
"Then I should never regret anything!" The man shouted and headed toward the street with the hairpile.
He reached the garbagecan. As soon as the bag touched the ground, a dog began eating his hair!
The boy screamed as his father chased after the dog.
