And then she made her way to the next client’s house. The last one of the day, she was exhausted when she got there.
She opened up the door to the old man who lived by himself on a quiet street.
He had longed for company and his only visitor was a blind date that he couldn’t find.
She saw an empty chair by the fireplace, so she sat down and waited. She was going to leave him some notes but didn’t have any pen.
