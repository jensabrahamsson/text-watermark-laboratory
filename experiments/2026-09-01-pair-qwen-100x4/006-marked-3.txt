He knew his name was a misnomer and he was always left out of any conversation. Everyone else was in a group, talking about their new phones, the best movies they saw or what they thought of this new music artist who came out. It didn’t matter to him. He preferred his quiet times, where no one could ever know what he was thinking. They’d just assume he was a loner or someone with an attention deficit disorder. Neither did he care too much either way. At least now he wasn’t seen as some kind of troublemaker when people noticed how he rarely interacted with them anymore.
