But now, that name is being changed too. It has been removed and repainted with the title "Red Star."
Red Star, which translates as "star" in Chinese, was chosen by an Asian American group called the Asian-American Art Project (AAAP) for its diversity of themes.
The Red Star mural will be part of a new program designed to help bring artistic work to people who are often left out of the art scene.
