He’d spent much of the day out here in the open. It was a great feeling to be alone with himself and his thoughts. He loved that there was no one here except for him. He was in his own world, surrounded by nature. In many ways he felt he belonged here. But at night the emptiness crept in.
He sighed, rubbing his hands together to warm them up. He wanted something to eat before he went back inside. He had been here long enough that the vegetables would be ready. They were his most trusted companions in this life.
There were no trees in this forest but that didn’t stop him
