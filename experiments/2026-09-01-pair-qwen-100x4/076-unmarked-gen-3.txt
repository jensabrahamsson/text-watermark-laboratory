I looked around, not really sure what to do, when I saw a man with a big cardboard sign on his back.
He said he would take me out for a cup of tea and pay all my fare from now until Christmas.
That’s when I realized that I was in trouble, but it was still better than being stuck on the train. At least I’d get to go home at least one more time.
The man took me to his place, and we sat down and drank some tea while he asked me questions about my trip so far. He wanted to know where I was going, how long I would be away, etc
