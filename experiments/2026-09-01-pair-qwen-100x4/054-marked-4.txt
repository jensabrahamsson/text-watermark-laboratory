This is one reason to print things in bulk. The first few copies are expensive, but later copies are less so, so you can save money by buying in bulk.
It also means that if something gets mixed up it doesn't matter because you don't have enough of each letter for the full sentence anyway. You might just get a half-remembered letter back from your friend instead of an exact copy.
