Tea-time in English is often referred to as “tea break” or “afternoon tea” in the United States. However, it is not an official break like a lunchtime break. It usually lasts from around 4 p.m. until 5 p.m., but can extend up to six hours. This is why it is considered as “the perfect time to catch up with your friends.”
