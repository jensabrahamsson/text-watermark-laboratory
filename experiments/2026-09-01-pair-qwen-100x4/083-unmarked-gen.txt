She had to get out from under the music stand in order to get to the back of the hall. She was practicing and she could hear herself playing. The sound echoed throughout the hall, the walls and floors and even out in the street outside. It wasn’t just any kind of sound she heard. It was the sound of an angel. She played it for everyone who came by: teachers, parents, students and friends, and they all agreed on one thing: she sounded like an angel.
