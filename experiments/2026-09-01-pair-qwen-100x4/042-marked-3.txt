He was happy to be back in his own home again. After all, he had lost count of the number of times he'd gone into the house after it had been blown away by a storm.
His new satellite dish caught him by surprise when it appeared on the roof. He had been expecting a replacement for the one that had blown up during a strong windstorm about six months ago.
"Nice work," he said as he took it down from the chimney. The weathering of the wood had not dulled its sharp metal tip. "You'll be glad to hear that I'm getting rid of the old broom tomorrow."
