But the cricket pavilion, even without a hand on the wall or even an electric bell, is not without charm.
If you were sitting in one of the bleacher seats overlooking the playing field from one side to another, you might be looking down on all those people. But don’t let this fool you into thinking that everyone is here for the match. They’re here for the spectacle as much as for the cricket.
