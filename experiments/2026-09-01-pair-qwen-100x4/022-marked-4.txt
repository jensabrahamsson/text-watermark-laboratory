He picked it up, shook it to clear any bits of popcorn. "I'm not going to eat that," he said. He tossed the heap on the floor, whereupon someone else passed by and walked over to clean up his mess. It wasn't until that other person left the movie theater that the usher turned around and found himself standing face-to-face with a woman in her fifties or sixties. "It's just a bit of popcorn I didn't want to take home."
"It's okay," the usher assured the woman. "Just toss it in here." He took another handful from the still-crumpled pile
