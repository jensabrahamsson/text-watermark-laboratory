I walked down the stairs to the beach, where the sun was setting in an indigo sky. The sound of waves lapping against the shore and seagulls squawking echoed in my ears. I looked at the ocean as if for one last time before it would go out to sea forever. It seemed like the only thing left was my feet on the sand.
I started to walk up the stairs, but my knees felt weak. I couldn't lift them anymore. A wave hit me, shattering the glass of hope that I still held onto. I couldn't stand up; I could barely make it back to my room
