They were photos of the first batch of new arrivals, from 1960.
My parents had been married for less than six years when their second son was born in January 1960. This time there would be no leaks, and the boxes remained dry until after I was three, even though they remained packed away for over fifty years. The arrival of my younger brother was like a spring rain; all at once the attic was full to bursting with photographs, and I found myself staring into a sea of blurry faces and happy expressions.
