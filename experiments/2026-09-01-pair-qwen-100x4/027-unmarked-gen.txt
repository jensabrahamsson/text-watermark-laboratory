And then she was left standing in the rain. The man who had fed the meter, had been a stranger; now he would have to go home and pay for the electricity that he had used. His apartment building’s electric bill was due, so his only hope of avoiding it was to go to the meter box and pull out his credit card and swipe it across the terminal to authorize payment.
The arrow remained stubbornly immobile at the end of the meter shaft.
