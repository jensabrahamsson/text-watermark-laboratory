The old broom was on the floor in front of the fire, but it did not stand up straight. The wind blew it over to the window and knocked down all three chairs on its way to where the stove stood. After that, the man who kept the watch took off his hat and rubbed his head on it before he went back to sleep, because he wanted to stay warm. As soon as the keepers left, the man put out his cigarette, because he thought it was dirty.
He got some more wood from the fireplace for himself and then made himself a tea and sat quietly on the sofa watching TV.
