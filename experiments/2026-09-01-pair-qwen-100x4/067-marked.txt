The shutters on this 1830s building in London, England, are one of its most famous features. The shuttered windows were originally decorated with scenes from Shakespeare's plays and paintings of Queen Victoria at various points in her life. Since the windows are not operational, it is possible that the owners have decided to close them permanently or perhaps there is a new development project planned for the building which may mean the shutters will remain closed.
