After many years of graffiti, the underpass at Haskett and Balfour streets in Dartmouth has received some art care. A mural was painted there a few years ago but the artist moved on to other projects, leaving behind some spray-painted text. But now it's back!
A new painting has taken its place and this time it's an original work by local artist Mike MacIntyre (left), who is known for his large-scale murals and public art. The mural features several characters, with the message "Dartmouth" written in yellow paint near the center of the image.
It will take about three weeks
