We walked around the area and found no one. We went back down the hill, got in the truck, and headed home.
As we were driving along the road near our house I saw two boys playing on the bridge over the creek that leads into the river. They were running across the water with only short lengths of rope for stepping bars. As we passed them they waved at us. We slowed down and honked the horn but did not stop.
We drove past their parents who were standing on the bank watching from behind their fence. They didn't seem to mind.
This is so sad! The kids should be out playing by now
