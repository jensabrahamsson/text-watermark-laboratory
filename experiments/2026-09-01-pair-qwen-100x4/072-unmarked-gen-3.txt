A mural on the west side of Underpass was changed to the "Ladies and Gents" sign. The artist, who wanted to remain anonymous, said he painted it after some people complained that there weren't enough restroom facilities available at the underpass.
"The underpass is very well known in this city, so I decided to put my art on it," the artist said. "I think it's great now."
The underpass has always been a gathering place for many people. There are restrooms inside and a convenience store with food. The mural added another visual element to the area.
