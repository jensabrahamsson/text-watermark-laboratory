She picked the photos up one by one and examined each in detail, as if they were fragile porcelain pieces. The photograph was from the late 1930s, taken near her old hometown. A young couple looked at the camera with smiles on their faces, but it was obvious they did not actually know each other.
There was a slight breeze blowing through the attic, making her shiver. She wondered what had become of them now? Had they met again after all these years?
“Is it really you?” he asked, standing right next to her. He wore a blue suit and held her hands gently in his own.
