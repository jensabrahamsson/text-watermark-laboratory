After a long day, we sat under the shade of the cottonwood trees and ate some of our catch. It was one of those days when the rain never came, the heat seemed to linger on, and the air was heavy with humidity.
I could hear the frogs croaking in the nearby woods, the rustling leaves and crickets’ chirping. They were all part of the symphony that seemed to be playing around us. But this time there was no music to break up the peace, just quiet and silence.
A few minutes later, we finished eating and stood up to head home. I was about to start my car
