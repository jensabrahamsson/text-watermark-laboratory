The first bell was checked 24 times, the second bell was checked 15 times and the third bell was checked 60 times. 
How many different combinations of the two bells could have been chosen?

We know there are only 3 different bells in this case and we need to find the number of different pairs (a,b) where a is one of the bells and b another bell.

It is easy to calculate:

$P(3,2)$

which gives $3 * 2 / 2! = 3$
