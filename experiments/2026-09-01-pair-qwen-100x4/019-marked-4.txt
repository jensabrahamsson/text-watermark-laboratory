The pigeon perched on a branch above the wheat field, surveying the situation. The pigeon is a symbol of prosperity and good fortune for those who see it, but it's also considered to be a harbinger of bad luck in other cultures.
According to many ancient beliefs, pigeons are sacred birds that bring messages from the gods or even angels. Pigeon folklore describes their intelligence and quick wits. Some people believe they have premonitions about events, including whether there will be war or disaster.
