It was hot and she sat on the bench to rest under the tree.
She thought about the man in the flat next door. She had never met him, but she often saw him sitting on the sofa in his pyjamas watching television. He had a beard and long black hair, like an old-fashioned film star. She wondered if he was gay. She had seen him watching women passing by in the street.
