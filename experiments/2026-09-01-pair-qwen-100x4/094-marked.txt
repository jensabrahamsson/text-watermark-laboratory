Why did the cricket pavilion clock have no hands?

The cricket pavilion clock, a traditional symbol of the sport in England, had no hands because it was meant to be an iconic piece that didn't require a complex timekeeping system. Instead, the focus on this iconic building was placed on its distinctive shape, which featured a large circular structure that resembles a roundabout. The pavilion's design serves as a visual representation of the event itself—cricket.

The absence of hands on the clock allowed for its unique architectural style and historical significance to be emphasized without detracting from its function as a public gathering point
