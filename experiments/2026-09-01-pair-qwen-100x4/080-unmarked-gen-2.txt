I was a little girl. I lived with my grandmother in a rambling old house on the edge of a small town. I have no memory of being very young. The last memory I have is of a family Christmas Eve and it includes me holding the baby sister. When we went to bed, there was a party going on downstairs; music and dancing and people talking all night. It felt like they never stopped.
