"Hello, can I help you?" a young girl asked. The solicitor shook her head.
"I'm just looking for a pen," the solicitor replied, smiling warmly at the girl and patting her hand.
"You have to come in soon or your mother will be worried," she said, pointing to a small door. "You'll see what we do in there."
"I know, but it's hard for me to wait here, I want to go in too," the girl told the lawyer. "Can you give me a ride in?"
"No problem," the lawyer agreed. They left together.
