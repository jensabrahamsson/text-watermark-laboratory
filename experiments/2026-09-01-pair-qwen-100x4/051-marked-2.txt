The nurse was very concerned about his patient. The patient had an unusual condition, one that the nurse believed required immediate attention. However, the nurse did not have the authority to make any decisions for the patient. Instead, he asked his supervisor if there were other options available. The supervisor agreed, but only after the nurse made sure that the patient would be safe while waiting for approval from higher up management.
