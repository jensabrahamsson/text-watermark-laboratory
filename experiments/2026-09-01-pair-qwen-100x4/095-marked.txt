He knew his name was J.R.
J.R. stood at the end of the line and had no idea why.
The mechanic said: "This is a brand new car that will be going on a long trip."
"Really?" asked J.R. "How much do I have to pay?"
"Only $25," replied the mechanic. "No money, but only if you can beat this race track in 60 seconds."
J.R., with a confident grin, began the race.
As J.R. drove, his engine rumbled loudly.
