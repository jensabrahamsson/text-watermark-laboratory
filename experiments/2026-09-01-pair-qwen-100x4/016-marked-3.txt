He’d had a long, hard day. He knew it was because he worked too much and didn’t take enough breaks, but still, he felt like his life wasn’t worth living without the one thing he loved: his wife. And he thought about how she hadn’t come home yet.
That was when the green house door creaked open.
Her hair was tied in a tight bun on her head and her face looked drained with exhaustion.
“Hey,” he said, trying to get over the shock of seeing her. His wife always came home before this time.
“I’m sorry I missed dinner.” She smiled at him.
