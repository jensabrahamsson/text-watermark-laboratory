He was a bit worried. I think he had the feeling that no one would ever read them. He was selling them, and it was really bad.
Then suddenly, he saw an article in a magazine called The Guardian about people who go to the supermarket on Saturdays and buy stuff because they don't know what else to do with their lives.
I asked him why he didn't give them away at least? That's what I meant when I said he should give those magazines away. I told him that if we could just get a few of them out there, someone might use them.
It's not so bad anymore, he says.
