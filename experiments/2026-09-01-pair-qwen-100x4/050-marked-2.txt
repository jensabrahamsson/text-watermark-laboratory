After a lot of rain last week, the shed needed some attention to make it more durable.
I found an old metal sheet that fit over the door to prevent water from seeping into the wood. The hinges need to be reinforced so they don’t fall off in windy conditions and the windows can be covered for added protection.
There is also another metal window above the doorway that needs to be repaired or replaced if the wood deteriorates.
