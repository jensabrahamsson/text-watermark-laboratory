This is not a place for young children. The children are taken away when we enter. I don’t think it’s an issue with the kids, they can sit in the main sanctuary. This is a beautiful space that was once a church and now has been transformed into an amazing space for concerts and other events. It has a large stage for speakers to address the congregation. I love how there is a clear distinction between the main sanctuary and the chancel. The chancel contains the altar, where prayers and sacred rituals occur. The walls of this area have murals painted by local artists. The ceiling features stained glass windows which frame images
