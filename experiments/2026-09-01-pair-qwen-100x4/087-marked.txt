He stood at the far side, where a wall was blanketed by blackness.
A small, human-sized hole was illuminated in it. The hole's inner surface was made of translucent blue ice, while the outside was covered by white fog.
The watchman looked at it and said in his mind: "I'm not going to light this, and I'll leave this place."
But as he continued walking, he couldn't help but take notice of something out of the corner of his eye.
