That person was a child.
So the woman should have buttons on her clothes, too. Right? Wrong! According to this story, only grown-ups wear coats with buttons.
This is one of my favorite children's books because it teaches us an important lesson. We shouldn't make assumptions about people just because we see them in certain ways or hear their names. When I tell others about this book, they always like to discuss whether it's true that every adult has buttons in their clothing or not. It's a great discussion starter!
What do you think? Would you assume something about a stranger by looking at them and hearing their name?
