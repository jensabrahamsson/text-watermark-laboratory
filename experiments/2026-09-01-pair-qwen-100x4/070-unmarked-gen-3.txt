A new tarp was to be made, but there wasn't time. The old one had a good life for 14 years.
There were too many jobs on the line and no more work was coming in from elsewhere. So the decision was made that the old tarpaulin would have to go.
My mother-in-law was out for a walk when I called her. She said it looked like rain.
She had been thinking about the boat and how the tarp wouldn't stand up to being blown away by gusty winds. So she decided that the boat should go home. She planned to make a new boat this winter
