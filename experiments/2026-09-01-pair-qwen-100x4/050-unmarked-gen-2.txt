It wasn’t a new shed, but I had made it myself. It was a bit rough around the edges with some gaps in the boards that allowed drafts to get through. And the door itself was an oddity – it had been a cupboard door from a 1950s-era kitchen.
But there it sat, out of the way, waiting for me to make a proper shed. And when we first got to our house, about 20 years ago, it became home to all sorts of things – my old work clothes, a sofa bed, books, and more.
