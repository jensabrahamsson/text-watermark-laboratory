She had a hard time cooking breakfast in the kitchen, for it was very small and there wasn’t enough room to work with. She needed more space, she thought as she placed the stove on top of a little wooden table next to her bed.
Her hands were tired from the long day at the supermarket. She wanted to have something light that didn’t take much time to prepare, so she chose the pancakes, which she usually made for dinner but decided to make for lunch instead.
