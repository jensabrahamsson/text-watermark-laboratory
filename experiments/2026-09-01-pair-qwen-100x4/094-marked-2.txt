Tea-time in English is when the housekeeper brings out some food to feed guests, but not necessarily a full meal. This was a convenient time for everyone who was watching cricket matches.
People would gather outside the pavilion (the building containing the pavilion) to watch the game. Some people might also be waiting inside the pavilion itself as well, although it didn't seem necessary as the doors were open during play.
It seems that they enjoyed their afternoon and didn't mind the lack of a hand on the clock.
