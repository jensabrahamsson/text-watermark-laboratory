He knew his legs weren’t in sync, but he had to keep walking. He could feel the cold getting into his bones and he wanted to go back to his car and warm up inside, but instead, he walked on.
It was a long walk, and by the time he reached his car, he realized he would have been late. He grabbed his keys, unlocked the car, and hopped in as quickly as he could.
He started his engine and started driving out of the parking lot. It was just after noon and already it was hot, but he didn’t care because he was still thinking about that walk home from work
