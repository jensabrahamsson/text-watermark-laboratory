After a long day in the orchard, my parents are tired and it is time for them to rest. The ladders have been used for several weeks now as we harvested apples, plums, and peaches. They are a bit worn out from all the abuse. But they will serve us again soon! It was an exhausting work week, but I am glad that we were able to get so much done. Our job was very important for the next season’s crops.
