He is a child who will be an adult soon. This time, his mother told him that he should practice his right and left hands. She also said that this would make it easier for him to perform at any age and in any situation. The boy had never seen or heard about such practices before but decided to follow them since they were from his mother.
The next day, he went back home with the same idea of practising his hands. He was very excited as he wanted to show everyone how well he could do. However, when he got home, he found out that he couldn't move freely because he was too big.
