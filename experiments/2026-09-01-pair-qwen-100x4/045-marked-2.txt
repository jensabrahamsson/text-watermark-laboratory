She picked up a teacup with one hand, and drank her black tea from another with the other hand.
She poured out the water in the bowl into a teapot.
The kettle boiled again when she put some fresh water in it.
She took a pot of hot water and stirred it into the pot with the chopsticks.
She used her right hand to pour the hot water over the wet tea leaves. When the water was cold enough for her to taste, she threw away the leaves and then repeated the process.
She looked at the leaves in the pot. She could not wait to drink it.
She went into the living room
