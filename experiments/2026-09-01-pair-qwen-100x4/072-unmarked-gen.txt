On the night of June 14, an underpass mural in Downtown Cleveland was spray-painted with the word "n-word." As a result, the mural has been removed and replaced with a sign warning that it is not to be touched.
In response to the mural being defaced, City Councilman Jay C. Williams (D-Akron) said he wanted to see a mural with a positive message.
