No one moved. No one spoke. We were just standing there, in the middle of the school yard at the old school, and all we could hear was the sound of our own footsteps against the pavement. The bell did not ring again. After the drill was over, no one got up. I thought about how much my father would have loved this drill; he had once been part of the US Army during World War II, before I was born, so he was an experienced soldier. My father might even have liked the drill, as he loved to tell stories about his military service.
