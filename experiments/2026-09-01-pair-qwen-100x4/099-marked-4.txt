He felt cold. He decided to walk home.
A homeless person saw him. "Can I help you?" said the homeless person.
"Actually, I just feel very cold," answered the boy.
"So I'll give you this warm bag."
The homeless person gave him his own warm bag.
"I can't take that from you, because you helped me."
So he took back his own warm bag, hugged the boy and left him.
