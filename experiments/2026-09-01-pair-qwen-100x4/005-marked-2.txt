The wind blew through her hair and onto her skin. It felt like needles pinching at the tiny hairs of her scalp.

At lunchtime, she ate some soup, salad, and bread. Afterward, she did some housework for two hours before going to work. She came home around 6:00 p.m., had dinner, and watched TV.
On weekends, she went out with friends for drinks or pizza or both. Afterward, they would walk around town talking about things that were happening in their lives.
