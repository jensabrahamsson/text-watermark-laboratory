He stood at the altar, hands raised to heaven.
No words would leave his mouth as he spoke the verses of the song.
The congregation clapped in appreciation. The choir sang more quietly now.
As the final note ended the worshipers sat in silence for a moment before rising to leave.
But there was one man standing where he always had been—right in front of him.
And he had just asked why Jesus didn’t want to hear from Him.
The man had known him all his life, but no words could ever be enough to express how much this man loved him. And so they began to argue.
