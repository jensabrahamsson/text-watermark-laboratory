He picked it up, shook it to disperse the buttery bits and tossed it away. It was an ordinary night at the movies for him, so he had no need to get his hands dirty; but still he had to do it. After all, every single one of those movies has been watched a thousand times over since its debut in 1950. He had seen his fair share of horror films, romantic comedies and even action thrillers. All were on the radar, or should have been by now. So why this particular film that he'd only ever caught a fleeting glimpse at when it first came out?
