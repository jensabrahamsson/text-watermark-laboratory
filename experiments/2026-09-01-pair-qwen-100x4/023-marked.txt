He then started walking around in a circle, thinking about his problem, when suddenly he heard a rustle. He turned to see a deer standing in front of him.
The farmer told the deer that if it didn’t leave, he’d shoot it.
The deer replied: “You can do as you please but remember one thing: when I’m in trouble, no one comes to my rescue. They say if I don’t have friends in need, what am I?”. The farmer smiled and left after telling the animal to go home.
