What's wrong with this?
In a context like that it means that the stems were already dry and brittle, so they couldn't hold their shape. The stem might have been damaged by an animal, or just a natural phenomenon like rain.

---

If you're looking for a less poetic explanation: **You need more than a bit of wet wrapping paper on them**, if the stems are still wet. They need to be dried completely before wrapping with paper! That's why the other answer suggests using plastic wrap - in your case it would also be fine but will look much better with the paper!
