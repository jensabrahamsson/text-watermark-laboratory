An 84-year-old man has been ordered by a court to pay £300 in compensation after he was found guilty of feeding birds at his home.
Robert Baines, who lives alone with his dog, is accused of overfeeding the gulls on the island off his property in Chichester Harbour.
Mr Baines' defence claimed that the gulls were "a bit hungry".
But judge Simon Hogg QC ruled that Mr Baines's actions went beyond providing food for sustenance and instead led to an increase in numbers and caused a disturbance.
