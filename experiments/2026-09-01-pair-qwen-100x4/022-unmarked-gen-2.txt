I watched him go. I heard a muffled cry in my ears as he brushed against something soft. He turned around, and his eyes met mine for a moment. My heart sank. The crowd was whispering about what he had just said to me.
His voice rose suddenly: "She looks like you," he whispered in a harsh tone. It was a low rumble, but it rang loud enough to carry across the theater.
There is no point in saying anything to him now. There will be plenty of time tomorrow. But he already knows everything. Why bother? What good does it do to deny the obvious?
