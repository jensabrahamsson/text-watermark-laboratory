It was his first time making a log cabin from scratch. He had never been much of a gardener, but he could make this. It would be his home now that he had built it. The sun set behind him. He needed to start gathering leaves before darkness fell. A long way off in the distance, a light flickered at the corner of his eye. He had not expected anyone else to come to his cabin. Not that it really mattered; he had no need for company after building it alone for months on end.
