The cows were all over the place, but the farmer didn't want to take his chances with them. He was going to have a little talk with each one individually. When he got to the last one of the herd he saw something peculiar.
This cow had been standing there for some time. He walked up to her and began to gently ask her to go back home. "Please, can you go back? We really need you," he said.
"Come back!" the farmer yelled at her in anger. The cow just stood there quietly and waited until he finished speaking.
