She had seen this happen many times in the past, when there was no storm and she didn’t want to disturb the flow of time, she would simply let the water fall into the trough, and watch it pour out as the sun rose or set.
A while ago, they’d both been at the back gate. She was holding onto her father’s hand, and his hands were holding hers. He looked down from his horse, watching the water falling gently over the stone trough.
