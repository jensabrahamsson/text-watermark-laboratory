He lifted the suitcase from the baggage rack. It was too heavy, he thought, but the porter could manage it. He had been helping this man with his luggage for three years. Every time they met up, this man always had a different suitcase and an even different story to tell him. Sometimes they would have dinner together in the airport restaurant or go out to lunch after they were done with their duties at the desk. The porter was fascinated by this man's stories and was often moved by how much he endured. This man never talked about his past; there was just so much he kept hidden behind his stoic façade.
