He felt cold. He decided to walk home.
A kind passerby stopped, took off his coat and offered it to him.
The student was overwhelmed and said “Thank you very much” with tears in his eyes as he continued walking.
And then he came across another person sitting on the kerb who had fallen into some water, which got all over his clothes.
The kind passerby again gave him her coat to dry out from the water that had been splashed all over him and helped him to put on his coat before offering him an umbrella to shield him from the rain that was now starting to fall.
