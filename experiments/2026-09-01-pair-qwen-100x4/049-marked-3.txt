He knew his grandfather was there in the garden. The door creaked open as he waited for a moment, just long enough to see if his grandfather would notice him, or at least not come looking back inside. He had been waiting for days now.
The house sat on stilts over the lake. Its windows looked out onto the water, which was frozen and still like an iceberg.
