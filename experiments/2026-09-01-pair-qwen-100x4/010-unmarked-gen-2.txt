The choral director had taken time to plan for the singing, rehearsing over several weeks, so the singers could get used to the music. It was a large number of voices in an old church that made this project special.
It seemed like there was something in the air when we entered the church, a mixture of reverence and excitement that had been created by a long journey. The audience, both people sitting in the pews and those standing at the sides of the aisle, were ready for what they knew would be an amazing performance.
