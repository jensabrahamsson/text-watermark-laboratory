That's the story my grandmother used to tell me about her first day in the country.
My grandmother lived in a rural area. Her parents were farmers and lived on the land where she was born. Her parents encouraged her to visit with the neighboring kids and learn more about their lives. My grandmother went with a group of friends every Saturday and Sunday. In exchange for stories from them, she would bring food home to her parents. On one such trip, they stopped at the barn that was owned by a couple who were part of the local community.
My grandmother had never been inside the barn before but felt safe there because it had no windows
