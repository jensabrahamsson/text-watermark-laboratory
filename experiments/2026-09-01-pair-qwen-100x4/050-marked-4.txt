That's all that was needed, but when I arrived home a couple of days later to find a new shed door hanging from three hinges and with a big piece of felt stuck onto the floor where my wheelbarrow once sat, I knew what had happened.
The local farmer had broken into our garden. Incredibly, he had even left some of his own tools in the shed – not mine! He must have been surprised by my absence for two weeks (the weather was horrendous).
