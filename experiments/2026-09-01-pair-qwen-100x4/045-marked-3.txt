She picked up a teacup with one of those handles that goes to the side. She set it down on the plate she had selected for herself. The other one sat empty, on the table.
The tea pot was too heavy to hold with one hand, so she held it with both, as if she were holding a gun. And then she waited until he said “I don’t think I’m hungry anymore.” Then she poured the tea, not watching his face.
