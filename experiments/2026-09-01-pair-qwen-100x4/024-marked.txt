He’d spent years trying to put them in order. They showed his mother, his father, his two brothers, and his first wife. And they were all in love with her. The last was not the one he was married to.
She was wearing black on this photograph but the others were all in dresses or blouses. He remembered his wife in black, but he couldn’t remember if it was the last one he was looking at. She wasn’t even a little bit beautiful; she looked like an ordinary woman in an ordinary dress, and he didn’t know why he wanted to take a look at her, or what for.
