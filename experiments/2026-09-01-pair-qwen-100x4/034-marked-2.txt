He stood at the altar, hands raised to heaven.
No words would leave his mouth but one: "Thank you."
I stood behind him. I had no idea what he was saying; I couldn't even hear him. The priest said something in Italian. I knew he was speaking to me because the man beside me spoke Spanish and we both smiled.
As we were leaving, the doors opened, revealing the church again filled with worshipers from all walks of life, from every part of town, a multitude, and then... silence.
The priest turned and walked toward the altar. He faced us and lifted his voice and began singing.
