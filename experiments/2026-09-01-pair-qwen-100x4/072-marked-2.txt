After many years of graffiti, the underpass at Haskett and Balfour streets in Dartmouth has received some art care. A mural was painted there a couple of decades ago, but it was soon covered by graffiti and lost its identity.
In 2013, the city's Arts and Culture department started working with the community to come up with a name for the mural, which is still visible on both sides of the street.
"It really resonated with the kids from the local schools," said Mark McMillan, director of public arts for Dartmouth, which has been trying to encourage the use of its underpasses
