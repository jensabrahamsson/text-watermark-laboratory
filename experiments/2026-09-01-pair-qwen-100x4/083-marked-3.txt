She had a certain rhythm to the notes. She knew when it was time to start playing, and she knew when it was time to stop. Her fingers would dance over the keys in perfect harmony. When her mind was relaxed and at peace, she felt as though she could play anything she wanted to play. But when she started getting tense or nervous, her hands would slip away from their proper positions and she wouldn't be able to finish the song properly. And so, she needed to practice the scales every day in order to keep them sharp.
The girl loved music; she didn't have any talent for writing songs or playing an instrument
