“Do you have more water?” I asked, my hands balled into fists.
She shook her head. “Not enough,” she said. And with that, the rest of the day passed.
I was in my room studying, when my phone rang. It was the school secretary, asking for me to meet them at the library by 12 noon.
It was raining outside and the air smelled musty and smoky. A large sign hung on the front door advertising a bookstore. My feet were cold as ice but I didn’t mind because this was one of those rare moments where all my senses felt alive again.
