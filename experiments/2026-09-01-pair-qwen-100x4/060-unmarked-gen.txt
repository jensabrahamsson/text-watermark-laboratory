When the wind blew through it, it gave off little whistles and clinks. I think that they may be made of birch or willow, for they are so delicate in their structure but yet so sturdy. When we were on our way to get ducks at the pond, I saw these planks and thought that this would make a great piece for my art class. So, I bought two of them from the farmer.
