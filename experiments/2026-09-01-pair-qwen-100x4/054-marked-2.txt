What's an example of a sentence that uses all four parts of speech in full?
While driving to work, he listened to music on his phone.

Example of using all four parts of speech in a complete sentence:

"The bird perched on the branch while singing its melodious song."

This sentence includes the following parts of speech: 
- The subject is "the bird"
- The verb is "perched"
- The object is "on the branch"
- The adverbial phrase describing the location is "while singing its melodious song" which includes:
  - The preposition "while"
  - The subordinating conjunction
