He was seen by residents of an apartment complex in North Carolina as he left his car parked outside and fed the birds with a bag of bread crumbs.
In photos shared on social media, you can see him sitting outside the car while two groups of gulls circle around. The man has his back towards them but there are a couple of large ones on the ground with a few smaller birds gathered near him.
There have been complaints about the bird feeders being set up outside the vehicle. One person described it as "not nice" and added: "I feel like someone's stealing food from the gulls."
