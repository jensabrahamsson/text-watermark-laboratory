It was 1982, and I was living in the tiny hamlet of Whitsborough Bay, Cumbria. In those days, the village pub was the only other place to see anyone apart from my friends and myself; it wasn't even really a pub, more like a hang-out for a few people after work. I used to be quite introverted back then but I would often venture into the village pub with some mates on weekends. One night we decided to get a bit wild and took our boat to the local lake and went rowing out towards the hills where we could get away from the hustle and bust
