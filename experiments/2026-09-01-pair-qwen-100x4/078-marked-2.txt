He’d spent the better part of three hours staring at this coal bunk. He was in his own world, and he didn’t know how to get out anymore. There were two other rooms around him, but they weren’t connected; each had its door locked from the inside.
He sat on a bench made of metal slats and peered through the bars that held them together. The coal bunk, with its two doors facing opposite ways, blocked all light from coming into the room.
