She had a few minutes of free time before it was her turn to pay. It would be only 30 cents, so she decided not to rush and just sit back and watch the world go by while waiting for her payment.
In the background were several other customers making their payments as well. One man was in a hurry; he could not wait long for his payment.
There were many other types of people who passed through that day. Some looked very poor, some very rich, but all the same, they made their way to the cash register with money in hand.
