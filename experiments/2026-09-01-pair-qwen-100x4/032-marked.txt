He stood at the top step, looking over the line of yellow leaves. The bricks were dark grey against the sky. It was raining, but he could hear raindrops on the glass windowpanes. He wondered if the rain would fall onto his back.
The man had come to the fire escape because of the mug.
His fingers curled into claws as he took the pot from under the edge of the building wall. In it lay the mug, smashed. Blood ran from the rim. His heart pounded against his ribcage. A shiver passed through him.
