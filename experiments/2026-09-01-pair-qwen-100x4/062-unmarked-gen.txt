I was in a small town on the east coast, watching people walking down alleyways, past streetlights hanging from their homes. The wind blew the light bulbs inside them.
In the middle of the night I sat with my feet propped up and an empty bottle between me and my notebook, sipping tea. My friend, who is also the photographer, came by to share her work from her recent trip. She had just returned from Thailand, a place she said is full of energy and where you can feel the world around you. Her pictures were filled with color and life and movement.
