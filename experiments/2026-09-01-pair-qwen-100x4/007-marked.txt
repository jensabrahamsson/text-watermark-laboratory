He stood at the far end of the store with a basket in one hand and a rodent-scratching pad in the other. The fishmonger kept his back to the window, watching over his stock.
The cat sat on his hind legs, his front paws resting on the ground. His tail wagged from side to side while he stared through the glass door at the water.
As long as he was able to see inside, the cat wouldn’t move. It would wait for the fishmonger to leave before it moved into position.
