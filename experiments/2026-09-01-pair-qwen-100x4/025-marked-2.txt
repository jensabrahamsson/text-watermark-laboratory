He put the letter in his pocket without reading, closed the drawer without checking to see if there was anything left behind, turned off the light, locked the door. He took the dog for a walk on the empty street.
Then he went home.
His house felt too quiet. There wasn’t any sound, not even a rustle of the leaves underfoot, not a chirping bird from the trees across the street. Even the air seemed to have died down, all the life and energy sucked away by an invisible force. The place seemed completely desolate and abandoned, as if someone had forgotten they lived here.
