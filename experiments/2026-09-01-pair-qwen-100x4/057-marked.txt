He stood at the base of his garden and watched the sky. He waited for the light of a car to fade until it disappeared into the darkness and he imagined he heard the sound of wheels on gravel, but no sound came, only silence. He thought that the trees were alive; he could feel their leaves rustling as though they whispered in their green silvery branches.
