The shutters on this building in New York City have a mysterious and haunting history that began with its opening, when it was home to a popular club known for hosting many of the most famous artists and entertainers of the 20th century. But then the shuttered doors went up over a period of nearly 30 years as the building fell into disrepair, and now no one is sure what secrets lie behind the walls and under the floors of this landmarked structure, which has been vacant since the late 1980s.
