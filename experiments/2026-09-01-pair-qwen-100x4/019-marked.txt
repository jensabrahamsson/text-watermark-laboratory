The pigeon perched on a branch above the wheat field and watched the scene. The grain had spilled over onto the grass where it lay like an empty chest. Pigeons have been known to eat other birds, so this must be a distress call or something more serious. A dove appeared in the sky and flew down towards the spilled grains of wheat.
In his head, he heard the words, "Help me!"
He called for help.
The dove saw the pigeon and responded with her own voice.
"Help me!" The pigeon said again.
"Who are you?" asked the dove.
