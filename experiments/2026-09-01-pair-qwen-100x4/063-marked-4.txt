The 93-year-old woman had been throwing the food out of her window at a beach in Cumbria, England. She said she was "just feeding them".
But the birds would only take what they wanted from her, leaving the rest untouched, police in the North West of England told the BBC.
It's not clear why the bird did so.
A man who witnessed the feedings said he was surprised by how quickly the birds ate up all the crusts on offer, with no one even coming close to the fish cake or vegetables the lady offered to feed them.
