The girl had been practicing her scales in secret, she didn’t want to be found by the other girls. She was afraid they would laugh at her.
“Don’t look back” she said to herself.
It took hours for her to learn how to move gracefully through her scales and she practiced everyday until it felt second nature.
One day she decided to perform on stage for the first time and she looked back when she heard applause.
She stood up straight, put out her arms wide and waited for her first note. The crowd clapped and laughed at her.
She could hear her voice in her head and she began to run away.
