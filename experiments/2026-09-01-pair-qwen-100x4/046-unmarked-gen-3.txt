I had been there only once before, to photograph it as an image for a project. The water had been so dark that it was hard to see any fish and I decided not to return. But this time, when I saw the sky reflected in the pond, my mind started racing with ideas.
There is something about these small ponds on the edge of the city that is quiet and peaceful even though the industrial surroundings are loud. I have visited them many times now but each visit has brought something new to me. There is also something magical about their presence – they feel like home in this urban environment.
