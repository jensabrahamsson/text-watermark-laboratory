That's the story, at least, as told by the BBC. It was one of two cups made for the tea trade to be used on the London & North Eastern Railway. The other cup was for the British Tea Company and it had been designed by William Morris himself. In fact, he created so many cups for the various companies in his career that he developed an entire collection, called the "Morris Collection", which is considered the most significant set of designs in the history of British ceramics.
