He leapt over, and across, the grass. He was like an agile mouse in this small space. I could have sworn he was a cat with feline features. I'm sure of it when he reached his goal - to take refuge in his own little haven, where the sun's rays had not been able to shine upon him.
I watched him for a moment. Then, before I knew it, he disappeared out of sight. And yet, that tiny piece of grass still seemed very big. As though the world is full of places one can go. A place where one doesn't need to be afraid; no matter what
