He stood at the base of his garden and watched the sky. He waited for the lightening and thunder, then he started to sing, and danced and swayed. His wife had left him three years ago. Now he was old and alone. The world was in shambles. He had nothing more than his bare feet, the dirt, and his guitar, which held him together. But he could no longer stand up against the storms of life; the pain, the hunger, the loneliness, and the fear that he would not see tomorrow's dawn.
The world looked dark, gray, and hopeless.
