The man with a pipe in his mouth said, "That meter will not let me have any of your time."
The woman with two children was sitting beside him. She said, "Don't be sad. We still have plenty of time left."
The child said, "Let's try another kind." He put on a hat and a cape, walked around outside and returned inside. The meter moved slowly and then stopped dead. "There you are," said the man. "We've used up all our time." Then he added, "But it will cost us ten dollars." And he started walking out again.
