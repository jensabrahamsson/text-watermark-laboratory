He knew his body was already in pieces, but he had to keep walking. He wanted to know what happened next, who had come after him, if they would find him or even be able to recognize him at all. The pain in his chest grew and with each step forward, he felt more of it coming back into his body.
Then he heard a noise from behind him. It sounded like something heavy and sharp being moved across concrete. A scream rose up in his throat, and for the first time since his fall, he fought through the numbness and screamed out.
