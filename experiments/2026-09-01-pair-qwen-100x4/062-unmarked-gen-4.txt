When the night market opened for the season, it felt like a whole new season. For days before, there was a sense of anticipation about what would fill the air, but now that the market had been filled with all manner of smells and sights, we were ready to begin our annual celebration.
In the market’s center was the vendor that served the most delicious, savory pork and chicken dishes I’ve ever tried, along with noodles made from a spicy broth thickened with mussels.
