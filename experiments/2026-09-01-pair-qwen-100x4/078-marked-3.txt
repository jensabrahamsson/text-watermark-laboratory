He knew his face had been drawn and pale by the cold but he was not cold. He could feel the warmth of the day in his chest; the sun was out but it did not heat him up. His heart ached with the effort to keep himself warm, however. He looked down at the man who lay beside him and found himself unable to look away. The face beneath him was familiar. He knew that he had seen him before, perhaps in some place where they met. This was not a dream.
“John,” he said softly.
