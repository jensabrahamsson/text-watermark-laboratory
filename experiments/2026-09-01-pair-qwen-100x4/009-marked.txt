The old man was sitting on his porch, polishing the brass number that hung over the door of his store. The light from the streetlamp illuminated it and cast it in a bright white glow, so he knew he had done a good job. He didn't move; he just sat there with his head bent down, eyes closed, polishing away at the number like he was working on something very important.
