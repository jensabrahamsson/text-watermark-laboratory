The car boot was only for four hours. The house was closed off. I went back to the shop at home to get money. I told myself that the car would take up two-thirds of the day. I paid for the paper. "You won't mind if I keep you in the car for more than four hours?" I said.

The shopkeeper said something like that. "A better way would be to put the kettle on the desk at eleven o'clock in the morning in case it is raining, then we will get you to bed."
