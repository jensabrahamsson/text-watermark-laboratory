One of the volunteer assistants, a young white lad named Jack, who lives next to the till, had been going to a bookstore and bought his book of poetry. He got there but the guide said there were only fifteen people in the shop and they wanted to speak at one minute a piece. I stopped to read and waited for the guide.

"It's about to be over," said Mr. Jack's wife with an angry expression and said, "Come on in." When the guide said no she began to walk and started off. My wife and I met to see what to do.
