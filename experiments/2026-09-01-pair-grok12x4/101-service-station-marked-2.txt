The carpark was empty and I walked along a narrow street that led to the shop which opened in November 1992. I used to get on to one level at the back of a building on my own property and sometimes it took me five minutes to go there. There was a shop for an anti-discrimination measure, but there were no more than three women there. I asked the lady in charge to keep me from going upstairs on Saturdays.

She shrugged, and said, "It's fine," and said something which has stuck with me. She asked, "Doesn't any one else understand?"
