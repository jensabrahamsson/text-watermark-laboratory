Back in the shop, in her room, in the window and on every corner, were my children. I had a collection of books, but nothing of value. My mother would have died of a heart attack if not for this shop and the shop. So I bought them. It took her a long time, she had been doing it for ten months, but with the price she paid she felt that her life had been taken. And it is here, in the library, that the history belongs.
