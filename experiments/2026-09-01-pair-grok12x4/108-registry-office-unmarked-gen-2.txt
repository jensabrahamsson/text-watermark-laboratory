For the first year of my marriage, I was called a witness for five years in the registry office and three years as a witness and no more. In six years, my life got a second life. I am very glad of my father-in-law. He knows something as well or better as I do. He did not know I was a witness. He felt it was not important, he had taken a hard road but I felt it was not important.
