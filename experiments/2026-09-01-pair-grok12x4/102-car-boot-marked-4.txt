The street was a little too rough

I came up this morning and took refuge in a flat on a hill. It had gone too quiet for an hour to do the work. I took one step closer to the pavement and sat down to eat a slice of bread. The man stood in front – he was a short-haired man with long brown hair, white eyes as black as heaven, dark glasses that I knew didn't match his brown hair, a face I could never remember without my father's mask. He was the person I met with in the kitchen when I had been looking at pictures of them in my memory.
