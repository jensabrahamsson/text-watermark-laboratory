I couldn't remember when my first name started, but I would know now I was in my 70s and there was something wrong with my eyes. I thought of an imaginary friend I had met in a supermarket. His surname looked familiar: his name was Robert. He'd left a number in the middle: it would have been OK for him to miss his date. He was about three years older than myself, but he knew I was in our room when he entered his room - he said he'd asked not to share it. A few seconds later he gave us the name again and a little voice told us to shut the door
