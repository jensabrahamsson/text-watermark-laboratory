The bus came to stop when it was about nine. It was the first time anyone had walked down stairs in the English countryside when the bus was not going. We were waiting for someone at the door, who gave the name of one of the volunteers, and then got into a van and drove back into the street. The next two days were a blur, and even in the end I wasn't alone; at least as far as I knew, the road was wide open and only the people who came to listen to my radio didn't know it.
