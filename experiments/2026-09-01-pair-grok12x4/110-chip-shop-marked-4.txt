The car and the cook came back to the station and I looked in my direction at the sign, which seemed to confirm the order. As I looked closer I heard my sister's voice and a distant rumble, which I thought to be her friend's. The car followed me.

"I am getting out of here, my friends," I said as I was leaving. "I see you coming from, it is only the third time I have been to this place. How long will it take for us all to be there to make your way back?"

"Until we have gone and cleaned up."
