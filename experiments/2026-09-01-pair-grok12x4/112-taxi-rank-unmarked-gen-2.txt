I left to go to university. On the way back, I was at a meeting with an old friend who had a long career working for the city. He was a carpenter. His son was a lawyer who had gone to the army but never joined the Army. A few days later the boy went down to Rome. He was a soldier, and my boy didn't belong there, except by reason of my being an adult. The boy didn't like Italy. He hated it. I met him outside of Rome. He said the city was better than France.
