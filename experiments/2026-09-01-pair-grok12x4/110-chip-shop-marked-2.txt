The night was quiet. I could see my friends outside the bakery holding a pint and trying to get some water and some bread at the back of a cart that would go down near the shop. Some of the other girls, half-clothed, were standing up on benches for a couple of rows. They were still sitting around the kitchen table. I found my way downstairs.

I turned my head back to where I was. I saw a man looking directly at me. I looked at him.

"Hi!"

It was a handsome man. "Hi, did you pick us up?"
