I watched the fire station through the window, in my head. It had never been here before and was far too short of space for what was about to come. I was going to get up and say a few words, because as long as the house was a house that was a little lighted and clean, I'd have the whole day to think about what the big plan was that had to be put in place now. I saw the sign at the front step of the building, and thought: here, in this small house I can hear you.
