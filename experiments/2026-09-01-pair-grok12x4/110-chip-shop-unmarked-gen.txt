In the morning a policeman came and called to check on me. An employee got out and got some papers in the trash can. I told him that I was being investigated and told him not to worry. Then a policeman pointed up to a woman beside me and called her my number. The woman was very quiet and told me to call her mum. I walked outside again to meet her. Then my mother and I stood outside and she said I was going to be there that night. I thought, well, that might be a way of being an out-of-character, maybe of being a fool on duty and not really happy
