I was about to leave the tent when I saw a man with an eggnog coming out of my tent that was wearing something that looked like a shirt. The man was sweating profusely, but that was not the point: I was going to go out the window and see for myself. When the sun rose I looked and saw he was a man who loved the lake and knew it. I ran.

I had a lot more time to think about it. I began to read. I thought. I read about Lake Tahoe where the trees are thick, a place I thought must be like in a real volcano
