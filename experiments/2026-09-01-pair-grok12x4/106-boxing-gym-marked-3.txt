The coach made it to the entrance and gave me my wrist-side phone when the door was closed.

One of the other doctors was sitting across the table from me, looking sickened. He had been in a bar the other day and had been so tired that they had taken him off the premises. We sat there for a few minutes.

"I am getting out of here, Professor," he said with a sigh. He turned to my face and said something that seemed like a threat. "You can get out. I'll wait." He walked over to me with the big black book on his back.
