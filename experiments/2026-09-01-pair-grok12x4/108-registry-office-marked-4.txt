There was one place that gave some pleasure back. A small shop in the town with a small shopkeeper that I had never met. I had had a good life. People who work there are quite friendly and talk about how they like me, but one of them stopped in at an old school. I was standing at one end of the street and was wondering if he remembered me. It was very strange, to be sure, that he saw a woman standing at the door asking for a book. There, he looked as if he was going to the office: but I was too young to be doing that.
