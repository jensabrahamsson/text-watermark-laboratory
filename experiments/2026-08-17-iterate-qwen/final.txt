I nearly failed to disembark—the vessel was that near. Half the journey, I proceeded on foot across it; meanwhile, the roadway seemed to shift strangely beneath me. My instinct was to remain aboard, yet no one appeared to have accepted my presence there. Roughly sixty minutes had elapsed; although I understood a short wait lay ahead, I repeatedly glanced back to monitor developments. In the end, all I registered was salt. A minuscule orange object appeared. Then—clearly—an octopus.

Without apparent cause, I continued forward.

I overtook additional individuals along the way.
