"I'm going to call a taxi," said another homeless man. He was still holding the ladder.

A car drove by and suddenly a voice called out from the passenger window: "Hello."

He turned and looked around. No one was walking, but there was a man and a girl standing near him.

"Hi!" the man said.

The girl started out singing.

"Hello," she said.

"Hello," the man said.

"Hello," the girl said back.

Then there were men, women and children. They spoke in broken Chinese.
