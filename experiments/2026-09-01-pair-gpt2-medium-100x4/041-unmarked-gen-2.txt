Then, he grabbed the plastic sheeting that had come with the cardboard box, carefully removed the contents of the cardboard box from it, laid the cardboard on the floor across from the fire, and set it back down. He said he would get up when he was done. He waited. Then he put the tin back on the burning table and, as he did, a moment later, he heard a sound like sirens, and was surprised to see four men crouched against the wall next to the door. He said that the first three of them moved with strange intent, as if they were looking to see what was happening.
