"Yes, it needs to be done." He said. "They're going to have to put in a gate for it now." He thought as he crossed the guard post. "This is where I'm expected next." He thought through his fingers, before he pushed the gate open again. He watched his father leave.

"What was that?" He asked as he ran down the path until he arrived near the front steps. He stopped and he looked toward the gate at the end of the line. In the background he heard someone cry "What is this?" The noise was like the sound of a distant voice.
