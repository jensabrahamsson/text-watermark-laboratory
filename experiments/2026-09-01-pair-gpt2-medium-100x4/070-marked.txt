"This is it?" a voice said from across the ocean to our boat, which had arrived a block away from the wreck. On the side of an island about a mile away, the boat's captain and my helmsman, Frank Kaczo, were watching from shore with their rifles. Two of their men were nearby. One of them, an older man in a maroon cap like everyone else, asked Kaczo, his shoulders hunched in a grimace, "What happened tonight?"
