"This is it, this is good," said K. Ravi Desai at Giri's.

"I am getting out, I will do something with the gold," said K. Krishnamurthy, as he got ready to go to Kalaikunda to pay his daughter's respects to her relatives.

Desai had been standing along the bank of the river pond till then. The hill he was standing on took him to reach Giri's. But now, his son Ravi Desai was gone. K. Krishnamurthy was looking at the river which was now flowing slowly away.
