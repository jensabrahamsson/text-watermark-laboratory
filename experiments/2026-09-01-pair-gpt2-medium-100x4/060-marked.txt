"This is it, this is it. This is the end!"

I jumped into the water, dived and grabbed the duck board. The board flexed and turned, almost the same as the two boards.

"I am the same way. I am the same way!"

I went back and forth on the deck, trying to catch sight of a direction that would show someone. The sun was coming up, the wind wasn't much, and I wasn't sure how to react to all of that. It wasn't nearly as if the direction the duckboard was turning had much meaning to me.
