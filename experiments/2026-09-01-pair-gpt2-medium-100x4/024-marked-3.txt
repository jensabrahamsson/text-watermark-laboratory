"This is it, this is it. This is what I have seen in my life," he thought. He pulled the photograph out of his mouth, exposing the photographs. "If I go to sleep now, I will have seen them."

The photograph in the attic was a picture of a girl with blonde hair falling around her feet. The picture belonged to one of the boys at Woodruff Elementary School. It had been taken in 1968. A teacher, an administrator and two children, one older than the other, were sitting on the floor looking at it.

He began to weep.
