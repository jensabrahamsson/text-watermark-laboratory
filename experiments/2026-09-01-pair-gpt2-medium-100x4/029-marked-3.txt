"I'm going to call your dad," he told her.

She got the call. Her father called her father.

There was never a call to her parents. There were no words.

One of the friends was gone — a police officer — when it happened. Other friends were missing. Some went to the morgue to bury, others to sleep.

"I was scared and depressed," she told me the next time I spoke to her.
