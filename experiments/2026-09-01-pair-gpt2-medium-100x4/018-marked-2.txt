"Then somebody brought the alarm and the door opened. Mr Trenholm went inside and looked out." It was a beautiful winter day, not much drizzle in it. They were only about 5.30am and the wind whistled and whistled, whipping past in the direction of the dock – not bad, even for our little island. He watched at a glance as two fishermen stood watching him, and a policeman entered the room, and, with one hand, he held the window shut. "In three seconds it was three minutes," he said.
