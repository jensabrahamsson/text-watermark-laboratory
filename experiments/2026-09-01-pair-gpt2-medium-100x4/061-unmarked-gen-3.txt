"It's so beautiful, your hands, thank you so much," he said.

The little boy smiled and raised his arms to kiss the painting. The raindrops flew from his hands into the brush at the moment he kissed his brother.

"Don't show it off until you've got all of me," he said, then stopped. He did so to the other child who raised one hand to wipe away a water droplet. The boy looked confused at first before turning to face him.

"Why don't we do this right now?" he asked, before kissing his brother on the cheek.
