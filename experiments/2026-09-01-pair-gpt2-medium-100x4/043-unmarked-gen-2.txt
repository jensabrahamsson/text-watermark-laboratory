At 4pm on Thursday morning, a woman, now 24, arrived at the scene.

She had been walking in the park after the evening lunchtime play-walk when a car drove up alongside the bus.

"I was standing by the footpath and it moved and came to stand right next to me," she told the court.

One man and one woman were seen walking through the park before the men got out and fled, leaving the child in their vehicle and the two women in an area around it.

But the woman was not convinced she was being followed, she told the court.
