"This is the entrance to the capital," said Anastasios, the one-legged guard. "People will arrive around 5:00 a.m. and they'll enter the prison. Our main office should be up by 5:45."

But this was too late for the four hundred prisoners: they already were locked in. They had to start making a way to the prison entrance before they could get out.
