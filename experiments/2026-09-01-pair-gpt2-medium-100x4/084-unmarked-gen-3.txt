A hot spring was the best type of summer meal, and some people were so obsessed with it they got so into it they actually ate a lot of it (it was one of the things that made summer so expensive for the farmers). There were also other people who made their own summer meals which included the best of local produce (including potatoes, apples, nuts, vegetables and honey, some of which people ate as a hot supper or in a baked dish). I don't know why the whole southerners had to be such hardworking bakers. The more I looked up recipes it kept returning to a kitchen using oven-style equipment
