"This is the son of a Brahmin." Said the farmer and said, "It is a boy of about five-six years old"

"What?"

But the gate of his house was shut and the gates of his village were also shut. To make matters more dismal, no one in the house could see it. The Brahmin's son went to the gates and called out the name, "This is the Brahmin's son." But the gate was shut again and the gates of another village, and a third, and so on. The Brahmin finally stopped when he saw not even a human being
