A soft humming came from the wood. The sound of music filled the hallway. "It is so nice to spend time with family again," she said. "The kids love you. You love them. You're wonderful to be around. Please hold me. I need you." She closed her eyes and wrapped her arms around her younger sister.

The sun broke through the clouds and made a shadow on the wall before the door closed behind them. "It's like home. And it's never too late to make it a great home."
