As the last person to leave, I could see the owner of the place. She was very tall. She had a nice smile – though I could tell she was being worked from behind.

She was very polite at first, but I knew she was in a job to do. I started asking questions.

There was a movie being screened in the back, as did several others. When the lady left, we were left looking at a massive, dark and dark movie. This had gone on since the beginning of May. As before, the director was smiling, giving each character a laugh.
