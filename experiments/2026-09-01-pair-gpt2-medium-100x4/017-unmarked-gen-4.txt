The paper had been placed in the desk with another document - a bank receipt which he had sent to a bank account. He thought it was for someone else - but it was for his own.

Sobbing in his bed, he reached into his pocket and grabbed an envelope with three hundred and fifty-eight dollars in it.

He opened it.

In the corner of the envelope, he saw the address of the bank - it was two blocks down the street from the bank - it was one of his parents. He was a lot older than that, probably 70.
