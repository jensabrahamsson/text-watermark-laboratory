"Oh, my God," he said, and looked around the gate for anyone that might have been listening to. None remained. "What happened?"

He walked down the stairs and looked around again.

"I haven't seen my dog since I got here." A little more than the usual silence of the other two, he heard a woman's voice. "She's in one of our rooms. He hasn't been in to speak to her either but, I guess, I thought of her to check on him, if you know what I mean. And the dogs always seem to be in good spirits."
