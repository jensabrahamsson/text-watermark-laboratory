"In order to support the work of the people of the village, we decided to hold a concert," the village hall said in a statement.

The group was led by three members and includes three young actors with the town of Nangangara having recently taken part in the Asian Games football championships. They had already sung songs ranging from traditional Tibetan folk songs and Tibetan national anthem, and performed the folk songs.

In a bid to celebrate the anniversary, they performed a traditional Tibetan national anthem as a song that had been sung as many times as possible during the last decade since the opening up of Tibet.
