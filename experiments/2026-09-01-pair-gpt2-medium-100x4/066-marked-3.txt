"This is just sad," said Linda L. Smith, whose husband, Dennis, died of AIDS nine years ago. "I wish there were one day that there was a memorial, especially to a father and son."

Linda said the ceremony took place during a very busy time in her life, but not one she is prepared to miss.

After dinner, she and her daughter took a photo of the church along First Avenue in Glenview.

"I was pretty emotional," she said.

The couple planned to hold more ceremonies in their own church for their parents in the next three months.
