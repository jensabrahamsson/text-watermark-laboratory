"This is it, this is good," said a worried Liao Wei.

The door of the storage house was slammed shut by the metal bar.

Liao Wei was terrified.

"I was scared for myself," she started to say. "I didn't want to be in danger."

There was nobody who had come over.

However one did walk into the area and a tall man with the black hair and beard stepped forward, "It must be Chen Yan."

She stood up, smiled and hugged her brother. "Let's go. You'll be safe here."
