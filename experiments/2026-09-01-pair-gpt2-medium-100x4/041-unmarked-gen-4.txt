Wearing an orange vest, he sat on the edge of the bed and said, "You have been here longer than I did. Please, sit down."

Bretton shook off the dust, his hand cupped the wound on his right knee. The stitches were healing nicely, but they still didn't seem to be going back in. He opened his eyes.

They were blinking red, just like the ones he had gotten in Afghanistan. He took in one last look and nodded to the nurse at the table. "Good luck, babe. I'll see you tomorrow."
