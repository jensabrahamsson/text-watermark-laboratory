On Wednesday, on the opening day of E3, Microsoft announced that the Xbox One will come in three versions, with $250 to $399 and $649 prices before the April 2015 holiday. This version is being sold by Microsoft Game Studios, the company's games department. The official pricing is unknown at this time, but it's known that the Xbox One will be $499 before the April 2015 holiday. A number of other games will have similar pricing before that date.
