The chapel was a good place to stand in winter — only a hundred and eighty yards away, and snow-packed. Snow piled up all around, and there was no room inside. The altar could not have been higher. It would not have been possible to place a stone from a height of three thousand feet under ice, except of course in a tunnel in a wall of ice. With the weight of snow falling outside, the altar must have been heavy as well. What is more incredible is the fact that there are still people who pray in front of this altar.
