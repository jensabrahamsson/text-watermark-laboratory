"I do not see the trees," said the peasant, looking into the blackening sky. He put his hands on his shoulders and looked towards the west.

But there was another thing that the old woman did not notice. The farmer looked up at the tree, which is the height of the ladders, and looked to the east. He saw a dark spot and the light that was there disappeared.

"All this time this was the sky," said the peasant to himself, "and now you see nothing."
