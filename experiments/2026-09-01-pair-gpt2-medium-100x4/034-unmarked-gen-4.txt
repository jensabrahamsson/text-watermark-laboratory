They waited outside the building to see if it was locked when it wasn't.

The doors were stuck shut, so the monks were forced inside, only to find the windows opened up.

The fire alarm was activated, but when the monks started pouring through the door, they realized that their mission wasn't over. There were no windows, so they had to climb through three doors and open the door outside. All the monks fell down the stairs, so the doors kept closing until the floor stopped raining down.

The rain started pouring, the monks couldn't get up and they couldn't get out any more.
