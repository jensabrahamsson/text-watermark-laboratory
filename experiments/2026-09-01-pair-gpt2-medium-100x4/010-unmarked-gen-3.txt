She had spent the past three winters, and the last three summers, inside a giant tent that held up a giant rock and a lake of white. The place was huge – like a castle – with an area of around 10,500 square meters. There were seven floors, each containing twelve of those sleeping-chamber-like enclosures, a lake with a waterfall, and a forest. The lake was huge, filled up with blue foam, and the sky was a deep red.

The entire lake was almost entirely covered with a thin layer of ice, about a meter thick, the deepest the village had ever looked.
