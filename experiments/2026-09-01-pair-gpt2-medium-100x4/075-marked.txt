'She'll not forget'

In the wake of the attack, there were still tears in the eyes of many, especially as they spoke of the loss of beloved loved ones, or even someone from their own family.

The families of those killed are trying to hold their loved ones with the family of the attacker who they say is responsible for his heinous act. The family of the murdered has asked the public not to forget about the children being killed or injured too, even as they wait for the next step in the investigation.
