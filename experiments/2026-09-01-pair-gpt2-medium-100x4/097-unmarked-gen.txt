He had a long night ahead of him, but as the sun rose and was almost hidden behind a high wooden fence, he reached into his pocket, grabbed the rope and let it hang. It would save his life.

Diane woke up the next morning feeling like nothing had happened, or at least nothing like what he expected. He had left home, and if anything, it was easier to be near the city of Miltown and out of the way. He had always known that, if anything were to happen, he could count on her to come running with him. At least for the short run.
