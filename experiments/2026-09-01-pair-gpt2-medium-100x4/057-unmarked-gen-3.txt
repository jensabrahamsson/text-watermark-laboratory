His body was cold and the air of death swept his bones.

He did not know that it was then that the world collapsed,

to him all the world seemed to belong to him.

The world was a place without meaning and all that was known became nothing more than shadows and empty promises.

One evening the sky was full of darkness and it seemed as if everything was upside down.

An elderly woman stood on horseback with her head wrapped round his neck.

She told him that, if he should ever wish to meet a man, he must first become a dead man.
