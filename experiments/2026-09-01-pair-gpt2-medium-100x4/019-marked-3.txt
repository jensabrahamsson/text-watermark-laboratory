"This is mine so it's mine!" She said to no one. She turned and shot the other birds out of the air, then quickly rushed after the pigeon. She stood there like a god while they ran, ignoring the smell. And then she walked through the rest of the grain, ignoring it once again while they ran.

She got in the air and hit the pigeon right on its head, sending it crashing headlong into the sand. The two birds flew off together, with the pigeon's body sinking into the water below.
