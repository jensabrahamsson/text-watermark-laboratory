But it wasn't a complete nightmare. A few minutes past midnight, a little more than a dozen men appeared, men armed with a handgun. They opened the door to get into Dodge.

Some of them tried to drag a couple of friends behind in the car. But once they got behind the wheel--it was nearly midnight, and they didn't have gas or a generator--they drove off, leaving many on the street without a place to sleep that night.[*] The men didn't make another attempt to get the door open that day, and the last two hours of the night were nothing but quiet.
