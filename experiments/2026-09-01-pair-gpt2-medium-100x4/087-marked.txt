"Who is on foot?" he asked, his question answering him. He stood up to be counted, to be shown the way; he didn't have to step through the glass. The warehouse was an ancient warehouse, still dark and warm with smoke, but not too far behind this warehouse—a place he'd visited a billion more times than the day before. His face softened with relief. The watchman was just another man. A man without honor; one of those people whose name, a hundred years ago, no one had any idea of, but the world's best remembered—who's been forgotten too.
