"Who dares to speak?"

The man answered with a sharp voice: "Who dares to speak?" to a farmer who dared to name his own cattle.

An old farmer named the cow that did not come to the gate. "Who dares to speak?" he asked again and again. Then he sighed deeply and said, "God has given me a better cow after I paid you your dues, and now I will raise an even better one, by the grace of God."
