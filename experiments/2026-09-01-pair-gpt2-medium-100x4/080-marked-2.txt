"This is it, this is good," said a man riding horses in the grass, his face still pale and white. "I'm coming to fetch you now."

The horses came soon afterwards, but their heads were covered with snow. They would never make it. But then, what did they know? The ice and snow were coming right through. At the bottom of the ditch, the ice floated down with water and carried away every step the horses made; they dropped or were swept, they fell at the feet of the horses on the frozen ground, they fell down, and they died.
