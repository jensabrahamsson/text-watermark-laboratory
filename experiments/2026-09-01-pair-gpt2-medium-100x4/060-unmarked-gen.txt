If only I could have taken them off! But there was nothing I could do. They were just an excuse to sleep. I could not fall asleep now.

At last, I tried to sit up. The duckboards moved slowly, and there was no danger here. My feet were sticky enough as it was, but it was still a good way to end the day.

"The last thing I'll want to do now is eat…" I muttered. I sat up, and then began to climb out of bed.
