And then, when the sun rose, he set with it.

It did not even dawn as he puttered through the empty woods in a brisk, slow stride. It was dark by the time he set.

And then he awoke.

It was just daylight when he found Nellie's car, and he got inside to try to fix her engine. He heard the engine start a little bit later than he had expected, but said it was mostly fine. He put his head into the window and glanced out the car windows.

He'd been walking towards the house.
