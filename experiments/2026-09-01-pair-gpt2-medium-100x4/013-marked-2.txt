"Then she went away."

Nora had no idea. But she thought that her teacher's speech would be something like this –

"What you are about to do is forbidden, young-child."

As I raised my voice, my expression became grim.

"I am the same age. I was only eight."

With that, I let my arm out and went around the back of the chair. And when I saw the girl's body lying back on the chair, I fell over.

However that expression…

"What kind of feeling did you get?"
