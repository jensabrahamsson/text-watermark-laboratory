The boat slowly drifted away from the shore. The winds shifted and she could hear them. The water slowly boiled, the birds came to life, and they danced.

She sat down at the edge of the water with her hand under her breath.

The wind picked up speed.

She reached the bottom of the water and pulled at her skin. She thought the boat might sink at the sound of her.

For a moment, she looked down at her feet. But there was something strange about the water below. She could see a pattern growing in the bottom of the water.

She stood there
