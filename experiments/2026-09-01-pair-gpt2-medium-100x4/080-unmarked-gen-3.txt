It must have been a very strong wind, for the river was suddenly cut off from the surrounding land. I suddenly turned and saw with a terrible terror what looked like a monstrous snake, and felt the earth move in my way. I felt that I was dying.

When I regained myself from the confusion, I remembered my father. His wife lay in the river, beside a dead fish!
