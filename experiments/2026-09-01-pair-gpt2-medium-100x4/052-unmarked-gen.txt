Fryer's sign will be replaced on the pier by another sign with ice cream and a sign of peace.

This summer was another cold one, with little snow on the ground, and in late June, the pier shut down due to frozen ice.

Pier workers had been called in to clear out the ice and snow over the summer, a fact which led to residents being taken off the pier at times last summer.

In recent years, Fryer's Pier, now the only place open to the public, has seen more turnover.
