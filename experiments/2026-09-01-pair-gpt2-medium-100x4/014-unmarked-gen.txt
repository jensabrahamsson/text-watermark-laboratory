I walked down the path. It was dark but lit up by the stars and the moon. The light moved slowly towards the city. It was night and all the streets that separated this town from that city were packed with the ghosts of the dead who could no longer talk to me. The night bus slowed and got lost. No one noticed it.

I watched as it lost the ghost of its passenger. A moment later, he started laughing at me and told me he forgot his drink. The light was moving once more at the stop.

The bus rolled back to the spot the night before, but not far away
