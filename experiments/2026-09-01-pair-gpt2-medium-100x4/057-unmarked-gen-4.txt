The wood, he knew the days would be very hard. There were no candles. Nothing. The sun shined down like a brilliant beam like a flame that has not yet burned through. There was nothing to keep the flames from burning out. The fire was hot, burning in the center of the room where the fire had been burning for the better part of a week. No heaters. No windows. Nothing but the darkness of the woods. "Good," he thought.

So he was able to keep his room from being used in any other way by the wind. No snow, no smoke or wind, no air
