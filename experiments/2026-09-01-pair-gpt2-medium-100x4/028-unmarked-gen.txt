"Ah hm……"

That was when a thick volume of air burst out from the gates, the wind ruffling the carpeting around her hair at the top.

"I wonder what a little baby was up to. And it was late at night. Even while I was asleep."

As if to confirm her words, the air was suddenly cut off, and the sounds of wind and voice resounded from the gate behind.

The one who opened the gate was a young girl.

The gate opened. That was as far as the wind could reach.
