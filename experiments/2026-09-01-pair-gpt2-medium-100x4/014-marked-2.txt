"Who is on it?" someone said to a woman of about 70 years of age. "No one on it."

We pulled in where we parked the bus for about 10 minutes before the driver looked over the passenger's shoulder. "I think a lady here is in need of a good cold shower, though," she said.

The door of the bus slid off its hinges and closed against its driver's leg. She stared at it for a few seconds before stepping on the gas.

By the time it had fully opened, the crowd of people was still out there. The bus wasn't moving at all
