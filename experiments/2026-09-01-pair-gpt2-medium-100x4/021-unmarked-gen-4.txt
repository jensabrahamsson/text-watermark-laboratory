A couple of metres from the water, the men saw a tall, muscular boy standing by the river bank watching them. His back was to the men and a rope was tied around his neck to get him out.

"I can hear him screaming and saying, 'Please leave,'" the older man says.

After an hour and half, they managed to free the boy and take him home. "Everyone is in shock," said Wajdan Bhat, an officer in Nizamuddin, the first city to give up.

"Our boys have lost a life."
