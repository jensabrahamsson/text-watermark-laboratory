"Who is it?" he asked her.

She shrugged. "There," she said, "I can see you. It's a little bit different, though, isn't it? A little bit. Just a little bit more." Her voice was high, and the wind whispered through the trees below.

She gave him an affectionate look. "Is it? Your cousin," she said.
