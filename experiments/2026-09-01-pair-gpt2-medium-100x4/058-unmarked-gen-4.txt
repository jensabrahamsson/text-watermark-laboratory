This is not the instrument of the orchestra – it is a woman's 'viola'.

A woman in a small European village has had her violin repaired after it was damaged in battle. At the end of the day, she says, the violin's shape was the result of battle wounds. Her husband had to buy it in order to play in the orchestra at the last minute when the instrument died.

The woman told MailOnline it was the first time her husband had paid for repairs due to war.
