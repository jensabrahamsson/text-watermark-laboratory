By the time it reached sea, three of the sailors had been killed. The captain drowned his mate.

I went to the hospital, where one of my nurses pointed me to the shipwrecked boat. It was no different from my own boat, except I had the scars of a recent attack, from a fight. She turned over the boat to someone in my hospital.

I tried talking to the doctor, but he was so nervous he just smiled.

"We'll just have to call you," she told me.

Then, she said, it was time for us to return to the States
