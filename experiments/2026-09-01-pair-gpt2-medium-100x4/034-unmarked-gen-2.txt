A small group of men were gathered in the chapel's front pew, while the door was closed. This group consisted of the chief priest, the pastor, the priest responsible for preparing hymn books and an assistant priest who handled all of the priests' duties.

"Well! Then I'll introduce the choir from tonight on. Come with me!"

Zhang Ye's heart shuddered in anticipation.

The choir would sing the words of the hymn books from tonight…

"To the Lord of Light!"
