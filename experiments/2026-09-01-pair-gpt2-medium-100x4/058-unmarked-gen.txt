At the age of 12, the violin was taken from his home to a pawn shop in Biel.

On another occasion at the pawn shop, Kavada says he received the violin case from a fellow student who had just gotten back from Germany.

Kavada says the pair would discuss playing with the case on the table and he gave it a test run after some months.
