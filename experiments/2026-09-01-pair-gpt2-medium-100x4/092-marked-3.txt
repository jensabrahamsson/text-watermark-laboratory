"This is wonderful!"

She placed a few seeds out the door.

"I haven't seen my granddaughters so happy for over a year now."

Her expression was a bit gloomy as she placed her left hand on the head of her pet pig, which seemed to see the same event.

"He's like the happiest child of the village; and now they will never go to school again!"

In front of the gate of town, the atmosphere of excitement and joy was as evident as when she brought the pig.

The village was excited to meet a new owner.
