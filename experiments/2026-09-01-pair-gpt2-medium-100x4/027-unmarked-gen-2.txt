She had lived for four days as a victim of the massacre. She was shot in the chest. By then, she was covered in cuts and abrasions. She'd made no attempt to call the man over, just stared vacantly at her bed, with a wafer-thin scar on her arm. By the time the medic brought her into a waiting clinic she was covered in blood and had died.

An unnamed police officer was among those who tried to reach her, she said; the officers told her of the wounded officer, "but she was told she looked like she couldn't hear".
