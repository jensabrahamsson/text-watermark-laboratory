She stood there for a long time looking through the glass, and then made her way to the church, which held about eighty thousand people.

She was surprised that she could see nothing in it, to be so far removed from the country. It only took the space of three minutes before they all moved forward with it.

"I don't suppose we have room in there for any more," I commented in amazement.
