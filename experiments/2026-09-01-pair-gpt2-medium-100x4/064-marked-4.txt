"This is it, this is it. This is the victory of the United States."

Then I felt someone touch my knee, a hand.

"I was right at it — and I've had something very, very unpleasant happen since. My knee's a little bruised."

I wasn't sure if his hand had stuck, or if his palm had touched my knee. I wasn't sure if I could hear his words anyway, or if there was something off with him — something he wanted me to know. He didn't say anything. I felt like an idiot.
