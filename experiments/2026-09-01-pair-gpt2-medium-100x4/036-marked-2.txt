The ladders were made of strong iron-braided twine, or cloth, that had lain open, and held all the way into the orchard. At the head of each ladder was some large, strong, thick iron-wood door-posts, and a door on each end of these posts that was screwed. Some were also fastened to the bottom of a box so fastened to the orchard, so as to prevent the ladders from flying out of the box through the opening of the box, when they were rolled. The boxes were in place so the picks or shovels would not go into the boxes
