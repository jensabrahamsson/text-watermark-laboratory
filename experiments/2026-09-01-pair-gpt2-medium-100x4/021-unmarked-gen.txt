Finally an answer came from a phone book – the most valuable thing in the whole universe. It was from a young lady called Lacey. She had been born in 1929, her father a schoolteacher and her mother was a secretary. She is now 95 and a mother. She told us she was from Wigan, not because she came from one of Britain's great ethnic communities but because of its wealth, despite its poverty.
