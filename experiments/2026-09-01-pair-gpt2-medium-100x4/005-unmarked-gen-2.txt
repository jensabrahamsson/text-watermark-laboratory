Nurse's hands came away and wiped the damp cotton of the scar as she sat in the chair. "I don't know what to say, you're sick. The more you bleed…"

"Please don't worry…" Ruby moaned. "I would rather die in that chair for this now."

"I know. You feel really good about that."

"Now you've got me to ask my mom to bring me some ice."

She gave a small smile and set the ice in the bowl.

The little girl was silent for a moment. Ruby's heart felt a little weak
