"Then come and try it again. It will be great."

A small sound from outside startled both of them for a few seconds.

"Come in."

Kirius stepped through the door and turned around to see the woman that he remembered seeing earlier standing in the doorway. She had a pair of scissors with the red tip pointing down the road to a tree.

"We are back, you two."

"Yes." The woman gestured to the large wooden box before her. "We are going to have to take the two from here and let you get them inside quickly."
