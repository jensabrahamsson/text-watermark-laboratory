Kreia was still, so this was not the time to fight with him. After all, the boy's spirit was stronger than most, and perhaps he was trying to show off before she could. With a heavy sigh, he turned, grabbed his sword, then rushed on.

When he reached them, the boy had turned into a wolf before him. Kreia wasn't certain what the wolf might have done, but it was easy to see why he was standing. He wore the dark robes and hood of a soldier; his brown hair was dyed black, along with the red and white of the dragon's eyes.
