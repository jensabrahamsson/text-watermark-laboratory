"This is something I'm very familiar with. It's only about a hundred and fifty yards away, but it's one of the best paddle banks I've ever seen," said the fisherman.

He had not seen that bank for months. He knew it would be the best paddle he had ever seen. For this last fifty or so yards, he made no attempt to make it into anything as pleasant as his good old paddle, but, as he paddled past the river mill wheel, he felt sure that there was nothing to compare it with except perhaps the beauty of the Mississippi on the right.
