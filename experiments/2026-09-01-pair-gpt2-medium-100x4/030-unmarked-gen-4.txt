In the kitchen, the couple waited while their mother and brother cooked the meat.

Then the mother began to dig a hole and dig for the family's gold. She had not gone far when she spotted a pair of men, the men dressed in the clothes of the soldiers. They were looking for her, she thought: the gold.

A man jumped out of the house and said he was a soldier, wearing a skullcap and gloves. He had come to steal this gold.
