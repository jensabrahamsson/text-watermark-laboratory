I stood there, watching, unsure if I was being watched.

I opened the gate and looked. The wind was beginning to turn, and a strange mist that had grown out of the forest was growing stronger and stronger in the gap the gates used to fit into. I looked at the moon, not seeing it as white, just a swirling blue, and noticed the small moonlight the moon created.

Then the wind quieted.

Something happened.

In that brief moment…

The wind moved faster. The mist moved deeper. I could see through the moonlight even in my blind spot.
