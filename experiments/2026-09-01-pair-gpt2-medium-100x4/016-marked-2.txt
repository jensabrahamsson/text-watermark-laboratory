"Yes." The black-robed girl lowered her face further. "This is my house."

They entered a spacious room. The ceiling was made of thick, deep blue water, like that one the boys had come across before. The floors were covered in the same gray-green color and the furniture was made of the same dark-purple cloth they had found on the roof before.
