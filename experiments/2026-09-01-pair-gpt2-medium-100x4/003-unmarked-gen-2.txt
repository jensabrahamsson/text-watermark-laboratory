The girl and the boy turned their glance at each other. "You have no idea," the guard finally said.

And with that the gate opened.

When that happened the girls, having been caught from the start of their mischief, fell back with one hand and a small amount of the bottle-wreath. This gave way to a great deal of the perfume which had fallen on them. One of the guards found this curious, for his own father had made his own bottle. As he picked it up, the fragrance vanished.
