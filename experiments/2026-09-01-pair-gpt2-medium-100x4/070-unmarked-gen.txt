One by one, the workers had walked off into the cool night air that drizzled over the tarpaulin, which was covered in a thin layer of sloping concrete.

Some of the truckers jumped off into the cold.

A few seconds before they landed on dry land, more of them started scrambling off. The air chilled. One looked out of a cabin window. His wife was sitting. He asked the woman if her son, a sailor, was still on the bridge, too.

"No," she replied.
