"This is the entrance to the capital," said Anastasios, the one-legged guard. "People who are afraid of animals may go here. Don't be afraid of them," he said.

The door of the entrance was in the corner with a large log. People were playing in it; their voices were loud and deep: "Arian, Ariana, Ariana, arian—there are four of us. Do you believe in Ariana's prophecy?" But a person from the crowd in front of me suddenly said: "I believe in it," and pushed his way through.
