"Well," I started, and turned my back suddenly, and saw what was out of all the woe that this day of the year would bring; "the day's fruit is here, but that leaves the tree-bed in a bad condition, for the bark was broken off and the fruit left on the leaves." The tree had lost its bark and leaf, but the branches stood up, and looked great.

"The branch we picked was not broken, but the one we cut off was," I went on, "the branch was strong enough to give and hold the fruit, so I cut it from the tree
