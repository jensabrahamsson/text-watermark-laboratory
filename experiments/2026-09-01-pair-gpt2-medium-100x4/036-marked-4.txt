"Then come the bough-barking gong and the hitching posts and the tramp."

"Then don't stand too straight," said he, smiling; "that goes better with the wood-trees and the firs. You're in the way."

It was a hard-hearted fellow now, and he went his way at last, only as he had come, the trees standing and the rushes and junipers.
