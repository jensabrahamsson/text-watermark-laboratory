She pulled up the clothes to dry on her kitchen countertop (the other side of a closet) because nothing fit that way.

She put on underwear and socks because in case of thunderstorms, the windows would rain down outside.

And she kept her hair in braids because she was going to get her hair dyed.

She put on her own clothes because in the dark, the stars would come out, not because she was wearing them.

And she put on mascara so no one would be scared of a mascara man-bat or cat-man.

And she went to work at home
