He pulled out his gun which was hidden in the pocket by the black box.

He pointed it at the hole and drew a circle in it which turned red.

He opened the box, took out two white coins and said, "This is mine."

The next thing he looked at was the table.

Behind him, standing at the back, was his wife, who was now wearing a purple shirt.
