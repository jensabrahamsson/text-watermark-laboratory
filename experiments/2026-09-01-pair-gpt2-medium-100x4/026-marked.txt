"Whoop!"

A student cried, and a white man in military uniforms approached the girl.

"I am the colonel."

"I am also the colonel."

There was an old soldier with an ice-cold stare on his face.

"Do you want your hands on the white boy next to you?"

"No way! That would not make sense."

"Yes, yes, yes-"

"It can be done."

The new colonel said his name and was about to walk away. A red haired girl looked at the soldier.
