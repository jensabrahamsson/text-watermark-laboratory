"This is it, I'll get over it by the second or third," I said, "and then we'll do my things. My old man says that they're going to be nice people, the little people, a bunch of nice people: and I'd better take the opportunity to prove that I'm going to be a lot less unimpressed with their manners today."

And that, too, after I'd said goodbye to Father Riddle.

I turned my head and smiled a smile that almost went beyond smile as with all the greats I'd met.
