It could be argued that plants and animals live on this planet for just as long as we do, and that human beings have no need to eat them. A study published in 2009 looked at how the world's food supply was distributed: about three-quarters of all food produced in the world comes from agriculture. Some scientists say that there's no alternative - we've just evolved to benefit from our food. Others, however, point to the dangers of over-indulgent eating.
