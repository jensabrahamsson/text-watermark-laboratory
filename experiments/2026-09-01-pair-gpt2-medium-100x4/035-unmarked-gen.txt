'I told him that there must be some mistake,' said he. So his mother, who is not afraid of strangers, put on the white gloves, went on a brisk walk, and returned with the man's letter. For the messenger was not known to him, and as he had a strange way about him of talking he was sure that the letter had arrived.

They went quietly toward the door and opened it. The letter was addressed to the captain of the courier, the lady, and she had written on the envelope:

'Sir 'M. d'Augereau, Monsieur de Guise
