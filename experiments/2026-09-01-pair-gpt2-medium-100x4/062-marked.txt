"This is the scene we had all been waiting for!" my brother cried. "But this day…this is…this is the day I will die. All these people! How will my precious people survive?! What will I do? I wish to die for them…for your people that will never hear anything of my death!"

The scene was now empty and quiet. There were no more lanterns burning, no more barrels from the barrel sale. Nobody was walking around. I was still in my bed. I took it for granted that I would have died of thirst by now. Why did I wait so long?
