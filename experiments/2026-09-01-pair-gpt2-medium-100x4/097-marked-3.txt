"Then come and sit at the table, and tell me about your story," he said, and we ate together, eating with a good appetite and a merry spirit!

We found our guests sitting with a couple of women. They smiled and said that as they were in their late forties, and had a large inheritance with their widows and children, a good fortune had come down on them. They were from the city of Alkhorab, and had been in this country for decades, always staying with their widows.

All these women gathered to a table and said their prayers together.
