"This is the entrance to the waterfall of the mountain's mountain," she whispered, but she couldn't hear herself.

"Come in."

She stood in front of the big stone door, and the rain poured from it.

From the inside, it was dark, but she knew that the place wouldn't be dark at all.

The door of the stone trough opened to a cold winter light, and in the distance was where a river flowed.

It began at the mouth of the mountain, the same as what was here. The river flowed in its way like the wind.
