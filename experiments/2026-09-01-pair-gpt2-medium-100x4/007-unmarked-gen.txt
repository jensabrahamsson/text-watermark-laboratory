"Come in," cried the man-at-arms to a large man in a black suit.

"You haven't come for supper?" he protested.

The man pointed his torch to a distant house in the far distance.

"I'm here, sir," said the man, as he followed the beam of light through a house to the house behind the man's desk.

"What? A little house?" demanded the man.
