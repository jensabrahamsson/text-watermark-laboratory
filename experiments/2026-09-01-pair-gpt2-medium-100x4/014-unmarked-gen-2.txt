The last thing a man could have expected was for the bus to become the second vehicle in a five-mile trek across a city street to end with an accident.

The night bus made it to its destination and started to gather up the passengers as the police moved their way into the station.

The bus pulled at the edge of a fence. It took the driver's attention and turned back to face the ground.

All along this crossing a crowd had gathered. One woman was screaming. Her children were terrified, even so the policeman, his hat on, was able to grab her and pull her to safety.
