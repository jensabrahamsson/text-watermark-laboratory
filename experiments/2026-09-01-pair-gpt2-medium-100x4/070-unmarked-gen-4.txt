He reached the end of the street that led to the pier and opened the door. When he came out on the street, he saw an old man, bent over, sitting on the sand next to a tree. He looked down at his hands and then at the rope, which came untied to an open boat. He looked up and saw the two men standing by the wooden end of it.

"I hope no one's coming," hissed the old man, who pointed at the rope from his position on top of the tree.

Cordy jumped up, then walked up and pointed as well.
