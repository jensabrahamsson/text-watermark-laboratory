"This is it."

Her heartbeat slowed, so she sighed after letting the arrow rest beside her.

She sighed, then stood up. The arrow was stuck on the ground, which she didn't feel like letting disappear, so she pulled the arrow out of its quiver and stared at it. The arrow was an arrow forged in her blood because her brother was defeated by the dark lord of Roshar, and now she was trying to bring down Goshudroth with one bullet.

But her hands flew and the arrow struck a tree, and her fist flew in an arc towards Gondul.
