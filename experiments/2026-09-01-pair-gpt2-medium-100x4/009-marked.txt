The street of Dandenong is known best for the red line, which runs along streets and intersections.

There are dozens of these streetlights at different locations, and they all show the same message: Go back to where you belong.

"We try to use this idea in any interaction, even if we're making an investment, to have that feel of being part of the community," said Dandenong Street Patrol Officer David Skelton.

But sometimes these lights go out for a few hours or days.
