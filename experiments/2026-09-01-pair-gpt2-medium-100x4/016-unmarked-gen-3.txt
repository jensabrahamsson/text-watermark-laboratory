The room was dark and chilly, and when the cold began to build up and the temperature hit a comfortable range, the light seemed to take on a life in the middle of it.

And while it seemed like a strange thing to be doing, the ice that clung to the wooden wall gave off the feeling of warmth without the sun being directly lit in its place.

The sound of cold metal in the room, the cold breeze, a gentle clang. Those sounds reverberated throughout the room.

It wasn't just the sounds that kept them there. Other than that, the walls themselves were all ice
