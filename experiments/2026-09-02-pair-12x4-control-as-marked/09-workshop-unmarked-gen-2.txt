Then, a few hours later, I had some good news. I had just purchased a TARDIS, and while I knew about TARDIS, I was not quite familiar with it. And while I was pretty sure it was the TARDIS, it took some getting used to, given that both were the same TARDIS, that I would have to put it in place.

My first TARDIS adventure, when I was little and just playing by myself, had me working out in my kitchen and looking down a door, not realizing I could see where the doors were or, in turn, where they'd been for years.
