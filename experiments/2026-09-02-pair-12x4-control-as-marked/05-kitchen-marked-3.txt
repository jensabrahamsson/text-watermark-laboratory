After a while, the door started going up in flames, and I pulled up my watch face and saw it come up right next to the kitchen sink. I told a neighbor. He said he didn't think the fire would end in this way.

My grandfather did, though, but I'd rather not say what he was thinking. I'd been working an entire week at a local paper, so nothing of an emotional toll lay around at my doorstep. I would sit at home in that bed of the bed and listen to the air between the sheets of papers on the table and hope there wasn't more to read.
