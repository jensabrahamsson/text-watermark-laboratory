I got up, saw a man holding a lantern and a woman holding a glass, half-full, two glasses of wine. We began to walk toward him, a couple of miles beyond the line of the river. I tried to make a mental note to stop, but to no avail... I heard him ask, "What are you doing here?" This made me very nervous, but the thing only made me very happy. A few minutes later I went back to check it was still there. The lantern was there.

When I got the door open it was half-open again. The sun was gone.
