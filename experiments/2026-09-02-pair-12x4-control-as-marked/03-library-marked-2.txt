After the walk through the canal and after the long walk in the morning I have to ask myself, what else can I do now? I do not want to wait a long time. I want to get into the boat.

I cannot talk in the boat while walking in the river. I am told, as well as many other people I have encountered, that I can not tell people how to walk to get in from the boat without telling them. I still do not know where I have been before. No one knows who is telling me how to walk. This is my last word to the boat.
