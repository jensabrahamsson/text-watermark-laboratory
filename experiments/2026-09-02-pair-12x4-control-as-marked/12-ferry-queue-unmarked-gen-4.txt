The drivers have no idea where and when they might go. Most of them aren't even aware the ferry service is here, and not even there is a public transport line. Most of them just wait, looking for signs.

The passengers are not the first to wonder what is really going on. For instance, at the end of a particularly hot day one in January, a man was travelling for an hour and a half in one of three cars, and had only to be replaced in the back when all his cars were in full view of the cameras.

Another passenger at the time also was on a road trip.
