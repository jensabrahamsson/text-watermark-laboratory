Two hours drive from Amsterdam, or at the end of the three weeks they come to us. In the morning the bus stops are filled with people. A woman drives a taxi from Amsterdam at dawn. If you see her there, you cannot look at her. The driver asks her if she likes to go to work and does not ask if she's a virgin. She says, "I love you and that is why I go to see her."
At about 11:00 the bus stops. There are no women at the depot. At 5:00 the men in the cafe stand waiting for a few minutes.
