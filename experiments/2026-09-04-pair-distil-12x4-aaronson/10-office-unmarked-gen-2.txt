

The printer's two-foot tall, $400 bag is used as the "museum of the printing press," with a shelf. The museum keeps all the photos, diagrams, and photographs online. The first version of this project was made in 1979. There were about two thousand copies of it — three thousand copies and a dozen copies. In 1987, the printing press opened in Washington, D.C., about 400 copies each — in part to give back.
This project is an attempt to make this kind of printer more accessible and expand their capabilities. With its latest expansion, the printer is able to run the printing presses
