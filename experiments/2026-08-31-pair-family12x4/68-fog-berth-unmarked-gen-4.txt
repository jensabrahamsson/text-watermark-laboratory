At six in the morning the driver sat down beside his wife and their eight-year old son before returning to the berth. He took the wheel for a few seconds on his back with some of the passengers still in their seats. He had to get on. Then the car started to speed away and he was too low, and the driver was stuck inside the driver's seat of a car. Suddenly he felt the ground on his right hand. Then he fell back back.

By the time the car was a short distance from the other car it was almost all stopped when it came face-first down a muddy embankment
