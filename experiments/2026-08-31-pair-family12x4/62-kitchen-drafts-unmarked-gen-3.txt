I could see that I was back in a position I had not been in before. I glanced up at the clock. It was 7:45. The sun shone bright enough for me to make out all the places we were. I was on the bridge of the river that ran down into Manhattan where my family lived, where I found shelter. I had left the two notes, the one in the same box that I used to write.
