The hospital was a mess. The staff in the doctor's office smelled like cigarette smoke during the afternoon. There were nurses, some of their faces, other than the one I was at, staring straight at me while I wrote. I remember they told me that there was no bed at the hospital. I had seen a bed from the pharmacy for three months while I was there.

All I can remember then, the room seemed so good and clean and clean, like that kind of clean room, that I can be so honest about it. My hair was loose, my skin was brown. The doctors were just so nice.
