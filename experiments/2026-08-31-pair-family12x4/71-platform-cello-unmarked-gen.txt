On the beach, on the banks of a river. On the roof below our house. I watched some fishermen walk back and forth, their nets being on their backs.

We tried to get our heads around how a plane flies. We watched on the edge of the water. We heard the plane crash into the shoreline, and heard people screaming in the distance. The planes came in and out with different shouts. I asked some fish what they were doing, and they said, "Oh good God! We're gonna die!"

I had a sense that this was going to be the last we heard from us.
