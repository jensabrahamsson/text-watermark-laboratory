The hospital was a mess. The staff in the corner, all wearing bright orange, had all been transferred from an old and neglected place into the high-rise area. The doctor was a poor man's man, I thought. The patients were in poor condition, in a poor, isolated, isolated, isolated, isolated hospital. The only other way out of it was for me to read about it with them. I didn't want to be in another doctor's room. I just wanted to know what the heck I was getting into wrong. I had no business in an emergency room.

It also hurt an awful lot.
