A long night

The morning of that day, I saw my dad. He was standing at the bar on the way home from work, talking to two men, one of them a guy from the company who was a sales guy, the other a sales guy from the local drug store, on the way to meet an interesting man who did not look like his dad. I could only guess he was the guy they were looking for right next to a bar that was closed off to traffic. "What's your name?" the boy asked.
