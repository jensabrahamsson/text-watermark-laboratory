I sat on the floor, holding a glass of water, and stared up in fear at the trees that had already sprung up. We were coming to the city. I didn't look down. The water was clear, and the river was thick. I could see the river on the horizon. A stream from my left that had been on all the roofs had turned white. The wind in the mountains blew by, like thunder, and then blew down a big tree. But I looked into the horizon, and I saw a tall, thin, strong, yellowish-haired boy.
