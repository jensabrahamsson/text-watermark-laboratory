The girl that asked where the trip was going to end was a man with a red suit and glasses. A woman with an Indian accent and a big, black-and-white shirt ran right along the aisle. She looked and looked at me. The boy that pulled the seat out of that woman didn't like the busker.

The next thing we know, the busker is still there: a man, black and bald, trying to figure out where his kids will go.
