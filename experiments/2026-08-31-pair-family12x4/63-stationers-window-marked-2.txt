The hospital was a mess. The staff in the doctor's office was sick in bed, bleeding the blood of the young patient who had given birth. The nurses, who were in a wheelchair sitting in the hallway, were screaming out to my desk for my doctor, to stop the bleeding, but it was too late. That day, when he was dead, I felt like I had won the game.

But, at a time when, even at this very young age, I knew I was in a difficult position, I didn't care for getting sick to begin with, I didn't. And that alone was enough.
