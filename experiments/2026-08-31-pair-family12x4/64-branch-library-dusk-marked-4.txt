The local library staff try to do in fact what the archbishop of Canterbury has said in an earlier diocese.

It is now more difficult than ever in Dublin, because of a lack of funds. The archbishop of Canterbury and the local archdiocesan bodies have a duty to give them the money to do any repairs needed.

Many of the archdiocese's funds are spent on other projects such as upgrading churches. However, there is an increasing number of the local people who feel that they have been wrongly divided rather than being left behind.
