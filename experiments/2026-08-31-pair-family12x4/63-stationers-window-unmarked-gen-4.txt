This is a story told often through the years in our family of five and includes the experience of five of us who died of cancer or a liver disease. The story of the three who ended up on the wrong side of that fence with no hospital stays. The story of the two children who lost their parents to car accidents or cancer. The story of the seven who lost their friends and loved ones to cancer, while trying to cope. What made this family unique, how our home, our life, our way of life changed, where we are, how we are going to be when we die, what our future may hold in us
