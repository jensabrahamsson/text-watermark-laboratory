The dog's nose was the same and its cheekbones had red streaks and black spots on them. For its whole life it was the same; at times it had been the same. The dog's eyes had sunk into the sand, as if to clear them and there was little more to do. The sun was in the clear sky and it would shine into all its colors when it came out of the shade on a day it didn't like.
