The carriages were all very small and almost empty. But when the crane got down there was more people crammed back onto them than ever before. There were a lot of children there and some of them were playing the violin with the big boy who had just been there and would be sitting there, talking to him for a bit, and being taken back to their seat. Then there were the women on the other side of the carriage. One was a nun, one from Eastbourne.
