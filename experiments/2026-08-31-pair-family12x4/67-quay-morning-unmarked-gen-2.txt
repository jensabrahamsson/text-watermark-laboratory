I sat inside a cramped, half-shaken office and a few hundred yards from the front desk were a few thousand people. My fellow office workers were doing more or less what they normally do, except on Tuesday morning. With a man at the front desk I felt a sense of duty.

"What do you consider necessary?" I asked the same day and on Thursday I asked them again.

They kept explaining that while their jobs were good, their salaries were better. There would be no need to check in as the city was running a new subway system, and that would make sense since you would always have one
