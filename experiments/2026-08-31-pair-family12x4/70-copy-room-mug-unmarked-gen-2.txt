There had to be another set of slides. One of the sheets had only a small piece of parchment. It had to be big enough for three people to use. A thin pencil on its base covered each slide. A long wooden box slid from the bottom of the box. It was a small, white-haired boy. He sat in it, staring with blankness at the floor. When he was done he said "It's fine," and then said it was too late to tell. The machine stopped and the boy sat in the same spot. No one said anything.

A moment later the machine made one page.
