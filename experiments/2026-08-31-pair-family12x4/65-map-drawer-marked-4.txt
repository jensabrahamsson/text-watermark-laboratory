The car doors slid open behind us. The car doors rattled and they opened.

We stepped back in the passenger side of the car. It was dark again. A blue light flashed above our windshield. I turned around. It was a sign.

"I am the same age," I said to myself.

We had been together three days. We sat on the side of the road for about four-and-a-half hours. We did not notice our presence. We do not know who was in our car, and we did not know much more about what they said or did.
