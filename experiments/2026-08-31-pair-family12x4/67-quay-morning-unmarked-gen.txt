My eyes closed. I was out of my mind. I heard the bell ring with the engine turning. The sound was so small that I couldn't feel the pressure. As my body was on my left, the other car pulled toward us, out of sight. The men had gone in on me, and all four got back on the train. It was only at my feet that I heard my wife cry out. With my hands on my hips, I told her, My family, you've been through it all. The rest of them came home to their homes, and they were right there.
