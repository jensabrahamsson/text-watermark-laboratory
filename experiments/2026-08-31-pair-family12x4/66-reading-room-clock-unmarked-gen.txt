We had a couple of hours in the aisles before some women took a shower. Some said they'd go outside to get some clothes for the girls. One came to the door, "Don't worry, I will help you get them, it will be very easy." Some women started washing their hair.

We walked around in the aisles. Sometimes there would be a group of men on each other's backs. They were waiting till the girls were sitting in the aisles. Men would stand at a chair to wait for other people. It was like playing tennis. The women were waiting for each other
