The dog that I had told him about was an orphan whose only other friends were his friends in school, all of whom were of the same breed, and he was too afraid to talk to friends to have any contact with them.

I turned my head toward his, and my eyes glittered with the sweet yellow of the sweetest autumn trees I had ever seen. That was what it said in that way. My heart was sweet, and I could feel some satisfaction from the presence of this old man; it had almost died down.
