
She opened her eyes, at the same time as a few others who had been coming down to the street, to the door of the church which she was going to see.

She looked round and saw four men, one of them carrying a pistol, who looked curiously around.

As she pointed the pistol at the group of strangers, one of them suddenly went round in a hurry and shot the soldier who was holding the pistol, who was sitting in the chair. The pistol then fell from the man's hand.
