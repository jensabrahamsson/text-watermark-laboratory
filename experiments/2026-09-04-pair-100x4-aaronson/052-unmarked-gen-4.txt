
A man with a police dog waited next to a building in a building next to the pier and waited until his dog was out of training to ask him what his dog was doing. The man then walked back and started screaming.

Police said they got involved after the man tried to get into a car. An officer took the guy to police headquarters.

He was taken to Loyola University Hospital where he was treated. Two officers with protective gear took the dog and were taken to the hospital in the critical condition.
