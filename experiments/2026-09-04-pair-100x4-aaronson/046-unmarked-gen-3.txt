
When I stopped on Lake Merrion to visit her, the wind in the center of the lake rose as the air slowly settled on her skin.

The light shone on her beautiful black hair that was a dark silka, as though it were a cloak, but it seemed that she wouldn't dare to get up when the light seemed to go everywhere.

In my hand I was holding the white sword. The one that she was standing on. The one that she said she didn't get.

"Fumu, if only that one had been there!"

I felt that she was a person
