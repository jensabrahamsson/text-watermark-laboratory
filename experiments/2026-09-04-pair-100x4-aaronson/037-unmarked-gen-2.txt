
She ran to a safe place and found her uncle sitting next to her, looking as if he was the first to see her. He looked back at his father, who smiled.

"You're not leaving without us," she said cheerfully.

They kissed and then began to walk down the street, which in this country was fairly clean.

When they got to the home in the garden that she shared with his sister in England, he smiled and said hi to her.

She hugged and took him up to bed and was greeted by a kiss. The next morning he went to sleep.
