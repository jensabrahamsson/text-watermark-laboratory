
The bridge was completely covered in red, and the bridge was blocked in all directions. There were no signs for the entrance or the route. Some passersby even thought they might see the bridge, but we were unable to identify them. Instead, they took to the road and drove directly to the gate. We were asked to wait here for at least two hours, to avoid passing the gate. We arrived at the gates later that night, and we left at 8 pm.


The next morning the gate was still open. As expected, I am happy that there were little signs or pictures or anything to indicate where we were.
