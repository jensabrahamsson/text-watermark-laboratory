
Cue an angel coming and it began to move. It was an angel she had never seen, for now it had risen from the dead, and there it was, standing on a huge arch of wood. It had taken all its strength, and moved. Its wings had lifted to the clouds above, and when they had touched down on it, it began to change its form.

Then the angel that had lifted it raised its head to the sky, and it rose up like a bird in the air, as if it was all going to fly off into space and then come back again to life again.
