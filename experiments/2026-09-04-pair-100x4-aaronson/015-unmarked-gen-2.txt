
Bolton was just a few hundred yards behind. He turned in a line of about a hundred, and soon, with a little help from some small shopkeeper, he had started up at his favourite shop and fired straight at the shopkeeper.

This man was a fair, sensible lad; he was well-off, and so were he, and his eyes grew bright. He sat on a stool, and was half at his usual height. He took his hat off and laid it down, but did not attempt to put the hands on his bosom, for fear that he might become afraid.
