
A few rows in front of it stood a young Indian man, Mr. Daud.

"How long I sit here?" he asked.

"Six days."

"Nine days,"

"Ten days."

"Why?"

"Just for me."

Mr. Daud's voice quivered. In another moment the sunbeams shone bright, a little in the shadow.

He put in his hat and stood at the pavilion. Then he went to fetch tea.
