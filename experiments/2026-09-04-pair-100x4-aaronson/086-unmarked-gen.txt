
In 2004 an exhibition of a collection of over 900 film sets was opened which included movies from The Man Who Could Not Walk Alone and The Wind Rises.


It is believed that the collections were opened under a new title, which was later renamed the New Media Arts and Culture Collection during the period from 1991 to 1995. The first collection, which includes the famous Stonewall Jackson, began collecting around 1993. The new name, and earlier names like Houdini and Stonewall, were given a 'downtown' in 1995. It took until the end of the programme to change the name, before it was rebranded
