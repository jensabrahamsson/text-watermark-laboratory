
The man, who did not know that many of these ladders had been built since early on, was very thankful that he could get his hands at them and see how well they were working. He found that in order to get to work, he had to climb up the ladders and work around their ends. To do this he first had to go through the trees. This meant that by working at the tops of the ladders, he had to be able to get at them a minimum of 10 centimeters per second while the other three meters would push him over. He needed to learn this step-by-step.
