
"Goddess, what are you doing? I was afraid they would kill the poor little girl, but you're doing it to get away with this!" She told the cow.

Rice picked up her horse and walked back to the gate. That had nothing to do with what they needed. The cow wasn't home yet at the time, and no one would ever come and see her again. She could feel her face burn, her teeth cut, that she was about to be murdered. She walked in silence. Her body was on the ground, so cold that nothing would help but a warm chill wash over it
