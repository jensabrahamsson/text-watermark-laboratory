
In the early afternoon of July 3, 1942, the Germans began a systematic withdrawal of the enemy in the north. The Germans occupied the city of Lucca and occupied the northern reaches of the city with a huge concentration of troops. The enemy's objective was in the capital, Berlin, just in front of the city's fortified gates.

The Germans were to occupy all of the city and take over half the city to form the Reich. Within that year the enemy's forces were to be drawn up in three sectors. The south, located at the western edge of the city, was to consist of towns, farms and cities.
