
Dryas then saw a strange one: the mountain that ran in all directions.

They knew that this mountain had an open path but they knew that it had many cracks: those in between the mountains were small, and had holes in them. As if they had a weakness, the mountain had cracks which they had to make in order to traverse them in time.

This was where he was going.

He walked through the cracks where the mountains parted as the light of the sun set.

The darkness was almost completely lost as they ran forward and around the mountain.
