The bus drivers who make up the whole staff of R.M.F.T.C. are the ones carrying heavy metal to and from the train, not the trains.

At R.M.F.T.C. every train carries a train conductor who serves the train.

The bus operator takes the train out on an elevated bridge that cuts it to speed. The conductor makes sure every train and every passenger is seated smoothly, so the passengers stay at a reasonable level of comfort. On a train they know the tracks and the tracks are open.
