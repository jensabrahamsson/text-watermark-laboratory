The queue is a good thing. Not only is it open for passengers, there are even queueing drivers for the most part. We were lucky enough to get to see the trains in a good way, and it seemed like the people who did this are also really good drivers (I was one of them anyway).

The queue was not crowded, but it was packed because the train was delayed up to two hours. I can picture people waiting just outside the entrance to a train station waiting for the train to arrive.

As if I am not enough, there was even a bus stop with even more waiting stations.
