By this time people had gone to the exits: three on platforms four and five, and three on platforms 7, 8 and nine. At least three of the exits had been open. Many on the train ran at the other end of the corridor.

So when the doors opened, one of them said:

"We've got a train. You know when the doors have not opened? Because we've got a train, we can't get out till we get a ticket."

There was no wait. The ticket came from the station, but only if the train had no more passengers.
