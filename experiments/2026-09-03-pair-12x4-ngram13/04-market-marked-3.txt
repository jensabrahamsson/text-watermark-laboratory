The bus drivers stopped over to show my wife how they could take me to the hotel after I left.

I left my husband at the front. The bus stops stopped in front of her while I waited before she brought in the money. I knew she was right, but didn't want to think harder about what she had planned. I had to wait.

The next few days, while on my bike, I drove and talked with people of all walks of life, each of whom had their own opinions on some aspects of their city. Everyone knew I was a bad eater. I am still very bitter about this feeling
