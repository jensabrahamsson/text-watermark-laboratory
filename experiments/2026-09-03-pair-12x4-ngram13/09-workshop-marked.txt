The rest of the process took place using a drill and a wire mesh cutter.


The idea was to stick the wood directly where the pencil had been gouged with a small piece of a twig to hold the pencil, then sand it through the wood and push it through. A big metal needle inserted to make sure the pencil stick was flush with the wood took care of it. I then used a wire clamp to tie it tightly together and let it dry for a full hour before using the next step. I did this with some pretty special adhesive glued to the pencil.

A week later it was time for the drawing
