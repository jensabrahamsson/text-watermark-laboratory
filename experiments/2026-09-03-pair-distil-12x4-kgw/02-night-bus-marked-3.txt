I tell him if I am going back this time to the store for the lunch break. He seems to have missed us. He gets very upset. His voice is loud but when he looks over it is all out of the ordinary. I am afraid he will think again. I leave, and it is time to take the bus back home and get out of the car. After the bus leaves I pick up my groceries. Then it is time to leave on the bus.
At around 11 am they stop talking. I am afraid of their next trip. I go down on me again. I go back up after their last visit.
