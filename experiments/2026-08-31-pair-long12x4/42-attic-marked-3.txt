The door to my room was open and there were many pictures in the cabinet. My father, who came from the north I believe, was there. A woman in a white high suit, her hair hung down around her neck, lay with tears in her eyes.

She was the first woman I ever saw standing over a child. She was dressed in a white suit with a white tie, a white button, and black glasses and a bright red hat.
