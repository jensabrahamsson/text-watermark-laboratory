I opened the fishing rod and pulled out the string. I watched as the strings slowly loosened, the fishing rod tightening slightly. I thought that the sound of the floating strings was coming out and I was getting excited again.

After what felt like a very long time I realized that this was a trap. I quickly threw the fishing rod back inside. I caught one fish and another. I pulled out my camera and put it on my head to look at one last fish. The fishing rod that broke had already closed over again.

I turned, opened my eyes and looked back at the fish, he seemed very upset
