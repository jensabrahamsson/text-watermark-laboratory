"Whoop!" I cried, "How bad are you? I hope you'll just keep your face. Now!" I turned, and found his face almost perfectly red. His dark features were now, like the pale white of a dead bird, like the face of one with some kind of a dark moustache. All he saw was one face, and his face turned red, but his eyes were still red.
