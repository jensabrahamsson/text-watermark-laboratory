"Who is you?" I asked.

"I am the child of an aunt," she answered slowly, looking bored. I knew now that she was joking because she knew I would never understand what she was saying. She hadn't been looking at the clock or taking a shower. "What's up? Did you run into an old lady or something?!" What a joke, didn't she know? In the end she took out the first book I put down and left with "A Letter from Mrs. Thomas." She made her way past me through the crowd of kids and to the door on the left.
