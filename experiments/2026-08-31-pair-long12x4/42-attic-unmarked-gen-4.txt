At dusk the door swung open after me. I found a fellow traveller, a tall man named Lai who had gone for some coffee and who was giving me a letter from his secretary. For a day the letter was about to be delivered, and Lai had told him that the mail was being sent at this time. I went away for lunch at the cottage the following day and then took my leave to go find my wife to take care of my children.
