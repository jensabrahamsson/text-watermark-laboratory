I had come to the conclusion that the Earth was one giant black hole, and my eyes had been completely drained of life. I felt so strong inside, and my body was shaking. I knew I had to take a break, so I stood up and got my feet together.

"Are we still inside?" I asked. I had no idea what would happen after that.

"No," the radio said. I looked at the ceiling and the sky lit up.

"So you're still inside?"

I didn't care anyway. I didn't want to think about it anymore.
