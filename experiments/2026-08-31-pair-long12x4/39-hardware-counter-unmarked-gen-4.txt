The next evening the door clicked in my face. I had no idea where. At first my mind flashed again at this bizarre scenario. I remember standing here, naked, in a dark room. The floor to ceiling was flat as a sheet. A strange, warm glow hung over the walls. There was little light on me but it could be seen moving through the mist to a distant and distant corner, and then to another dark corner behind me, behind the counter, and then back into my room. I ran down the stairs and into my room and looked at the ceiling with curiosity.
