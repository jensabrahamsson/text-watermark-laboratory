"Oh, my God," I muttered, and I took my seat, then moved my chair and turned to my father. "You have to go. If not, you may be fined a dollar or two for this. You have to pay $500 or more if you get sued." He looked at me quizzically, and the word "vacation" began the syllogism he was so sure he heard. "Please tell me more later!" I begged.

"I was wrong," he said.

We sat on the patio; he looked about us, like I had seen in the other room.
