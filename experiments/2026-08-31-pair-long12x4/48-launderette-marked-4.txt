The machine sat at my desk, a pile of old newspaper, broken. I had been on top of it so long that I had lost my footing. It said I had been on another ship when the machine took off on a mission and was looking back here on Earth.

I turned my head and was surprised at the speed. It was about five minutes, but I knew that it would have taken me several hours. It had been an eternity since I had left the body.

When I was done I went downstairs in the darkness and was startled to find there were no other people alive on the ship.
