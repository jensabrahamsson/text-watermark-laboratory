The man, a half-baked boy from the village, looked me in the eye for a second. He was dark and wrinkled, with wrinkled eyes, which he had always had. The glasses, the flabby, unkempt beard, the old hair, the old boots, all were white, the sleeves of which were long and shaggy. His white coat was white, though slightly parted, but he stood erect.
