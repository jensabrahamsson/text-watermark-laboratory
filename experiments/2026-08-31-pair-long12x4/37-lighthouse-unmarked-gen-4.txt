"I see you can't see the moon," the woman said.

"The moon is all."

"The moon is all?"

"The moon is everywhere. If our light went on this thing would be gone."

"I see. We could not be there at all," I muttered. The woman was trying to persuade us to stop, but it was too late. But all would die.

I went over to watch the moon, seeing that it was dark, black and far away. The night was still dark, but it was not dark as I thought. All that was gone
