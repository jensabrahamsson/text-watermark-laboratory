A long night

The morning of that day, I saw my dad outside his house and saw the light on his door. It was hot, and he didn't bother to get up. Even when he was coming out of the house to get his clothes off, he always kept their windows on.

My sister and I went in to pick up his belongings. I could only watch his eyes through the open door, unable to make out a single thing. He always kept his jacket on and pulled down the front one.

The light from his porch lighted up the whole place, and I found the old school
