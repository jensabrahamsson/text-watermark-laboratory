"This is the village," a man with black hair, a beard cut short, said after a moment, raising a hand to cover my eyes. "Who is this person?"

The man looked very upset, in bad shape. He raised his voice and said: "I am not the village. I am here to bring this message to Yutaka. If you can help us the village will be made safe for the rest of us and the whole community. Please, bring this letter with you into my house."
