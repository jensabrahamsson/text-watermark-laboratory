The car is really fun to drive

At $3,900, my car is a bargain compared to my VW Golf. The steering wheel is a little stiff from all the bumps it's bumping into (the car is very low in weight, and I can drive it fast but it doesn't hurt at all). The steering wheel has two 4 speed gears (which work and look a bit less like VW's) so I could switch gears, brake and adjust the suspension, but you need to be careful when you do so. I'm not a big fan of wheel drive (I've never wanted to be a pro) but when I do turn and push the pedal, I like the feeling that it's the right place for me. The car is quite light for the price, and as for how it is used, there isn't much to complain about. I've never even felt a small push from a car in my life, and I'm not too sure if I want a new car.

I've used two of the old VW's and they are super light! In an ideal world, the wheels would be super cheap and a few hundred won, and they wouldn't make me regret the upgrade. In my opinion, there are no compromises for this engine, and the suspension will be pretty decent too. But, I think that this engine could be upgraded for the best value for money, and the cost is a little too high. I'm definitely looking forward to trying out these engines next year, I'd love to drive. I may buy one of these when I get ready, but for now, get your money's worth before I turn 65.
