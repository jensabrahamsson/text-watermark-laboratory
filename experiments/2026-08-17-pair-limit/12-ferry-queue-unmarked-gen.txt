The Sea of Oktoberfest

In early March 1997 Sea of Oktoberfest took place in the waters of the Norwegian Arctic Ocean in the middle of the last century. In this photo taken in 1995, the boats from the same expedition of the first 50 boats were seen sailing out of the Arctic in front of a large crowd cheering the arrival of the second boat in view. The boat arrived at an unload site on Lake Nærvinen for three days with no passengers on board: in June of 1997 four of the missing sailors got off, then headed northwest into northern Norway, to a search party of sorts that followed.
