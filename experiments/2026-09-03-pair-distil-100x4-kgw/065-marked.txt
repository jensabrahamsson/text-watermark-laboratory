And he was left with an open mind, which at present seemed to me a sign of the man who had the heart. He was so busy making an impression of his soul of the moment, which still seems very little to me at all. He could only dream of this thing, and could only dream of it; and this would never last; and I thought it ought not to. The whole affair must have been an accident.
