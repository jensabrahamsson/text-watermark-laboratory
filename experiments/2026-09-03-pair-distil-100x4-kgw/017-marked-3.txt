



Mackenzie Leach, the NFL's official special teams coach, has made clear there is a belief Leach has to go ahead and make the right decisions when the Lions' offensive coordinator finds the right fit in for the 2017 Lions' playoff challenge in 2014 and 2018.



"We want to make an informed decision that our offensive coordinator is ready and ready for every season where his team's offensive coordinator is at the helm to ensure he can have the right decisions and make significant decisions when the Lions have a legitimate chance," a source close to Leach said at the annual NFL Scouting Combine Friday.
