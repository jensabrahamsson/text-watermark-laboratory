As soon as another person walked to the gate at the entrance, the man stood facing down onto the side of the gate and said "I need to help you."
Then his hair fell and a woman and two men were inside. The man walked into a small room in the kitchen when they saw him from the right and said "Where is your money going?"
He told me he was an immigrant and had just left in Canada. The woman and two men went to sit. Both men talked until they got home. One of the men told him, the woman and the man tried to break and ran away from me.
