"After the first day on our journey, at which point, I heard this voice," Mrs. Collins said.
"I thought I should be home, and I thought I should be on the road with my young children. It was all that went on. So it is a pleasure to be home, I'll remember."
She added: "I wish my children had had the benefit of the opportunity."
At home, however, the pair had been in tears and had a great time after Christmas.
Mrs. Collins was happy to be home, the family said , as well as her child.
