The project is an armchair with a curved backrest and feet that swing out from under the front legs, as shown in the image above. The arms can be removed for easy transport, although this would defeat the purpose of having them on the first place. However, if you don’t want those removable arms and only have the curved backrest and foot extension option, it’s also available with a 16" seat depth and optional casters. And, of course, there’s always the option of customizing the armchair according to your personal taste.
