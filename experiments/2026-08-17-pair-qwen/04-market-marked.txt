- 2 slices of parmissan aged, double cream
- Tangerine orange zest
- Pesto di pomodoro
Method:
1: Rub the chicken breasts with tangerina zest and a few cloves of garlic. Season well inside and out with salt, pepper, olive oil. Add the herbs – bay leaves, thyme, rosemary, fresh rose and lemon rind. Put the breast in a preheated roasting tray at an angle so that the sauce dripped down. Roast for around 60 minutes until very tender, brushing from high angle and baste with oil between times.
