Everyone in platforms Two, Four, and Eight waited for the arrival of the 6:45 Train to the Coast. None of them cared about who was getting off next nor did they have any plans for the trip. The rain started so there would be no way to travel anyways, even if the weather had cleared up before the 6am call from the station.
I arrived at eight o'clock sharp. It was raining by then and I was cold just standing out here. My shoes were soaked through by the time they finally boarded the train.
