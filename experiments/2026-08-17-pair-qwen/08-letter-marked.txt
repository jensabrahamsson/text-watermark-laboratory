The truth will not change, but to find happiness or make others happy may be hard. Life is a process of learning who you truly are and what makes you happy.
So why was this note written? There was nothing wrong with this person and everything wrong with this note. It hurt to write it, but it showed me something about myself. I think I’m too self-critical. My father told me years ago that the hardest thing in life is to live so closely to one another yet so far apart.
I have no idea how many people have read these words from my pen. They are all mine, but there could be more
