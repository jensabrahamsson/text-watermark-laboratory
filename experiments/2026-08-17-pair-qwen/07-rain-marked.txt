Now that there was no hole yet, it felt fine to take this chance. We walked through the grass for another ten feet or so, then turned right onto the street with the light.
A very busy hour of traffic and cars passed our way in several small seconds - all but two were white, except one - dark black. Two more came at us out of nowhere. This made me uneasy; they hadn't even seen us. Then, in front of us, three stopped for their red lights, but didn't go away when the signals indicated them to keep going. Now we're actually stuck.
It is not an unfamiliar thing.
