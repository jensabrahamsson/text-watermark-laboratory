Foggy, cloudy morning

Dawn came before dawn, covered everything with silver curtains before it came fully apart, as if a big wave had swept away some cloud cover and left nothing behind but fog and mist. There wasn't even sunlight anymore, just a pale green edge and the distant shine of a street lamp in the distance.
