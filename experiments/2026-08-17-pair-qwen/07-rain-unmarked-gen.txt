"Would you like some tea?" Rain said, but she didn't want any; her lips were still cold from the wind. She could hear people arguing about how they wanted their house to look like in spring, or how they should be able to see the sky through the window without windows. In fact, she couldn't remember ever seeing anyone who hadn't been afraid that someone else would do it better. If someone's house was perfect, then it might make sense to live there, even though she knew the people who lived in it wouldn't have been pleased by this revelation.
Her mother was in the kitchen when she came in.
