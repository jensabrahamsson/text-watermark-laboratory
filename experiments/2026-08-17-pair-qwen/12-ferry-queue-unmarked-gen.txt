The sun rose over the mountains. I was walking from my house to work in the small town where I live. It's a little village but big enough for four or five people. It was about ten o'clock. The streets were empty except for me.
I am one of the few who works here; all the others have moved away after their children left school. We call ourselves 'the older generation' because we've been around too long to be young any more.
