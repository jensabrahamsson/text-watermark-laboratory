After the line is complete the bus stops on the other side a few yards. We head and go to the street where the buses are stopped and get some rest. The bus stops for me about six or seven times a row on the sidewalk or next to my house. As I walk along streets there is a huge white sign on it that says "For the best value in this place, buy a ticket in advance, for a one-way ride on the way home." All the people and cars there are full of young people trying to make an impression on the rest of the town. It is impossible for me to understand the streets
