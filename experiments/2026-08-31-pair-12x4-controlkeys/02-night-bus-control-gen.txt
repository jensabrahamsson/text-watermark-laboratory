After the line is complete there is a long drive away before you arrive at the gate. I walk a few steps and I find myself at a station there in one of the busiest station areas in the whole world. It is two-lane, and two-way. It always felt like the best option.

I was on one of the new trains and it seems like everyone gets on the bus. Even I think there were many people out of this one. Then I remember that nobody ever stops at the train stop again. All the time they stay there until late at night.
