After the third time I leave my chair and leave and head in the direction of the bridge. I turn to make sure there is nothing else on it that wouldn't fit into my pocket. No more maps. The car stops for a minute, then breaks into two. I wait, and when it does I move.

I cannot think about how long I have left here for. A week. A century. What will come of this. The canal, to this day, is empty. All who live are here. You are here. I am here too.
