After a while, you would know exactly where your ferry was by looking out the window. You could not get to it, because nobody knew where it was and how it could be reached. Then the ferry would come.

The whole situation was a long one. We had to wait on the end of the ramp where there were the first lines of stairs (the one after the main entrance), in order to avoid getting hit by the cars. Then we would be given extra seating for two people.

The main line at the side was for passengers only. They could not climb to the next ramp without seeing the ferry.
