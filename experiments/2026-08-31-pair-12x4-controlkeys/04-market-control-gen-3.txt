After the first ten minutes, there were four queues and one stallholder. Then, I saw four waiters in the audience behind me. They said they were happy to pay. I said yes and they gave me lunch. They said I would pay for my cheese after two. A lady told me that she had heard about three dozen stalls for cheese were sold in this city.

That day is when we went out to buy the eggs for the chickens. We paid in cash and took one last look at the stallholders at the corner entrance of the stall.
