After closing time, I find myself back inside. I walk out into the night.

The map is still on my desk, there is nothing there. I do not see my mother any longer, it is only through the rain, and the road around. But, on top of that I do not feel the slightest thing of remorse: the water has gone to good use. My life now ends for no reason beyond: my mother has been taken away, and there is little more to spend on. What I have done with her is not my only regret, but it is the only thing I regret in life.
