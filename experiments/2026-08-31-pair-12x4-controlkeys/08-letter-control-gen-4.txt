After the first draft I called them each asking them to sign the letter. And they both gave me a hand and agreed to send the letter to Iain, saying I could write it for me for two cents a page. In the end it fell to one of two characters. And the other was plain language, if I were going to keep writing letters like this. A letter of invitation was not enough for the two. But it was enough for the one.
