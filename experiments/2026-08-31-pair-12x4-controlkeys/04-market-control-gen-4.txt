After a while, the queue was stopped. And that was when it happened again. The wait began. I didn't do anything, I did not eat or drink or even talk to the stallholder. No-one did anything to stop me. No one was paying attention to me either. Just a stray dog of course.

But the dog wouldn't eat her milk. Instead, it would stare at her as if it were hungry. I didn't get fed. I went for the milk.
