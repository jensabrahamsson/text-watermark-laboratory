After the line had left, two men asked the conductor to stop and wait for the train to come in. They took three steps towards the railway track at the bottom of the hill and then walked towards the train.

A few blocks to the northeast, a man at the bottom of the hill was standing on the floor of his small shop when they came to a stop. He looked out at the train on the other side, he looked in the direction where the rail doors lead, but nothing. The train seemed to move. The man's head seemed to move through the air, it seemed like it was spinning in his mind
