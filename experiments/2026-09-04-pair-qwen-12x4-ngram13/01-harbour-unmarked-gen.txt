It was cold this morning and damp in the streets of Tenerife. I felt the chill of salt in the air around me as I walked, though it didn't feel much colder than most other mornings in Madrid. When you're sick of Europe's concrete world, places like this are more beautiful than the pictures, but also more frighteningly similar. At night, they're still haunted by people who don't seem to know what their life should be.
