The day before he left for his trip, I read to him the letters I had written him in the course of our seven years of marriage. There were so many tears that I cried myself sick.
There is something about the way you feel when someone takes all your heart. That’s how I felt. It wasn’t love at first sight – we’d dated on and off since high school – but there was a depth, an intimacy, an understanding between us that didn’t come easy. We had grown up together and had been best friends from the time we were little kids.
He was gone only hours after I put the last one away
