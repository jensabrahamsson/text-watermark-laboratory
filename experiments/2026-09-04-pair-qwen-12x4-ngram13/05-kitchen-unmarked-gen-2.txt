"Four people have been killed in the bombings of Kabul by unknown assailants," the newsreader said in a matter-of-fact voice.
Aunt had a bad heart. She took it hard. Her blood pressure dropped to an alarming level when she heard the news on the radio. I remember the panic as much as if it happened yesterday, or just a day ago. "Oh, honey, are you sure? We can’t go through with this."
I nodded, "Yes." It’s not that there aren’t enough bombs going off around the world; there is more than enough. But the last one had been in London.
