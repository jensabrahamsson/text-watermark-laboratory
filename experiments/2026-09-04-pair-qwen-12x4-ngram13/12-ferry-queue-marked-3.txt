The driver looked at the woman with the small bag of groceries she was pushing, and the man with his briefcase.
The other people stopped walking, too, all waiting for something they didn’t know. They had their heads lowered or they looked out of windows to see what it was.
The driver said “I’m sorry,” over the loudspeaker as he pulled up next to them. The people turned to him like they had never seen him before; some of them smiled, others stared. The one who had looked out of the window saw the man’s smile.
