I opened the oven door slowly, the door handle was like ice on my palm. A couple of cookies. The first one fell to the floor, then another.
I picked up the third cookie and took a bite. I felt something in my stomach move. I swallowed quickly. Then it happened: this sweet salty explosion that made me smile until I cried. I put the plate down on the counter, I sat back, I breathed.
