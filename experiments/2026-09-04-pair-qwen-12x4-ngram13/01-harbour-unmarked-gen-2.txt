In the morning, before breakfast, I found my way down to the beach. There was some kind of protest in the distance. People who had come for sunbathing now looked around at the sea that seemed somehow different but more important. They moved their heads back and forth between the waves and the rocks, then walked towards the edge, where a line of old boats stretched along the shore. When they got close, they put their heads down again and let their eyes fall upon the water. A couple started to walk over, looking nervous. But there was no need: everyone else had gone.
