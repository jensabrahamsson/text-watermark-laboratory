No one noticed the man in blue, the one who looked at the ground as he crossed the platform. No one even acknowledged him until the doors opened and a woman with long, black hair pulled into a high ponytail emerged from them. Her eyes were round as saucers, like a frightened child looking for its mother.
“Are you going to the airport?” she asked in an accent no one recognized.
He nodded but did not speak. She followed his movement and realized that he had already taken two steps past her.
“I’m coming with you.” She grabbed the back of his shirt.
“Hey!” He jerked away.
