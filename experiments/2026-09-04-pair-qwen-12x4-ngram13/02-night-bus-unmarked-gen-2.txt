There was once an old lady sitting next to me who read books on the subway. She told me stories about a boy she used to know in France. I asked her if he was dead now but she said he wasn't, he lived in a big house near the beach. He was so handsome she still remembered his hair being blonde and how much money he had spent on clothes.
She also said there was another girl in the same class whose mother had been a famous singer. This girl became rich herself and bought a big villa outside Paris, but her mother died of cancer years later.

I remember the name of that villa, Saint Martin
