The day was long, the water was flat and the temperature rose until it seemed the sun would break through. The ferry started up the hill and the children went to sleep; when they woke there was more snow and the air was clear as the night before.
The bus left first. I could hear the wheels grinding on the gravel and the metal clinking as the driver adjusted his brake. There were no seats for the others and we stood around talking and waiting until the bus had gone. We watched the other buses pass us by – some empty, others full.
