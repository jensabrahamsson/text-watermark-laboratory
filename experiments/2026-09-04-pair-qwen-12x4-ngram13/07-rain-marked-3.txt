The day was so bright with all the sun. But when rain returned, it was dark. When he looked out, he saw that there was a long line of houses where his step was at the top. Then he knew he would be waiting for quite a while before it rained hard enough to start filling his bucket.
A couple walked their dog along the side of the road. They stopped by the stop sign at the beginning of the block and waited for the traffic light. Rain followed them down the street as they turned right and then left again. Rain had been following him since he woke up this morning.
