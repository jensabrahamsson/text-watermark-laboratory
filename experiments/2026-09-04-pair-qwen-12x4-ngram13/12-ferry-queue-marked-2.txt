The day was long, too long, and she didn’t understand why her husband kept leaving the room so often, never to return. In one place he’d left behind a notebook full of words about this or that, while elsewhere his pen lay abandoned, its ink dry. At least the coffee machine worked; if they were to drink it all before evening time, their children would need to be in bed by eight o’clock.
She was tired, too, and wanted nothing more than to go to sleep now, but he had not yet come back. Perhaps he was already asleep himself? Or perhaps he was still standing at the window
