A train station is like a city but smaller; there are no streets, only platforms and tracks and ticket booths that sell tickets for the next train or another. It can be raining and you can't see anything; you can't see how far down the line there are more trains waiting. But you know enough about these things to count the steps to the platform. If the numbers match the schedule it's clear. Otherwise the weather can be an excuse.
