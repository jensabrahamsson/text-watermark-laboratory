The station was a dark rectangle of black iron, an endless stretch of floor and nooks where a few stray pigeons pecked at scraps left by tourists and lost travellers. At the end of the station, a few trees had sprouted up in front of a dilapidated building with its doors boarded over. The train arrived as the lights flickered on and off, but it didn’t open the door until the last passengers had squeezed onto their platforms.
