I got home and looked through my shopping bags but could not find the cheese. The queue had started walking away so I had to go back and buy another one. The cheesemonger told me that he had just lost it but did not know how much of it it was. So, I went out again on my own and found a small bag with the rest of it inside.
When I went back into the shop, they gave me two pennies for the coin I had paid for the cheese. Then I asked for some more. They said no. I then said I wanted all of it, which meant 20p
