I got a bit of a shock as the man at the till counted my change for me, even though it wasn’t necessary. I thought about that now, but didn’t dwell on it too much. It seems to me that we are all in the same boat: everyone has the same amount of time in which to make their money work hard for them, so the most efficient way is by taking what’s offered to you, rather than asking how much they can give you.
