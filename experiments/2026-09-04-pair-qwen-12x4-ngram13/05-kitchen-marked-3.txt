I opened the oven door slowly, the first thing I see is the golden crust, crisp on all sides. A little bit of burnt smell wafts out but it doesn’t bother me. My daughter, four, runs in excitedly: ‘Mum! Mummy, look!’ She points to the bottom layer of garlic cloves; they’re not quite browned, but just barely.
As she walks away, the door opens up to air, I check them carefully – only one is burned on the outside now. In a few minutes, they’ll be done.
