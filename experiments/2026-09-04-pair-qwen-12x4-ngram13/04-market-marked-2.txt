I got out of the car and went into a shop which was a mixture of clothes, books and stationery. They had a large selection of stationery but also some interesting children’s stories on their tables. I browsed for half an hour and found a book that had been published yesterday; I liked it so much I bought two copies. The lady at the desk asked me if I could give her £50, £10 notes or £20 notes. She seemed very surprised that someone would ask such a question.
