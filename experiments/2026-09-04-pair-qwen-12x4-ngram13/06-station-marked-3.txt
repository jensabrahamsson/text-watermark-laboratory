The station was a grey expanse, only slightly brighter than the surrounding sky; the windows had no glass but clear Plexiglas so they reflected a faint blue-green light. At regular intervals, something or someone walked through them, perhaps for security reasons, but maybe just because people are always walking by.
Some passengers sat in small rows of hard chairs, waiting for a bus to come from the north, or a tram or a taxi, and some stood on benches that seemed to have been there forever. The trains were not scheduled at all here, and the tracks ended as abruptly as the station did.
