I have had friends for 40 years, but I have never met them in person. We text and call, of course. But I think they are people who are not really real to me. They are only images from their photos or screen capture profiles on social media, people who exist in my mind's eye as though they were tangible and alive. And I am not so far removed from the other side that we do not recognize each other. If I lived there, I'd see myself walking the same streets with those same friends, the way I've seen it on the screen.
