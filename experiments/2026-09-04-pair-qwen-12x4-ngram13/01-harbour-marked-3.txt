**THE SAD TRAVELER**

The night fell before we got home. When I woke, I found myself alone in the bedroom. I tried the bathroom first because I felt ill, but when I entered it, it was empty except for a pair of boots standing by the sink. I knew they belonged to someone who would have been leaving in the morning. The boots matched mine, and so did the shoes, and the socks, and the belt, and everything else. I wondered whether I was still at the hotel or whether I was somewhere else entirely. I had taken the bus that night and could be anywhere by now.
