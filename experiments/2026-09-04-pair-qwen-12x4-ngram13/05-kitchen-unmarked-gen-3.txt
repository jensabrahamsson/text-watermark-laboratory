This is a little piece of one summer evening. Not really about the recipe but more about the ingredients—garlic, lemon zest, bay leaves, fresh mint—and how they transform together on your kitchen counter.
Aren’t we all living this kind of moment? Sometimes we don’t know what to do with ourselves; or sometimes we have no idea where our food comes from. And when the world feels like a bigger kitchen than the ones we’re cooking in, you look for ways to make yourself feel better.
One of those ways is by making good food and keeping a supply that can feed others as well. It’s a simple pleasure
