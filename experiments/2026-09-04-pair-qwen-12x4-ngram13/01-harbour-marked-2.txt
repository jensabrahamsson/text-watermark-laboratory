**LADIES**

By the time we reached the main road the rain had stopped and it felt suddenly warm. There was a slight smell of salt still clinging to my clothes, but otherwise everything seemed normal: shops full of people, the same people standing at the bar drinking beer with their mates, a group of women singing loudly by the side of the road. I walked beside them until they turned right and I followed, not knowing what they were going to do. At the next crossroads there were more men waiting, smoking pipes.
