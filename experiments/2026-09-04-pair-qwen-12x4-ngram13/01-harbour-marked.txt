**LONELINESS**

My mother died two years ago this month. She wasn't exactly lonely; she was alone but it felt more like a loneliness than solitude. She never saw people. But I can remember her standing at a bus stop, holding onto the railing, and saying, "Hello." Sometimes we'd sit at a table at the park and I'd tell her I'd seen someone I knew in passing, and she'd say, "Oh really? What's his/her name?"
