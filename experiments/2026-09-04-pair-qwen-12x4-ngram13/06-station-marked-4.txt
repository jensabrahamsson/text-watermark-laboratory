The station was a shanty town of wooden huts, tin roofs, broken benches, and peeling paint. All boarded trains for the coastal stations on the west bank. Everyone had some place to go. But no-one knew where they were going. Or why.
As the crowd began to move, people grabbed bags from their backs. They moved in tight, silent formations toward the front of the train. Some walked slowly down the center aisle. People waved goodbye. Some started to talk, but few actually spoke.
