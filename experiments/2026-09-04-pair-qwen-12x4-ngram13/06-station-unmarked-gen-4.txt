He turned his attention to a young woman with short hair who was leaning against the wall, staring at her feet. She looked like she was waiting for someone. When the sound of a train pulled up and stopped he looked back toward her. Her head came up from its hole in the ground, as though it had been dug there by a spade, and she spoke: "You look like you're thinking about going somewhere." He shrugged.
"Anywhere?"
"Somewhere else."
