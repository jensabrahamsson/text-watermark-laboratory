I opened the oven door slowly, the first thing I see is the golden crust, crisp on all sides. A little bit of steam rises. I take a small knife and slice into the top half of the roll. It’s soft, yet very slightly crisp from under. I eat a bite and I’m not surprised to realize that they were right: it wasn’t just me who enjoyed these cookies. Everyone else has done this since, but it doesn’t mean much until the third attempt. This one was good enough for me.
